Clans in **The Division Resurgence** look like a social feature and play like a progression system. They unlock a shared Clan Base, a vendor that sells things you can't buy anywhere else, passive stat bonuses and a big cut to your crafting timer. Ubisoft's own Help Center says active clan members "can progress faster than solo players, especially in mid and endgame content." That's as close to an official "please join a clan" as you're going to get.

Everything below comes from Ubisoft's Help Center clan section and the official patch notes. One honest caveat: the Help Center clan pages were last updated about six months ago, in spring 2026, so a menu name or a price may have moved since. Where it matters, we say so.

> [!INFO] Quick answers
> Clans unlock at **level 10**. The member cap starts at **30**. Creating one costs **300 Phoenix Credits** (per the Help Center). Membership is **per character**, not per account. A full clan crafting boost cuts craft time by **50%**.

## What clans actually give you

From Ubisoft's [What advantages do clans offer?](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2498-what-advantages-do-clans-offer/?p=all) page:

| Perk | What Ubisoft says it does |
|---|---|
| **Clan Base** | A private, instanced hub with facilities you upgrade together |
| **Clan vendor** | Sells high-end and rare gear you can't get elsewhere |
| **Passive bonuses** | Damage, survivability and efficiency boosts unlocked by base upgrades |
| **Weekly clan projects** | Reward gear, materials and currencies |
| **Shared progress** | Upgrades are shared, so coordinated play moves everyone forward |

The two that matter most for your build, in our experience of reading too many patch notes:

- **The clan vendor.** It sells Tactical Augmentation Modules for weapons and gear, and Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 2 notes added one more copy of each regular Weapon and Gear module to it. Creator GGReborn lists ignoring clan credits as one of his biggest early mistakes, which we cover in our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/).
- **The crafting bonus.** Season 2 Phase 1 cut base crafting time from 24 hours to **15 hours** and set the maximum clan duration reduction at **50%**. A fully boosted craft takes **7.5 hours**. That's two crafts a day instead of one, which is the difference between "eventually" and "this week" on a gold roll. Full details are in our [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/).

## How to join a clan

Per Ubisoft's [How do I join a clan?](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2499-how-do-i-join-a-clan/?p=all):

1. Reach **level 10**. Clans unlock there.
2. Open the **Clan** menu from the main screen.
3. Use **Clan Search** to browse clans.
4. Join. **Open** clans let you straight in; others send your request to the leader for approval.

Membership is **character specific**, so if you run an alt, it joins (or doesn't) on its own.

**What to look for in a clan:** you want people who actually log in. Clan points come from members playing, upgrades are paid from the shared pool, and the crafting reduction comes from the base. A 30-person clan where 4 people log in is a very nice empty building.

## How to create a clan

Per Ubisoft's [How do I create a clan?](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2500-how-do-i-create-a-clan/?p=all):

1. Reach level 10.
2. Open the Clan menu and choose **Create Clan**.
3. Pay the one-time fee, which the Help Center lists as **300 Phoenix Credits**. Check the in-game price before you confirm.
4. Set a **name** (letters only, no numbers or symbols), a **description**, an **emblem**, and whether people can **join freely or need your approval**.

You become the **Clan Leader** automatically, which means every complaint about the vendor rotation is now technically your fault.

## Running a clan

Ubisoft's [How do I manage a clan?](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2501-how-do-i-manage-a-clan/?p=all) says only the Clan Leader (and promoted officers) can manage it. That covers:

- Approving or declining join requests
- Promoting or removing members
- Changing the clan's name, description and emblem
- Choosing which clan upgrades to prioritize
- Tracking each member's activity and contributions

To **kick** someone, the leader taps the player and picks **Kick from Clan** at the bottom of the action list ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2502-how-do-i-remove-someone-from-my-clan/?p=all)).

**Clan size:** the cap starts at **30 players** and rises with Clan Base upgrades ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2503-how-many-people-can-i-have-in-my-clan/?p=all)).

**Leaders, log in.** Ubisoft's known-issues board has listed clan leaders losing the role after only one day of inactivity, with the team looking at lengthening that timer. We broke that board down [here](/news/division-resurgence-known-issues-trello-board-breakdown/). Until it changes, a weekend away can cost you the crown.

## Clan points, supplies and credits

Per Ubisoft's [clan points page](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2507-how-do-i-collect-and-gift-clan-points/?p=all), clan resources come from:

- Daily and weekly activities
- Missions and world events
- Weekly clan projects

The basic income is automatic, so there's no manual donating. It all goes into a **shared pool**, and the leader decides which upgrades to spend it on.

Ubisoft's disband page explains how the two currencies fit together: **supplies** go toward facility upgrades, and you earn **clan credits** by contributing supplies, then spend those credits at the clan vendor. Supplies can't be kept, moved or carried to another clan, which Ubisoft says is there to stop people farming supplies in one clan and dumping them in another.

One economy note from the patch notes: since Season 1 Phase 2, Tactical Augmentation Modules dismantle into **Encrypted Data** instead of clan credits. So don't dismantle a module expecting a clan credit refund.

## Leaving or disbanding a clan

**Leaving:** open the Clan menu, go to **Clan Info / Roster** and choose to leave. You can leave at any time unless you're the Clan Leader ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2504-how-can-i-leave-a-clan/?p=all)).

**Disbanding** is leader only, and it is the nuclear option ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2505-how-do-i-disband-a-clan/?p=all), [steps](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2506-disbanding-steps/?p=all)):

1. Open **Clan Management**.
2. Remove every other member.
3. Select **Disband Clan** and confirm.

Ubisoft is blunt about it: the action is permanent, and all remaining supplies, credits, upgrades and progression are lost and can't be recovered or transferred. If you're disbanding to merge with another clan, spend everything first. Your future self, staring at a level 1 Clan Base, will appreciate it.
