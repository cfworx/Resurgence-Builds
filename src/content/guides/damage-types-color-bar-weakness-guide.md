> Season 2 Phase 1 (August 5) removed the Blast, Shredding and Piercing Weapon/Skill Damage bonus attributes from weapons in favor of All Weapon Damage or All Skill Damage (new weapon mods lost them too), because a type bonus on a weapon of a different type "didn't bring any benefits and was frustrating." Weapons still have their own damage type, shown in the Armory, so the bar-matching advice below still stands as far as we can tell. You just can't chase type-specific rolls anymore. ## What Does the Color Bar Above Enemy Health Mean in Division Resurgence?

There is a small colored bar sitting above the health of every elite, named, and yellow-bar enemy in The Division Resurgence. It is the single most useful piece of UI in the entire game. The game does not explain what it means. Not in the tutorial. Not in the SHD manual. Not in the journal. Not in the loading screen tips that you already skip anyway.

Here is what the bar means. One sentence each. Free of charge.

- **Dark blue bar** = this enemy is weak to **Piercing** damage
- **Light blue bar** = this enemy is weak to **Shredding** damage
- **Red bar** = this enemy is weak to **Blast** damage

If you are running a Blast weapon (Warlord, Lady Death, anything whose own damage type is Blast) and the enemies in your mission are showing light blue bars, you are doing reduced damage. You have been doing reduced damage since you equipped that gun. The game just never told you. You're welcome.

This was confirmed on camera by [AgentHuntzman in his SHD Manual video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mt3E4A0l0Zk) and verified against community testing, where every Warlord owner has been told for months that Blast is best against red-armor enemies but nobody connected it to the visual indicator that's been sitting on screen since launch.

## Where Is the Damage Weakness Bar and Why Did Nobody Tell You?

Open the game. Walk into any open-world hostile zone. Find a yellow-bar enemy. Look above their health bar.

There's a thin colored strip flush against the top edge. It's short, it's subtle, and it looks like decoration. It is not decoration. It is the game telling you, in plain UI, what damage type will hurt this enemy the most.

If you've been playing since launch and never noticed this, congratulations. You're in the majority. The post in our research channel that first surfaced this had three Resurgence content creators in the comments going "wait, that's what that's for?" One of them has been making class guides since day one. Nobody is immune to missing the obvious.

Here is the part that should be in the tutorial but isn't:

**The bar color is the enemy's weakness, not their strength.** A red bar doesn't mean the enemy deals blast damage. It means they take extra damage FROM blast. It reads exactly the way every other vulnerability indicator works in every other looter shooter ever made. The game just assumed you'd figure it out. You didn't. Neither did we.

## What Color Matches What Damage Type?

| Color Above Health Bar | Enemy is Weak To | Bring | Example Weapons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark blue | Piercing | A Piercing-type weapon | T2 Snipers, PPSh-41, Piercing-type marksman rifles and heavy ARs |
| Light blue | Shredding | A Shredding-type weapon | Strawberry Milkshake (Shredding exotic LMG), Shredding-type LMGs, MK17, M249 |
| Red | Blast | A Blast-type weapon | Warlord (Blast exotic), Lady Death (Blast SMG), explosive snipers |

The example weapons are community-reported types from spring 2026 (Strawberry Milkshake's is official). Confirm any non-exotic in the Armory before you build around it.

A few caveats before you grab this table and sprint to your loadout screen:

1. **The attribute numbers from older guides were never active damage.** "Weapon Blast Damage: 870" on Lady Death didn't mean 870 flat blast damage per shot. It was the attribute roll value that fed into the damage formula, and Season 2 Phase 1 pulled those type-specific attributes out of the weapon pool anyway, so don't go hunting for them on new drops. The formula is still more complicated than your last tax return.
2. **Damage type matters even when the bar isn't visible.** Red-bar armor types show up on enemies that don't always have the full elite frame. If one random dude in an open-world event is taking forever to die, look for the bar. He probably has one.
3. **Hard difficulty leans heavily on Blast-weakness enemies.** This is why Warlord and Lady Death feel disproportionately strong at Hard but get less spectacular in Heroic and Legendary, where the enemy mix balances out across all three types.

## How Do Piercing, Shredding, and Blast Damage Work in Division Resurgence?

These aren't cosmetic labels. As far as community testing can tell, each type routes through a different damage equation and interacts with armor differently.

**Piercing.** The anti-armor tool. Piercing performs best against armored enemies. Dark blue bar = the game flagged this enemy's armor as vulnerable to piercing. Think heavy gunners and named elites with extra armor layers. AgentHuntzman's summary: Piercing "performs extremely well against armored enemies." Bring a sniper or a Piercing-type AR and watch the armor plates peel off.

**Shredding.** The sustained pressure tool. Shredding scales well against enemies with thick HP pools but lighter armor. Light blue bar = strip them with continuous fire. LMGs and high-RPM weapons love this damage type because shredding rewards holding the trigger, not one-tap bursts. If your play style is "hold left click and pray," shredding is your damage type.

**Blast.** The raw HP demolition tool. Red bar = bring the boom. Warlord, Lady Death, any other Blast-type weapon, anything that detonates. Blast deals the biggest single-instance damage spikes in the game. It was also the most common exotic damage type when we wrote this (two of the first three exotics), which is why half the player base thinks Blast is the best type. It's not the best type. It's just the type that shipped on the guns everyone wanted.

Here is the important part:

> **A Blast weapon shooting a Shredding-weakness enemy is not doing zero damage. It's doing reduced damage compared to a Shredding weapon shooting that same enemy.** Think of the mismatch penalty as a damage tax, not a damage wall. You can clear content with the wrong type. You'll just clear it slower, eat more cover time, and burn more ammo. For solo content, that tax is annoying. For Legendary group content where every second matters, that tax is what separates a smooth run from a wipe at the final phase.

## How Do You Check Your Weapon's Damage Type?

Check the weapon in the **Armory**. That's where Ubisoft's patch notes say the damage type is displayed: Season 1 Phase 3 fixed Strawberry Milkshake showing up there as Piercing instead of Shredding, and Season 2 Phase 2 fixed incorrect damage type info for certain other weapons. If you checked a weapon before September 16 and the answer looked weird, check again.

**The old shortcut is dead.** Before Season 2 Phase 1, people read the weapon's attribute panel instead:

- **Weapon Blast Damage: X** = Blast
- **Weapon Piercing Damage: X** = Piercing
- **Weapon Shredding Damage: X** = Shredding

That shortcut was always a trap, because a weapon could roll a damage-type bonus that didn't match its own type. The Police M4 was spotted rolling Weapon Blast Damage even though community testing shows it deals Shredding on impact. Ubisoft confirmed the problem in the Season 2 Phase 1 notes (a mismatched bonus "didn't bring any benefits"), then removed all damage-type bonuses from the weapon attribute pool in favor of All Weapon Damage or All Skill Damage. New weapons won't roll them. The notes don't spell out what happened to rolls already sitting on old weapons, so if one of yours still shows a mismatched type bonus, treat it as dead weight.

**Exotics are locked to one damage type.** Warlord = Blast. Lady Death = Blast. Strawberry Milkshake = Shredding. Scorpio = Piercing (Died Again Gaming read it off the weapon card in June), and TikTokAndy's Bighorn showed Piercing in his September 16, 2026 video. You cannot reroll any of them. The two Blast exotics shine against red-bar enemies and tax you against everything else. Strawberry Milkshake has the opposite profile, strong against light-blue-bar enemies. The Diamondback is Shredding: Capitão Wasabi's in-game card (September 23, Portuguese client) labels its damage "Dano de trituração" with the claw icon, which isn't the Blast or Piercing icon, so it pairs with light-blue-bar enemies like the Milkshake. Details in our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/).

## Why Does the Warlord Feel Weak in Some Missions?

This section is going to upset the Warlord truthers. Get it over with like ripping off a bandaid.

Warlord is a powerful Blast exotic AR. It deletes red-bar elites. It also underperforms significantly against shredding-weakness and piercing-weakness enemies. In any mission where the enemy mix is mostly light-blue or dark-blue, our rough estimate is that your Warlord is doing something like 60 to 70 percent of the damage it does on a perfect red-bar Hard mission. You have not been imagining the inconsistency. You've been ignoring the color bar.

Same logic for Lady Death. The 870 Weapon Blast Damage stat it showed in spring looked impressive on paper. It's doing real work on red-bar enemies. It's doing noticeably less work on the shredding-weakness Cleaners in late campaign content.

The community read:

> A Warlord with Blast damage and good talents is a top 3 AR in the game IF you match it to the right enemy type. If you're running it as your only AR across every mission, you're leaving damage on the table on roughly two thirds of your content. The fix is not a different exotic. The fix is a second weapon with a different damage type.

Warlord is not bad. It's mismatched in a big chunk of the missions you'll run it in. That's a different problem and the fix is below.

## Which Damage Type Should You Bring to Each Mission?

Broad-stroke field guidance. Specific rosters vary, and nobody has published an enemy-by-enemy chart, so treat this as a starting point and trust the bar on your screen over the table:

| Content | Dominant Bar Color | Bring | Skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hard difficulty (open world/story) | Red (Blast) | Warlord, Lady Death, Blast-type AR/SMG | Heavy shredding LMG, piercing snipers |
| Heroic mainline missions | Mixed, lean light blue (Shredding) | Shredding LMG or other Shredding-type weapon | Pure blast loadout (you will see damage drops) |
| Legendary content | Heavy mix of all three | Dual-type loadout (see below) | Any mono-type build |
| Dark Zone (PvE) | Mixed with light blue lean | Shredding primary, blast secondary | Mono-piercing |
| Conflict (PvP) | No weakness bars on players | Whatever you shoot best | Building around damage type vs players is a waste |

The single biggest jump in clear time you can get today, for free, with zero crafting: **bring a second weapon of a different damage type to every mission.** If your primary is blast, your secondary should be shredding or piercing. Period.

## How Do You Build a Loadout That Covers Every Damage Type?

You have two weapon slots. Use them.

**The lazy-but-effective loadout (most players):**

- **Primary:** Warlord (Blast) for red-bar work
- **Secondary:** Police M4 (Shredding, per community testing) or any other Shredding-type AR for everything else

**The endgame loadout:**

- **Primary:** Shredding LMG or Shredding-type AR (covers the majority of Heroic/Legendary content)
- **Secondary:** Piercing T2 Sniper (covers heavy-armor dark-bar enemies and ranged threats)
- **OS Protocol:** Collateral Damage for AoE blast flavor when red-bar enemies show up in a mixed mission. Routes blast through your skills instead of your gun, freeing both weapon slots for the more frequent shredding/piercing matchups.

**The "I only own Warlord and Lady Death" loadout:**

Both your weapons are blast. We're not going to sugarcoat this. You will struggle in any mission with majority shredding or piercing-weakness enemies.

Fix: lean on your skill kit for the missing damage types. Aegis Operations? Your skill damage scales off Engineering and sidesteps your guns' type problem. (Skills appear to have damage types of their own, judging by the Skill Blast/Shredding/Piercing Damage attributes Ubisoft removed in Season 2 Phase 1, so check what yours deals before assuming it's tax-free.) Vanguard? Tactical Smart Cover routes Engineering into team weapon damage regardless of damage type.

Real fix: farm a Shredding-type AR or LMG. In our spring testing, even a T1 Shredding LMG outperformed Warlord on light-blue-bar missions. Yes, even a T1. The damage type match mattered more than the gear tier in those encounters.

---

## Why Does Damage Type Matching Matter More at Endgame in Division Resurgence?

In the early game, most missions lean one direction. Story Hard mode is mostly red bars. The DCD missions are mostly light blue. You can run one weapon for entire content stretches because the mix is consistent.

At endgame, the mix breaks down. Legendary mission rosters deliberately mix all three damage-weakness types in the same room. You'll engage a wave of red-bar elites, then immediately rotate into dark-bar heavy gunners, then a final boss with a light-blue weakness. If you run mono-type at Legendary, you're doing reduced damage for two of the three waves in every fight.

This is where the dual-type loadout stops being optional and starts being the price of admission.
