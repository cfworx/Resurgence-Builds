**Checked November 23, 2026.** The Division 2 has an official code site, but Ubisoft does not publish a list of free codes for it, and we could not confirm any public code that works this month. Codes come attached to promotions, partner offers, purchases and creator campaigns. What you can get free right now comes from the Event Pass, events, referrals and the rewards for also playing The Division Resurgence. Ubisoft has announced no Twitch Drops since the campaign that ended November 17.

## Where codes are redeemed

- **The site:** [redeem.ubisoft.com/thedivision2](https://redeem.ubisoft.com/thedivision2/). Log in with your Ubisoft account, type the code and submit it.
- **Platforms:** the site lists PC, PlayStation 4 and 5, and Xbox One and Series X.
- **Format:** Ubisoft's examples are `AAA-BBB-CCC-DDD` and `AAAA-BBBB-CCCC-DDDD`.
- **Delivery:** "It will be available next time you start the game."

Two addresses that other pages send people to do not exist: `redeem.ubisoft.com` without the game name, and `connect.ubisoft.com/redeem`. Both returned "not found" when we checked.

## The rewards for playing both Division games

Ubisoft gives players of each game something in the other. Its conditions: launch each game at least once, on the same Ubisoft account or with your platforms linked to it.

| You play | You get, in the other game |
|---|---|
| The Division Resurgence | In The Division 2: Resurgence Jacket, Resurgence Shirt, Resurgence Hat, a Level 40 boost |
| The Division 2 | In The Division Resurgence: Trekking Elite Pack, Legacy Jacket, Washington D.C. Cap |

- **To claim in The Division 2:** log in to Ubisoft Connect (the website, the PC app or the game's main menu), open the Rewards section and choose the rewards.
- **To claim in Resurgence:** open the Events tab, then "Claim your rewards". The items arrive in your stash by in-game mail, and they stay with the character you claimed them on, so claim on the agent you want them for.

Resurgence is free on iPhone, Android and PC, so the Level 40 boost costs a download and one login. [Where to download it](/guides/division-resurgence-download/), and [what Resurgence players can redeem](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/).
