You hit level 40, your gear is a patchwork quilt of blues and yellows, and the game says "go farm." Farm what, where, and for how long? Here's the map: what each tier is for, which missions lean toward which gear slots, where Tier 2 actually drops, and what's worth upgrading on the way.

**The short version:**

1. **T0** is story gear. Wear it, don't love it.
2. **T1** starts showing up around Challenging and becomes the farm on Heroic. Build your set on T1 first.
3. **T2** is the endgame tier: level 40 dailies (an official chance), Elite missions and the weekly Elite chest, Legendary runs, and the Dark Zone on a good day.
4. **Upgrade keepers only.** Level 40 to 50 costs purple materials, which Oldfartsgaming found hard to come by.
5. **Mods are Power Score you're leaving on the table.** Run Heroic Hostile Nests, then run them again.

> **How we put this together:** Ubisoft's patch notes for everything official, AgentHuntzman's "Gear Farming Made EASY" (June 3, embedded above) for the farm itself, plus notes from TikTokAndy, Oldfartsgaming, GGReborn and Died Again Gaming. Ubisoft doesn't publish mission loot tables, so the mission list below is one creator's experience from June, before Season 2. If a mission's reward preview says something different today, the preview wins.

## T0, T1, T2: What Each Tier Is For

AgentHuntzman's rule of thumb for the climb:

- **T0** comes from the story. It's not the worst gear in the game, he says, just not what you want to keep. It gets replaced constantly while you level, so don't invest in it.
- **T1** starts dropping around Challenging difficulty (he puts that near level 30 to 36, by his own estimate) and turns up much more often on Heroic. This is the tier to build your first real set on.
- **T2** is the final tier for now, and it's what endgame builds are made of. His advice: get your build working on T1 before you start chasing T2.

One difference the tooltip won't shout about: mod slots. Died Again Gaming (June 7) counts one mod slot on T0 gear and two from T1 up, and four on T0 weapons against five on T1 and T2. So a T0 piece with slightly better stats usually loses to a T1 once that extra slot is filled.

Don't judge any of it by the number on the item. A T1 piece from the right set with the right stats beats a T2 piece with the wrong ones, which our [hidden mechanics guide](/guides/hidden-mechanics-explained/) explains at length.

## Where to Farm T1: Missions by Gear Slot

According to AgentHuntzman, most repeatable missions lean toward certain weapons and gear slots (two hand out random gear). His list from June, farmed on Heroic:

| Mission | Leans toward |
|---|---|
| Chinatown Rescue | Random gear (his pick for filling empty T1 slots early) |
| Wall Street Woes | SMGs, masks |
| Liberty and Justice for All | LMGs, body armor |
| A New Enemy | Assault rifles, kneepads |
| Water is Life | Sniper and marksman rifles, backpacks |
| On The Radio | Shotguns, holsters |
| Precious Cargo | SMGs, masks |
| Take the Castle | Random gear |

Three things to know before you queue:

- **It's a lean, not a lock.** Other slots still drop from every mission, and longer missions give you more chances at extra pieces, he says.
- **Start with the backpack and the body armor.** In his opinion they matter most early, because they carry the talents.
- **Take the Castle's reward list showed a T2 key item in June** that he never got in about 40 runs, so he suspected a display bug. No patch note mentions it either way.

TikTokAndy adds the payout that makes Chinatown worth repeating on Heroic: Superior Fabric, random T1 gear and the weapon components you need to take weapons to level 50. His solo route is on our [Any-Weapon Commando](/builds/any-weapon-commando-beginner-vanguard-build-tiktokandy/) page.

Season 2 Phase 2 helps here too: Ubisoft increased the weapon and gear rewards for replaying Main Missions.

## Where to Farm T2 (Level 40)

| Source | What we know |
|---|---|
| Weapon Kill daily quests | Official (Season 2 Phase 2): weapons of the quest's weapon type, "with chances of T2 at level 40" |
| Main Mission daily quests | Official (Season 2 Phase 2): random weapons and gear, same T2 chance at level 40 |
| Elite missions | "The best place" for T2, per AgentHuntzman. TikTokAndy says the week's Elite Legendary mission drops T2 guns "quite regularly" |
| Elite Mission Challenge chest (weekly) | Official: High-End Gear and Weapon Selection Crates (Season 1 Phase 3 notes), plus one random exotic mod since Season 2 Phase 1. AgentHuntzman and Died Again Gaming both said in June that you can claim it twice a week by finishing the mission twice, and Died Again Gaming says each chest holds two T2 pieces, so that's four a week. He also says the Elite reward pool is T2 only, and that regular enemies in the mission can drop it too, not just the bosses |
| Legendary missions | TikTokAndy's solo Legendary Precious Cargo run ended with "a bunch of T2 gear" |
| Lone Wolf Challenge | One-time floor rewards. Died Again Gaming spotted random T2 gear among them in June and again on his October 25 stream. Officially the challenge refreshes every three weeks ([patch 1.2.1](/patch-notes/patch-1-2-1/)), so it's a top-up, not a farm |
| Dark Zone Heroic landmarks | Rare. AgentHuntzman says the Q Building, the Heroic landmark, gives the most, and by June he'd had two T2 pieces from it. Died Again Gaming (June) calls it a very small chance |
| Dark Zone enemy leaders | AgentHuntzman calls the Heroic enemy leader and his cache the best Dark Zone farm for it |

**About that Power Score requirement.** AgentHuntzman worked from a recommended 19.2k Power Score for Elite missions in June. Died Again Gaming put Elites at "at least 19.2" in early July, and Heroic missions at about 15,000 to 16,000. The game's own tooltip explains what the number means: below the recommended score, an activity is very hard; above it, it plays at the expected difficulty; far above it, it gets much easier. You can go in under, AgentHuntzman says, but only with your stats close to perfect.

The Dark Zone side of this (contamination, extraction, what the Q Building is) is in our [Dark Zone guide](/guides/division-resurgence-dark-zone-guide/). TikTokAndy says the level 40 Dark Zone is where he got access to T0, T1 and T2 gear.

## Turning a T0 or T1 Weapon Into T2

You don't have to wait for a T2 copy of your favorite gun to drop. The Armory can raise a weapon's tier with an **Enhancement Kit**. Ubisoft's own fixes confirm the pieces exist: Armory levels hand out enhancement kits (the [October 14 hotfix](/patch-notes/october-2026-hotfixes/) fixed levels that displayed two but gave one), and the official bug reporter tracked a blank "T2 Upgrade Window For Exotic Weapons" until marking it Done on September 22. The rules below come from Died Again Gaming's June 29 walkthrough, so they're one creator's reading from before Season 2:

- **Where:** open the weapon in the Armory and go to **Power Tier Enhancement**. One kit does the job, he says, whether you're going T0 to T1, T1 to T2 or T0 straight to T2.
- **The gate:** first you need **six weapons of the target tier at max proficiency (level 10)**. Six maxed T1 guns open T0 to T1, and six maxed T2 guns open T2. Any weapon class counts, so keep the T2 guns that drop, even the ugly ones, and level them.
- **The bonus:** once you enhance a weapon, he says future drops of that gun come at the new tier. His advice is to spend kits on your favorite guns instead of hoarding them.
- **Kits:** he gets them from the weekly quest and from the Armory proficiency level rewards. In June he read the later Armory levels as paying two kits each, but Ubisoft's October 14 fix says some levels displayed 2 while giving 1, so plan on one.
- **Exotics:** he believes they all drop as T1 (his Scorpio did in June). They need an **Exotic Weapon Enhancement Kit** plus the same six maxed T2 guns, and Ubisoft's notes list those kits in the Dark Zone vendor's stock (one added in Season 1 Phase 2, another in Season 2 Phase 1). Enhance an exotic, he says, and later copies drop at T2 too.

Short version: proficiency isn't only a damage bonus, it's the key to the T2 door. Our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/) cover the proficiency grind.

## Mods: Free Power Score

This is the part AgentHuntzman says people ignore. Weapon and gear mods add a lot of Power Score, and they stack on top of the item's own:

- **Weapon mods** at level 20: in June, his were worth roughly 350 to 500 Power Score each, on both weapons.
- **Gear mods:** one of his added about 200 on its own. A Toughness mod he's still replacing added 89.

That matters because the game compares your Power Score with each activity's recommended score, and good mods come with real stats too.

Where to get them: Heroic Hostile Nests. His trick is to run the nest again as soon as you finish, instead of leaving: the repeat still pays out the purple rewards he uses to level mods faster, and he credits repeats for the five gold mods he had in June. Ubisoft increased Hostile Nest mod rewards in Season 2 Phase 2, and our [mod farming guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/) has TikTokAndy's favorite nest.

## What to Upgrade, and When

- **Before level 40:** Oldfartsgaming upgrades freely to item level 40. AgentHuntzman is more careful: in April he upgraded a set once, when its bonus was what he needed, after running out of resources in the beta. Items can go up to 10 levels above your own level, he says.
- **At level 40:** take your keepers to 50. That stretch needs the purple materials (he names Superior Metal Components and Superior Fabric), which mostly come from repeating Heroic missions.
- **What's a keeper?** Oldfartsgaming's answer for his skill build: a tier one piece from the right set, with Engineering (his core stat) and at least one skill-related stat. When one drops, upgrade it as high as you can. Everything else waits. Our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/) have his full list.

## What to Do With Everything Else

- **Dismantle before you sell.** Dismantling gear gives the components and fabric that upgrades eat. AgentHuntzman only sells gear that's no use to any build he plays.
- **Sell the white junk.** The plain white items that pile up from activities, caches and missions sell for credits. In April, AgentHuntzman sold about half his stack every Friday, at 150 credits apiece by his count. That was before Season 2, so check the vendor price yourself.

---

## A Weekly Farming Plan (Our Take)

**Every day:** your dailies (the T2 chance lives there at level 40), a few Heroic Hostile Nest repeats for mods when one is up, and one run of whichever mission leans toward the slot you're missing.

**Every week:** the Elite mission and the Elite Mission Challenge chest, plus the rest of the weekly checklist in our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/).

**Always:** know what you're farming for before you queue. GGReborn's rule, and the one that saves the most time: work out which piece your build is missing, then go where it drops.
