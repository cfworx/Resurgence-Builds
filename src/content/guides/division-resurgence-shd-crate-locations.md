SHD crates are orange agent caches with the SHD logo, hidden around Midtown, usually on a roof, a gantry or a stack of containers. There are **13**. None of them is a parkour puzzle: look for the ladder, the van, the forklift or the crate you can climb next to it.

Every crate is pinned on our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/): turn on the SHD Crates layer, or open the Checklist tab and tap a number to fly to it. We checked all 13 pins against in-game footage of each crate. Fast travel to the nearest safe house or the Base of Operations first, since five crates are within a few blocks of it.

![The in-game map in Division Resurgence, zoomed out over Midtown with the safe houses and the Dark Zone](/images/guides/division-resurgence-shd-crate-locations-in-game-map.webp "The in-game map of Midtown. Our map adds a pin for each SHD crate.")

## What's inside

In the footage we checked, each crate gave:

- a **purple chipset part** for your specialization (break spares down to upgrade other chipsets)
- **40 Phoenix Credits**
- **5,000 credits**
- a few basic materials, such as Standard Fabric

Opening them also moves the exploration commendations along, which pay out on their own.

## All 13 SHD crates

The numbers match the pins and the checklist on our map.

### SHD Crate 01: East of Hell's Kitchen, past the main junction

Cross the big junction heading east and look for a small playground with slides on your left. Climb the ladder at the back of the playground, cross the roofs, and step up a short sloped roof to the crate. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-1)

### SHD Crate 02: Hell's Kitchen, west side

From crate 04, go a little north and east. The crate sits on boxes behind a green military hut at an outpost (the kind that hosts Outpost Restoration). [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-2)

### SHD Crate 03: Southeast of Times Square, the street of parked buses

Take the main road south from Times Square and turn into the street lined with parked buses. Go past the roadworks, the big truck and an ammo crate to a raised lift basket. The crate is on the roof of the theater building: climb the van, the box, the balcony over the entrance, then the gantry. Open it from the side you climbed up. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-3)

### SHD Crate 04: Far northwest, the last street before the river

Head to the very western edge north of Hudson Yards, on the final road before the water. Past a big truck there is a courtyard of blue shipping containers, and the crate is on top of them. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-4)

### SHD Crate 05: East of the NYC Base of Operations

Head east on the road just north of the Base of Operations and the round building next to it. An alley opens on your left; the crate is up on a gantry above two parked vans, so climb the vans. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-5)

### SHD Crate 06: Northwest corner of the round building by the NYC Base of Operations

Find the forklift at the northwest corner of the big round building next to the Base of Operations. Climb over the forklift and the containers behind it; the crate is tucked behind the CERA sign. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-6)

### SHD Crate 07: West of the NYC Base of Operations, on the elevated railway

Under the railway bridges west of the Base of Operations, find the stairs up onto the elevated track. Only one direction is open: follow the track to the small footbridge on your right, and the crate is there. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-7)

### SHD Crate 08: West of the NYC Base of Operations, under the bridge

Go west from the Base of Operations to the work site under the bridge, with a generator. Behind it, in a narrow alley, stands a green JTF truck. Climb the crate beside the truck, then the truck, and the SHD crate is on the fire escape above. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-8)

### SHD Crate 09: Southwest corner of the NYC Base of Operations

Walk past the big red truck and the small parking lot at the southwest corner of the Base of Operations, then around the block into the alley. Climb the fence and the ladder; the crate is on top. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-9)

### SHD Crate 10: A block south of the gas station, west side

Find the gas station west of the Base of Operations and go one block south to a yellow excavator next to some taxis. The crate is on a container on top of a small hut on that corner. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-10)

### SHD Crate 11: Tenderloin, at the Dark Zone wall

Near the eastern edge of the open world, at the outpost with military trucks and a burning police car. The crate sits on a red container between the trucks. It's an easy climb. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-11)

### SHD Crate 12: Chelsea, the courtyard through the columns

Walk through the columns into the courtyard near crate 13. Players have reported since April that the blue barriers block the climb, so this crate could not be reached. Tell us if you get it open. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-12)

### SHD Crate 13: Chelsea, behind the liquor store near the fountain park

Find the park with the fountains in Chelsea and the liquor store sign beside it. Go into the alley behind the store, past the piles of trash; the crate is on the roof above, up a ladder. [Show on the map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/#poi=shd_crate-shd-crate-13)

## Route

A sensible order is the Base of Operations cluster first (9, 6, 5, 8, 7, then 10), Chelsea next (13, and try 12), then the west edge (4 and 2), crate 1 east of Hell's Kitchen, crate 3 by Times Square, and crate 11 at the Tenderloin wall last.
