## The Dark Zone in One Minute

Ubisoft calls the Dark Zone a "PvPvE extraction mode": you fight NPCs for loot, and other agents may fight you for it. The short version:

- **The format:** a **20-minute** run with up to **20 agents** on the map. Ubisoft's 2023 preview put it as "up to 19 other players" and "20 minutes to find and extract as much loot as you can," and added that if you die, you can respawn and keep looting.
- **The loot:** everything you pick up is contaminated until you extract it, and it rides in a backpack you drop if you die.
- **The way out:** a helicopter at an extraction zone, or the **Smuggler** in a safe house.
- **The danger:** NPCs, contamination, and any agent who decides to go **Rogue**. PvP is off until someone does.
- **Stats are evened out for PvP:** the game's activity list tags the Dark Zone, like Conflict, with "normalized PvP attributes" ([Porps's October 28 stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FOrckQW_S8c), Portuguese client, our translation). That screen doesn't spell out how, but expect your sheet numbers not to carry over one to one against other agents.
- **The payoff:** gear, skill mods (creators call them chips, and the gold ones are the prize), and **DZ Credits** for the Dark Zone vendor, which sells exotics.

## How a Run Starts

- **Queueing:** AllThings.How says you queue from the Activities Playlist in the radial menu, solo or with a team. A solo queue puts you in a Dark Zone where everyone else is solo too; a team queue means organized squads.
- **Twenty agents per instance.** Five teams of four, as TikTokAndy points out.
- **Level brackets:** AllThings.How says the lobby scales to your level, plus or minus roughly two, and suggests level 20 or so as a comfortable starting point. Level 40 gets a Dark Zone of its own, as TikTokAndy found out. He went in on his first day of playing, in a bracket that topped out at level 15, and after his first level 40 sessions his verdict was blunt: his build "was good in the level 39 dark zone," but at 40 "it ain't the same." The upside, as he puts it: "the level 40 dark zone changes everything," because it gave him access to T0, T1 and T2 gear.
- **Crossplay is your choice:** Season 2 Phase 1 added a crossplay matchmaking selector and mobile-only Dark Zone instances, and Season 2 Phase 2 improved Dark Zone matchmaking for mobile and cross-platform groups. Details in our [Season 2 Phase 1](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) and [Season 2 Phase 2](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/) patch notes.
- **Group up before you go in.** In TikTokAndy's experience, you can't pull in a player who isn't already in your Dark Zone instance. His theory is that the game balances the teams on each server.
- **Spawn immunity:** since [Season 1 Phase 3](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/), every Dark Zone spawn and respawn grants ten seconds of immunity. Firing your weapon, using a skill or throwing a grenade ends it early.
- **You don't pick your spawn.** TikTokAndy notes you spawn at random, which keeps anyone from camping one area for hours.

## Contamination

Contamination is the Dark Zone's way of saying "hurry up."

- **Contaminated areas** raise a contamination meter while you're inside. At 100%, AllThings.How says you die a few seconds later, even if you've already left. The Dark Zone is the eastern part of our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/), which marks 23 Extremely Contaminated Areas; that layer is off by default, so switch on **Extremely Contaminated** under Dark Zone.
- **It climbs faster than you expect.** On his second Dark Zone video, TikTokAndy went underground to take on a boss, made a grab for the loot with his contamination at 93% and died: "I didn't realize my contamination's going up that fast." Earlier he walked out of one at 92% and lived to complain about it.
- **The clock does the same thing.** When the 20-minute timer ends, AllThings.How says a short countdown starts, then contamination drains your Health in about five to seven seconds. Anything you haven't extracted by then stays in the Dark Zone, which is why TikTokAndy doesn't care if he dies at the very end, as long as the bag already left.
- **The Smuggler can reset it.** AllThings.How says the Smuggler in a safe house can also clear your contamination meter.

## Loot and the Contaminated Backpack

- **Nine slots.** AllThings.How lists the contaminated backpack at nine items, and TikTokAndy's "9 out of 9 on me again" agrees.
- **Contaminated means unusable.** Dark Zone items can't be used on the regular map until they're extracted (AllThings.How).
- **Die and you drop it.** Your backpack stays where you fell, and anyone can loot it.
- **Loot is shared.** TikTokAndy says that when someone on your team opens a crate, everyone gets something, and ammo boxes refill the whole team.
- **Mods follow your specialization.** AllThings.How says most of the skill mods that drop in the Dark Zone are tied to the specialization you have active, so switch before you go in. Died Again Gaming believes it's around 80 to 90 percent for the daily's skill mods, and his advice is to swap to the specialization you want to level up first.
- **Tiers:** TikTokAndy says the level 40 Dark Zone gave him access to T0, T1 and T2 gear.
- **North beats south.** In his experience the good loot is up north, and "you don't really get good stuff" in the south. The Q Building is "really all I want to be farming," he says.

## Extraction: Helicopter or Smuggler

**By helicopter** (steps per AllThings.How):

1. Go to an extraction zone. Our [map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) marks eight: 36th, 45th, 46th and 47th Street, the Park, and three lettered zones.
2. Fire the flare. The helicopter arrives about a minute later and drops a rope.
3. Hook your backpack to the rope within about a minute of its arrival.
4. A second rope takes you out of the session, if you want to leave. Ignore it and the helicopter leaves, and the zone goes on a short cooldown.
5. The items land in your storage, and you stay in the run with an empty backpack.

The catch: **Rogue agents can cut the rope** and take what's on it. TikTokAndy goes for a rope himself in his October 15 Tactical Pharma video, so it's not a hypothetical. He thinks the extraction turret stopped him, though he allows he may have just run out of time.

**By Smuggler:** a Smuggler in the safe houses will send loot to your storage without a helicopter. AllThings.How says he does it three times a day for DZ Intel. TikTokAndy found him on his first day and read the deal as up to three items for a fee. His advice when a group follows you to an extraction: skip the rope and walk to the safe house.

**Extraction habits that work:**

- **Extract early and often.** TikTokAndy's best team got three extractions out of one 20-minute run, and he calls leaving on the first helicopter "the most counterintuitive thing I've ever seen." Extracting your loot doesn't extract you.
- **Take the second rope at the end.** AllThings.How says that if you don't extract yourself, you earn less DZ Intel and fewer DZ Credits from the run. So once your last bag is out late in the run, the ride home is worth taking.
- **Avoid zones next to an active boss.** AllThings.How says players who missed the boss tend to camp nearby extractions.
- **Turn on the turrets.** Extraction zones have turrets you can activate (AllThings.How lists DZ Intel as their cost), and TikTokAndy flips them on "just in case anybody rolls up on us."
- **Use the wait, if the lobby is quiet.** Died Again Gaming's routine on his October 25 stream: fire the flare, then clear a nearby landmark while the helicopter is on its way, for a few extra DZ Credits and the occasional purple. He was in a quiet lobby, on a Normal landmark right next to the extraction, by his description. With Rogues around, holding cover at the zone is the safer call.
- **Watch what you pick up on your way out.** On the same stream he picked up an item by accident just as he went for the rope, and it cost him the ride: he says the game won't let you extract yourself while you're carrying contaminated gear. Once your last bag is on the rope, the shiny thing on the floor stays on the floor.

## Landmarks, Leaders and Bosses

**Landmarks** are wave fights. AllThings.How lists Normal at two waves, Hard at three and Challenging at four, with a heavily armored mini-boss in the last one, and says landmarks reactivate on a timer after a clear. Our map marks 12 landmarks, with the difficulty the community map gives each:

- **Heroic:** Q Building
- **Challenging:** Kalkesse Sports Store, Construction Site
- **Hard:** Midtown Music, Abandoned Office, Refueling Station
- **Normal:** The Pit, Arch Plaza, Garden War, Blockade, Korean Shopping Street, The Library

The Q Building is the one TikTokAndy keeps coming back to; in his September 19 build video he points out you can get "a free farm on heroic" there.

**See a red pillar of light?** Going by community answers on the official subreddit, it marks a landmark where a fight is happening right now. Ubisoft hasn't documented it. More in our [June 16 Reddit roundup](/news/reddit-weekly-highlights-june-16/).

**Bosses**, per AllThings.How, are named enemies with backup troops. Their chests hold three or four items and spawn extra guards nearby, and everyone who deals enough damage shares the reward, so strangers will join your boss fight. Our map marks five **DZ Enemy Leader** spots (switch that layer on; it's off by default).

**The underground.** Not every subway section is open, but TikTokAndy found that a lot of them are. AllThings.How mentions a Juggernaut boss in a contaminated subway that it doesn't think is worth soloing. TikTokAndy farms an underground boss for gold chips (gold skill mods), and warns that he's seen players kill the people farming it from above ground with a signature ability.

## Going Rogue

- **How:** press Go Rogue next to the timer, and you can attack other agents (AllThings.How).
- **Who can shoot whom:** agents who aren't Rogue can only damage Rogues, and a Rogue can attack anyone.
- **If you die while Rogue:** AllThings.How says you drop only about 30 to 40% of your loot, three or four items at most. A normal death drops the whole backpack, so the real cost of going Rogue is being everyone's target.
- **Clearing it:** the hacked terminal in a safe house removes your Rogue status. TikTokAndy found it on his first day ("you can actually take your rogue status off with that"), and AllThings.How says it costs DZ Intel.
- **Reporting cheaters or harassment:** Ubisoft's Help Center says to use the dedicated in-game report buttons, which open a report that goes straight to its support team ([Ubisoft Help Center](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2490-how-can-i-report-a-player-for-breaking-the-game-s-terms-and-conditions-harassment-inappropriate-conduct-or-other-offenses/)).
- **Being hunted:** in TikTokAndy's experience, a long Rogue streak doesn't trigger a Manhunt-style status or put a marker on you. Other agents can still find you: AllThings.How says the Smuggler in a safe house will reveal every agent's position for 200 DZ Credits, and [Willspot's June 22 Dark Zone video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZwNM-je8AM) shows the Smuggler's announcement when someone pays: "Every agent is now marked on the map." TikTokAndy points out it's only a snapshot, because a player can be anywhere by the time you get there. He also says players can tell when a Rogue is coming.

**If you go Rogue, pick your fights.** On his 11-kill solo run, several of TikTokAndy's kills were players who weren't paying attention, and he's blunt about it: why would you be in your menu in a PvP zone?

## DZ Credits, DZ Intel and the Vendor

**DZ Credits**, per AllThings.How, come from killing NPCs, bosses, landmarks, looting and the small containers on the streets near landmarks, and you keep what you gathered even if you die. There's a weekly cap, and dismantling extracted skill mods adds credits on top of it. Since [Season 1 Phase 2](/patch-notes/phase-2-patch-notes/), dismantling a skill mod pays **15, 75 or 750 DZ Credits** for blue, purple or gold. Died Again Gaming says the "extracted" part matters: in his experience, breaking skill mods down inside the Dark Zone doesn't pay out.

**DZ Intel** is spent inside the Dark Zone: on extraction turrets, the Smuggler and the hacked terminal (AllThings.How).

**The Dark Zone vendor** (at the Base of Operations):

- **Tiered weapons**, with the talents and attributes shown before you buy, plus Tactical Augmentation Manuals, per TikTokAndy.
- **Exotic weapons at 15,000 DZ Credits** since [Season 2 Phase 2](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/) (down from 17,500): the Warlord since Season 1 Phase 2 and the Scorpio since Season 2 Phase 1. Exotics no longer come with exotic mods.
- **A weekly exotic mod crate for each exotic weapon**, 2,500 DZ Credits each, since Season 2 Phase 1.
- **Tactical Augmentation Modules:** a weekly random module crate (Season 1 Phase 2) and exotic weapon Tactical Augmentation caches. TikTokAndy bought one of those caches and pulled a Bighorn module.
- **Diamondback rewards**, added in Season 2 Phase 2.

Our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) tracks which exotics the vendor sells.

**Dark Zone quests:** Dark Zone daily quests reward skill mods since Season 2 Phase 2, which makes the Dark Zone the skill mod farm (more in our [mod farming guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/)). Died Again Gaming says the daily guarantees one gold and one purple skill mod, so seven of each a week if you never skip it, and that you don't need loot to finish it: fire the flare, leave once the helicopter gets there, and it counts. Oldfartsgaming's tip for the daily: team up. In his experience a lot of the players you group with are further along than you and carry the run at a high pace, and before you know it you're extracting with decent gear and a daily ticked off. His full routine is in our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/). Some Dark Zone missions pay Tactical Augmentations too: patch [1.1.2.2](/patch-notes/patch-1-1-2-2/) fixed the Shotgun Tactical Augmentation reward for the "Ringleaders" mission.

## PvP: What You'll Run Into

Everything in this section is TikTokAndy's experience from his Rogue runs in late September and October, not our testing.

- **Bulwark is the wall.** "Toxic pharma versus bulwark, and bulwark usually comes out on top," is how he sums up the Dark Zone. A properly built Bulwark, he says, is close to unkillable without focus fire.
- **Tactical Pharma melts people,** even from the floor below them. Our [Tactical Pharma Season 2 build](/builds/tactical-pharma-season-2-build-ggreborn/) page (GGReborn's build) includes TikTokAndy's variant and his Dark Zone take.
- **Demolitionist explosives** are the other big threat: Explosive Seeker Mines that he says can one-shot you, and a grenade launcher that stuns and keeps firing.
- **Striker Drones:** manageable, in his view. "All you got to do is shoot the striker drone."
- **Fast healers:** players with stacked movement speed can simply outrun you.
- **Guns still work.** His 11-kill solo Rogue run was a P90 and a Lightweight M4, and his [Double Barrel Commando build](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/) is built for close-range fights.

For the tanky side of that meta, see our [Bulwark Breacher](/builds/bulwark-breacher-build-saiynergy/) and [Juggernaut](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/) builds, and check their Season 2 notes first.

## Survival Checklist

1. **Extract early, extract often.** A full backpack is a bigger target than an empty one.
2. **Watch the contamination meter** in contaminated areas, and leave before it does the math for you.
3. **Don't stand in menus.** Do your inventory at a safe house.
4. **Hold cover at extraction zones** and turn the turrets on.
5. **Being followed? Use the Smuggler.** A rope you never hook can't be cut.
6. **Split up after a landmark.** AllThings.How's advice: the stranger who helped you clear it might not stay friendly, so watch for a non-Rogue agent who drifts in behind you at an extraction.
7. **On a phone, lower the frame rate in busy lobbies.** Full Dark Zones were the heaviest load on TikTokAndy's phone; dropping from high to medium frame rate fixed his lag. He also recommends a field of view of at least 90.
8. **Gear up first.** TikTokAndy's own blunt rule: "If you die to NPCs, stay out of the Dark Zone." Our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/) cover the gearing side.
