You hit **level 40**. Congratulations: that's the **Legendary Agent** achievement Ubisoft added in Season 2 Phase 1, and it's the level where Ubisoft's daily quest rewards start rolling **Tier 2** loot. If Oldfartsgaming and GGReborn are any guide, it's also where side systems that are easy to overlook start to matter. The short version of what to do next:

1. **Run your daily quests every day.** Since Season 2 Phase 2, Weapon Kill and Main Mission dailies have a chance of T2 at level 40.
2. **Stop upgrading everything.** Save purple upgrade materials for gear and mods you'll keep.
3. **Level the side systems:** weapon proficiency, SHD level, maybe a second specialization.
4. **Keep the crafting bench busy:** 15 hours base since Season 2 Phase 1.
5. **Farm with a plan:** mods from Hostile Nests, skill mods from the Dark Zone, exotics from the vendor, the pass or Exotic Sealed Caches.

Not at 40 yet? Our [beginner guide](/guides/division-resurgence-beginner-guide/) covers the road there, and our [stats guide](/guides/division-resurgence-stats-explained/) explains the numbers you'll be optimizing once you arrive. Below, Ubisoft's confirmed changes come first. Then the best advice from Oldfartsgaming's "7 Things I Wish I Knew Before Endgame" (October 11, embedded above) and GGReborn's video on the seven mistakes he made (September 29), labeled as creator advice, because that's what it is.

## What changes at level 40, officially

- **Legendary Agent** achievement for reaching level 40 (Season 2 Phase 1).
- **Tier 2 chances on dailies:** Weapon Kill quests reward weapons matching the quest's weapon type, and Main Mission quests reward random weapons and gear, both "with chances of T2 at level 40" (Season 2 Phase 2).
- **No daily XP cap:** Phase 2 removed it "to support longer progression sessions."

One unofficial extra: Died Again Gaming says XP past 40 fills a gauge that pays out a Field Proficiency Cache, with two items and some resources. His were T0 in June, and he didn't know whether they can roll T2. With the daily XP cap gone you'll open more of them, so let us know if one ever does.

## Your daily routine

| Daily quest | Reward (Season 2 Phase 2) | Why you care at 40 |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon Kill | Weapons of the quest's weapon type, chance of T2 at level 40 | Targeted Tier 2 weapon rolls |
| Main Mission | Random weapons and gear, chance of T2 at level 40 | Tier 2 lottery tickets |
| Dark Zone | Skill Mods | Skill builds, plus dismantle fodder for DZ Credits ([our Dark Zone guide](/guides/division-resurgence-dark-zone-guide/)) |
| Hostile Nest | Weapon and Gear Mods | The mod farm ([our guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/)) |
| World Event | Upgrade Materials | Fuel for upgrading keepers |

Phase 2 also increased weapon and gear rewards from replaying Main Missions, which makes the Main Mission daily a little sweeter.

What the dailies pay in practice, going by Died Again Gaming's [October 25 stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tn4ME4MVGyY): the **Hostile Nest** daily guarantees **two gold weapon or gear mods**, the **Main Mission** daily gives **three items** and a **Weapon Kill** daily **two weapons** of the quest's type (two assault rifles for an AR quest), any of which can be T2, and the **Dark Zone** daily guarantees **one gold and one purple skill mod**. By his count, that's seven gold skill mods a week for the price of showing up.

How Oldfartsgaming runs his dailies:

- He does them every day and says they feed his weekly quests too, so he lines up activities like mission replays, Dark Zone missions and Hostile Nests to tick daily and weekly boxes at once.
- He rerolls ("recycles") dailies until he has a Hostile Nest quest.
- For Dark Zone dailies, he teams up and says higher-level players often carry him through quickly.

How AgentHuntzman ran his in April, before Season 2 (check that the rewards still match):

- **Dailies first:** he checks them, rerolls the ones he doesn't want, and knocks out the quick kill and vendor quests.
- **Then the map:** if a Heroic Hostile Nest is up, he runs it for gear and weapon mods (golds included) and the materials to upgrade them.
- **Then the clan:** the daily donation at the clan counter, and clan nests if the clan still needs them, for clan currency.
- **Then world events:** he says they reset daily and you can complete 10 a day for rewards. He does three each of Arms Deal Disruption, Hostage Rescue and Supply Run (nine, sometimes the full 10) for Electronics Alpha, Beta and Gamma Components, which he calls the most needed materials for upgrading a specialization.
- **Weekly Bounties** reset on Sundays, and he farms them for OS Protocols: blue and purple, with a chance of gold.

## Your weekly checklist

From Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 2, Season 2 Phase 1 and Season 2 Phase 2 notes:

- **Weekly Rewards** include SHD Keys and Exclusive SHD Keys, and give more Classified Ops Pass XP than they used to.
- **Weekly Quest** and **Elite Mission Challenge chest:** one random exotic mod each.
- **Dark Zone vendor:** a random exotic mod crate for each exotic weapon every week (2,500 DZ Credits each), exotic weapons at 15,000 DZ Credits (down from 17,500), the Scorpio (added in Season 2 Phase 1) and a weekly Random Tactical Augmentation Module Crate (added in Season 1 Phase 2).
- **Weekly Bounties:** new mission sets arrived with Season 2 Phase 1.

Creator layer: Oldfartsgaming buys the vendor's weekly upgrade materials every week, both purple items included, plus the maximum of everything else, even the green stuff. He says you'll need all of it. And the Weekly Bounty looks better than it did in April, when AgentHuntzman described a chance of gold: Died Again Gaming says the fourth boss's Key Rewards now guarantee a **gold OS Protocol**, so that's one a week. He calls it a really good change, and if it holds, we're not going to argue. His SHD Keys chase the same thing: back in July he recommended spending Exclusive SHD Keys only on the OS Protocol Sealed Cache, calling it one of the few routes to a gold outside the Weekly Bounty's Key Rewards.

## Rule 1: stop upgrading everything

This was Oldfartsgaming's first point, and it's the one that saves the most materials:

- **Upgrade freely to item level 40.** The materials for that are plentiful, he says.
- **From 40 to 50, you need purple materials,** and he found them hard to come by.
- **Wait for a keeper.** For him, that's a tier one piece from the right gear set with the core stat his build uses (he plays an Engineering-focused Demolitionist) and at least one skill-related stat. When you find one, upgrade it as high as you can.
- **His regret:** spending purple materials on set pieces that weren't keepers while he was stuck between levels 30 and 40. He says he didn't need to.
- **Same rule for mods:** max the ones with the right stats and skip the rest.

### Don't run out of credits

AgentHuntzman's April video on credits ("Money making 101") is old, but the habits hold up:

- **Repeat missions.** Every activity pays something. In April, a Heroic run paid him 8,000 credits and story mode 3,500 to 4,000, and story is fast enough to spam.
- **Dismantle, don't sell, gear you might use.** Dismantling gives the components every upgrade eats. He only sells gear no build of his could use.
- **Sell the white junk.** The plain white items you collect from activities, caches and missions stack up fast. He sold about half his stack every Friday, at 150 credits each by his count.
- **Don't upgrade everything at once.** He says credits go fast if you do (his April 12 starter video calls running out of credits "the easiest thing to happen"), and he admits he still hadn't upgraded everything because he'd switched classes a few times.

Check today's numbers in game: Season 2 has changed rewards since then. Where the gear itself drops is in our [gear farming guide](/guides/division-resurgence-gear-farming-guide/).

## Rule 2: max one good OS Protocol

Oldfartsgaming says upgrading a decent OS Protocol that fits his build as far as it would go was one of his biggest boosts in the late mid-game. On his Demolitionist it added a large Engineering bonus, some skill damage and a good chunk of health, and he did it even though he later found a better protocol. AgentHuntzman's caveat from his May SHD Manual video: OS upgrades need duplicates, so this is a long-term project, not a weekend. Not sure which one fits your build? Start with our [OS Protocol tier list](/tier-list/os-protocols/).

## Rule 3: level the side systems

### SHD level

- **Oldfartsgaming:** your SHD level gives bonuses (on his account at the time, 11% armor, 10.5% max health, a flat health bonus and signature ability charge efficiency), and its level rewards add more. He raises it through commendations: clearing missions on every difficulty, crafting high-end items, playing every mode and collecting things.
- **GGReborn:** not understanding SHD level early is one of the seven mistakes on his list.
- **Official:** Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 3 notes say achievements sync based on SHD commendations. We haven't found a published table of SHD level bonuses, so check yours in game.

### Weapon proficiency

- **Oldfartsgaming:** each proficiency level a weapon earns in the Armory adds a little weapon damage and skill intensity on his account, from tasks like neutralizing enemies shortly after hitting weak points. Using a weapon for your dailies counts toward its tasks, he says, so the game rewards rotating weapons.
- **Died Again Gaming:** the payoff has a number. In his June 15 video the Armory shows its own proficiency level out of 100, and he says each level adds **0.2% Weapon Damage and 0.2% Skill Intensity**, so maxing it is worth 20% of each (he was at level 88, on 17.6%). Proficiency also gates Tier 2 upgrades: see [turning a T0 or T1 weapon into T2](/guides/division-resurgence-gear-farming-guide/).
- **GGReborn:** not focusing on proficiency from the beginning is the second mistake on his list.
- **Official:** Ubisoft has fixed several proficiency bugs. Season 2 Phase 2 fixed LMG Proficiency Quests counting kills from any weapon and Armory progression levels staying locked, and the bug reporter marked two proficiency reports Done in August and September ([details](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)).

Died Again Gaming's grinding tips, from June. Task wording may have moved since, so read each task in the Armory:

- **Farm with the gun you're leveling.** Run nests, world activities and bounties with a weapon that still needs levels, then swap. Rifles and marksman rifles suit open world activities, where you get the range their tasks want. He says enemies five or more levels below you don't count.
- **Assists count.** Many tasks take a kill or an assist, so in a group, tag every enemy you can before going for kills.
- **Hip fire includes cover and shields.** Shooting from cover without aiming, or with a Bulwark shield up, counted as hip fire for him.
- **Aim tasks mean the scope.** For rifles and marksman rifles, only first-person scope view with a 2x or stronger optic (4x for marksman rifles) counted reliably for him. Third-person aiming was hit and miss.
- **Weak points include damage type.** Headshots and the satchels, gas cans and ammo pouches enemies carry count, and so does hitting an armor bar with the matching damage type. The separate task that needs a matching damage type takes longest, so he saves it for Challenging or higher, where armor bars with a weakness show up.
- **Do level 3 early.** On most weapon classes it asks you to upgrade the weapon. Below 40 that costs common materials. At 40 the next upgrade wants a Superior (purple) component, so his workaround is to craft a level 1 copy and upgrade that. Slow, since crafts take hours, but it saves the purple.
- **Exotics have their own tasks.** His examples: Lady Death wants 10,000 Breathe Free stacks (swap away and back to rebuild them), the Strawberry Milkshake wants Anarchy triggered 1,000 times, and the Warlord's level 8 wants agent kills or assists, which means PvP.

### A second specialization

- **Oldfartsgaming:** he capped Demolitionist, then started leveling Tech Operator (which he says overlaps a lot with his Engineering setup) and tried Bulwark, running his dailies on them while keeping his main for harder missions. He says the extra levels give skill points and push his SHD level, and that after 40 the game encourages you to go broad.
- **AgentHuntzman's counterpoint:** switching your *main* every few days is, in his words, one of the biggest progression traps, because your farming priorities, gear requirements and stat needs change each time, and he has more wasted recalibrations than he can count. Level a second specialization on the side if you like, but commit your materials to one.
- **Official:** Ubisoft's August 5 PC launch press release describes "the flexibility to switch roles at any time." Our [specialization guide](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/) covers what each one does.

He also says it took him a while to understand how his specialization's skill stacks work together, and that putting points into the skills that feed them made a big difference. Read your skill tree tooltips slowly; future you will thank you.

## Rule 4: don't ignore clan credits

GGReborn's list includes treating his clan as a purely social thing and ignoring clan credits. He says clan credits buy clan rewards, including Tactical Augmentations for gear and weapons, which he calls very important for builds. Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 2 notes back up the vendor part: that update added one more copy of each regular Weapon and Gear Tactical Augmentation Module to the Clan Vendor. The notes don't list prices, so check them in game. Your clan also matters for crafting, which is next.

## Rule 5: keep the crafting bench busy

- Base crafting time is **15 hours** since Season 2 Phase 1 (down from 24), and the clan duration reduction tops out at **50%**, so a fully boosted craft takes **7.5 hours**.
- Crafting levels now come faster early and slower later, with fewer crafts needed overall to reach max level, and rarities per level went up slightly, so purple items show up earlier (Season 2 Phase 1).
- Oldfartsgaming points out that crafting high-end items also earns commendations toward your SHD level. Died Again Gaming's May checklist for those: take at least one item in every weapon class and gear slot to crafting rank 10, which a clan bench makes much quicker. The pure-luck one is crafting three high-end items in a row. Season 2 Phase 1 says fewer crafts are now needed to reach max crafting level, so it may go faster than it did for him.

Our tip: start a craft before bed and let the bench take the night shift.

## Rule 6: spend Phoenix Credits with a plan

GGReborn's list opens with spending Phoenix Credits without thinking, on the assumption he could always earn more. His advice: before you spend, ask whether the item actually helps your build and whether you might need the credits for something more important. If you're eyeing cosmetic draws or caches, two official things help: since Season 2 Phase 1, Cosmetic Draw screens show detailed pricing in game, and Ubisoft publishes cache odds in its Help Center, which we break down in our [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

## Rule 7: farm with a plan

GGReborn's list closes with the one we'd tattoo on every fresh level 40: ask yourself "what exactly am I farming for?" Work out what your build is missing, find where it drops, and spend your time there. A cheat sheet from Ubisoft's notes:

| You need | Where Ubisoft says it comes from |
|---|---|
| Weapon and gear mods | Hostile Nests and Hostile Nest dailies |
| Skill mods | Dark Zone dailies |
| Tier 2 weapons and gear | Weapon Kill and Main Mission dailies (a chance at level 40) |
| Upgrade materials | World Event dailies |
| Exotic mods | Classified Ops Pass, Weekly Quest, Elite Mission Challenge chest, DZ vendor crates |
| Exotics from earlier seasons | Exotic Sealed Caches; the DZ vendor added the Warlord (Season 1 Phase 2) and the Scorpio (Season 2 Phase 1), so check its current stock |
| SHD Keys | Weekly Rewards and Season 2 Phase 2 event rewards |

One creator addition each: TikTokAndy says Elite missions are where he farms T2 guns, and AgentHuntzman runs a Heroic nest again right after he finishes it. Which mission leans toward which gear slot, and every other T2 source we know of, is in our [gear farming guide](/guides/division-resurgence-gear-farming-guide/).

Not sure where something comes from? Highlight it and tap **Find**. Died Again Gaming uses it for everything from specialization upgrade materials to Enhancement Kits. Treat it as a lead, not gospel: Ubisoft fixed some incorrect Find entries in [Season 1 Phase 2](/patch-notes/phase-2-patch-notes/).

GGReborn's other gear mistakes deserve a line each: judging gear by level and rarity instead of its set, talents and attributes, and not checking the character stats screen to see whether your build actually gets what you think it does. A higher Power Score isn't the same as a better build, which our [hidden mechanics guide](/guides/hidden-mechanics-explained/) explains at length.

## Rule 8: learn the missions you'll farm

Some missions you'll run dozens of times, so a few creator notes go a long way. Most of these come from TikTokAndy's runs (the Lone Wolf notes add Ubisoft's patch notes, GGReborn's tests and a Died Again Gaming tip); the full routes are on the build pages linked below.

### Chinatown (Heroic)

TikTokAndy calls Chinatown the main mission you'll want to be able to solo, "because you're probably going to have to run this mission a lot." The payout he names: Superior Fabric, random T1 gear and weapon components to take your weapons to level 50. From his solo Heroic run:

- **Bring two damage types:** Piercing for most of the mission, Blast for the final boss.
- **Kill the Grenadier first,** keep everything in front of you, and watch the minimap.
- **Leave the truck alone during the final boss.** He says you get more Tactical Link headshots without blowing it up.

He calls about 6,300 Armor and 100,000 Health the bare minimum to solo it. His build for it: [Any-Weapon Commando](/builds/any-weapon-commando-beginner-vanguard-build-tiktokandy/).

### Precious Cargo (Legendary)

TikTokAndy calls Precious Cargo the final mission in the game and, from his experience, the one that pays out the most gear, a lot of it T2. His solo Legendary run went up on October 6:

- **Snipers first, then the Grenadier.**
- **Save Tactical Link for the second wave,** which comes out of the door on your left.
- **Don't count on Kevin.** The NPC who comes along with you likes to take the lead, and your cover.
- **The mission ends as soon as all four bosses are down.**

For the finale his two videos disagree: solo with a shotgun he fights from a nook by the shipping container, and as a team healer he prefers the bridge you start on. Full routes: the [Double Barrel Commando](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/) and the [Aegis Team Healer](/builds/aegis-team-healer-tech-operator-build-tiktokandy/).

### Lone Wolf Challenge

- **The format:** Ubisoft's launch article calls it "a series of training stages that increase in difficulty" (and describes its sibling, the Legendary Challenge, as "a random main mission set to Ultimate difficulty level"). TikTokAndy counts 20 floors with up to six stars each, 120 stars in all. Officially, it refreshes every three weeks (the Season 1 Phase 2 notes fixed it to refresh on that schedule, and [patch 1.2.1](/patch-notes/patch-1-2-1/) fixed the reset schedule again), and since a [June 26 hotfix](/news/server-hotfix-june-26-2026/) the timer starts as soon as an enemy takes damage, even if the doors are still closed.
- **There's a leaderboard:** TikTokAndy says his floor 20 run (his video calls it "Lone Star Stage 20") placed third in NA, and he didn't know the leaderboard existed until someone told him.
- **His floor 20 tip:** the boss sometimes parks at one spot (about one run in ten, he estimates). When it does, crouch on the boxes beside it, go for headshots, and save Tactical Link for the wave that follows.
- **Claim on the spec you want:** Died Again Gaming says the rewards include two gold skill mods, and that they come for whichever specialization you have equipped when you claim them. So clear the floors on whatever you play best, then switch to the spec whose skill mods you need before you collect.
- **A good test bench:** GGReborn uses Lone Wolf runs to compare builds, which is how he tested stat setups and OS Protocols on our [Tactical Pharma Season 2](/builds/tactical-pharma-season-2-build-ggreborn/) page.

### Heroic Hostile Nests and the Dark Zone

The Heroic Hostile Nest with a named boss is the mod farm in our [mod farming guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/). For the Dark Zone (20-minute runs, contamination, extraction and Rogues), read our [Dark Zone guide](/guides/division-resurgence-dark-zone-guide/) before you go in with anything you'd mind losing.

## Our post-40 routine

**Every day:** all your dailies (reroll for a Hostile Nest one), start a craft, empty your stash, and put proficiency tasks on a weapon you don't usually use.

**Every week:** Weekly Quest, Elite Mission Challenge chest, Weekly Bounties, Weekly Rewards (SHD Keys), and the DZ vendor (exotic mod crates and weekly materials).

**Always:** upgrade only keepers, and if you have time left over, run a few dailies on a second specialization.
