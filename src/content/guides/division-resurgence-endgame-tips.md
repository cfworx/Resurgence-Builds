You hit **level 40**. Congratulations: that's the **Legendary Agent** achievement Ubisoft added in Season 2 Phase 1, and it's the level where Ubisoft's daily quest rewards start rolling **Tier 2** loot. If Oldfartsgaming and GGReborn are any guide, it's also where side systems that are easy to overlook start to matter. The short version of what to do next:

1. **Run your daily quests every day.** Since Season 2 Phase 2, Weapon Kill and Main Mission dailies have a chance of T2 at level 40.
2. **Stop upgrading everything.** Save purple upgrade materials for gear and mods you'll keep.
3. **Level the side systems:** weapon proficiency, SHD level, maybe a second specialization.
4. **Keep the crafting bench busy:** 15 hours base since Season 2 Phase 1.
5. **Farm with a plan:** mods from Hostile Nests, skill mods from the Dark Zone, exotics from the vendor, the pass or Exotic Sealed Caches.

Below, Ubisoft's confirmed changes come first. Then the best advice from Oldfartsgaming's "7 Things I Wish I Knew Before Endgame" (October 11, embedded above) and GGReborn's video on the seven mistakes he made (September 29), labeled as creator advice, because that's what it is.

## What changes at level 40, officially

- **Legendary Agent** achievement for reaching level 40 (Season 2 Phase 1).
- **Tier 2 chances on dailies:** Weapon Kill quests reward weapons matching the quest's weapon type, and Main Mission quests reward random weapons and gear, both "with chances of T2 at level 40" (Season 2 Phase 2).
- **No daily XP cap:** Phase 2 removed it "to support longer progression sessions."

## Your daily routine

| Daily quest | Reward (Season 2 Phase 2) | Why you care at 40 |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon Kill | Weapons of the quest's weapon type, chance of T2 at level 40 | Targeted Tier 2 weapon rolls |
| Main Mission | Random weapons and gear, chance of T2 at level 40 | Tier 2 lottery tickets |
| Dark Zone | Skill Mods | Skill builds, plus dismantle fodder for DZ Credits |
| Hostile Nest | Weapon and Gear Mods | The mod farm ([our guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/)) |
| World Event | Upgrade Materials | Fuel for upgrading keepers |

Phase 2 also increased weapon and gear rewards from replaying Main Missions, which makes the Main Mission daily a little sweeter.

How Oldfartsgaming runs his dailies:

- He does them every day and says they feed his weekly quests too, so he lines up activities like mission replays, Dark Zone missions and Hostile Nests to tick daily and weekly boxes at once.
- He rerolls ("recycles") dailies until he has a Hostile Nest quest.
- For Dark Zone dailies, he teams up and says higher-level players often carry him through quickly.

## Your weekly checklist

From Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 2, Season 2 Phase 1 and Season 2 Phase 2 notes:

- **Weekly Rewards** include SHD Keys and Exclusive SHD Keys, and give more Classified Ops Pass XP than they used to.
- **Weekly Quest** and **Elite Mission Challenge chest:** one random exotic mod each.
- **Dark Zone vendor:** a random exotic mod crate for each exotic weapon every week (2,500 DZ Credits each), exotic weapons at 15,000 DZ Credits (down from 17,500), the Scorpio (added in Season 2 Phase 1) and a weekly Random Tactical Augmentation Module Crate (added in Season 1 Phase 2).
- **Weekly Bounties:** new mission sets arrived with Season 2 Phase 1.

Creator layer: Oldfartsgaming buys the vendor's weekly upgrade materials every week, both purple items included, plus the maximum of everything else, even the green stuff. He says you'll need all of it.

## Rule 1: stop upgrading everything

This was Oldfartsgaming's first point, and it's the one that saves the most materials:

- **Upgrade freely to item level 40.** The materials for that are plentiful, he says.
- **From 40 to 50, you need purple materials,** and he found them hard to come by.
- **Wait for a keeper.** For him, that's a tier one piece from the right gear set with the core stat his build uses (he plays an Engineering-focused Demolitionist) and at least one skill-related stat. When you find one, upgrade it as high as you can.
- **His regret:** spending purple materials on set pieces that weren't keepers while he was stuck between levels 30 and 40. He says he didn't need to.
- **Same rule for mods:** max the ones with the right stats and skip the rest.

## Rule 2: max one good OS Protocol

Oldfartsgaming says upgrading a decent OS Protocol that fits his build as far as it would go was one of his biggest boosts in the late mid-game. On his Demolitionist it added a large Engineering bonus, some skill damage and a good chunk of health, and he did it even though he later found a better protocol. Not sure which one fits your build? Start with our [OS Protocol tier list](/tier-list/os-protocols/).

## Rule 3: level the side systems

### SHD level

- **Oldfartsgaming:** your SHD level gives bonuses (on his account at the time, 11% armor, 10.5% max health, a flat health bonus and signature ability charge efficiency), and its level rewards add more. He raises it through commendations: clearing missions on every difficulty, crafting high-end items, playing every mode and collecting things.
- **GGReborn:** not understanding SHD level early is one of the seven mistakes on his list.
- **Official:** Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 3 notes say achievements sync based on SHD commendations. We haven't found a published table of SHD level bonuses, so check yours in game.

### Weapon proficiency

- **Oldfartsgaming:** each proficiency level a weapon earns in the Armory adds a little weapon damage and skill intensity on his account, from tasks like neutralizing enemies shortly after hitting weak points. Using a weapon for your dailies counts toward its tasks, he says, so the game rewards rotating weapons.
- **GGReborn:** not focusing on proficiency from the beginning is the second mistake on his list.
- **Official:** Ubisoft has fixed several proficiency bugs. Season 2 Phase 2 fixed LMG Proficiency Quests counting kills from any weapon and Armory progression levels staying locked, and the bug reporter marked two proficiency reports Done in August and September ([details](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)).

### A second specialization

- **Oldfartsgaming:** he capped Demolitionist, then started levelling Tech Operator (which he says overlaps a lot with his Engineering setup) and tried Bulwark, running his dailies on them while keeping his main for harder missions. He says the extra levels give skill points and push his SHD level, and that after 40 the game encourages you to go broad.
- **Official:** Ubisoft's August 5 PC launch press release describes "the flexibility to switch roles at any time." Our [specialization guide](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/) covers what each one does.

He also says it took him a while to understand how his specialization's skill stacks work together, and that putting points into the skills that feed them made a big difference. Read your skill tree tooltips slowly; future you will thank you.

## Rule 4: don't ignore clan credits

GGReborn's list includes treating his clan as a purely social thing and ignoring clan credits. He says clan credits buy clan rewards, including Tactical Augmentations for gear and weapons, which he calls very important for builds. Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 2 notes back up the vendor part: that update added one more copy of each regular Weapon and Gear Tactical Augmentation Module to the Clan Vendor. The notes don't list prices, so check them in game. Your clan also matters for crafting, which is next.

## Rule 5: keep the crafting bench busy

- Base crafting time is **15 hours** since Season 2 Phase 1 (down from 24), and the clan duration reduction tops out at **50%**, so a fully boosted craft takes **7.5 hours**.
- Crafting levels now come faster early and slower later, with fewer crafts needed overall to reach max level, and rarities per level went up slightly, so purple items show up earlier (Season 2 Phase 1).
- Oldfartsgaming points out that crafting high-end items also earns commendations toward your SHD level.

Our tip: start a craft before bed and let the bench take the night shift.

## Rule 6: spend Phoenix Credits with a plan

GGReborn's list opens with spending Phoenix Credits without thinking, on the assumption he could always earn more. His advice: before you spend, ask whether the item actually helps your build and whether you might need the credits for something more important. If you're eyeing cosmetic draws or caches, two official things help: since Season 2 Phase 1, Cosmetic Draw screens show detailed pricing in game, and Ubisoft publishes cache odds in its Help Center, which we break down in our [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

## Rule 7: farm with a plan

GGReborn's list closes with the one we'd tattoo on every fresh level 40: ask yourself "what exactly am I farming for?" Work out what your build is missing, find where it drops, and spend your time there. A cheat sheet from Ubisoft's notes:

| You need | Where Ubisoft says it comes from |
|---|---|
| Weapon and gear mods | Hostile Nests and Hostile Nest dailies |
| Skill mods | Dark Zone dailies |
| Tier 2 weapons and gear | Weapon Kill and Main Mission dailies (a chance at level 40) |
| Upgrade materials | World Event dailies |
| Exotic mods | Classified Ops Pass, Weekly Quest, Elite Mission Challenge chest, DZ vendor crates |
| Exotics from earlier seasons | Exotic Sealed Caches; the DZ vendor added the Warlord (Season 1 Phase 2) and the Scorpio (Season 2 Phase 1), so check its current stock |
| SHD Keys | Weekly Rewards and Season 2 Phase 2 event rewards |

One creator addition: TikTokAndy says Elite missions are where he farms T2 guns.

GGReborn's other gear mistakes deserve a line each: judging gear by level and rarity instead of its set, talents and attributes, and not checking the character stats screen to see whether your build actually gets what you think it does. A higher Power Score isn't the same as a better build, which our [hidden mechanics guide](/guides/hidden-mechanics-explained/) explains at length.

## Our post-40 routine

**Every day:** all your dailies (reroll for a Hostile Nest one), start a craft, empty your stash, and put proficiency tasks on a weapon you don't usually use.

**Every week:** Weekly Quest, Elite Mission Challenge chest, Weekly Bounties, Weekly Rewards (SHD Keys), and the DZ vendor (exotic mod crates and weekly materials).

**Always:** upgrade only keepers, and if you have time left over, run a few dailies on a second specialization.
