**Division Resurgence is on Steam.** It launched there on **Wednesday, August 5, 2026**, free to play, alongside the start of Season 2 Phase 1 "The Call." It's also on PC through **Ubisoft Connect**, where it ran in early access from April 28 until that same full launch. This guide covers what you need on PC today: specs, how to bring your phone progress with you, crossplay, controllers, the settings worth changing, and the rough edges.

## Release date and what launched with it

Ubisoft confirmed the Steam date on July 31 ([our coverage](/news/steam-release-date-august-5/)) and the game went live on **August 5, 2026**. Ubisoft's launch article: "Starting today you can download and play The Division Resurgence for free on Steam!" The press release added that it was now available worldwide on Steam and Ubisoft Connect "following its early-access period on the platform."

Launch day also brought Season 2 Phase 1, with the free **Bighorn** exotic on the new pass ([patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/)), plus launch gifts for everyone: 5 Regular and 5 Exclusive SHD Keys, and a wishlist-goal reward of the FYNC Parade apparel set with 10 Exclusive and 10 Regular SHD Keys. Twitch Drops returned too. Details in the [launch rewards post](/news/steam-launch-rewards-twitch-drops/).

If the family PC can run a modern free-to-play shooter, it can probably run this. Ubisoft's Help Center puts the PC version at **around 17 GB** and recommends **at least 30 GB free**, which leaves room for seasonal patches.

## How to install

### On Steam

1. Open the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3749720/The_Division_Resurgence/) and install it like any free game.
2. On first launch you'll be prompted to install a **light version of the Ubisoft Connect launcher**. Say yes; that's how your account and progress work.
3. Sign in with your Ubisoft Connect account (or create one).

### On Ubisoft Connect

1. Install the Ubisoft Connect launcher and sign in.
2. Find The Division Resurgence in the Ubisoft store inside the launcher.
3. The game first downloads a launcher, then the game files.

Either way, you can use an existing Steam account, an existing Ubisoft Connect account, or a new one.

## Moving your mobile progress to PC (read this before you log in)

Progress lives on your **Ubisoft Connect account**, not on the storefront. Ubisoft's own order of operations:

1. **On mobile, log into a Ubisoft Connect account first** so your progress is linked to it.
2. **Then launch on PC** (Steam or Ubisoft Connect) and sign in with the **same** Ubisoft Connect account.

**Guest account players, this is the trap:** link your guest account to Ubisoft Connect from the in-game options ("Link to Ubisoft Connect") *before* you log into an existing Ubisoft Connect account on another device. Do it in the wrong order and you can end up with two separate characters and one very bad evening.

If you made your Ubisoft Connect account with Google or Apple ID and never set a password, Ubisoft's fix is the "I have trouble logging in" option on ubisoft.com.

## Crossplay and Dark Zone matchmaking

Ubisoft's Help Center says the game is **fully cross-play on PC and mobile**, and you can play with friends on any device "as long as you are in the same regional server." Servers are regional, so pick the same one as your squad. Season 2 Phase 1 added:

- A dedicated **crossplay selector button** with crossplay matchmaking options and notifications.
- **Mobile-only groups can stay in mobile Dark Zone instances** instead of being forced into cross-platform Dark Zones.

Season 2 Phase 2 then improved Dark Zone matchmaking for mobile and cross-platform groups and fixed crossplay settings sometimes showing the wrong state.

## Purchases on PC

- Steam purchases go through the **Steam shop** and show up in your Steam profile.
- Ubisoft Connect purchases go through **Ubisoft Connect**.
- All purchases are **linked to the Ubisoft Connect account you used when buying**, and you can use them on any platform with that account.

## Settings worth knowing about

Most of these arrived with Season 2 Phase 1, alongside the full PC launch:

- **Field of view slider.** Turn it up. Your neck will thank you.
- **Borderless and Windowed** display modes (their missing labels were fixed) and a **Resolution selector**.
- **Steam language settings** are now respected by the game.
- **Crossplay selector** for matchmaking.
- Earlier, Season 1 Phase 3 fixed the framerate being capped at 30 FPS in windowed mode, and in September Ubisoft's bug reporter marked a "framerate locked to 30fps after recent BP patch fix" issue as Done ([details](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)). If you're stuck at 30 FPS, check your display mode and the bug reporter.

Our [center-ADS peek guide](/guides/center-ads-release-cover-peek-guide/) covers the control settings that matter in fights.

## Controllers, mouse and keyboard

Ubisoft's Help Center says you can play with **mouse and keyboard or a controller** on PC, without listing specific pads. (On phones, Ubisoft officially supports PlayStation, Xbox and Backbone controllers; see the [device compatibility guide](/guides/division-resurgence-device-compatibility/).) The UI was designed for touch first, so some menus feel chunky on a monitor, but in our play, combat, inventory and the map work fine on mouse and keyboard (some Steam reviewers did report mouse bugs; see below).

## Steam Deck

The Steam release ended the old Ubisoft Connect workaround on Deck (NonSteamLaunchers plus Proton-GE) that the community used in the spring. **Season 2 Phase 2 improved the Steam Deck control configuration.** We haven't tested the game on a Deck ourselves and we don't have Valve's compatibility rating on record, so check the badge on the Steam store page before you buy, er, download.

## Steam achievements, trading cards and Points Shop

All three are live. The Steam page lists Steam Achievements and Steam Trading Cards, and Ubisoft's launch post confirmed Trading Cards and Points Shop rewards. Season 2 Phase 1 also added new achievements: **Pet the Dog**, **Legendary Agent** (reach level 40), **Enhanced Combat** and **Properly Equipped**. Yes, there's an achievement for petting the dog. ## Reporting PC bugs

Ubisoft runs an official **bug reporter** for Resurgence: you can upvote existing issues, add details, screenshots or video, and report new ones ([bug reporter](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/the-division/the-division-resurgence/bug-reporter/issues)). For anything else, use Player Support through the support icon in game. Our [error codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-error-codes/) covers the common connection errors.

## How was the Steam launch received?

Mixed. PC Gamer reported on August 9 that the Steam reviews sat at 46% positive, and as of October 24 the store page shows **Mixed** (218 recent reviews, 1,403 English reviews overall). Common complaints included login issues, mouse bugs and the reward-versus-monetization balance. Ubisoft's Season 2 Phase 2 later removed the daily XP cap, reworked daily quest rewards and made the early game easier for new players. Full roundup: [Steam reviews: what players said](/news/steam-reviews-mixed-launch/).

## Five-minute quickstart for new PC agents

1. **Pick your regional server carefully** and match your friends.
2. **Pick a specialization by playstyle:** Vanguard (weapons), Demolitionist (explosives), Tech Operator (drones and skills), Field Medic (healing and skill damage) or Bulwark (tank). Our [specialization guide](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/) explains each.
3. **Run your dailies.** Since Season 2 Phase 2 there's no daily XP cap, and daily quests reward weapons, gear, skill mods and mods by quest type.
4. **Upgrade gear you'll keep** instead of swapping every drop. Our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/) explain why.
5. **Read the patch notes.** The live version is **Season 2 Phase 2**, extended by Ubisoft until "a much bigger update later this year" ([details](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)).

## Common questions

### Do I need Ubisoft Connect if I play on Steam?

Yes, in the background. Steam prompts you to install a light version of the Ubisoft Connect launcher, and your Ubisoft Connect account is what holds your progress and purchases.

### Can I play PC and mobile on the same character?

Yes, as long as both are signed into the same Ubisoft Connect account. Link your mobile account first.

### Is there a console version?

Not announced as of October 24, 2026. The platforms are iOS, Android, and PC (Steam and Ubisoft Connect).

### Does it support ultrawide monitors?

We haven't seen Ubisoft confirm ultrawide support, and we haven't tested it. If you have, [tell us](/contact/) what you see.

### What languages does the Steam version support?

Steam lists 11 supported languages, with full audio in English. French, Italian, German and Spanish, among others, have interface and subtitles.
