**Phoenix Credits** are The Division Resurgence's premium currency: the gold-colored number at the top of the store that every new agent spends way too fast. You can buy them, but you also earn a steady trickle for free just by playing. This guide covers where the free ones come from, what they buy, what a Sealed Cache costs and how to spend them without regretting it a week later.

Sources first, because currency questions deserve straight answers: everything here comes from Ubisoft's Help Center and patch notes, except the cache prices, which come from [Dotesports](https://dotesports.com/mobile/guides/the-division-resurgence-phoenix-credits) (August 2026) and are marked as theirs.

> [!INFO] Quick answers
> Phoenix Credits are earned from **missions, dailies, weeklies, commendations and events**. They buy **Sealed Caches, store bundles, cosmetic draws and clans** (300 to create one, per the Help Center). They **don't transfer** between characters, and purchases stay on their **server**.

## Where free Phoenix Credits come from

| Source | What it pays | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Main and side missions, daily and weekly quests, events | A steady trickle | [Dotesports](https://dotesports.com/mobile/guides/the-division-resurgence-phoenix-credits) |
| Commendations | Varies; the encrypted crate set alone pays **65** | Community thread, see our [safe codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-safe-codes/) |
| Encrypted crates themselves | Some crates drop Phoenix Credits as loot | [Safe codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-safe-codes/) |
| Twitch drops | Phoenix Credits at every watch tier of the launch drops | [Codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/) |
| Compensation | **750** to every player with patch 1.2.1 in May 2026 | [Patch 1.2.1 notes](/patch-notes/patch-1-2-1/) |

The commendation route is the one most people skip. The first three encrypted crate commendations pay **15, 20 and 30 Phoenix Credits** for opening 5, 10 and 21 crates, according to the mod-pinned community thread we link in our [safe codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-safe-codes/). That's 65 credits for a sightseeing tour of Manhattan, which beats most of our weekends.

Keep an eye on our [codes page](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/) too. When Ubisoft runs Twitch drops or an apology payout, we add it there.

## What Phoenix Credits buy

- **Sealed Caches.** The gacha boxes with published odds. More on cost and odds below.
- **Store bundles.** Dotesports lists upgrade and material bundles in the store.
- **Cosmetic draws.** Since Season 2 Phase 1, cosmetic draw screens show detailed pricing in game ([patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/)), so the cost is on screen before you tap. Some apparel and weapon skins come only from draws: Ubisoft's Help Center says they "may appear in the shop" but "you will not be able to purchase them at this time" ([Help Center](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2475-why-can-i-see-some-apparel-and-weapon-skins-in-the-store-but-can-t-purchase-them/)).
- **Creating a clan.** Ubisoft's Help Center lists a one-time **300 Phoenix Credits**. Our [clan guide](/guides/division-resurgence-clan-guide/) covers whether you should found one or just join one.

## What a Sealed Cache costs, and what you get

Per [Dotesports](https://dotesports.com/mobile/guides/the-division-resurgence-phoenix-credits) in August 2026, opening a **Sealed Cache costs 160 Phoenix Credits**, or **50 for a Standard Sealed Cache**, and an **SHD Key** can open one instead of credits. Check the price on the cache screen before you open, because store prices can change between seasons.

The odds are official. Ubisoft's drop rate table gives every sealed cache the same tiers:

| Tier | Chance | Pity |
|---|---|---|
| S | 2.00% | Guaranteed within 50 caches |
| A | 10.00% | Guaranteed within 10 caches |
| B | 88.00% | None |

Pity guarantees a **tier**, not the item you want. Our [drop rates database](/database/drop-rates/) lists every item in every cache, and the [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) does the math for chasing one specific exotic (about 220 caches on average, so maybe don't).

## Is it pay to win?

Ubisoft's Help Center answer is direct: players **can't directly buy weapons, gear, abilities or upgrades** that give them an advantage over players who don't spend, and the goal is for everyone to be competitive whether or not they've bought anything ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2473-is-the-game-pay-to-win/?p=all)).

What that means in practice: caches can hold gear, so spending can buy more rolls at the slot machine. But the free routes to the best weapons are real. Since Season 2 Phase 2, the **Dark Zone vendor sells exotics for 15,000 DZ Credits**, and the **Diamondback sits on the free track** of the Classified Ops Pass ([Season 2 Phase 2 notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/)). Patience is free. It just doesn't feel free at 2am.

## How to spend Phoenix Credits without regret

1. **Decide what you're saving for.** Creator GGReborn lists spending Phoenix Credits without thinking as one of his biggest early mistakes, and his rule is to ask whether a purchase actually helps your build first. More in our [endgame tips](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/).
2. **Spend on the character you'll keep.** Currency belongs to the character that earned it and can't be transferred, and purchases stay on their server ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2487-can-i-transfer-loot-equipment-skins-or-currency-from-one-character-to-another/?p=all)). Pick your server with your squad before you spend. Our [accounts and servers guide](/guides/division-resurgence-account-server-guide/) explains why.
3. **Check the free route first.** Before you chase an exotic in caches, check the Dark Zone vendor and the pass. Our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) says where each one comes from.
4. **Read the odds before you open.** Ubisoft publishes them, and we keep them searchable in the [drop rates database](/database/drop-rates/).
5. **Guest players: link your account first.** Phoenix Credits on a guest account are only as safe as the guest account. Link it to Ubisoft Connect before you spend anything.
