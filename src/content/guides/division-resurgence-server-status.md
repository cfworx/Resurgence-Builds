**Checked November 16, 2026.** Ubisoft has no live status page for The Division Resurgence that we could find, so "is it down?" takes two checks: the official channels, then your own connection. This page walks through both, and lists every maintenance and hotfix so far.

## Check the official channels first

| Where | What you find there |
|---|---|
| [@TheDivMobile on X](https://x.com/TheDivMobile) | Maintenance notices and outage acknowledgements. Ubisoft's help center names this account and the game's website as the places for official announcements. |
| [The official Discord](https://discord.gg/vDPvEU69jw) | The invite on Ubisoft's game page. Announcements, and other players telling you whether it is just you. |
| [Our update page](/patch-notes/) | Ubisoft's latest official posts in one list, and the latest patch and hotfix. |

If one of them reports maintenance or an outage, nothing on your side will fix it. Wait for the all-clear.

## If nothing is announced, it is probably on your side

Work down this list. Each step takes under a minute.

1. **Switch networks.** Go from Wi-Fi to mobile data or the other way round. The most reported login failure is the network connection error, [error 6000010](/guides/division-resurgence-error-6000010/).
2. **Restart the game**, then the device.
3. **Check for an update.** Open the App Store, Google Play, Steam or Ubisoft Connect and install any update that is waiting.
4. **Stuck downloading?** A download that fails partway is [error 271581191](/guides/division-resurgence-error-271581191/), which has its own fixes.
5. **Check your server.** Your characters live on the regional server they were made on. If you do not see your agent, look at which server is selected before anything else. See [accounts and servers](/guides/division-resurgence-account-server-guide/).
6. **Still stuck?** Every code we know and its fix is in the [error codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-error-codes/). To reach Ubisoft, use the contact button inside the game: that is the route its launch update asks players to use.

## What about "is it down" websites?

For big games those trackers work because thousands of people report at once. For this game the reports are a few dozen a day, so a "problems reported" banner there can mean a handful of people with a bad connection. Trust the official account and Discord over a tracker, and a tracker over a guess.
