Six exotic weapons are live in Division Resurgence as of **October 24, 2026 (Season 2 Phase 2)**: **Lady Death** (SMG), **Warlord** (assault rifle), **Strawberry Milkshake** (LMG), **Scorpio** (shotgun), **The Bighorn** (assault rifle) and **The Diamondback**. A seventh name, **Deadlock**, sits in Ubisoft's official drop table and nowhere else. This page is our single source of truth for every exotic: what it does, how to get it today, and what's still unconfirmed.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Rewritten for Season 2. The Bighorn (Season 2 Phase 1) and The Diamondback (Season 2 Phase 2) are live, the old Diamondback "leak" section is gone because the gun is real now, and every exotic's acquisition route is updated for the Dark Zone vendor changes and the new [Exotic Sealed Caches](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/). We also corrected Lady Death's optimal range to the 18m in our database.

## What counts as an exotic in Division Resurgence

Exotic weapons are the rarest weapon rarity in Resurgence. Each one carries a fixed unique talent that can't be recalibrated, which is what separates it from High-End weapons. The talent is the whole point of the gun. Most exotics are designed to break a specific rule of the game: debuffs that transfer between enemies, damage reduction while firing, movement stacks that pay out weapon damage, or headshot stacks that punish you for missing.

They're not always the highest raw DPS in the slot, but they're almost always the most build-defining. Season 2 changed the plumbing around them: exotic mods became removable and lootable in Season 2 Phase 1, and since Season 2 Phase 2 exotics **no longer come with exotic mods pre-installed** (Ubisoft moved those mods into the battle pass instead).

## All live exotic weapons (Season 2 Phase 2)

### Lady Death · Exotic SMG

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | SMG |
| Damage | 299 Blast Damage |
| RPM | 900 |
| Magazine | 32 |
| Accuracy | 50 |
| Reload Speed | 2.98s |
| Optimal Range | 18m |
| Movement Speed | 91 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Breathe Free**
"While moving, grants 4 stacks per second, or 8 stacks if sprinting, up to a maximum of 40 stacks. A stack is consumed with each round fired, granting +15% Weapon Damage. Deploying a Skill consumes 10 stacks and grants +15% Skill Damage. Kills grant +10% Movement Speed for 10 seconds."

Lady Death rewards movement. Sprint to build up to 40 stacks, then dump the 32-round magazine for +15% Weapon Damage per round fired. A full magazine eats 32 of your 40 stacks, so the rhythm is: sprint between fights, anchor just long enough to empty the mag, then move again to recharge.

The Skill Damage clause is what lets it flex into hybrid builds. Deploying a skill consumes 10 stacks for +15% Skill Damage, so a Tech Operator or Field Medic can squeeze offensive value out of it on builds that aren't pure weapon damage.

**How to get it now:** Lady Death is in the [Exotic Sealed Cache](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) pool (0.29% per cache for its weapon cache, 1.43% for its Tactical Augmentation Module).

### Warlord · Exotic Assault Rifle

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | Assault Rifle |
| Damage | 324 Blast Damage |
| RPM | 600 |
| Magazine | 30 |
| Accuracy | 68 |
| Reload Speed | 3.1s |
| Optimal Range | 29m |
| Movement Speed | 54 |
| Stability | 56 |

**Talent · Play Rough**
"While firing, reduces Incoming Damage by 25%. When firing ceases, applies 60% of the reduced damage and grants +10% Skill Damage for 10 seconds."

Warlord is the brawler exotic, straight out of a gamer-dad fever dream: hold the trigger and you take 25% less damage. Stop firing and you eat 60% of what you dodged as delayed damage, plus you get +10% Skill Damage for 10 seconds. Aggressive players sustain through fights by never letting go of the trigger, then bank the Skill Damage buff for a turret or drone follow-up. The clean 29m optimal range keeps it useful almost everywhere in PvE.

**How to get it now:** the **Dark Zone vendor** has sold it since Season 1 Phase 2. Since Season 2 Phase 2 the vendor price for an exotic is **15,000 DZ Credits** (down from 17,500, because exotics stopped shipping with mods). It's also in the Exotic Sealed Cache pool. We originally covered it as the launch-season pass exotic.

### Strawberry Milkshake · Exotic LMG

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | LMG |
| Damage | 300 Shredding Damage |
| RPM | 550 |
| Magazine | 100 |
| Accuracy | 74 |
| Reload Speed | 5.6s |
| Optimal Range | 29m |
| Movement Speed | 19 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Anarchy**
"Hits inflict a debuff, dealing 2.40% Weapon Damage over 4.50 seconds, up to 50 stacks. Enemies dying with this debuff transfer all stacks to a nearby enemy within 12 meters."

One of the cleanest debuff designs in the franchise. Every hit stacks a damage-over-time debuff, capped at 50 stacks. The transfer clause is what makes it nasty: when the target dies, every stack jumps to a nearby enemy within 12 meters. In a packed Hostile Nest or a bounty boss room with adds, every elite becomes a stack reservoir that spreads through the whole pack.

It was the free exotic on the **Season 1 Phase 2** Classified Ops Pass (May 2026). Ubisoft has patched it a few times since: Season 1 Phase 3 fixed its damage type to Shredding and reissued copies, and Season 2 Phase 1 fixed its debuff being applied on heal. It anchors our [Milkshake Machine build](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/).

**How to get it now:** the Season 1 Phase 2 pass is long gone, so the official route is the [Exotic Sealed Caches](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

### Scorpio · Exotic Shotgun

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | Shotgun |
| Damage | 210 Piercing Damage |
| RPM | 130 |
| Magazine | 7 |
| Accuracy | 56 |
| Reload | 2.34s |
| Optimal Range | 16m |
| Movement Speed | 50 |
| Stability | 56 |

**Talent · Septicemia**
"Hitting an enemy inflicts Venom for 2.50 seconds, up to 7 stacks. The target receives a debuff increasing with Venom stacks: 1 - Inflicts Bleed for 10 seconds. 3 - Inflicts Confusion for 2 seconds. 6 - Inflicts Shock for 3 seconds. 7 - Deals additional damage equal to 10% of Damage Dealt for 10 seconds."

The Season 1 Phase 3 exotic (June 2026). Yes, it shares a talent name with the Division 2 Scorpio, but the numbers are all Resurgence's own. Septicemia is a debuff ladder: every hit climbs the Venom stacks, with Bleed at 1, Confusion at 3, Shock at 6, and at 7 stacks the target takes extra damage equal to 10% of damage dealt for 10 seconds. At 130 RPM with a 7-round tube, it's a deliberate close-range debuff platform, not a spray weapon. In his October Tactical Pharma video, TikTokAndy recommends pairing that build with a shotgun that confuses and then shocks its target for Dark Zone PvP. He couldn't remember its name, but that description matches the Septicemia ladder above ([our Season 2 Tactical Pharma build](/builds/tactical-pharma-season-2-build-ggreborn/) covers that matchup).

It launched at **tier 5 of the premium track** of Classified Ops Pass 1.3 (the free track had its Tactical Augmentation Module), (Ubisoft's "grabbed for free" line referred to that free-track module).

**How to get it now:** Ubisoft added the Scorpio to the **Dark Zone vendor** in Season 2 Phase 1 (15,000 DZ Credits since Season 2 Phase 2), and it's in the Exotic Sealed Cache pool.

### The Bighorn · Exotic Assault Rifle

Ubisoft's description: "Blast through your enemies in full auto, or aim down the sight for a semi-automatic experience, giving you a better chance of utilizing its special headshot damage-boosting ability. That's right, each headshot will add extra damage, and more headshots mean more stacked bonuses."

Translation: hip-fire it like an assault rifle, or aim down sights and it turns into a semi-auto headshot machine that stacks damage the more heads you hit. It's the rare exotic that gets better the better you are, which is either thrilling or deeply rude depending on your aim.

- **Released:** Season 2 Phase 1 "The Call," August 5, 2026.
- **Original source:** the free track (and premium track) of the 50-tier Season 2 Phase 1 Classified Ops Pass, plus its Tactical Augmentation Module on the free track. The Bighorn TAM was also added to the Dark Zone vendor.
- **Known fixes:** a bug that forced first person on the Bighorn was marked Done on Ubisoft's official bug reporter on September 22 ([details](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)).
- **How to get it now:** each phase gets its own pass, and The Call's pass made way for Phase 2's on September 16, so the Exotic Sealed Caches are the route now.

We haven't published verified stat lines or the talent's exact numbers yet, so we're not listing them. Ubisoft also ran a [Bighorn headshot community event](/news/bighorn-headshot-weekend-event/) in August.

### The Diamondback

- **Released:** Season 2 Phase 2, September 16, 2026.
- **Source:** the **free and premium track** of the current Classified Ops Pass. Ubisoft also added Diamondback rewards to Dark Zone vendors, reward caches, weekly reward pools and exotic mod sources, and the Diamondback Tactical Augmentation Module was a Season 2 Phase 2 event reward.
- **Talent:** **Agonizing Bite.** Ubisoft named it in the [October 14, 2026 patch notes](/patch-notes/october-2026-hotfixes/), which fixed the in-game build database listing the wrong talent.
- **Weapon class and numbers:** Ubisoft's patch notes and article don't spell them out. Players on the official subreddit describe a 5-round magazine and a skill critical chance bonus in the talent, but we haven't verified those numbers. The Division 2 has a lever-action rifle with the same name, and we're not going to copy its stats into a different game.

Since Season 2 Phase 2 is extended "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year," the Diamondback pass is sticking around longer than usual. If you want a free exotic right now, this is it.

## How to get exotic weapons in Season 2

1. **The current Classified Ops Pass.** One exotic per phase so far, and recent ones (Strawberry Milkshake, The Bighorn, The Diamondback) were on the free track. The Scorpio was the exception, on premium tier 5.
2. **The Dark Zone vendor.** Warlord since Season 1 Phase 2, Scorpio since Season 2 Phase 1, plus Diamondback-related rewards since Season 2 Phase 2. Exotic price: 15,000 DZ Credits since Season 2 Phase 2.
3. **Exotic Sealed Caches.** Added in Season 2 Phase 2 "as a new way to acquire Exotic Weapons from previous seasons." Read the [odds](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) first: about 220 caches on average for one specific exotic, by our math.
4. **Exotic mods** are their own hunt now: the pass, weekly quests, the Elite Mission Challenge chest, and weekly exotic mod crates at the Dark Zone vendor (2,500 DZ Credits each).
5. **Duplicates aren't worthless:** since Season 2 Phase 2, dismantling an exotic grants 420 Encrypted Data on top of the usual materials.

Pass XP tip from our Season 1 testing: Weekly Bounties were among the best pass XP sources. Season 2 Phase 1 increased the pass XP from Weekly Rewards, so that advice has only gotten better.

## Can you recalibrate exotic weapons?

Yes, partially. You can't touch the unique exotic talent, but you can recalibrate the bonus attributes and regular talents using **Exotic Weapon Recalibration Booklets and Manuals**. Season 2 Phase 1 made all exotic manuals and booklets lootable in the Dark Zone, and both show up in the bottom tier of the Exotic Sealed Caches.

## What is the best exotic weapon in Division Resurgence?

It shifts every patch, and Season 2 shifted a lot: damage type bonus rolls are gone (All Weapon Damage replaced them), weapon mods were reworked, and exotics lost their pre-installed mods. Our honest ranking needs fresh testing, so here's how each one fits instead of a fake tier:

- **Strawberry Milkshake** for crowd PvE where Anarchy can chain through packs ([Milkshake Machine build](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/), with Season 2 notes).
- **Warlord** for builds that want to sustain in long firefights and bank Skill Damage for skill rotations.
- **Lady Death** for mobility builds that already move between cover.
- **Scorpio** for close-range debuff and crowd control.
- **The Bighorn** for players with good aim who want an AR that rewards headshots.
- **The Diamondback** because it's free on the current pass, and free is a strong stat.
