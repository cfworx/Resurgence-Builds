Six exotic weapons are live in Division Resurgence as of **October 24, 2026 (Season 2 Phase 2)**: **Lady Death** (SMG), **Warlord** (assault rifle), **Strawberry Milkshake** (LMG), **Scorpio** (shotgun), **The Bighorn** (assault rifle) and **Diamondback** (rifle). A seventh name, **Deadlock**, sits in Ubisoft's official drop table and nowhere else. This page is our single source of truth for every exotic: what it does, how to get it today, and what's still unconfirmed.

> [!INFO] Update (October 28, 2026)
> Every stat table on this page now comes from the in-game Build-Making Database: Level 1, T1 base values with no mods, from screen recordings made on October 28, 2026. That corrected numbers we had taken from creators' cards with mods fitted (Lady Death's reload and range, Warlord's accuracy, reload, range and stability, Strawberry Milkshake's accuracy and range, and Scorpio's accuracy, reload and stability), put Scorpio's damage at 1.68k rather than 210, and gave the Diamondback its official English talent text. The Bighorn is Shredding in the database; some early copies from its pass dealt Piercing instead, a bug Ubisoft's bug reporter marked Done in September.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Rewritten for Season 2. The Bighorn (Season 2 Phase 1) and the Diamondback (Season 2 Phase 2) are live, the old Diamondback "leak" section is gone because the gun is real now, and every exotic's acquisition route is updated for the Dark Zone vendor changes and the new [Exotic Sealed Caches](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

## What counts as an exotic in Division Resurgence

Exotic weapons are the rarest weapon rarity in Resurgence. Each one carries a fixed unique talent that can't be recalibrated, which is what separates it from High-End weapons. The talent is the whole point of the gun. Most exotics are designed to break a specific rule of the game: debuffs that transfer between enemies, damage reduction while firing, movement stacks that pay out weapon damage, or damage stacks that reward headshots.

They're not always the highest raw DPS in the slot, but they're almost always the most build-defining. Season 2 changed the plumbing around them: exotic mods became removable and lootable in Season 2 Phase 1, and since Season 2 Phase 2 exotics **no longer come with exotic mods pre-installed** (Ubisoft moved those mods into the battle pass instead).

## All live exotic weapons (Season 2 Phase 2)

### Lady Death · Exotic SMG

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | SMG |
| Damage | 299 Blast Damage |
| RPM | 900 |
| Magazine | 32 |
| Accuracy | 50 |
| Reload Speed | 2.4s |
| Optimal Range | 16m |
| Movement Speed | 91 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Breathe Free**
"While moving, grants 4 stacks per second, or 8 stacks if sprinting, up to a maximum of 40 stacks. A stack is consumed with each round fired, granting +15% Weapon Damage. Deploying a Skill consumes 10 stacks and grants +15% Skill Damage. Kills grant +10% Movement Speed for 10 seconds."

Lady Death rewards movement. Sprint to build up to 40 stacks, then dump the 32-round magazine for +15% Weapon Damage per round fired. A full magazine eats 32 of your 40 stacks, so the rhythm is: sprint between fights, anchor just long enough to empty the mag, then move again to recharge.

The Skill Damage clause is what lets it flex into hybrid builds. Deploying a skill consumes 10 stacks for +15% Skill Damage, so a Tech Operator or Field Medic can squeeze offensive value out of it on builds that aren't pure weapon damage.

**How to get it now:** Lady Death is in the [Exotic Sealed Cache](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) pool (0.29% per cache for its weapon cache, 1.43% for its Tactical Augmentation Module).

### Warlord · Exotic Assault Rifle

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | Assault Rifle |
| Damage | 324 Blast Damage |
| RPM | 600 |
| Magazine | 30 |
| Accuracy | 50 |
| Reload Speed | 2.5s |
| Optimal Range | 25m |
| Movement Speed | 54 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Play Rough**
"While firing, reduces Incoming Damage by 25%. When firing ceases, applies 60% of the reduced damage and grants +10% Skill Damage for 10 seconds."

Warlord is the brawler exotic, straight out of a gamer-dad fever dream: hold the trigger and you take 25% less damage. Stop firing and you eat 60% of what you dodged as delayed damage, plus you get +10% Skill Damage for 10 seconds. Aggressive players sustain through fights by never letting go of the trigger, then bank the Skill Damage buff for a turret or drone follow-up. The 25m optimal range keeps it useful almost everywhere in PvE.

**How to get it now:** the **Dark Zone vendor** has sold it since Season 1 Phase 2. Since Season 2 Phase 2 the vendor price for an exotic is **15,000 DZ Credits** (down from 17,500, because exotics stopped shipping with mods). It's also in the Exotic Sealed Cache pool. We originally covered it as the launch-season pass exotic.

### Strawberry Milkshake · Exotic LMG

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | LMG |
| Damage | 300 Shredding Damage |
| RPM | 550 |
| Magazine | 100 |
| Accuracy | 50 |
| Reload Speed | 5.6s |
| Optimal Range | 25m |
| Movement Speed | 19 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Anarchy**
"Weapon hits inflict a debuff, dealing 2.40% Weapon Damage over 4.50 seconds, up to 50 stacks. Enemies dying with this debuff transfer all stacks to a nearby enemy within 12 meters."

One of the cleanest debuff designs in the franchise. Every hit stacks a damage-over-time debuff, capped at 50 stacks. The transfer clause is what makes it nasty: when the target dies, every stack jumps to a nearby enemy within 12 meters. In a packed Hostile Nest or a bounty boss room with adds, every elite becomes a stack reservoir that spreads through the whole pack.

It was the free exotic on the **Season 1 Phase 2** Classified Ops Pass (May 2026). Ubisoft has patched it a few times since: Season 1 Phase 3 fixed it showing Piercing instead of Shredding in the Armory and dropping LVL1 talents, with Ubisoft promising an updated copy to everyone who already had one, and Season 2 Phase 1 fixed its debuff being applied on heal. It anchors our [Milkshake Machine build](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/).

**How to get it now:** the Season 1 Phase 2 pass is long gone, so the official route is the [Exotic Sealed Caches](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

### Scorpio · Exotic Shotgun

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | Shotgun |
| Damage | 1.68k Piercing Damage |
| RPM | 130 |
| Magazine | 7 |
| Accuracy | 50 |
| Reload Speed | 1.8s |
| Optimal Range | 16m |
| Movement Speed | 50 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Septicemia**
"Hitting an enemy inflicts Venom for 2.50 seconds, up to 7 stacks. The target receives a debuff increasing in severity based on their number of Venom stacks: 1 - Inflicts Bleed for 10 seconds. 3 - Inflicts Confusion for 2 seconds. 6 - Inflicts Shock for 3 seconds. 7 - Deals additional damage equal to 10% of Damage Dealt for 10 seconds."

The Season 1 Phase 3 exotic (June 2026). Yes, The Division 2 has a Scorpio too, but its talent (Septic Shock) is a different talent; everything below is Resurgence's own. Septicemia is a debuff ladder: every hit climbs the Venom stacks, with Bleed at 1, Confusion at 3, Shock at 6, and at 7 stacks the target takes extra damage equal to 10% of damage dealt for 10 seconds. At 130 RPM with a 7-round tube, it's a deliberate close-range debuff platform, not a spray weapon. In his October Tactical Pharma video, TikTokAndy recommends pairing that build with a shotgun that confuses and then shocks its target for Dark Zone PvP. He couldn't remember its name, but that description matches the Septicemia ladder above ([our Season 2 Tactical Pharma build](/builds/tactical-pharma-season-2-build-ggreborn/) covers that matchup).

It launched at **tier 5 of the premium track** of Classified Ops Pass 1.3 (the free track had its Tactical Augmentation Module). Ubisoft's main article said the Scorpio "can be grabbed for free," but its June 24 events article lists the weapon on the Premium Track and its TAM on the Free Track, which matches what we saw in game.

**How to get it now:** Ubisoft added the Scorpio to the **Dark Zone vendor** in Season 2 Phase 1 (15,000 DZ Credits since Season 2 Phase 2), and it's in the Exotic Sealed Cache pool.

### The Bighorn · Exotic Assault Rifle

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Class | Assault Rifle |
| Damage | 216 Shredding Damage (see below) |
| RPM | 800 |
| Magazine | 40 |
| Accuracy | 50 |
| Reload Speed | 1.9s |
| Optimal Range | 23m |
| Movement Speed | 44 |
| Stability | 50 |

**Talent · Big Game Hunter**
"Aiming switches the weapon to semi-auto firing mode, dealing an extra 300% Weapon Damage per shot. Headshots grant +3.30% Headshot Damage for 20 seconds, up to 50 stacks. On max stacks, loses 10 stacks every 4 seconds. Headshots prevent from losing stacks."

Ubisoft's description: "Blast through your enemies in full auto, or aim down the sight for a semi-automatic experience, giving you a better chance of utilizing its special headshot damage-boosting ability. That's right, each headshot will add extra damage, and more headshots mean more stacked bonuses."

Translation: hip-fire it like an assault rifle, or aim down sights and it turns into a semi-auto headshot machine that stacks damage the more heads you hit. It's the rare exotic that gets better the better you are, which is either thrilling or deeply rude depending on your aim.

- **Released:** Season 2 Phase 1 "The Call," August 5, 2026.
- **Original source:** the free track (and premium track) of the 50-tier Season 2 Phase 1 Classified Ops Pass, plus its Tactical Augmentation Module on the free track. The Bighorn TAM was also added to the Dark Zone vendor.
- **Known fixes:** a bug that forced first person on the Bighorn was marked Done on Ubisoft's official bug reporter on September 22 ([details](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)).
- **How to get it now:** each phase gets its own pass, and The Call's pass made way for Phase 2's on September 16, so the Exotic Sealed Caches are the route now.

So the semi-auto mode isn't just a fire-rate switch: every aimed shot carries +300% Weapon Damage, and a full 50 stacks is +165% Headshot Damage. TikTokAndy showed his own copy in his September 16 video (at 13:00): a Level 1, T1 Bighorn with five level 1 mods fitted, reading 259 damage, Accuracy 62, Reload Speed 2.36s, Optimal Range 27m and Stability 62, and 47 rounds thanks to the +18% Magazine Size on his gear (the base is 40). His card says **Piercing** damage, and the Build-Making Database says **Shredding**. That's not a misread: early Bighorns didn't all match. On Ubisoft's official bug reporter ([TDR-134](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/the-division/the-division-resurgence/bug-reporter/issues/TDR-134)), players found copies from the Season 2 Phase 1 pass dealing Piercing or Shredding damage, one player had both, and the Armory showed Blast for all of them. Ubisoft marked the report Done on September 25, 2026, and the database now lists Shredding, so that's what we go with. If your Bighorn is from the pass, check its own card. He landed headshots of about 7,000 to 11,000 just before he hit level 40, then mentions 15,000 to 17,000 in the level 40 Dark Zone. He says his first Bighorn Tactical Augmentation Module added +20% damage, and that the next level (+40%) needs a second module. How TAMs work for every exotic is in our [Tactical Augmentation Modules guide](/guides/tactical-augmentation-modules-guide/). Ubisoft also ran a [Bighorn headshot community event](/news/bighorn-headshot-weekend-event/) in August.

Ubisoft's [Season 2 Phase 2 article](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/2vmOLuRVDMIMBxG3MZlPQX/the-division-resurgence-season-2-phase-2-is-out-now) calls it an assault rifle in its page summary, but the card says Rifle.
- **First look:** Brazilian creator Capitão Wasabi unlocked it on camera on September 23 ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cwmO9_0k8Fc), Portuguese-language client). Our translation of his Portuguese tooltip, which ran here until October 28, got every number right; the official English text is above.
- **The catch,** in Capitão Wasabi's own pinned comment: five seconds of guaranteed crits on the slowest-firing weapon class. He'd rather the buff carried over to an SMG or shotgun after a swap.

The Division 2 has a lever-action rifle with the same name, and we're not going to copy its stats into a different game. That earlier subreddit report of a 5-round magazine and a skill critical chance bonus turned out to be right.

## How to get exotic weapons in Season 2

1. **The current Classified Ops Pass.** One exotic per phase so far, and recent ones (Strawberry Milkshake, The Bighorn, the Diamondback) were on the free track. The Scorpio was the exception, on premium tier 5.
2. **The Dark Zone vendor.** Warlord since Season 1 Phase 2, Scorpio since Season 2 Phase 1, plus Diamondback-related rewards since Season 2 Phase 2. Exotic price: 15,000 DZ Credits since Season 2 Phase 2.
3. **Exotic Sealed Caches.** Added in Season 2 Phase 2 "as a new way to acquire Exotic Weapons from previous seasons." Read the [odds](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) first: about 220 caches on average for one specific exotic, by our math.
4. **Exotic mods** are their own hunt now: the pass, weekly quests, the Elite Mission Challenge chest, and weekly exotic mod crates at the Dark Zone vendor (2,500 DZ Credits each).
5. **Duplicates aren't worthless:** since Season 2 Phase 2, dismantling an exotic grants 420 Encrypted Data on top of the usual materials.

Pass XP tip from our Season 1 testing: Weekly Bounties were among the best pass XP sources. Weekly Rewards are a separate thing, and Season 2 Phase 1 increased the pass XP they give, so check both every week.

## Can you recalibrate exotic weapons?

Yes, partially, as far as we can tell. You can't touch the unique exotic talent. Every exotic has three talents: that fixed one plus two that roll from the exotic's own pool of 18 (each exotic's [database page](/database/exotic-weapons/) lists its pool). **Exotic Weapon Recalibration Booklets and Manuals** are the materials for recalibrating the rest of the gun. Season 2 Phase 1 fixed them so they can be looted in the Dark Zone as intended, and both show up in the bottom tier of the Exotic Sealed Caches.

## What is the best exotic weapon in Division Resurgence?

It shifts every patch, and Season 2 shifted a lot: damage type bonus rolls are gone (All Weapon Damage replaced them), weapon mods were reworked, and exotics lost their pre-installed mods. Our honest ranking needs fresh testing, so here's how each one fits instead of a fake tier:

- **Strawberry Milkshake** for crowd PvE where Anarchy can chain through packs ([Milkshake Machine build](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/), with Season 2 notes).
- **Warlord** for builds that want to sustain in long firefights and bank Skill Damage for skill rotations.
- **Lady Death** for mobility builds that already move between cover.
- **Scorpio** for close-range debuff and crowd control.
- **The Bighorn** for players with good aim who want an AR that rewards headshots.
- **Diamondback** because it's free on the current pass, and free is a strong stat.
