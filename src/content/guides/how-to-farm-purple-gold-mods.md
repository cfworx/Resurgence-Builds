**The short answer: farm Hostile Nests.** Since Season 2 Phase 2 (September 16, 2026), Ubisoft says Hostile Nests give more Weapon Mod and Gear Mod rewards, and Hostile Nest daily quests now pay out weapon and gear mods on top. The richest version of that farm, according to creator TikTokAndy, is a **Heroic Hostile Nest with a named boss** that only appears for a limited time but can be replayed through team matchmaking while it's up. He reports a purple on every run and a gold roughly one run in three. Skill mods come from a different farm (Dark Zone dailies), exotic mods have their own sources, and none of it helps if you spend your materials upgrading the wrong mods. Below, Ubisoft's facts and creator claims are kept clearly apart.

> [!INFO] Purple, gold or yellow?
> Ubisoft's patch notes call the top mod rarity yellow: Season 1 Phase 2 lists "Blue/Purple/Yellow Skill Mods," and the Season 2 Phase 1 rework mentions "a yellow mod." Most players, TikTokAndy included, say gold. As far as we can tell it's the same rarity, so this guide says gold.

## Where mods come from in Season 2

Everything in this table comes from Ubisoft's patch notes and its official drop rate article.

| Source | What Ubisoft says it gives | Since |
|---|---|---|
| Hostile Nests | Increased Weapon Mod and Gear Mod rewards | Season 2 Phase 2 |
| Hostile Nest daily quests | Weapon and Gear Mods | Season 2 Phase 2 |
| Dark Zone daily quests | Skill Mods | Season 2 Phase 2 |
| Weekly Quest | One random exotic mod | Season 2 Phase 1 |
| Elite Mission Challenge chest | One random exotic mod | Season 2 Phase 1 |
| Dark Zone vendor | A random exotic mod crate for each exotic weapon, one per week each, 2,500 DZ Credits | Season 2 Phase 1 |
| Classified Ops Pass | Exotic mods, added when exotics stopped shipping with them | Season 2 Phase 2 |
| Exotic Sealed Caches | A Weapon Mod Selection for each exotic, 4.19% each | Season 2 Phase 2 |

Phase 2 also "added new Random Exotic Mod Caches and expanded Exotic Mod reward sources," without listing where those caches drop. What Ubisoft hasn't published, as far as we can find, is drop rates for Hostile Nests or quests: the official drop rate article covers sealed caches. So any "1 in X" number you hear about nests, including the one below, is somebody's personal tally. Full context is in our [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).

## The Season 2 mod rework in one minute

Season 2 Phase 1 (August 5) rebuilt the mod stat pools, which changes what's worth farming:

- **Weapon mods:** main stats up 100%, attribute bonuses down 25%. Weapon and Skill Piercing, Blast, Shredding and All Damage left the attribute pool; Weapon Damage %, Skill Intensity %, Headshot Damage %, Weapon Critical Hit Damage % and Skill Critical Damage % joined it. Only new weapon mods roll from the new pool, and leveling a legacy mod updates its growth to the new values.
- **Gear mods:** Elite Enemy and Enemy Leader Resistance are out. In came Max Health %, Armor %, Received Healing %, Weapon and Skill Critical Hit Damage %, Weapon and Skill Multi-Shot Chance %, Weapon Damage %, Skill Intensity %, Headshot Damage %, Weapon and Skill Critical Hit Chance %, and Signature Ability Charge Efficiency %.
- **Exotic mods:** reworked to match the weapon mod changes, and they can now be unequipped from exotic weapons.

Our read: if you want one of the newer weapon mod attributes, like Headshot Damage, you need a mod that dropped after the rework. Leveling an old weapon mod changes its growth, not its attributes. Details are in our [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/).

## TikTokAndy's Heroic Hostile Nest farm

In his September 26 video (embedded above), TikTokAndy farms a **Heroic** Hostile Nest he identifies as the Deserted Warehouse, which ends with a **named boss**. Everything in this section is his report from his own runs, not Ubisoft data.

### What he says it drops

- **A purple every run.** He reports purple mods or weapon attachments on every clear, and no blues at all.
- **Gold roughly one run in three.** That's his estimate from his own session. He says regular nests rarely give him even purple attachments.
- **All kinds of mods:** weapon attachments and gear mods of many types, by his account.
- **Upgrade materials:** he calls out Specialized Maintenance Lubricant and Specialized Maintenance Tools.
- **Gear:** he thinks it drops tier one gear, but not tier two.
- **Short runs:** a couple of minutes per run with a team, in his words.

### The catch: it isn't always there

He says this nest appears randomly and only for a limited time. When he recorded, he expected it to stay up for about 15 more hours, and he'd been playing for a couple of weeks before he first noticed it. While it's up, he says you can repeat it as often as you like through **team matchmaking**. So this is a "drop everything when you see it" farm, not a daily routine.

### How he runs it

- **Bring Blast.** He says the enemies are Rikers and recommends Blast damage. Our [damage types guide](/guides/damage-types-color-bar-weakness-guide/) shows how to read an enemy's weakness bar.
- **Hang back and use cover.** He estimates the enemies hit 50 to 60 percent harder than in a normal nest, says there are more of them spawning from several directions, and calls staying behind cover the best thing you can do. Smart Cover helps, he says.
- **Don't solo it.** He doesn't recommend going alone unless your build is really good, and argues a team is better anyway: everyone gets rewards, and you can help players who couldn't clear it alone.
- **Don't panic about deaths.** He says you can respawn in this nest, so dying doesn't end the run.
- **Kill the boss.** He describes the named boss as a sniper with very little health.
- **Watch your backpack.** With his backpack full, his loot went to the stash, and he says stash items are deleted after 48 hours if you don't collect them. We haven't verified that timer, so empty the stash every session to be safe.

His loadout in the video is a Lightweight M4 with Vanguard's Tactical Link doing a lot of the work. He doesn't walk through the full build in this one; he points viewers to his previous day's video for the setup.

### Finding nests on our map

TikTokAndy names the nest but doesn't give a map spot, and since it comes and goes, we can't pin it for you. Our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) has a **Hostile Nests** layer and a **Nest Investigations** layer: open Layers and tick both (they're off by default). Both are flagged as partial layers because the community is still mapping them, and neither shows which nest is Heroic on a given day. Use the map to learn the regular nest locations, and keep an eye out for the named-boss version.

## Work the nest clock

Regular nests run on a timer, and knowing it is most of the farm. This is from Died Again Gaming's June videos, recorded before Season 2 Phase 2 raised nest mod rewards, so the loot may be better now and the timers could have moved too:

- **15-minute rotation.** Each nest pays its Key Rewards once, then not again until it respawns about 15 minutes later. Watch for the exclamation mark that shows they've reset.
- **20 a day.** He says nest Key Rewards cap at 20 clears a day (open world activities cap at 10).
- **His loop:** clear both active nests, do a few open world activities while they cool down, then go back. Normal nests take him under two minutes each.
- **Challenging and Heroic nests** turn up some days and stay for 24 hours. Their Key Rewards pay once, but the cache at the end pays every run, so keep repeating them. In June the Challenging cache topped out at purple; the Heroic cache, and the bags and crates inside the Heroic nest, could drop gold.
- **Bring Blast to Challenging nests.** Their enemies were usually Blast-weak for him, which fits TikTokAndy's Blast advice for his Heroic nest above.

## Stack the daily quest on top

Since Phase 2, **Hostile Nest daily quests reward weapon and gear mods**. Creator Oldfartsgaming says he always makes sure he has a Hostile Nest quest in his dailies, rerolling ("recycling") quests until one shows up. Do that, and a nest run pays out the nest's own loot plus the quest reward. While you're in the quest menu: Dark Zone dailies pay skill mods, and World Event dailies pay upgrade materials, which you'll need for the upgrade section below.

## Exotic mods are their own farm

Since Season 2 Phase 2, exotic weapons no longer come with exotic mods pre-installed. Ubisoft added exotic mods to the Classified Ops Pass ("so you'll still get the same amount of mods from it") and cut the Dark Zone vendor's exotic weapon price from 17,500 to 15,000 DZ Credits to compensate. Your weekly exotic mod checklist:

1. **Weekly Quest:** one random exotic mod.
2. **Elite Mission Challenge chest:** one random exotic mod.
3. **Dark Zone vendor:** a random exotic mod crate for each exotic weapon, once per week each, 2,500 DZ Credits apiece.
4. **Exotic Sealed Caches:** a Weapon Mod Selection for each exotic sits in the bottom tier at 4.19% each. Odds and math are in our [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

Funding tip, using Ubisoft's own numbers: dismantling skill mods pays 15, 75 or 750 DZ Credits for blue, purple or yellow (since Season 1 Phase 2). Four yellow skill mods (3,000 credits) cover one 2,500-credit exotic mod crate; three (2,250) don't quite. Check your skill mod pile before you sell anything.

## Don't waste the loot: upgrade discipline

Farming purples is the easy part. Not wasting them is the skill. From Oldfartsgaming's October 11 endgame video:

- **Max only the keepers.** He fully upgrades a mod when it has the stats his build wants (for his Engineering build, things like Skill Critical Damage plus Engineering) and skips the rest, saving materials for the next really good one.
- **Save purple materials.** On gear, he says upgrading to level 40 is cheap, but going from 40 to 50 needs purple materials that are hard to find, and he regrets spending them on set pieces that weren't keepers.
- **Dismantle, don't sell.** He dismantles mods instead of selling them, because you'll want the materials.
- **Dismantle, don't feed.** Died Again Gaming (June 7) breaks spare mods down instead of feeding them straight into an upgrade, because a maxed clan dismantling machine raises the chance of extra materials by 60% (more in our [clan guide](/guides/division-resurgence-clan-guide/)). He also says dismantling a mod you've leveled gave back about 90% of what you put in, so leveling a purple with the right stats while you wait for its gold version doesn't cost much. We haven't checked that 90%, so test it on a cheap mod first.
- **Buy the weekly materials.** He buys the vendor's weekly upgrade materials every week, in every rarity.

TikTokAndy makes the same point in fewer words: spend your upgrades on your best mods and attachments. And one official footnote: if you love a weapon mod from before August 5, leveling it updates its growth to the new Season 2 values, per Ubisoft's rework notes.

More of Oldfartsgaming's post-40 advice is in our [endgame tips guide](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/).

## A simple mod-farming routine

Our suggestion, built from the official sources above plus the creator tips:

**Every day**

- Run your Hostile Nest daily (weapon and gear mods) and Dark Zone daily (skill mods). First time in the Dark Zone? Our [Dark Zone guide](/guides/division-resurgence-dark-zone-guide/) covers contamination, extraction and going Rogue.
- Farming gear rather than mods? Which missions lean toward which gear slots, and where Tier 2 drops, is in our [gear farming guide](/guides/division-resurgence-gear-farming-guide/).
- If the Heroic named-boss nest is up, farm it through team matchmaking until your backpack begs for mercy.
- Empty your stash.

**Every week**

- Weekly Quest and Elite Mission Challenge chest for exotic mods.
- DZ Credits on exotic mod crates for the exotics you actually use.
- The vendor's weekly upgrade materials, per Oldfartsgaming.

**Every time you loot**

- Keep mods with the right stats for your build, upgrade those, and dismantle the rest.
