ECHOs are frozen moments from the outbreak: walk up to one and orange holograms replay a scene from where it happened. Division Resurgence has **24**, and the Collectibles screen numbers them ECHO 01 to ECHO 24. 21 are in the open world. The other three play inside missions, so they never get a pin of their own.

Every open-world ECHO has a pin on our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/). Turn on the ECHOs layer, or open the **Checklist** tab and tap a name to fly straight to it. Tick them off as you go and the map keeps count.

![An ECHO playing in Division Resurgence: two orange holographic figures replay a conversation](/images/guides/division-resurgence-echo-locations-echo-hologram.webp "An ECHO playing back. The figures are orange holograms of the people who were there.")

The game names some districts more than one way: "Garment" and "Garment District" are the same area. Chelsea and Hudson Yards hold nine of the 24 between them, so those make the best first sweep.

## How to collect them

1. Turn on the ECHOs layer on the [map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) and pick a district.
2. Walk up to the ECHO. The scene plays with subtitles, and you can skip it.
3. The ECHO lands in Collectibles, ECHOs. Open the Rewards ring there when a stage is ready.

![The ECHOs page of the Collectibles screen in Division Resurgence, with False Leads selected in Pennsylvania Plaza and the rewards ring at 08/24](/images/guides/division-resurgence-echo-locations-collectibles-screen.webp "The ECHOs page: name, district, a line about the scene, and the reward ring counting to 24.")

The three mission ECHOs come with the mission. Triage plays in Take The Castle (Clinton), Kidnapped in Precious Cargo (Chelsea) and Liberty Island in Wall Street Woes. If you rushed past one, replay the mission from its map icon.
