Most "I lost my character" posts in **The Division Resurgence** come down to three things: a guest account logged into the wrong Ubisoft account, a character left behind on another server, or a reward claimed on the wrong agent. None of them are bugs. All of them are permanent. So here is how accounts, servers and characters actually work, using only what Ubisoft's Help Center says.

> [!WARNING] The one rule that saves characters
> If you play as a **guest**, link that guest to a Ubisoft Connect account **that has never played Resurgence** before you log into any other account. Log into an account that already has Resurgence progress and your guest progress is gone.

## Guest accounts: how to keep your progress

From Ubisoft's [guest account FAQ](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2479-i-started-playing-on-a-guest-account-what-happens-when-i-log-into-ubisoft-connect-for-the-first-time/?p=all):

| Your situation | What happens when you log in from the guest account |
|---|---|
| The Ubisoft Connect account has **never** played Resurgence | Your guest progress links to it and you carry on where you left off |
| The Ubisoft Connect account **already** has Resurgence progress | Your guest progress is **lost** |
| You want to keep the guest progress and your usual account already has a character | Log into a **brand new** Ubisoft Connect account directly from the guest account |

Translation: the guest is the one that moves. It can move into an empty house, never into an occupied one.

## Cross-save between mobile and PC

Ubisoft's [cross-progression FAQ](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2480-is-my-progress-shared-across-platforms-what-about-in-app-purchases/?p=all) keeps it simple: use the **same Ubisoft Connect account** on every platform and your **progress and in-app purchases** are saved and shared. That covers iOS, Android and PC (Steam and Ubisoft Connect).

Moving from phone to PC? Log into Ubisoft Connect on your phone first, then sign in on PC with the same account. Our [Steam PC guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/) walks through that, including the Google or Apple ID login snag.

## Playing with friends on other platforms

Ubisoft says the game is fully cross-platform, and you can play with friends on any device **as long as you are on the same server** ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2481-can-i-play-with-friends-on-other-platforms/?p=all)). Season 2 Phase 1 added a crossplay matchmaking selector and let mobile-only groups stay in mobile Dark Zone instances ([patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/)). If your phone squad would rather not meet PC aim in the Dark Zone, that's the switch. Our [Dark Zone guide](/guides/division-resurgence-dark-zone-guide/) covers the rest.

## Changing servers

From Ubisoft's [server FAQ](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2486-can-i-change-servers-once-i-ve-started-playing/?p=all):

- You can **switch servers at any time**.
- Your **characters and their progression stay on the server** they were made on.
- **Purchases belong to the server** where you made them.

So picking a new server to join your friends means starting a new character there. Your original agent is fine, just parked on the old server. Pick your server with your squad before you spend money, not after.

## Characters, rewards and transfers

Two Help Center answers that catch people:

- **Items don't move between characters.** Loot, equipment, skins and currency unlocked on a character stay on that character ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2487-can-i-transfer-loot-equipment-skins-or-currency-from-one-character-to-another/?p=all)).
- **Out-of-game rewards go to the next character you log into.** Rewards claimed outside the game, like a redeem code, land on the first character you log into after claiming, and can't be moved afterward ([Ubisoft](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2482-how-do-i-claim-rewards-on-a-specific-character/?p=all)). Log into your main before you redeem anything from our [codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/).

Clan membership works the same way: it belongs to one character, not the account. More in our [clan guide](/guides/division-resurgence-clan-guide/).

## iPhone keeps asking you to sign in

Ubisoft's [iOS sign-in FAQ](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2557-i-have-to-sign-in-to-my-ubisoft-account-every-time-i-launch-the-game-on-my-ios-device/?p=all) says this happens when iOS doesn't keep the sign-in data in the Keychain. The fix:

1. Force close the game.
2. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
3. Scroll down and tap **The Division Resurgence**.
4. Turn on **Reset sign-in on next launch**.
5. Launch the game. It rebuilds the sign-in data and switches the setting back off.
6. Close and launch again normally to check it stuck. If not, restart the device and try once more.

Ubisoft says this shouldn't delete your account or guest progress, but warns there is some risk if your progress isn't fully synced. **If you're on a guest account, link it to Ubisoft Connect first**, or contact support before trying this.
