**Exotic Sealed Caches** arrived with Season 2 Phase 2 on September 16, 2026, and Ubisoft's own description is simple: "a new way to acquire Exotic Weapons from previous seasons." Missed the Strawberry Milkshake pass in May? The Scorpio in June? This is your second chance. It's also a lottery, and this page shows you the numbers before you scratch the ticket.

Everything below comes from Ubisoft's official **Drop rates** article in the Help Center (Purchases and Monetization section), the closest thing to a live in-game loot database that Ubisoft publishes. The table we read was last updated around September 9, 2026.

## The odds at a glance

| Tier | Total chance | What's in it | Chance per item | Pity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 2.00% | 7 exotic weapon caches | 0.29% each | Guaranteed within 50 caches |
| A | 10.00% | 7 exotic Tactical Augmentation Modules | 1.43% each | Guaranteed within 10 caches |
| B | 88.00% | 21 items: exotic weapon mod selections, recalibration booklets and manuals | 4.19% each | None |

These tier rates and pity timers are identical to every other sealed cache in the game (Standard, OS Protocol and all ten specialization focus caches), which Ubisoft lists at 2% / 10% / 88% with S pity at 50 and A pity at 10. The difference is what sits inside.

## Every item inside

### Tier S (0.29% each)

- Lady Death Cache
- Warlord Cache
- Strawberry Milkshake Cache
- Scorpio Cache
- The Bighorn Cache
- The Diamondback Cache
- **Deadlock Cache** (more on this mystery below)

Ubisoft writes the newer two as "The Bighorn" and "The Diamondback," so we do too in this table. Want to know what each gun actually does before you gamble for it? Our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) has what's confirmed about each one.

### Tier A (1.43% each)

One **Tactical Augmentation Module** for each of the same seven exotics: Lady Death, Warlord, Strawberry Milkshake, Scorpio, The Bighorn, The Diamondback and Deadlock. What a TAM does once you have it is in our [Tactical Augmentation Modules guide](/guides/tactical-augmentation-modules-guide/).

### Tier B (4.19% each)

- **Weapon Mod Selection** for each of the seven exotics (from the name, a pick of that exotic's mod).
- **Exotic Weapon Recalibration Booklets (x3)** and **Exotic Weapon Recalibration Manuals (x3)** for each exotic.

Ubisoft's table lists a booklet and a manual (3 of each) for all seven exotics, Deadlock included: 21 items at 4.19% each, about 88% in total.

## How many caches for the exotic you actually want?

This is the part a per-item percentage doesn't show you. The per-cache chance of one specific exotic is **0.29%, roughly 1 in 345** if there were no pity at all. The pity timer helps, but only with getting *an* S result, not *your* S result.

Our math, assuming duplicates can drop and the pity resets after any S result (the table doesn't say otherwise):

1. **Caches per Tier S result:** with a 2% rate and a hard guarantee at 50, an S result takes about **32 caches on average**, and never more than 50.
2. **S results for one specific exotic:** each S result is one of seven exotics with equal odds, so you need about **7 S results on average** to land a specific one.
3. **Total:** roughly **7 × 32 ≈ 220 caches on average** for one specific exotic. Bad luck can push it well past that, because duplicates don't stop dropping just because you're sad.

If you already own most of the pool, the math gets a little better in spirit but not on paper: a duplicate is still a duplicate. Ubisoft did make duplicates less painful in Season 2 Phase 2, when dismantling an exotic started granting **420 Encrypted Data** on top of the usual materials.

## The Deadlock mystery

The Exotic Cache table lists a **seventh exotic, Deadlock**, with its own cache, its own Tactical Augmentation Module and its own mod selection. As of October 24, 2026, Ubisoft hasn't announced a Deadlock in any patch note, article, Steam post or X post we've checked. We don't know its weapon type, its talent or when it arrives.

Two things we can say without turning them into a rumor:

- Every other exotic in the table is already in the game, so a listed-but-unannounced weapon is unusual.
- On October 21 Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year" ([our coverage](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)). Whether Deadlock belongs to that update is anyone's guess, including ours.

## How do you get Exotic Sealed Caches?

Ubisoft's patch notes don't spell out every source, so here's only what's on the record:

- Ubisoft's Season 2 Phase 2 article lists an **Exotic SHD Keys** daily login event from September 16 to October 26. By name those keys sound like they go with these caches, but Ubisoft doesn't say so outright.
- Season 2 Phase 1 added **SHD Keys and Exclusive SHD Keys to Weekly Rewards**, and Phase 2 event rewards include Regular and Exclusive SHD Keys.
- Ubisoft's drop rate article lives under **Purchases and Monetization**. In his August 5 Steam overview (before these caches existed), GameJoy Explained said premium currency can buy cache keys; we haven't confirmed which keys open Exotic Sealed Caches, so check the cache screen in game.

If you've seen a cache source we haven't, [tell us](/contact/) and we'll add it after we verify it.

## Cheaper routes to an exotic

Before you sink keys into a 0.29% roll, check whether you can just buy or unlock the gun:

| Route | What you get | Since |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Zone vendor | Warlord (since Season 1 Phase 2), Scorpio (since Season 2 Phase 1) and Diamondback-related rewards (Season 2 Phase 2), at 15,000 DZ Credits for an exotic | Price cut from 17,500 in Season 2 Phase 2 |
| Classified Ops Pass | The Diamondback on the free and premium track of the current pass | Season 2 Phase 2 |
| Exotic mods | No longer pre-installed on exotics; they come from the pass, weekly quests, Elite Mission Challenge chests and the DZ vendor's weekly exotic mod crates (2,500 DZ Credits each) | Season 2 Phases 1 and 2 |

Full details are in the [Season 2 Phase 1](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) and [Season 2 Phase 2](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/) patch notes, and the [exotics database](/database/exotic-weapons/).

## Our take

Exotic Sealed Caches are good news for collectors and a long shot for people chasing one gun. If you want the Warlord or the Scorpio, grind DZ Credits instead. If you want Lady Death or the Strawberry Milkshake, caches are now the official route, and the honest expectation is "hundreds of caches," not "a few lucky pulls." And if you crack a Deadlock cache before Ubisoft tells anyone what it is, please, for the love of all that is holy, send us a screenshot.
