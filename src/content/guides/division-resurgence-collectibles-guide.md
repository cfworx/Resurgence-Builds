Division Resurgence fills Midtown with things to pick up: artifacts (objects and notes, in themed collections), audio logs (recorded comms, sorted by who is talking), ECHOs and crates. This guide covers the artifact and audio log collections, with the two sets people ask about most listed in full: the **5 Mysteries** and the **11 Freemen comms**.

Everything here is pinned on our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/). Its Checklist tab lists every collectible by collection, so you can tick them off and fly to the next one.

![The Mysteries page of the Collectibles screen in Division Resurgence, with the Medicinal Plants Book selected](/images/guides/division-resurgence-collectibles-guide-mysteries-screen.webp "The Collectibles screen: each item shows its district, a description, and the collection's reward ring.")

## How collecting works

Each collection has a Rewards ring on the Collectibles screen that fills as you find items, and pays out in stages. The Mysteries stage we saw paid 10 Phoenix Credits, 2,000 credits and five each of two crafting materials. Each artifact also drops a little loot when you pick it up: +1,000 Basic Credits, +1 Standard Metal Components and +1 Standard Fabric on the Mysteries pickups we saw.

To find one, track it. The in-game map draws a ring around the tracked item and your HUD counts down the distance until "You have reached the target".

![The in-game map in Division Resurgence with a tracked artifact ringed in orange in Clinton, and Tracking Artifact 21m in the corner](/images/guides/division-resurgence-collectibles-guide-tracked-artifact-map.webp "A tracked artifact on the in-game map, 21 meters away. This one is the Antique bird call.")
