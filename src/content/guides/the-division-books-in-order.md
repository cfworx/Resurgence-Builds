**Checked November 15, 2026.** There are ten Division books and audio titles so far, and no official reading order. Ubisoft and the publishers do say where most of them sit, so we put those statements together:

1. **Hearts on Fire** (audio drama): before the first game
2. **New York Collapse** (companion book): during New York's collapse
3. **Extremis Malis**, **Remission** and **Broken Dawn**: between the two games, in any order
4. **Recruited**, **Compromised**, **Hunted** (the Operation Crossroads trilogy): after The Division 2

**The World of Tom Clancy's The Division** and **The Art of Tom Clancy's The Division** are reference books. Read them whenever you like.

Six of these are on our own shelf, and each of those links to a page with photos of our copy.

## The four we have not got yet

**Hearts on Fire** is the only one you listen to. It is an Audible Original in six episodes, about five and a half hours, written and directed by Kc Wayland with a cast led by Katee Sackhoff and Shannon Woodward. Ubisoft's summary: a New York firefighter-paramedic is recruited by the Division as the virus spreads.

**Recruited**, **Compromised** and **Hunted** are a trilogy by Thomas Parrott called Operation Crossroads. The first follows Maira Kanhai, a new recruit, after the virus has wrecked Washington, D.C. The second takes her cell to the Gulf Coast, and the third moves to the American Southwest and ends the story. All three are also sold as ebooks and audiobooks.

We will add photos of these when they are on the shelf.

## Where does The Division Resurgence fit?

Ubisoft has not placed Resurgence against the books. Its game page says Resurgence has a new storyline set in New York City, and that you play one of the first wave of SHD agents, seeing the events around the first two games from a new angle. No book on this list is a Resurgence tie-in, and we found none announced.

So nothing here will spoil the mobile game or explain its missions. What the books give a Resurgence player is the world behind it. For the game itself, start with our [beginner guide](/guides/division-resurgence-beginner-guide/).

## Which one first?

- **New to the series:** [The World of Tom Clancy's The Division](/reviews/world-of-tom-clancys-the-division-book/). It explains what the Division is.
- **Want a story:** [Broken Dawn](/reviews/the-division-broken-dawn-novel/). A full novel, still sold new.
- **Want something quick:** [Extremis Malis](/reviews/the-division-extremis-malis-comic/). 72 pages of comic.
- **Want the unusual one:** [New York Collapse](/reviews/new-york-collapse-division-book/). Check the price first.
- **Want pictures:** [The Art of Tom Clancy's The Division](/reviews/art-of-tom-clancys-the-division-book/).

## What is hard to find now

Checked November 15, 2026:

- **Extremis Malis** and **Remission** each showed as sold out or out of stock at one bookseller we checked.
- **Broken Dawn** and **The Art of Tom Clancy's The Division** were in stock at Barnes & Noble.

## The tabletop game

There is also an official tabletop roleplaying game, made by the French publisher Arkhane Asylum with Ubisoft. It was announced on March 24, 2026 and crowdfunded on Kickstarter from May 12 to June 3, 2026, with an Agent's Manual, a Coordinator's Manual and a Starter Set. We could not confirm whether the books have shipped or whether shops sell them yet.
