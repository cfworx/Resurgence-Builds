**Checked November 15, 2026.** Yes. The Division Resurgence has full crossplay between iPhone, Android and PC, and your progress follows your Ubisoft Connect account from one device to the next. Ubisoft sets two conditions: you and your friends have to be on the **same regional server**, and to keep your character you have to sign in with the **same Ubisoft Connect account**.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| iPhone and Android together? | Yes |
| Phone and PC together? | Yes, on Steam or Ubisoft Connect |
| Does my character carry over? | Yes, with the same Ubisoft Connect account |
| Do my purchases carry over? | Yes, on the same account and the same server |
| Can I switch crossplay off? | Yes |
| PlayStation, Xbox or Switch? | No console version has been announced |
| Progress from The Division or The Division 2? | No |

## What Ubisoft says

Ubisoft's PC announcement of April 28, 2026 calls the game "fully cross-play on PC and mobile (iOS and Android)", and says you can play with friends on any device "as long as you are in the same regional server". Its help center puts it in one line: the game is fully cross-platform. When the game reached Steam on August 5, 2026, Ubisoft repeated it as full cross-play and cross-progression across mobile and PC.

## Condition one: the same regional server

Servers are regional, and a character lives on the server where it was made. Ubisoft's help center says you can change servers at any time, but your characters, their progress and your purchases stay on the server they came from. So agree on a server with your squad before you start, and before you spend money. Our [accounts and servers guide](/guides/division-resurgence-account-server-guide/) covers changing servers and what stays behind.

## Condition two: the same Ubisoft Connect account

Progress is saved to your Ubisoft Connect account, not to the phone or to Steam. Sign in with the same account on another device and you continue the same agent. Ubisoft says your progress and in-app purchases are saved and shared.

The one way to get this wrong is a guest account. If you started as a guest on a phone, link that guest account to Ubisoft Connect in the game's options first, and only then sign in on the PC. The step-by-step order is in our [PC and Steam guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/).

## Turning crossplay off, and the Dark Zone

You are not forced to play against people on a different kind of device.

- The Steam page describes the feature as being able to "toggle full cross-play support on or off at will".
- [Season 2 Phase 1](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) (August 5, 2026) added a dedicated crossplay selector button with matchmaking options and notifications, and better matchmaking between Android and iOS players.
- The same patch lets groups made up only of mobile players stay in mobile Dark Zone instances instead of being placed in cross-platform ones.
- [Season 2 Phase 2](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/) (September 16, 2026) improved Dark Zone matchmaking for mobile and cross-platform groups and fixed the crossplay setting sometimes showing the wrong state.
- The mission result screen shows the setting: GGReborn's Legendary run of On The Radio on November 12, 2026 ends on a Mission Completed screen marked "Crossplay Off".

We have not recorded the selector itself yet, so we do not describe its options here. A picture of it goes on this page when we have one.

## What does not carry over

- **Consoles.** The game is on iOS, Android and PC. Ubisoft has not announced a version for PlayStation, Xbox or Switch. A console controller still works: see [which controllers work with The Division Resurgence](/guides/division-resurgence-controller-guide/).
- **The other Division games.** Ubisoft's help center says Resurgence is a new game with a new character, and that it does not affect your progress in The Division or The Division 2. What does cross over is a small set of rewards for having played The Division 2 on the same Ubisoft account: our [codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/) lists them.
- **Another server.** A character on one regional server does not appear on another.
