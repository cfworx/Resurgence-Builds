## A controller, for anyone who plays on a phone

Resurgence is a full cover shooter on a touchscreen, and a controller is the obvious upgrade. Ubisoft says the game supports PlayStation 5 controllers, Xbox controllers and Backbone controllers.

**First, find out what phone they have.** Controllers that plug into the phone need the right port: USB-C for Android phones and iPhone 15 or newer, Lightning for older iPhones. If you cannot find out, buy a Bluetooth controller, which does not care.

## The game that started it

If their only Division game is Resurgence, the 2016 original is an easy present. It came out on disc for [Xbox One and PlayStation 4](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Tom+Clancy%27s+The+Division+2016+game&tag=resurgencebuilds-20) (paid link), and the Ubisoft Store sells a digital Definitive Edition that includes its expansions.

One thing to tell them: it is a separate game. Ubisoft's help center says Resurgence does not share progress with the other Division games.

## What to skip

- **The collectibles from 2016 to 2019.** The Ubicollectibles SHD Agent figurine from 2017 is no longer sold by Ubisoft. The agent watch replica only ever came inside the first game's Sleeper Agent Collector's Edition. You will only find these on the resale market.
- **The tabletop RPG, for this Christmas.** It was crowdfunded in May and June 2026, and we could not confirm that shops have it yet.
- **A plug-in controller for a phone you have not checked.** The wrong port means a return.
