**Checked November 16, 2026.** The Division Resurgence is a free download on iPhone and iPad, on Android, and on Windows PC through Steam or Ubisoft Connect. Those four are the only downloads Ubisoft's own game page links to, and one Ubisoft Connect account carries your character between them.

| Platform | Official download | What it needs | Space |
|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone, iPad | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/the-division-resurgence/id1606349410) | iOS or iPadOS 15.0 or later and an A12 Bionic chip or newer | App: about 4 GB. Whole game: around 10.5 GB |
| Android | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ubisoft.the.division.mobile.combat.shooting.open.world.rpg) | Android 6, 4 GB of RAM, a device on Ubisoft's list | Around 10.5 GB |
| Windows PC | [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3749720/The_Division_Resurgence/) | Windows 11 | Around 17 GB |
| Windows PC | [Ubisoft Connect](https://store.ubisoft.com/62e7cc7251f14849aaca73c8.html) | Windows 11 | Around 17 GB |

## How much space you need

- **Phones and tablets.** Ubisoft's launch update of March 28, 2026 says: "You will need around 10,5GB of space for the game, so we recommend a total of 17GB available space on your device." The App Store lists the app at about 4 GB; Ubisoft's figure for the whole game is the larger one. Google Play does not show a size on its web listing.
- **PC.** Ubisoft's PC announcement says the game takes "around 17GB" and recommends "at least 30GB available". Steam lists 30 GB of available space, and the Ubisoft Store asks for an SSD.

Those phone figures date from launch, and the game has had two seasons of updates since, so leave yourself room above them.

## What your device has to be

- **iPhone and iPad:** the App Store requires iOS or iPadOS 15.0 or later and a device with the A12 Bionic chip or later.
- **Android:** Ubisoft's compatible device list gives Android 6 and 4 GB of RAM as the minimum, and your model has to be on the list.
- **PC:** Windows 11, 64-bit. The full table of minimum, recommended, high and ultra specs is in our [system requirements guide](/guides/division-resurgence-device-compatibility/), and the install steps are in the [PC and Steam guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/).

## Is there an official APK?

We found none. For Android, Ubisoft's game page links only to Google Play, and the help center says no more than "The game is available for iOS and Android devices." There is no Ubisoft page that offers an APK file or an installer for phones outside the stores.

Search results for "The Division Resurgence APK" are third-party sites, some of them offering modified versions. None of them is Ubisoft's, so there is no way to know what is in the file. If Google Play says your device is not compatible, an APK from elsewhere does not change what the device can run: check the [system requirements](/guides/division-resurgence-device-compatibility/) instead.

## Countries where it was not released

Ubisoft's launch press release (March 30, 2026) called the game available worldwide, with a footnote: "Not available in Belarus, China, Cuba, Iran, Russia, Sudan, and Vietnam." We have not seen Ubisoft announce a change to that list.

Everywhere else, the thing to know is that servers are regional. You can switch servers, but characters and purchases stay on the server they were made on, so pick the same one as your friends before you start. Our [accounts and servers guide](/guides/division-resurgence-account-server-guide/) covers it.

## What it is not on

- **Mac:** no Mac version. Steam lists Windows only.
- **PlayStation, Xbox, Switch:** no console version has been announced. A console controller does work on phone and PC: see [which controllers work](/guides/division-resurgence-controller-guide/).
- **Other app stores:** Ubisoft's game page does not link to any store other than the four above.

## After you install

1. **Sign in with Ubisoft Connect** instead of playing as a guest, so your character is saved and follows you to other devices. [Crossplay and cross-progression](/guides/division-resurgence-crossplay/) work between phone and PC on the same account.
2. **Pick your specialization.** [Which class to pick](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/) takes two minutes and saves you a restart.
3. **Claim the free rewards.** The [codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/) lists what can be redeemed right now.
4. **Read the first-40-levels plan** in the [beginner guide](/guides/division-resurgence-beginner-guide/).
