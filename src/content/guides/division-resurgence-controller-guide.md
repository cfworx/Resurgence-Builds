**Checked November 15, 2026.** Yes, The Division Resurgence supports controllers on every platform it runs on.

- **iPhone and Android:** Ubisoft names three kinds as supported: PlayStation 5 controllers, Xbox controllers and Backbone controllers. Ubisoft also says you can connect a Bluetooth controller directly.
- **PC (Steam and Ubisoft Connect):** Ubisoft's FAQ says mouse and keyboard or "a controller of your choice".
- **What we use:** a GameSir G8 Plus over Bluetooth. It is not on Ubisoft's list and it works. Photos and details are in our [GameSir G8 Plus review](/reviews/gamesir-g8-plus-division-resurgence/).

Ubisoft has not published a list of specific models, so the rest of this page separates what is official from what we and others have seen.

Three things to check before you order:

- **Your phone's port.** Plug-in pads need the right one. iPhone 14 and older use Lightning, so they need a Lightning model (Backbone still sells one) or a Bluetooth pad.
- **Headphones.** Backbone's help center says Backbone One units bought on or after August 1, 2026 no longer have a headphone jack. Tom's Guide lists no jack on the G8 Plus either.
- **The Backbone reward.** Backbone ran an in-game reward bundle with Ubisoft, claimed through the Backbone app. Our [codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/) has the steps and the date we last checked it.

## How to connect a controller

**Bluetooth pads** (PS5, Xbox, GameSir G8 Plus, Scuf Nomad): put the controller in pairing mode, then pick it in your phone's Bluetooth settings. On our G8 Plus that means holding the small button next to the charging port for about two seconds until the light blinks fast. Apple's support page confirms iPhones pair with Xbox, PlayStation and other Bluetooth controllers, and that iOS 16 and later let you remap buttons under Settings, General, Game Controller.

**Plug-in pads** (Backbone One, G8 Galileo, Kishi, Vagabond): slide the phone in so the connector seats in the port. There is nothing to pair.

Then open the game. With our pad connected, the HUD showed controller prompts.

## Known limits

- **iPhone:** Ubisoft's May 20, 2026 patch notes list "Issues with gamepad support on iOS" as under investigation. We have not found a later official note that says it was fixed.
- **PC:** on June 5, 2026 Ubisoft acknowledged that controller players on PC needed sensitivity fixes and an option to invert the Y axis. The September patch notes mention improved controller navigation. We have not confirmed whether the invert option shipped.
- **Not stated by Ubisoft anywhere we could find:** which exact models are supported, whether controllers get aim assist, and whether buttons can be remapped inside the game.

We will add the game's own controller settings to this page once we have recorded that menu.

## PC and Steam Deck

On PC the simple route is an Xbox or PlayStation controller, which Steam handles itself. Valve had not rated the game for Steam Deck when we checked on November 15, 2026, though Ubisoft's September patch notes mention a Steam Deck control configuration. Installing, linking your mobile account and the PC requirements are covered in the [PC and Steam guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/), and phone and tablet requirements in the [system requirements guide](/guides/division-resurgence-device-compatibility/).
