Agents, a lot has happened in the last 48 hours. Ubisoft rolled out **Patch 1.2.1** earlier today, the Phase 2 Classified Ops Pass exotic (Strawberry Milkshake) is officially on Ubisoft's "looking into it" list, the subreddit has been loudly documenting disappearing inventory, and Phase 3 is just around the corner. Here's what happened, with zero spin and exactly the right amount of suffering.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Patch 1.2.1 never changed the Strawberry Milkshake's numbers, and no straight buff followed: [Season 1 Phase 3](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/) (June 24) fixed it dropping with level 1 talents instead of level 2 and showing as Piercing instead of Shredding (owners were promised an updated copy), and Season 2 Phase 1 fixed its debuff applying on heal. Phase 3 also fixed Outpost Annihilation and brought the Scorpio, on the premium track (free players got its Tactical Augmentation Module). The game is now on [Season 2 Phase 2](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/), which Ubisoft has [extended](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/).

## Patch 1.2.1: Full Breakdown

Patch 1.2.1 is a hotfix, not a content drop. It cleans up the issues that came with Phase 2's May 12 launch, and it contains no balance changes at all. Full source: [Ubisoft Mobile Help Center, Patch Note 1.2.1](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2513-patch-note---1-2-1-update-20-may-2026/).

### Compensation Package (Everyone Gets It. Yes, Even You)

Ubisoft is mailing this to every account automatically. Check your in-game mail:

- **500,000 Basic Credits**
- **750 Phoenix Credits**

No claim button. No special offer. No "limited time" timer counting down to make you feel worse about your life choices. Just money in your mailbox because Phase 2's launch week was rough and Ubisoft owned it. This is the largest blanket payout we've seen from them so far.

### Vendor Fixes

The vendor had several issues. Here's what's now fixed:

- Fixed vendors not showing items the player could sell. (Your junk drawer thanks you.)
- Fixed players being able to sell locked or favored items. (Your god roll thanks you.)
- Fixed the Dark Zone vendor missing item-name text. (How do you sell a gun with no name? You don't. That's the bug.)

### Strawberry Milkshake: Fixes and the Big Admission

This is the section you've been waiting for. Directly from the patch notes, verbatim:

> *"We are aware that many players report underwhelming perks for the weapon and are looking into how this may be addressed."*

Translation: **Ubisoft has heard you and is looking at it.** That is not a buff, a timeline or a number, and nothing in 1.2.1 changes the gun's stats. But putting it on record mid-phase, instead of waiting for Phase 3 to quietly ship a new exotic, is a good sign, and we're choosing to read it that way.

Other Strawberry Milkshake fixes in 1.2.1:

- **Anarchy talent description fixed**: it was showing the wrong effect value before. Now you can see exactly what the DoT does, in accurate numbers.
- **Weekly mission Exotic Manual / Booklet rewards** can now correctly drop Strawberry Milkshake recalibration material. Farm those manuals and have them ready in case the numbers ever change.

### Lone Wolf Challenge

The reset schedule was broken and is now fixed. It should refresh every 3 weeks as intended. If it doesn't, report it via in-game support so the team can investigate.

### Classified Ops Pass

Two ugly bugs got fixed:

- Fixed players who purchased the pass **not receiving their purchase**. If you paid real money and got nothing, you have our condolences and now also your pass.
- Fixed pass levels **costing more than the listed in-game price**. If you were hit by either of these, Ubisoft says to contact support via the in-game support button. Document your transaction ID.

### Inventory Items Disappearing

The bug where items would randomly vanish when inventory was full has been fixed. Ubisoft also notes that **other issues may have a similar effect**, so if it happens again, submit a ticket immediately and document everything with screenshots.

If you lost items before this patch? That's handled case by case through support tickets, so send screenshots and dates with yours.

### Localization Cleanup

Not glamorous, but real:

- "Sold Out" now translates correctly in the Specials store.
- Exotic Weapon Tactical Augmentations Cache translations added in the Dark Zone vendor.
- Untranslated text after purchasing deals in non-English languages fixed.
- "Text not found" placeholder for the Exclusive SHD Key reward fixed.

If you've been playing in a non-English language, Phase 2 has been an *adventure* in mystery text. 1.2.1 cleans a lot of that up.

### Still Under Investigation (Known Issues, Still Not Fixed)

Ubisoft explicitly lists these as ongoing: they are aware, and they are not fixed yet.

- Gamepad support issues on iOS
- "Outpost Annihilation" side mission not progressing (players say since launch)
- Some purchases still not being received in-game
- Tactical Augmentation reward from the Dark Zone quest cannot be claimed
- Weapon proficiency blocked in some cases

Players say Outpost Annihilation has been broken since beta, so seeing it on an official known-issues list is genuinely welcome. (Spoiler from October: Season 1 Phase 3 fixed it on June 24, and patch 1.1.2.2 on May 29 fixed the Shotgun Tactical Augmentation reward for the Dark Zone "Ringleaders" mission.)

## Phase 2 Recap: In Case You Missed the Chaos

Phase 2 of Season 1 went live on **May 12, 2026** at 3:00 PM PDT ([Ubisoft, Phase 2 Starts Now](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/the-division/resurgence/news-updates/3ygDDXxg6dv6aQD5MJvbKv/phase-2-starts-now-whats-new)). The big-ticket items are still active right now.

The MM04 speedrun (May 15 to 19) has already ended. Don't worry about it. Grieve privately.

### Phase 2 Balance Changes Worth Knowing

These are not in 1.2.1; these came in Phase 2 itself and are still in effect:

- **Dark Zone Credits on skill mod dismantle buffed:** Blue/Purple/Yellow went from 10/50/450 → **15/75/750**. A long-requested QoL change that the community actually liked. A genuine win.
- **Tactical Augmentation Modules** (weapon, gear, exotic) now give **Encrypted Data instead of Clan Credits** on dismantle. Better long-term economy.
- **Warlord added to the Dark Zone Vendor**: finally a reliable path to one of the strongest exotics in the game. **Tip:** switch to **Vanguard or your intended Firepower spec BEFORE buying**. In our experience the rolls follow your active specialization's primary stat (Firepower vs. Engineering). Ubisoft hasn't documented that, but switching first costs nothing.
- **Strawberry Milkshake Tactical Augmentation Module** added to the Dark Zone Vendor.
- **+1 Exotic Weapon Enhancement Kit** added to Dark Zone Vendor stock.
- **Weekly Random Tactical Augmentation Module Crate** added to the Dark Zone Vendor.
- **+1 copy** of regular Weapon and Gear Tactical Augmentation Modules added to the Clan Vendor.
- **Up to 60 FPS** on some high-end devices, depending on the device (per Ubisoft). Players report Snapdragon 8 Elite-class phones holding it.
- **Brains Over Brawn OS** bug fixed: it was scaling off the wrong stats. It now does what it says on the tin.

## Strawberry Milkshake: Weapon Watch

The Phase 2 free exotic LMG is the dominant conversation in the community right now, and for good reason. Let's break down what's actually confirmed vs. what's Reddit math.

### What's Confirmed (Official Sources)

- Free reward on the Classified Ops Pass 1.2 track.
- **Anarchy** talent: the description was wrong before 1.2.1 and is now fixed. The patch notes don't print the numbers, but the corrected in-game tooltip reads: **hits inflict a debuff dealing 2.40% Weapon Damage over 4.50 seconds, up to 50 stacks; enemies dying with the debuff transfer all stacks to a nearby enemy within 12 meters.** The pre-fix tooltip reportedly showed 100% WD / 10 seconds / 25 meters, which would explain why early build guides had wildly wrong expectations.
- Tactical Augmentation Module sold at the Dark Zone Vendor.
- Ubisoft is "looking into" the underwhelming perks. That part is in writing, in the official patch notes. The word "buff" is not.

### What's Unconfirmed (Community Reports)

- One bullet reportedly does ~7,900 damage. The Anarchy DoT reportedly does ~38 per tick. If you're doing that math right now, yes, it's as underwhelming as it sounds. (From u/N43n1r4's "About the Strawberry Milkshake" post on r/thedivisionresurgence, mid-May.)
- The DoT allegedly spreads across multiple enemies on hit; the community comparison to Division 2's Pestilence is everywhere right now.

### The Verdict

**Do not burn your recalibration materials on Strawberry Milkshake until Ubisoft actually changes it.** Farm the Weekly Mission Exotic Manual drops to stockpile recalibration materials now, so you're ready to move if updated numbers ever hit. Our [Milkshake Machine build](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/) is where we'll track any changes.

## What the Community Is Frustrated About (And Not)

### Frustrated About

- **Disappearing inventory.** Multiple players have screenshots showing pre-Phase 2 inventories with items that vanished post-patch. 1.2.1 should stop new losses, and recovery of already-lost items is handled ticket by ticket. The 500K Basic Credits / 750 Phoenix Credits mass payout today is a meaningful step toward compensating affected players.
- **Outpost Annihilation** is still bugged. Players say it has been broken since beta, and it's on Ubisoft's known-issues list.
- **Warlord proficiency wiping** on some accounts: a community-reported bug that players say has hit iOS especially hard. Ubisoft's known-issues list does include weapon proficiency being blocked in some cases.
- **The sell function** being unavailable for 3+ days for some players. Patch 1.2.1 directly addresses this one.

### Actually Enjoying

- **60 FPS on supported hardware.** The visual upgrade is real, and the community on Snapdragon 8 Elite devices is noticeably happier.
- **Dark Zone Credit buffs** on skill mod dismantling. Small change, loud reception: this is exactly the kind of QoL fix that keeps grinders grinding.
- **Warlord in the DZ vendor.** A real, predictable acquisition path for a top-tier exotic. The community waited for this. It's here.

## Bottom Line

Patch 1.2.1 is Ubisoft's "we hear you" patch. The 500K Basic Credits and 750 Phoenix Credits compensation is the largest blanket payout we've seen from them so far, and the list of acknowledged-but-not-yet-fixed issues at the bottom of the patch notes is unusually honest by live-service standards. Most studios bury those acknowledgments. Ubisoft put them in writing.

The Strawberry Milkshake acknowledgment is the most important signal in this patch. Ubisoft is willing to revisit a flagship Pass exotic **mid-phase** rather than waiting until Phase 3 quietly makes it irrelevant. That's a good precedent.

Phase 3 in June is the next real milestone. If you've been on the fence about grinding the current Pass, 1.2.1 should make the vendor flow functional again. The Encrypted Data conversion on Tactical Aug dismantles is a quiet but meaningful long-term economy upgrade that rewards consistent grinders.

We'll update our Milkshake build the moment Ubisoft changes the numbers. Full Phase 3 preview drops in June. Until then: keep your inventory screenshotted, keep your recalibrations planned, and give Outpost Annihilation a rest until it's fixed.
