**Ubisoft's Bighorn community challenge runs from Friday, August 28 at 6PM CET until Sunday at midnight.** The goal: a collective **300,000 headshot kills** with the Bighorn exotic. Hit it, and Ubisoft says that unlocks "an extra Tactical Augmentation for all participants." To pitch in you need the Bighorn, which sits on the free track of the Season 2 Phase 1 Classified Ops Pass. Then aim for heads. That's it. That's the event.

Ubisoft's Steam post: "Help us get a collective 300 000 headshot kills ingame this weekend with the Bighorn exotic weapon and unlock an extra Tactical Augmentation for all participants!"

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> We found no official post confirming whether the 300,000 goal was reached or when the reward went out: nothing on Steam, on Ubisoft's news pages or in the X posts we could see. If you took part and got the extra Tactical Augmentation, [tell us](/contact/). If you missed the Bighorn entirely, it's in the Exotic Sealed Cache pool added in Season 2 Phase 2 ([our guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/)).

## The challenge in plain numbers

- **Goal:** 300,000 headshot kills with the Bighorn, community-wide.
- **Window:** Friday, August 28, 6PM CET, to Sunday, August 30, at midnight.
- **Reward:** "an extra Tactical Augmentation for all participants." The post doesn't say which Tactical Augmentation Module, or exactly what counts as participating. We'd assume landing at least one Bighorn headshot kill in the window counts, but that's our guess, not Ubisoft's rule.
- **The math:** reading "Sunday at midnight" as the end of Sunday, the window is about 54 hours, so the community needs roughly 5,500 Bighorn headshot kills an hour. Every single one of your Saturday afternoon headshots is load-bearing.

**Time zones:** Ubisoft wrote CET. Central Europe runs on summer time in August, so if that's Paris clock time, the start is 4PM UTC, which is noon US Eastern and 9AM Pacific. If it's CET in the strict sense, add an hour. We'd assume Sunday's midnight uses the same clock.

## What the Bighorn does, in Ubisoft's words

From Ubisoft's Season 2 launch article: "Blast through your enemies in full auto, or aim down the sight for a semi-automatic experience, giving you a better chance of utilizing its special headshot damage-boosting ability. That's right, each headshot will add extra damage, and more headshots mean more stacked bonuses for devastating strikes!"

Translation for this weekend: hip-fire is full auto, aiming down sights switches it to semi-auto, and every headshot stacks more damage. Ubisoft's own pitch says aiming down sights gives you a better chance at that headshot bonus, so for a headshot event the plan writes itself. Ubisoft's posts don't give the exact stack values or cap, and we're not going to guess them, so read the talent text in the Armory. Our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) has everything Ubisoft has confirmed about the Bighorn so far; its stack values aren't verified yet.

## How to get the Bighorn

- **Free track:** Ubisoft lists the Bighorn exotic and the Bighorn Tactical Augmentation Module on the free track of the 50-tier Season 2 Phase 1 Classified Ops Pass.
- **Premium track:** "Grab it now through the premium Classified Ops Pass, or unlock it for free through the normal Classified Ops Pass."
- **Dark Zone vendor:** the Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes added the Bighorn Tactical Augmentation Module to the Dark Zone vendor. That's the module, not the gun.

If you haven't unlocked it yet, there's still time to push pass tiers before Friday evening in Europe. Full pass details are in our [Season 2 launch coverage](/news/season-2-the-call-live-bighorn/).

## Squeezing out more headshots

None of this is secret tech, and we haven't tested anything special for this event:

- **Aim down sights.** Ubisoft's own description says semi-auto ADS gives you the better shot at the headshot bonus.
- **Look at Headshot Damage rolls.** Season 2 Phase 1's mod rework added Headshot Damage % to the weapon mod and gear mod attribute pools (for weapon mods, only new ones get the new pool). In our understanding, more headshot damage helps turn headshots into headshot kills faster. Details in our [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/).
- **Double-dip with High Caliber.** The Assault Rifle quest event runs until August 31, and the Bighorn is an assault rifle ([event calendar](/news/take-the-castle-speedrun-paratrooper-set/)).
- **Play what you can repeat comfortably.** More enemies per minute means more heads per minute. The best farm is the one you won't quit after 20 minutes.

And if your aim is like ours, the community is counting on everyone else. Contribute anyway. Every headshot counts, even the accidental ones.
