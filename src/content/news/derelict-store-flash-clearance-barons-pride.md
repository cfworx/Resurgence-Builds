Ubisoft posted on Steam again. On **Friday, November 13, 2026**, the game's Steam page got its first announcement since October 19, titled "The Baron's Pride & Derelict Store." It's short: two headings, two pictures and two sentences. Here's all of it, plus what we can add from Ubisoft's earlier posts.

## Derelict Store: a flash clearance

Ubisoft's text, in full: "A flash clearance is underway. Move through the abandoned store at speed and empty every aisle of Freemen."

So it's a timed run through the Derelict Store, and the Freemen are the ones blocking the aisles. If the name sounds familiar, that's because this is its third outing as an event:

| When | What Ubisoft called it | How it worked |
|---|---|---|
| May 29 to June 2 | OW Nest, Derelict Store (Season 1 Phase 2) | A speedrun with separate solo and team leaderboards. "Rewards are doled out based on your ranking." |
| September 18 to 21 | Challenging (Season 2 Phase 2) | "There's nothing left to buy at the Derelict Store, but everything to fight for." Finish inside the time limit for rewards |
| Announced November 13 | Derelict Store flash clearance | "Move through the abandoned store at speed." No dates or rewards in the post |

> [!WARNING] No dates in the post
> Ubisoft's announcement doesn't say when the event ends or what it pays. The three timed missions earlier in this phase (Derelict Store, Wall Street Woes and Liberty & Justice For All) each ran Friday to Monday. This one was announced on a Friday, so **Monday, November 16** could be the last day if it follows the same pattern. That's our reading of the calendar, not Ubisoft's word. The in-game Events tab has the real timer.

If you're going in, bring whatever clears rooms fastest. Our [builds](/builds/) are over here, and nobody has ever lost a speedrun by killing things too quickly.

## The Baron's Pride

Ubisoft's text, in full: "A fitting companion for those who prefer giving orders instead of following them."

That's the whole description. The post doesn't say what kind of item The Baron's Pride is, how you get it or what it costs, and we'd rather tell you that than invent a category for it. Whatever it turns out to be, the price is on the screen in game: read it first, then decide.

## Where this leaves the season

- **Still Season 2 Phase 2.** Ubisoft [extended the phase](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/) on October 21 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year." Nothing in this post changes that, and there's still no date for the bigger update.
- **The first event on Ubisoft's Steam feed since the extension.** The schedule Ubisoft published for Phase 2 ended with the Exotic SHD Keys login event on October 26 ([the full list](/news/season-2-phase-2-live-diamondback/)). An extended phase with something to do in it beats an extended phase without.
- **No patch.** No new patch notes on Steam or in Ubisoft's Help Center as of November 16. The current rules are still the [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).

We'll add the dates and rewards here once we've confirmed them.
