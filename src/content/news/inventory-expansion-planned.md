Good news for every agent who spends more time in the dismantle menu than in the Dark Zone: Ubisoft's official bug reporter now lists **TDR-537, "Expand Inventory Capacity,"** as **Planned**, updated **Friday, October 23**. The one thing to keep in mind: Planned is a status on Ubisoft's tracker, **not a release date**, and there's no word yet on how much space, when, or in which update. The same day, the official account's Fun Fact Friday said agents dismantled **34.5 million weapons and gear pieces** in the game's first 33 days, which explains a lot about everyone's backpack.

## What Planned means, and what it doesn't

Planned is one of the statuses Ubisoft sets on its tracker, alongside Done, In Progress and Deprioritized. TDR-537 reads like a feature request rather than a bug, and by the plain meaning of the word, Ubisoft intends to do it. What the status doesn't give you:

- a date
- a number of extra slots
- the update it ships in

On October 21 Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year" ([our coverage](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)). Is inventory expansion part of that? Ubisoft hasn't said, so anyone who tells you it is, is guessing.

Want to show support? Ubisoft has asked players to search for existing reports and contribute to them before filing new ones, so upvote TDR-537 rather than posting a duplicate ([how the tracker works](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)). Other community suggestions include mod recalibration, which a [community post](https://www.reddit.com/r/thedivisionresurgence/comments/1x5osoi/can_we_please_get_mod_recalibration_our_sanity/) on October 14 pointed to on the tracker as TDR-652. That one didn't get the same news: Ubisoft set it to Deprioritized on October 22 ([our bug reporter roundup](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-october-23/)).

## 34.5 million dismantles, in perspective

Ubisoft's October 23 Fun Fact Friday: "34.5 MILLION weapons and gear pieces were dismantled by Agents in the first 33 days of The Division Resurgence!" The post doesn't put dates on those 33 days (the game launched on iOS and Android on March 31). Our math on the number it did give:

| Per | Dismantles (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Day | 1.05 million |
| Hour | 43,600 |
| Minute | 726 |
| Second | 12 |

Twelve a second, around the clock, for a month. Somewhere right now, a dad is dismantling his fortieth junk drop while his kid asks what's for dinner.

## Inventory tips until the extra space arrives

### What Ubisoft's notes say about dismantling

- **Skill mods** dismantle into **Dark Zone Credits**: 15, 75 or 750 for blue, purple or yellow, since Season 1 Phase 2.
- **Tactical Augmentation Modules** dismantle into **Encrypted Data** (instead of Clan Credits), also since Season 1 Phase 2.
- **Exotic weapons** dismantle for **420 Encrypted Data** on top of their existing materials, since Season 2 Phase 2.
- **Leave some headroom.** Patch 1.2.1 (May 20) fixed items randomly disappearing, "generally when inventory is full." That's fixed, but we still like keeping a few slots free.

### What creators recommend

- **Dismantle mods, don't sell them.** That's Oldfartsgaming's advice in his October 11 endgame video: you'll want the materials to upgrade the mods worth keeping. More of his rules are in our [endgame tips guide](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/).
- **Empty your stash.** TikTokAndy says that when his backpack was full, loot from his Hostile Nest runs went to his stash, and that stash items are deleted after 48 hours if you don't collect them. We haven't verified that timer or found it in Ubisoft's notes, though a player's report on the official bug tracker ([TDR-600](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/the-division/the-division-resurgence/bug-reporter/issues/TDR-600)) confirms packages can expire: he couldn't claim one because of a false "inventory full" error, and he wrote that it was about to expire. Treat it as "check the stash every session" rather than gospel. His nest farm, which fills a backpack fast, is in our [mod farming guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/).
- **There's a paid option.** GameJoy Explained's Steam overview says the monthly subscription adds inventory space, among other perks. We haven't confirmed the details, and we're not telling you to buy it, only that the creator describes it.

### How much space you have now

Creator videos show the counters. On a brand-new account, [Porps's October 28 stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FOrckQW_S8c) reads **150** gear pieces, **400** mods and **300** OS Protocols, and the gear counter reads x/150 in [Demø2x's](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ufNZIXHoR00) and [GGReborn's](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AUfSHF6gYjg) October videos too. Some accounts have more: [RogueMadras's August 23 stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KNgtd0m4QP4) shows 163/180 gear, and TikTokAndy's [October 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHKfmW4BtEk) and [October 27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RYtZyN_0P0Y) videos show x/180 as well, with mods still at 400. None of them says where the extra 30 gear slots come from. GameJoy's subscription description would explain it, but we haven't confirmed that.
