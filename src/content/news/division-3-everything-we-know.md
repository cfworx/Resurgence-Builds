**Checked November 17, 2026.** The Division 3 is real and in production at Massive Entertainment, the studio behind the first two games. Ubisoft has not shown any footage of it, and has not announced a release date, platforms or a setting.

If you play The Division Resurgence, this is the game that comes next for the series. Here is what Ubisoft and Massive have actually said, and what is only guesswork.

## What is confirmed

- **The game exists, and Massive leads it.** Ubisoft announced The Division 3 on September 21, 2023, in the same post that made Julian Gerighty executive producer of the series: "Tom Clancy's The Division 3, which will be led by Massive Entertainment."
- **It is in production.** At the New Game+ Showcase in January 2026, Gerighty said: "So The Division 3 is in production, right? This is not a secret, it's been announced." He added: "It's shaping up to be a monster." Windows Central reported both lines.
- **The leadership changed, the game did not stop.** A week later, Gerighty left Ubisoft for Battlefield Studios at EA. Ubisoft said Yannick Banchereau and Mathias Karlson stay on as creative directors for The Division 2 and The Division 3, as Video Games Chronicle reported.
- **Massive is still hiring for it.** The studio's project page says it is "actively recruiting for the project" and lists open roles in Malmö. It also says Massive "cannot unveil any details about the third entry in the series at this time."
- **The studio got a bigger name.** On September 30, 2026, Ubisoft put its teams in Montréal, Paris, Sweden and Toronto that work on The Division, Ghost Recon and Splinter Cell under the Massive Entertainment name, led by Christoph Hartmann. Ubisoft's post does not mention The Division 3 by name.

## What job listings suggest

These come from Massive's job ads as reported in June 2026. Ubisoft has not confirmed them as features.

- **An online game with live content.** GamingBolt quoted the Lead Game Designer listing: the role covers "design and execution of key Social and Online features" and helps "shape the foundations required to support the game as a live experience." That points to new content after launch, though Massive has not said what form it takes.
- **The Snowdrop engine.** Tech4Gamers reported that the Animation Director listing refers to Snowdrop, the engine behind The Division and The Division 2.

## Rumors and guesses

- **"Out in 2028" or "2030."** These are estimates, not dates. ComicBook.com (June 5, 2026) reasoned from typical development times that 2027 or 2028 would be normal, then said the June job listings point closer to 2029 or 2030. The same article says: "right now, we only have speculation."
- **"Division 3 trailer" videos.** Ubisoft has released no trailer, so anything with that title is fan-made or cut from the earlier games.
- **"Division 3 builds."** Ubisoft has not shown a single piece of gear, talent, skill or weapon from the game. A Division 3 build guide written today is a guess.

## Is Resurgence The Division 3?

No. [The Division Resurgence](/guides/division-resurgence-download/) is a free game for iPhone, Android and PC, made by Ubisoft Mobile Games. Ubisoft says it is set in New York City during the Green Poison outbreak, "between the events of The Division and The Division 2." The Division 3 is a separate game from a different team, and nothing about it changes Resurgence.
