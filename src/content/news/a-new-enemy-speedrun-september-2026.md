**The A New Enemy speedrun weekend runs from Friday, September 4 to Tuesday, September 8, 2026.** Ubisoft's Steam post: "This weekend a new challenge awaits you in New York! The Freemen are roaming the streets and we need your help to finish the mission 'A New Enemy' in record time and win great rewards." Ubisoft's Season 2 Phase 1 event list says those rewards include the **"Lightspeed Agent"** title. It's the last of the four events Ubisoft listed for The Call, so if you still want the title, this is the one.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> This was the final event on The Call's list. Season 2 Phase 2 launched on September 16 with the Diamondback exotic, and its timed missions were a Derelict Store challenge, Wall Street Woes and Liberty & Justice For All, with no A New Enemy. Details in [Season 2 Phase 2 is live](/news/season-2-phase-2-live-diamondback/).

## The event

- **Dates:** September 4 to 8, 2026. It's billed as a weekend event and the dates stretch to Tuesday, so the "I'll do it Sunday night" crowd (us) gets two bonus evenings.
- **Mission:** A New Enemy. Ubisoft's event list sums it up as "A new threat emerges in the city," and the Steam post names the threat: the Freemen.
- **Goal:** finish the mission in record time. Ubisoft's article puts it as racing the clock.
- **Rewards:** include the "Lightspeed Agent" title, the same title Ubisoft listed for the Wall Street Woes (August 7 to 11) and Take the Castle (August 21 to 25) speedruns this phase. Our [Take the Castle post](/news/take-the-castle-speedrun-paratrooper-set/) has the rest of the Season 2 Phase 1 calendar.
- **Where:** our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) lists A New Enemy under Mission Instances.

## Been here before

A New Enemy was also a Season 1 Phase 3 speedrun, tagged MM04, from July 10 to 15 ([our July post](/news/a-new-enemy-speedrun-july-2026/)). The mission is the same. Your build probably isn't.

Season 2 Phase 1 reworked weapon mods (main stats up 100%, attribute bonuses down 25%), removed Blast, Shredding and Piercing bonus attributes from the weapon pool in favor of All Weapon Damage and All Skill Damage, and retuned several talents, skills and skill mod sets, including Steady Reload, Recycling, Shockwave Spike and the Ammo Refill combo set. If you cleared this in July and haven't touched your loadout since, run it once before you go for time.

One specific heads-up: if your July speedrun build was a Vanguard Double Barrel shotgun leaning on Commando's "Bullet Back," Ubisoft deliberately made that mechanic less reliable on the Double Barrel while keeping it similar for weapons with bigger magazines. Target Acquisition's signature ability charge went from 30% to 5% and its cap from 90% to 20%, and Concerted Strike now triggers on shots rather than hits. Full notes in our [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/).

## Speedrun prep

We haven't timed this version, so no route guide. The basics:

1. **Bring a build that clears fast today,** not the one that cleared fast in July. Our [builds page](/builds/) is a good place to start.
2. **Do one practice run** so the Freemen don't surprise you on the clock.
3. **Read the event screen** for the time limit and the full reward list.
4. **Optional style points:** wear the Freemen apparel set from Season 1 Phase 3 while you hunt Freemen. It's purely cosmetic as far as we know, but the irony is free.

## What's next

Ubisoft's roadmap promises a new phase every six weeks, and six weeks after The Call started on August 5 is Wednesday, September 16. That's our math, not an announced date. Until the next phase arrives, The Call's Classified Ops Pass and its free Bighorn are still yours to finish.
