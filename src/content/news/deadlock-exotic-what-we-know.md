**Division Resurgence has a seventh exotic waiting in the wings.** It's called **Deadlock**, it already sits in Ubisoft's official drop table, and Ubisoft's own Season 2 launch trailer listed it as the **free exotic weapon of Classified Ops Pass 2.3**. That pass never started, because Season 2 Phase 2 was extended. With the [Winter Update landing December 1](/news/winter-update-december-1-agent-briefing/), here's everything that's confirmed about Deadlock, and everything that isn't.

## Where Deadlock shows up

**1. Ubisoft's Season 2 launch trailer.** The trailer for Season 2 "The Call" (August 2026) ends on a card that lays out the whole season as three Classified Ops Passes:

| Pass | Apparel set | Free exotic weapon | Weapon skin | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Vintage Elegance | The Bighorn | Antique Weaponry | Ran in Season 2 Phase 1 (August 5) |
| 2.2 | Football League | The Diamondback | Milky Way | Season 2 Phase 2 (since September 16), extended |
| 2.3 | Jogging Enthusiast | **Deadlock** | Stickers Galore | Never started |

![The closing card of The Division Resurgence's Season 2 launch trailer: Classified Ops Pass 2.1 with Vintage Elegance, The Bighorn and Antique Weaponry, Pass 2.2 with Football League, Diamondback and Milky Way, and Pass 2.3 with Jogging Enthusiast, Deadlock and Stickers Galore](/images/news/division-resurgence-deadlock-season-2-pass-card.webp "Ubisoft's Season 2 trailer card. Deadlock is the free exotic on Pass 2.3, the third column.")

**2. Ubisoft's official drop table.** The Help Center's drop rates page lists Deadlock in the Exotic Cache right next to the six exotics that are out: a **Deadlock Cache** (Tier S, 0.29%), a **Deadlock Tactical Augmentation Module** (Tier A, 1.43%), a **Weapon Mod Selection: Deadlock**, and Deadlock **recalibration booklets and manuals** (Tier B). Every other exotic in that table is already in the game. The full breakdown is in our [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

## Why it never came out

Each Season 2 phase brought one pass and one free exotic: The Bighorn on August 5, the Diamondback on September 16. Pass 2.3 should have been next. Instead, on **October 21**, Ubisoft [extended Season 2 Phase 2](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/) "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year." On November 24, Ubisoft dated that update: an **Agent Briefing on November 30**, the **Winter Update on December 1** and a dev livestream on December 3.

So Deadlock is the one exotic Ubisoft planned for Season 2 that hasn't arrived. Whether it comes in the Winter Update, in a later pass or in a different form, Ubisoft hasn't said. We'll know more after the November 30 showcase, when Ubisoft says the "full patch notes will be published."

## What we don't know yet

- **Its weapon class.** The trailer shows the gun but doesn't name the class.
- **Its exotic talent and stats.** No Ubisoft source describes them, and the in-game Build-Making Database still lists only the six live exotics (we checked it on October 28).
- **Its release date, and whether it's still free.** The trailer card says "Free Exotic Weapon", but that was the Season 2 plan.

If you see a Deadlock talent or stat sheet before Ubisoft posts one, treat it as a guess.

## How to get ready

1. **Finish the Diamondback pass** if you haven't. It's the free exotic you can still get right now, and Ubisoft hasn't said what happens to the pass on December 1.
2. **Watch the Agent Briefing on November 30** (6 PM CET, 9 AM Pacific). We'll update this post and our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) as soon as Deadlock gets a class, a talent or a date.
