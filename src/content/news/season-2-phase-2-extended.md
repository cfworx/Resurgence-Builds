**Ubisoft has extended Season 2 Phase 2.** On **October 21, 2026**, the official @TheDivMobile account posted: "We have decided to extend Season 2 Phase 2 until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year." The post came with a video and a nudge to watch the game's social channels for updates. No date for the bigger update, and no Season 2 Phase 3 announcement.

Here's what that changes, what it doesn't, and what's still a question mark.

## Why this is a big deal

Since April, Resurgence has run on a tight clock. Ubisoft's roadmap promised "fresh content to the experience every six weeks," with a new season every four months and new phases every six weeks. It kept to that pretty well:

| Phase | Started |
|---|---|
| Season 1 Phase 2 | May 11, 2026 |
| Season 1 Phase 3 "A New Fire" | June 24, 2026 |
| Season 2 Phase 1 "The Call" | August 5, 2026 |
| Season 2 Phase 2 | September 16, 2026 |

Six weeks after September 16 would have landed around **October 28**. Instead, Phase 2 keeps running until the "much bigger update."

## What this means right now

- **The current pass should stick around.** Ubisoft's post doesn't mention the pass, but the Season 2 Phase 2 Classified Ops Pass, with the **Diamondback** on the free track, belongs to the extended phase. Check the pass timer in game; if it moved, that's more time to finish it and less pressure on your weekends.
- **The scheduled events wrap up as planned.** Ubisoft's Phase 2 lineup ended with Liberty & Justice For All (October 16 to 19), and the Exotic SHD Keys daily login event runs until **October 26**. Ubisoft hasn't announced new events for the extension yet.
- **The patch you're on is the patch you're on.** All the Season 2 Phase 2 changes stay in place: the Demolitionist buffs, the Dominating Presence cap, the Exotic Sealed Caches. See the [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).

## What we don't know yet

- **When the "much bigger update" lands.** "Later this year" is all Ubisoft has said.
- **Whether it's Season 3.** The April roadmap said Season 3 "this winter will bring a major story expansion to the game." A bigger update later this year could be that, or something on the way to it. Ubisoft hasn't connected the two, so neither will we.
- **What's in the video.** The October 21 post includes a video with more details. We'll add anything specific once we've confirmed it.

Two breadcrumbs are on the record, and we're labeling them as exactly that:

- On October 19, @TheDivMobile posted "We have a letter for you! Can we count on you to help us save New York?" with a link to the game's website. What the letter is about, we can't say yet.
- Ubisoft's official Exotic Cache drop table lists a seventh exotic, **Deadlock**, that hasn't been announced anywhere else ([details](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/)).

Neither one is confirmation of anything. If they turn into real news, you'll read it here.

## What to do while you wait

1. **Finish the Diamondback pass** without rushing.
2. **Claim the Exotic SHD Keys** before the login event ends on October 26.
3. **Farm mods** in Hostile Nests, which pay out more mods since Season 2 Phase 2 ([mod farming guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/)).
4. **Patch up your build** for Season 2. Every [build on the site](/builds/) now lists what Season 2 changed.
5. **Clear your inventory**, because the expansion you want is on Ubisoft's bug reporter as "Planned," not shipped ([details](/news/inventory-expansion-planned/)).

We'll update this post when Ubisoft shares a date or details.
