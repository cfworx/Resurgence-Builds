The Division Resurgence is **free on Steam as of August 5, 2026**, and Ubisoft brought gifts. Everyone gets **free SHD Keys**, the wishlist goal unlocked a **free apparel set**, and **Twitch Drops are back** with a jacket you can't get anywhere else. Here's what's in the box and how not to lose your progress on the way to PC.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> These were the launch-week rewards. For everything else about playing on PC, see our [Steam and PC guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/), and our [codes page](/guides/division-resurgence-codes/) keeps the full history of free rewards.

## The launch gifts

From Ubisoft's "Now available on Steam" post:

| Reward | Who gets it |
|---|---|
| **5 Regular + 5 Exclusive SHD Keys** | All players, for the Steam launch |
| **FYNC Parade Apparel Set** | All players, because the wishlist goal was reached |
| **10 Exclusive + 10 Regular SHD Keys** | All players, also from the wishlist goal |

Add it up and that's **15 Regular and 15 Exclusive SHD Keys** plus a parade outfit, for doing nothing more strenuous than existing. Since Season 2 Phase 1, SHD Keys also show up in Weekly Rewards. If you're spending keys on sealed caches, check the odds first: every sealed cache in Ubisoft's official drop table uses the same 2% / 10% / 88% tiers, with pity at 50 and 10 ([our breakdown](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/)).

## Twitch Drops are back

Ubisoft relaunched Twitch Drops for the Steam launch:

1. **Link your Twitch account to your Ubisoft Connect account.**
2. **Watch Drops-enabled creators** while the campaign runs.
3. **Claim** the rewards as you hit each watch-time tier.

Two details worth knowing:

- **Duplicates aren't wasted.** If you already got an item in a previous Drops campaign, you can grab it again and dismantle the duplicate for **Cosmetic Shards**.
- **The final tier is the Red Puffer Jacket**, which Ubisoft says isn't obtainable any other way in the game. If you care about fashion in the apocalypse, this is the one.

## Steam extras

Steam Trading Cards and Points Shop rewards for The Division Resurgence launched alongside the game. The Steam page also lists Steam Achievements, and Season 2 Phase 1 added a few new ones, including **Pet the Dog**. Priorities.

## How to bring your progress to Steam

This is the part where people lose characters, so slow down:

1. **On mobile first:** log into a **Ubisoft Connect account** so your progress is linked to it.
2. **Then on Steam:** install the game (Steam will prompt you to install a light version of the Ubisoft Connect launcher), launch it, and sign in with the **same** Ubisoft Connect account.
3. **Guest account?** Link it to Ubisoft Connect from the in-game options ("Link to Ubisoft Connect") **before** you log into an existing Ubisoft Connect account on another device.

Purchases follow the account too: Steam purchases go through the Steam shop, and everything is linked to the Ubisoft Connect account you used, usable on any platform.
