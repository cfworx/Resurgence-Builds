**Season 2 Phase 2 is live** as of **Wednesday, September 16, 2026**. The new pass headliner is the **Diamondback** exotic, free on the free track. Old exotics are back in circulation through new **Exotic Sealed Caches**, **Demolitionist** got a long overdue buff list, the **daily XP cap is gone**, and brand-new agents get an easier first few missions.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> On October 21 Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year." [Here's what that means](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/).

## The new Classified Ops Pass

Per Ubisoft's article, it's 50 tiers with a free and a paid track:

| Reward | Track |
|---|---|
| **Diamondback** exotic weapon | Free and premium |
| **Football League** apparel set | Premium |
| **Milky Way** weapon cosmetic | Premium |

Ubisoft's notes also say the Diamondback got rewards across Dark Zone vendors, reward caches, weekly reward pools and exotic mod sources. Ubisoft hasn't spelled out the weapon class in the patch notes or article, and The Division 2's Diamondback is a different gun in a different game, so we're not borrowing its stats. We'll add verified numbers to the [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) as soon as we have them.

## Exotic Sealed Caches

The other headline: **Exotic Sealed Caches**, "a new way to acquire Exotic Weapons from previous seasons." If you missed Lady Death, the Warlord, the Strawberry Milkshake, the Scorpio or the Bighorn, this is your route back. The official odds are 0.29% per exotic, with a Tier S pity at 50, and the table lists a mystery seventh exotic called Deadlock. Full breakdown: [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).

## Five limited-time events

| Dates (2026) | Event | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 16 to Oct 13 | **Trigger Happy** | "Test your aim in this weapons training event" |
| Sep 18 to 21 | **Challenging: Derelict Store** | "There's nothing left to buy at the Derelict Store, but everything to fight for." Finish inside the time limit |
| Sep 16 to Oct 26 | **Exotic SHD Keys** | Log in every day for a reward |
| Oct 2 to 5 | **Wall Street Woes** | Finish the mission inside the time limit |
| Oct 16 to 19 | **Liberty & Justice For All** | "Compete for justice, speedrun for liberty" |

Rewards across the events include the **Diamondback Tactical Augmentation Module**, Regular and Exclusive SHD Keys, Electronic components and Encrypted Data. The daily login one is the easiest exotic-related reward in the game: open app, collect key, close app, go back to pretending to work.

## The patch highlights

- **No more daily XP cap**, plus reworked daily quests with better rewards by quest type.
- **Easier start:** less enemy health in the tutorial and in the first playthrough of Main Missions 1 to 3.
- **Exotic economy:** exotics no longer ship with exotic mods (mods moved to the pass), the Dark Zone vendor's exotic price dropped from 17,500 to 15,000 DZ Credits, and dismantling an exotic now also gives 420 Encrypted Data.
- **Demolitionist buffs:** Explosive Seeker Mine cooldown 18s to 14s, Strong Arm cooldown 30s to 6s, plus bigger Combat First Aid, Severe Concussion, Healthy Shooter, Charged Shooter and Targeted Blast values.
- **OS Protocols:** Dominating Presence's maximum scaling cut to 35/42/49/56/63/70%; Concerted Strike and Relentless Assault fixed.
- **More mods from Hostile Nests**, and more weapon and gear rewards from replaying Main Missions.

All of it, with tables: [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).
