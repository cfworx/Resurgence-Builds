**The Season 2 Countdown daily login event runs from Monday, July 27 to Monday, August 10, 2026.** Ubisoft's description: "Log in every day to begin Season 2 with a Head Start." The announcement doesn't list the daily rewards, so the plan is simple: open the game once a day for the next two weeks, even if it's just to claim the reward and go to bed. Your login record is only as good as your worst Tuesday.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Ubisoft announced in a July 30 developer diary that the Steam launch and Season 2 would both arrive on Wednesday, August 5 ([our report](/news/steam-release-date-august-5/)). Season 2 Phase 1, "The Call," went live that day with the free Bighorn exotic ([our coverage](/news/season-2-the-call-live-bighorn/)), so this countdown kept running for five days into Season 2.

## The event, at a glance

- **Name:** Season 2 Countdown: Daily Login Event
- **Dates:** July 27 to August 10, 2026
- **Ubisoft's line:** "Log in every day to begin Season 2 with a Head Start"
- **Rewards:** not listed in Ubisoft's announcement. Check the event screen each day.
- **Missed days:** the announcement doesn't say whether you can make them up, so we'd plan on not missing any.

It overlaps the last stretch of Season 1 Phase 3: the Liberty and Justice for All speedrun runs until July 29, Trigger Happy until August 5, and the Lethal Stinger/Lichen and Tactical Diver cosmetic draws until August 4. The full calendar is in our [July event schedule](/news/a-new-enemy-speedrun-july-2026/).

## What Ubisoft has said about Season 2 so far

As of July 27, here's the official record:

- **Season 2 brings the official PC launch.** From Ubisoft's April 28 roadmap: "Season 2 will see the official PC launch for The Division Resurgence." The same article described PC early access on Ubisoft Connect as "technical testing ahead of its official launch in August," and said early access progression will carry over when the game officially launches on PC in Season 2.
- **The cadence:** "A new season launches every four months, with each season introducing new phases every six weeks." The roadmap also promises fresh content every six weeks across iOS, Android and PC.
- **Steam:** the Steam page went live on June 5 with wishlists open. Ubisoft's Steam post said the game is "now available to wishlist on Steam, with plans to launch later this year" and called it "fully cross-platform and cross-play." Our [June write-up](/news/division-resurgence-coming-to-steam-pc-2026/) has the details.
- **After that:** Season 3 is planned for "this winter" with a major story expansion, per the same roadmap ([our roadmap breakdown](/news/season-1-2-3-roadmap/)).

Not announced yet, as far as we've seen: an exact Season 2 date, a Steam release date, or what's on the next Classified Ops Pass. We'll cover each one as soon as Ubisoft posts it.

## Doing the math (ours, not Ubisoft's)

Season 1 Phase 3 started on June 24. Six weeks after June 24 is **Wednesday, August 5**. The game launched on March 31, and four months after that is July 31. The countdown ends August 10. All of that points at the turn of the month, which fits the roadmap's August PC launch. It's arithmetic, not an announcement, so maybe don't book the day off yet. Or do. We're not your manager.

## Your pre-Season 2 checklist

1. **Log in daily** through August 10 for the countdown.
2. **Link your account to Ubisoft Connect** if you play as a guest on mobile. Ubisoft's PC guidance has said since April that progression follows your Ubisoft Connect account, and guest accounts need linking before you log into an existing Ubisoft Connect account on another device. Our [Steam PC guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/) walks through it.
3. **Wishlist on Steam** if you want Steam to ping you when it launches.
4. **Finish what you want from Classified Ops Pass 1.3.** Each phase so far has had its own pass (1.2 in Phase 2, 1.3 in Phase 3), so we'd assume this one wraps up when Season 2 arrives. The Scorpio is on its premium track and the Scorpio Tactical Augmentation Module on the free track.
