**The MM04 speedrun, "A New Enemy," runs from Friday, July 10 to Wednesday, July 15, 2026.** Ubisoft's pitch: "Race the clock and beat the new threat." Short, clear, and exactly what it says on the tin. It's one of six limited-time events in Season 1 Phase 3, and July is where most of them pile up: the Cleaner faction hunt ends July 21, Trigger Happy starts the same day, Liberty and Justice for All runs July 24 to 29, and a Season 2 Countdown login event kicks off July 27. The full calendar, cosmetic draws included, is below.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> A New Enemy came back as a Season 2 Phase 1 speedrun weekend from September 4 to 8, with rewards including the "Lightspeed Agent" title ([our September post](/news/a-new-enemy-speedrun-september-2026/)). We also covered [Liberty and Justice for All](/news/liberty-and-justice-speedrun-july-2026/) and [the Season 2 Countdown](/news/season-2-countdown-login-event/) when they started.

## A New Enemy speedrun: what we know

- **Dates:** July 10 to July 15, 2026. Ubisoft lists dates, not start or end times, so don't plan your attempt for the last evening.
- **Mission label:** Ubisoft's event list tags it **MM04**. The same list puts Wall Street Woes at MM02 and Liberty and Justice for All at MM03. We read MM as "main mission."
- **Format:** a speedrun, so clear the mission fast. When Ubisoft introduced speedrun challenges in Season 1 Phase 2, it described them as main story missions on **Challenging** difficulty. The Phase 3 announcement doesn't restate the difficulty, so check the event screen before you queue.
- **Rewards:** Ubisoft's Phase 3 announcement gives each event a date range and a one-liner, not a reward breakdown. The in-game event screen is the authority here, not us.
- **Leaderboard rule:** the [Phase 3 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/) closed the loophole that let players collect multiple rewards from the Speedrun Challenge Team Leaderboard by ranking with different teams. One squad, one payout, which is fairer for everyone who plays it straight.
- **Where:** our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) lists A New Enemy under Mission Instances, if you need to find the door.

Wall Street Woes, the first Phase 3 speedrun (MM02, June 26 to July 1), is already over. If you missed it, grieve privately.

## Before you hit start

We haven't timed A New Enemy ourselves, so there's no secret route in this post. The boring advice still works:

- **Bring a build you already know.** A timed run is the worst place to learn a new rotation. Pick something from our [builds page](/builds/) that you can pilot half asleep, which, let's be honest, is how most of us play after 10 PM.
- **Do a practice lap.** Learn where the fights are before the clock matters.
- **Pick your squad and stick with it,** since squad-hopping for extra leaderboard rewards no longer works.

One more thing worth noticing: the Season 2 Countdown runs until August 10. Ubisoft's April roadmap ties Season 2 to the official PC launch, which it said is coming in August. We haven't seen a firmer date than that yet, so for now August is the target. We'll post the moment there's a day to circle.
