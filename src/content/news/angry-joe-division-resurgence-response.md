**Angry Joe just called The Division Resurgence mobile game crap.** His crew played about 30 minutes, hit level 10, pulled a pile of blues out of the Dark Zone and told his audience to skip it. Joe has been calling games like he sees them for years, and we respect that. We've also covered this game since April, so we'd like a word. A friendly one. Mostly.

## What Joe Got Right

Fair is fair.

- **Level 10 is a slog.** Blue gear, thin loot, enemies that take a while. That's where every looter shooter starts. The fun shows up when the build comes together.
- **Skins cost real money.** Joe priced a skin at $25 to $30 in Phoenix Credits. Whether that's worth it is between you and your wallet, and we're not going to argue his math. Gamer dad rule: the gun gets new paint after the kids get new shoes.
- **The default pants are snug.** No rebuttal. Some hills aren't worth dying on.
- **It's free.** Joe said it himself: "It doesn't take any of your money if you're smart." We'll take it.

## Bullet Sponges? At Level 10, Sure

Joe's crew spent a good chunk of the video waiting on enemies to fall over. At level 10 in blues, they will take a while. That's not the game being broken. That's the game telling you you're level 10.

What changes the math:

- **Read the colored bar.** Elite enemies show the damage type they're weak to: dark blue for piercing, light blue for shredding, red for blast. Match it and they drop faster ([damage types guide](/guides/damage-types-color-bar-weakness-guide/)).
- **Build for it.** Creator sheets on our site run from **237k DPS** ([Tampishach's Tech Aegis](/builds/tech-aegis-engineering-build-tampishach/)) to **727k** ([LtFoe's Double Agent glass cannon](/builds/aegis-chad-glass-cannon-tech-operator-build-ifozzy/)), and LtFoe's screens show a single **1.78 million** hit on a bounty boss.

And the health bar Joe was looking for? It's the orange bar floating next to your agent.

## Mobile Game? It's on Your PC Too

Resurgence launched on **Steam and Ubisoft Connect on August 5, 2026**, after PC early access opened in April. On PC you get:

- **Mouse and keyboard, or a controller** ([Steam PC guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/))
- **Cross-play and cross-progression** with iOS and Android through your Ubisoft Connect account
- **A crossplay selector** and mobile-only Dark Zone instances since Season 2 Phase 1, for phone players who'd rather not meet a 240Hz monitor

And yes, you can also play it on your phone in the bathroom. Every dad reading this knows that's the only room in the house with a lock. No judgment here.

## Did Resurgence Cost Us The Division?

No. The Division 2's **Echoes of Central Park** DLC lands **November 3**, and Massive Entertainment said in January that **The Division 3 is in production**. Resurgence is one more way to play The Division, on the phone in your pocket or the PC on your desk. More Division, not less.

## Nothing but Blues? That's the Level 10 Loot Table

Loot scales with level. **T2**, the endgame tier, is a level 40 thing: daily quests with an official chance at T2, Elite and Legendary missions, and the level 40 Dark Zone ([gear farming guide](/guides/division-resurgence-gear-farming-guide/)). Also, per his own crew, Joe already had a gold backpack.

Exotics have free routes too: the Dark Zone vendor sells them for **15,000 DZ Credits**, and the **Diamondback** sits on the free track of the current pass. Ubisoft's Help Center says you can't directly buy weapons, gear, abilities or upgrades that give an advantage over players who don't pay ([Phoenix Credits guide](/guides/division-resurgence-phoenix-credits-guide/)).

## What 30 Minutes Didn't Get To

- **Five specializations:** Demolitionist, Tech Operator, Bulwark, Vanguard and Field Medic ([which one to pick](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/)).
- **Builds:** gear sets, weapon talents, OS Protocols and skill mods that stack into the numbers above ([all builds](/builds/)).
- **Seasons:** a new season every four months and a new phase every six weeks, per Ubisoft's roadmap. On October 21 Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 until a much bigger update later this year ([what that means](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)), and the roadmap promises a major story expansion with Season 3 this winter ([roadmap](/news/season-1-2-3-roadmap/)).
