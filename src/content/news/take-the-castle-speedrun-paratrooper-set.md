**The Take the Castle speedrun runs from Friday, August 21 to Tuesday, August 25, 2026.** It's a harder version of the classic story mission with Ken Ito waiting for you, and finishing inside the time limit earns rewards including the **"Lightspeed Agent"** title. Ubisoft's Steam post also announced a **paratrooper-themed apparel set** in the shop for a limited time. And **High Caliber**, the Assault Rifle quest event, keeps running until August 31, which pairs nicely with the free Bighorn exotic assault rifle.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> The next Season 2 Phase 1 events were the [Bighorn headshot weekend](/news/bighorn-headshot-weekend-event/) (August 28 to 30) and the [A New Enemy speedrun](/news/a-new-enemy-speedrun-september-2026/) (September 4 to 8). Season 2 Phase 2's event list, published September 16, didn't include Take the Castle.

## Take the Castle: the details

Ubisoft's Steam post sets it up: "Ken Ito awaits your challenge in the Take the Castle mission. This harder version of the classic story mission will require all your skill..." Ubisoft's Season 2 Phase 1 article adds the tagline "It's time to lay siege" and the goal: complete the mission within the time limit.

- **Dates:** August 21 to 25, 2026.
- **Difficulty:** a harder version of the story mission. The posts don't put a difficulty name on it, so check the event screen.
- **Rewards:** include the "Lightspeed Agent" title. Ubisoft lists the same title for Wall Street Woes (August 7 to 11) and A New Enemy (September 4 to 8). The posts don't say what happens if you already own it.
- **Where:** our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) lists Take The Castle among its mission instances, and the community map data behind it says the story mission unlocks at level 19. We haven't confirmed whether the speedrun version has the same requirement.

## Speedrun prep (no invented routes)

We haven't timed the harder version, so no route guide. What we'd do:

1. **Bring a build you can clear fast with today.** Season 2 Phase 1 reworked weapon mods and removed Blast, Shredding and Piercing bonus rolls from weapons in favor of All Weapon Damage and All Skill Damage. If you haven't looked at your loadout since July, skim our [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) first, then grab something from our [builds page](/builds/).
2. **Do one practice run** at a normal pace so the fights don't surprise you on the clock.
3. **Read the event screen** for the actual time limit.

## The paratrooper apparel set

Ubisoft describes the look as "an elite agent, skilled in paratrooping operations," and says it's in the shop for a limited time. The Steam post's headline, "Paratroopers storm the Castle," ties it to this weekend's speedrun, so the outfit and the event are a matched set. The post doesn't name the set, list a price or give an end date, so check the in-game store if you want it. We'll be over here pretending we don't already know which outfit our agent is wearing on Saturday.

## High Caliber and the Bighorn

- **High Caliber** runs August 5 to 31. Per Ubisoft's event list, agents complete a series of quests using Assault Rifles.
- **The Bighorn is an assault rifle.** Ubisoft calls it "the Bighorn assault rifle," and it's on the free track of the Season 2 Phase 1 Classified Ops Pass (the premium track has it too).
- **Does it count for High Caliber?** In our understanding an exotic AR is still an AR, and we haven't seen anything excluding exotics. Equip it and let the quests double-dip.
- **The gimmick:** full auto from the hip, semi-auto when you aim down sights, and each headshot stacks extra damage. More in our [Season 2 launch coverage](/news/season-2-the-call-live-bighorn/).
