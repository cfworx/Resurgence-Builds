**Four days after its August 5 Steam launch, The Division Resurgence sits at Mixed on Steam, with 46% of user reviews positive when PC Gamer checked on August 9 (US time).** PC Gamer's roundup of the negative reviews centers on a short list of specific issues: login trouble, mouse bugs, reward pacing next to the in-game store, busy menus, and daily limits on progression. The positive case is real too: the core Division formula made the trip, progress follows you between phone and PC, and it costs nothing to try. Here are both sides, plus the official routes for getting problems in front of the team.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> As of October 24 the Steam page shows Mixed, with 218 recent reviews and 1,403 English reviews overall. Ubisoft has since addressed several of the complaints below: Season 2 Phase 2 (September 16) removed the daily XP cap, reworked daily quest rewards, increased rewards from replaying Main Missions and from Hostile Nests, and reduced enemy health for new players. The full list is at the end of this post and in our [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).

## The score in context

Mixed, with 46% positive at the time of PC Gamer's report. The game launched on mobile on March 31, came to PC through Ubisoft Connect early access in April, and arrived on Steam on August 5 alongside Season 2 ([launch details](/news/steam-release-date-august-5/)). PC Gamer's framing is that the game's mobile roots show on PC, which cuts both ways: it explains some of the friction, and it's also why the cross-progression story works.

## What players flagged

Per PC Gamer's roundup of Steam reviews:

- **Login issues** during launch week.
- **Mouse bugs** on PC.
- **Reward pacing:** some reviewers felt rewards were light compared with what the in-game store offers.
- **Menus:** one review found the interface convoluted.
- **Daily limits:** another described loot tied to daily completions, with XP and rewards scaling down after a few runs.

A note on the PC side: the Season 2 Phase 1 patch that launched with Steam already carried PC fixes (mouse cursor state and PC menu behavior, plus a resolution selector) alongside general UI and control changes such as ESC key behavior in menus and a field of view slider ([patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/)). If something is still broken for you, Ubisoft's PC help article points to the official bug reporter, where you can upvote an existing issue, add details, screenshots or video, or report a new one, and to Player Support through the in-game support icon. For login trouble specifically, our [error codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-error-codes/) covers the common messages, and if you made your Ubisoft Connect account through Google or Apple ID and never set a password, the help article sends you to "I have trouble logging in" on ubisoft.com.

## What players like

The positive side is easy to find too. GameJoy Explained's launch-day video, "Only 3 Mins Needed To Decide If You Should Play The Division Resurgence on Steam," lays out the case for trying it:

- **The Division formula survived the trip.** Cover, weak points, skills, gear and a session-based Dark Zone. GameJoy says it "feels surprisingly close to the main games."
- **A new story in a familiar place.** Snowy Manhattan, a new storyline and familiar factions.
- **Cross-progression is the killer feature.** The same Ubisoft account works across PC and mobile, and PC players can join friends who are on their phones. Short sessions on the train, the same character on a monitor at night. GameJoy argues that gives Resurgence "a purpose the Division 2 cannot copy."
- **Free to test.** You can judge the shooting, interface and progression before spending a cent.
- **Proper PC inputs.** Mouse and keyboard or controller, plus cross-play and cross-progression.

## The monetization question, precisely

GameJoy is careful here, so we will be too. In GameJoy's telling, Ubisoft says you cannot directly buy a weapon, gear piece, ability or upgrade that gives an immediate advantage, so the store isn't selling power outright. GameJoy adds that premium currency can buy keys and limited upgrade materials, and that a monthly subscription adds currency, inventory space, a credit bonus and 40% faster crafting (GameJoy's description; we haven't verified the subscription contents ourselves). Because the game has PvP, GameJoy thinks those progression boosts are worth weighing. GameJoy's bottom line: free players can access the game, and paying "reduces parts of the grind."

## The mobile-first design, on a big screen

GameJoy also lists design choices PC players will notice: smaller encounters, menus that still feel adapted from touch controls, health that recovers between waves, medkits on cooldowns, and a second-chance system that can leave you at one health instead of killing you. In GameJoy's words, those choices "suit short mobile sessions." Whether that's a feature or a bug depends on whether you play in 20-minute chunks between bedtime stories. (Guilty.)

GameJoy's verdict: try it if cross-progression matters, if your friends play on mobile, or if you want the new Manhattan story. For a PC-only player choosing a single Division game, GameJoy points to The Division 2 instead.

## Our read

Most launch-week complaints are the fixable kind (login, mouse, menus), and there's an official channel for reporting them. The daily-limit complaint is a design question rather than a bug, and it's the one we'll be watching most closely. If you're arriving from Steam, start with our [Steam PC guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/) and [which specialization to pick](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/), then judge it yourself. It's free. Your only real risk is discovering the Dark Zone at 11 PM on a work night.

## What changed after launch (October 2026 update)

Ubisoft's follow-up changes that line up with the launch-week feedback:

- **Daily limits:** Season 2 Phase 2 "removed the daily XP cap to support longer progression sessions."
- **Rewards:** Season 2 Phase 2 updated daily quests with improved objectives and rewards (Weapon Kill quests now reward weapons of the matching type, Main Mission quests reward random weapons and gear, both with chances of T2 at level 40; Dark Zone quests reward Skill Mods; Hostile Nest quests reward Weapon and Gear Mods; World Event quests reward Upgrade Materials). It also increased weapon and gear rewards from replaying Main Missions and Weapon Mod and Gear Mod rewards from Hostile Nests.
- **New players:** reduced enemy health in the tutorial and during the first playthrough of Main Missions 1, 2 and 3.
- **Menus and controls:** improved cosmetic previews, Battle Pass reward presentation, store information panels and weapon skin displays, plus better navigation and controller support, including the Steam Deck control configuration.
- **PC bugs:** the official bug reporter has marked PC issues such as "My F Key Will Not Revive A Player" as Done.
