**The MM03 speedrun, Liberty and Justice for All, is live from Friday, July 24 to Wednesday, July 29, 2026.** Ubisoft's tagline: "Speedrun for Liberty. Compete for Justice." It overlaps with **Trigger Happy**, a weapon-training event that started Tuesday, July 21 and runs until August 5. If you only have one free evening this week, spend it on the speedrun, because it closes first.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Both events came back in Season 2 Phase 2: Trigger Happy ran again from September 16 to October 13, and Liberty & Justice For All returned as a speedrun from October 16 to 19, which Ubisoft's October 19 Steam post paired with the Lethal Stinger Lichen cosmetic draw. Our [October post](/news/liberty-justice-speedrun-lethal-stinger-lichen/) covers that rerun.

## Liberty and Justice for All: the basics

- **Dates:** July 24 to July 29, 2026. Ubisoft lists dates, no times.
- **Mission label:** MM03 in Ubisoft's Phase 3 event list.
- **It's instanced.** Our [interactive map](/division-resurgence-interactive-map/) lists Liberty and Justice for All among the instanced missions outside the open-world map, launched from its bubble on the edge of the in-game map. If you've been jogging around Manhattan looking for the door, that's why.
- **Rewards:** not itemized in Ubisoft's Phase 3 announcement. The event screen is the authority.
- **Leaderboard:** since the [Phase 3 patch](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/), you can't collect multiple rewards from the Speedrun Challenge Team Leaderboard by ranking with different teams.

## Trigger Happy: weapon training until August 5

Ubisoft describes it as: "Test your aim in this weapon-training event." The in-game event tab has the task list, so that's the place to look rather than our guesses. Open it, read the tasks, and knock out whatever fits how you already play. It runs **July 21 to August 5**, so it's the longer window of the two, and the one you can safely do on a random Tuesday.

## Speedrun tips that don't require us to make things up

We haven't timed this mission, so there's no route guide and no magic loadout here. The generic stuff that holds for any timed run:

1. **Bring a build you can clear fast with.** Not the one you theorycrafted at lunch. The one you've actually run. Our [builds page](/builds/) has full setups if you need a starting point.
2. **Know your damage types.** If enemy armor bars and damage colors are still a mystery, read our [damage types guide](/guides/damage-types-color-bar-weakness-guide/) before you blame the timer.
3. **Do one slow lap first.** Learn where the fights are, then go for time.
4. **Pick one squad.** Squad-hopping for extra leaderboard rewards stopped working in Phase 3, so find three people who show up on time and keep them. If you have three friends who show up on time, you're already winning at life.
5. **Read the event screen** for the actual time limit and difficulty. Ubisoft described its Season 1 Phase 2 speedruns as main story missions on Challenging difficulty, but the Phase 3 announcement doesn't restate it.
