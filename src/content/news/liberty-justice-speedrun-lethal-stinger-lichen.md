**Liberty & Justice For All**, the last speedrun on Ubisoft's Season 2 Phase 2 schedule, runs **October 16 to 19**, which makes today the final day on that schedule. Today's Steam post also announced a **Lethal Stinger Lichen** cosmetic draw. On Friday the official X account promoted the **Milky Way** weapon cosmetic with a web store link, and today it posted a teaser that opens with "We have a letter for you!" Here's what we can confirm, and where we can't.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Two days later, on October 21, Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year" ([our coverage](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)). Ubisoft's text didn't connect the extension to the letter teaser, and we haven't confirmed what either video shows, so we're not connecting them either.

## Liberty & Justice For All: October 16 to 19

Ubisoft's September 16 Phase 2 article lists the event for October 16 to 19: "Compete for justice, speedrun for liberty. Complete the mission within the time limit to earn rewards." The official X account followed up on October 17 ("Complete the Liberty & Justice for All mission this weekend for great rewards"), and today's Steam post invited everyone to "prove your skill against the clock." Consider this your final-day reminder: if your squad has been saying "we'll do it Sunday," it is now Monday.

Rewards: as with the other Phase 2 events, Ubisoft lists rewards for the whole phase together (the Diamondback Tactical Augmentation Module, Regular and Exclusive SHD Keys, Electronic components and Encrypted Data) rather than per event.

Been here before? Liberty and Justice for All (listed as MM03 in Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 3 events) was also a speedrun in July, which we [covered here](/news/liberty-and-justice-speedrun-july-2026/). Earlier this month it was [Wall Street Woes](/news/revive-bug-no-respawn-zones-fixed/), October 2 to 5.

## Lethal Stinger Lichen cosmetic draw

From Ubisoft's Steam post: "Get your venomous look with the Lethal Stinger Lichen cosmetic draw. This deadly design leaves a lasting mark, combining striking detail with a look tailored for close encounters."

The names will ring a bell. In Season 1 Phase 3, Ubisoft introduced **Lethal Stinger** as an exotic weapon skin for the **Scorpio** shotgun and ran a "Lethal Stinger Exotic Weapon Skin and Lichen Weapon Skin Set" cosmetic draw from June 24 to August 4. Today's post doesn't list the October draw's contents, so we can't tell you whether it's the same set returning or something new. "Venomous" and "close encounters" certainly sound like the Scorpio, which fires venomous rounds, but that's us reading tea leaves, not Ubisoft confirming anything. More on the Scorpio in our [exotics database](/database/exotic-weapons/).

Before you pull: since Season 2 Phase 1, Cosmetic Draw screens show detailed pricing directly in game. Read it, then decide with your head instead of your trigger finger.

## Milky Way weapon cosmetic: what we can confirm

On Friday, October 16, @TheDivMobile posted that "The Milky Way Weapon Cosmetic is now available," pointing players to a Coda Shop web store (thedivisionresurgencestore.codashop.com) "for the best deals."

Worth knowing: Ubisoft's September 16 article lists Milky Way as a **Premium Track** reward in the Season 2 Phase 2 Classified Ops Pass. The October 16 post doesn't spell out what the offer is, and we couldn't confirm it. So before you spend anything:

- If you already own the Phase 2 premium pass, check your pass track first, since Milky Way is listed there.
- If you shop through the web store, check exactly what the offer contains before you buy.

## "We have a letter for you!"

Today's other official post, in full: "We have a letter for you! Can we count on you to help us save New York? Gear up at thedivisionresurgence.com." A video is attached. The text doesn't say what the letter is, and we're not going to build a theory out of a teaser. If Ubisoft shares more details, we'll add them here.

## What's left on the Phase 2 calendar

After Liberty & Justice, Ubisoft's Phase 2 schedule lists one more event: the **Exotic SHD Keys** daily login event, running until **October 26**. By name, those keys sound like they go with the [Exotic Sealed Caches](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) that Phase 2 added, though Ubisoft's article doesn't spell out the link. Either way, logging in daily until the 26th costs you a minute a day.
