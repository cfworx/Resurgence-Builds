**The Division Resurgence launches on Steam on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, and Season 2 starts the same day.** MassivelyOP reported Ubisoft's confirmation today, citing Ubisoft's press release and social posts, and Ubisoft's news hub lists two new videos dated July 31: a Steam Launch Trailer and "Season 2 Phase 1 is now live." The Steam version is free and supports cross-play and cross-progression with mobile. If you play on your phone, the one job before Wednesday: make sure your progress lives on a Ubisoft Connect account.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> It launched on schedule. Ubisoft's "Now available on Steam" post went up on August 5, the Steam store lists an August 5, 2026 release date, and Season 2 Phase 1, "The Call," started the same day ([our coverage](/news/season-2-the-call-live-bighorn/)). The launch post repeated the progress advice below (log into Ubisoft Connect on mobile first, then use the same account on Steam), and every player got launch gifts, detailed in [Steam launch rewards](/news/steam-launch-rewards-twitch-drops/). How Steam players received it is in [our review roundup](/news/steam-reviews-mixed-launch/).

## What's confirmed

- **Date:** Wednesday, August 5, 2026.
- **Season 2 the same day.** Per MassivelyOP, the Steam launch "will also coincide with the release of Season 2."
- **Price:** free to play, same as mobile and Ubisoft Connect.
- **Cross-play and cross-progression with mobile.** Ubisoft's June 5 Steam post called the game "fully cross-platform and cross-play," and MassivelyOP's report includes cross-progression.
- **The end of early access.** MassivelyOP reads this as the wrap-up of PC early access on Ubisoft Connect, which fits Ubisoft's April roadmap: early access was "technical testing ahead of its official launch in August." PC players have been in since [April 28](/news/pc-early-access-launch/).
- **The same game.** MassivelyOP's rundown lists open world missions, PvPvE Dark Zones and Conflict, with cross-platform play.

## How to keep your progress

Your progress belongs to your Ubisoft Connect account, not to your phone and not to a storefront. Ubisoft's PC help article lays out the rules:

1. **On mobile, link your account to Ubisoft Connect first.** If you're on a **guest account**, use "Link to Ubisoft Connect" in the options **before** logging into an existing Ubisoft Connect account on any other device. Ubisoft is explicit about that order, and guest-account progress has been worth protecting: Patch 1.1.2.2 and Season 1 Phase 3 both shipped fixes for guest progress issues.
2. **On August 5, sign into that same Ubisoft Connect account on Steam.**
3. **Can't remember the password?** If you created your Ubisoft Connect account through Google or Apple ID, the help article points you to "I have trouble logging in" on ubisoft.com.

Playing with friends: the same article says you can play together on any device "as long as you are in the same regional server." Steam doesn't change which regional server you're on.

(Ubisoft has since updated that Help Center article for Steam. It now says Steam installs prompt you to add a light version of the Ubisoft Connect launcher, and that Steam purchases go through the Steam shop while staying linked to the Ubisoft Connect account you used.)

## PC specs (from the Steam store page)

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| OS | 64-bit Windows 11 | Windows 11 |
| Processor | Intel i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 3400G | Intel i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 3 3100 |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | 8 GB RAM |
| Graphics | Intel UHD 630 or AMD Vega 11 (integrated) | Nvidia GTX 1650 (4GB), AMD RX 5500 (4GB) or Intel ARC A380 (6GB) |
| DirectX | Version 11 | Version 11 |
| Storage | 30 GB | 30 GB |

The minimum line also lists a broadband internet connection.

Two things jump out. First, the minimum graphics are integrated GPUs, which is good news for anyone whose "gaming PC" is the family desktop that also does homework. Second, the AMD chip on the recommended line is a Ryzen 3 3100, while the minimum line lists a Ryzen 5 3400G. Our guess is the 3400G is there for its built-in Vega 11 graphics, which is also the minimum GPU, but that's our reading of the sheet, not something Ubisoft has explained.

**Storage:** Ubisoft's PC help article says the PC version is around 17GB and recommends at least 30GB available, in line with the Steam page's 30 GB. Inputs are mouse and keyboard or a controller. Our [Steam PC guide](/guides/division-resurgence-steam-pc-guide/) has the full setup walkthrough.
