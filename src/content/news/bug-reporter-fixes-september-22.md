On **Tuesday, September 22**, Ubisoft's official Division Resurgence bug reporter moved four issues to **Done**: the 30fps frame rate lock (TDR-135), the Bighorn forcing first person (TDR-141), the blank Tier 2 upgrade window on exotic weapons (TDR-104) and Armory/Proficiency progress not counting (TDR-108). An older proficiency bug, TDR-100, was already marked Done on August 27. One thing to know before you celebrate: "Done" is the status on Ubisoft's tracker, and we found no separate patch notes for these fixes in the Help Center or on Steam, so the tracker is the official record here. If one of them still happens to you, the tracker is also where to report it. How to do that is below.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> TDR-99, "The 'High Caliber' Event Is Not Appearing," went to Done on September 29 (the event itself ran August 5 to 31). In early October Ubisoft patched the [revive bug in no-respawn zones](/news/revive-bug-no-respawn-zones-fixed/) and asked players to search for and add to existing reports before filing new ones, and the tracker now lists [inventory expansion as Planned](/news/inventory-expansion-planned/). The rest of October's fixes are in our [October hotfixes post](/patch-notes/october-2026-hotfixes/).

The dates are the last-updated dates the tracker shows for each issue, and the titles are copied as written.

### The 30fps lock (TDR-135)

The title says the frame rate got stuck at 30fps after a "recent BP patch fix," BP presumably being the battle pass. The tracker doesn't say which patch the report refers to, only that the issue is now Done. Frame rate caps have come up before: the Season 1 Phase 3 patch notes in June fixed the PC frame rate being "limited to 30fps in windowed mode." If your game still feels sluggish, check your graphics settings first, then add your platform and device to TDR-135.

### The Bighorn forcing first person (TDR-141)

The Bighorn, the free exotic assault rifle from the [Season 2 Phase 1](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) pass, is designed to switch from full auto to semi-auto when you aim down sights, with each headshot stacking extra damage. Ubisoft's description doesn't mention first person, and TDR-141 tracked the camera switch as a bug. It's now Done, so if the camera still flips on you, add your details to the issue. Our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) has more on the gun.

### The blank exotic T2 upgrade window (TDR-104)

If you opened the Tier 2 upgrade window on an exotic weapon and found it blank, that's TDR-104, now marked Done. The tracker entry doesn't add details beyond the title, so if the window is still empty for you, a screenshot on the issue is the most useful thing you can post.

### Proficiency (TDR-100 and TDR-108)

Weapon proficiency has had several rounds of fixes this year. Patch 1.2.1 (May 20) listed "weapon proficiency blocked in some cases" among the known issues under investigation. The Season 2 Phase 1 notes fixed incorrect Proficiency level displays after Proficiency Quests, and the [Season 2 Phase 2 notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/) (September 15) fixed Armory progression and reward issues, "including rewards that could not be claimed and progression levels remaining locked despite meeting requirements," plus LMG Proficiency Quests progressing from kills with any weapon. On the tracker, TDR-100 went Done on August 27 and TDR-108 on September 22. Ubisoft doesn't say which fix closed which report, but both are now marked Done.

## What the bug reporter is and how to use it

The bug reporter is Ubisoft's public issue tracker for The Division Resurgence, on ubisoft.com. Each issue has a TDR number, a title and a status that Ubisoft updates. So far we've seen Done, In Progress, Planned and Deprioritized. Ubisoft's Help Center article on playing on PC (Steam or Ubisoft Connect) points players with PC issues to it, and the list covers plenty beyond PC settings: proficiency, exotic upgrades, the Bighorn.

What the Help Center tells you to do:

1. **Check the list first.** If your bug is already there, **upvote** it.
2. **Add details** to the existing issue. Ubisoft specifically mentions screenshots and videos.
3. **Report a new issue** if yours isn't listed.
4. If the bug reporter isn't the right place, **contact Player Support** through the in-game support icon.

Our advice: say which platform you're on, what you were doing and what happened instead. "Game broken" is a mood, not a reproduction step.

## Were there patch notes?

Not that we could find. Ubisoft's Help Center patch notes section has nothing newer than the Season 2 Phase 2 notes posted on September 15, and the game's Steam news feed has no separate hotfix post. Smaller fixes have sometimes been announced on the official subreddit instead (that's where October's hotfix notes appeared), so if notes for this batch turn up, we'll add them. Until then, the tracker statuses are the official record. They landed six days after Season 2 Phase 2 went live, and if any of these bugs are still in your game, the issue page is where Ubisoft asks to hear about it.
