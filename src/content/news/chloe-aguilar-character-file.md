Ubisoft's official X account posted a new **character file** on **October 27, 2026**: "Meet Chloé Aguilar, a cybersecurity expert with a mysterious past. Once working for the NSA, she now puts her skills to use helping the Division keep communications running." The attached dossier, styled as a classified SHD file with redacted lines, fills in the rest.

## What the file says

| Field | What it says |
|---|---|
| Name and role | Chloë Aguilar, NYC Comms Expert |
| Faction | NYC SHD (the rest of the line is redacted) |
| Main location | Spring Blossom Settlement |
| Demeanour | Cheerful, talkative, plus one redacted trait |
| Personality | Smart, stubborn, plus one redacted trait |
| Age | 26 |

The background section is the good part. Chloe is "a gifted mathematician and cybersecurity specialist" who earned her doctorate (the details are redacted) at "an exceptionally young age," specializing in encryption and quantum computing, and that expertise got her a job with the NSA. Then the redactions start. What's left: after she discovered she was being watched, she disappeared off the grid and took a new identity in New York City. When the Dollar Flu struck she "eventually contacted the" (redacted, naturally), where **Faye Lau** recognized her technical abilities and recruited her into the **Base of Operations communications team**.

## Where she fits

Spring Blossom Settlement isn't new: it's already on our [interactive map](/map/), with main and side missions of its own. Ubisoft's post doesn't say whether Chloé is a new face or an existing NPC getting a spotlight, so we won't guess.

The bigger question is whether this is the start of something. On October 21, Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year" ([our coverage](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)), and the April roadmap promised a Season 3 story expansion this winter that would let you "learn more about the people and factions you have been introduced to" ([roadmap](/news/season-1-2-3-roadmap/)). A dossier on a support character fits that description. It's also exactly the kind of post a social team publishes in a quiet week. Until Ubisoft says more, treat it as lore, not a release date.

## Everything else this week

- **No new patch.** Ubisoft's Steam news feed still ends with the October 19 speedrun post, and the Help Center's patch notes section has nothing newer than Season 2 Phase 2.
- **The bug reporter is quiet on Ubisoft's side.** No issue has changed status since the [October 23 batch](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-october-23/). Players have kept filing reports, including one about stash items that can't be claimed because of a false "inventory full" error (TDR-688) and one asking why the daily login rewards disappeared (TDR-687). That one probably has a simple answer: the Exotic SHD Keys login event was scheduled to end on October 26, the day before the report.
