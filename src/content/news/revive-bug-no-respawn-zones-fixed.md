Good news for anyone who spent last week face down in a boss room: Ubisoft has **patched the revive bug**. In a post on the official subreddit on **Friday, October 2**, the dev team announced a new game patch with this fix: "Fixed an issue preventing players from being revived while in a No Respawn Zone." The matching report on Ubisoft's bug reporter, **TDR-556**, "Cannot Revive Team Mates On No Respawn Zones (After The September 24 Patch)," was marked **Done** the same day, and so was **TDR-175**, "Can't Respawn In Dark Zone After Being Killed By Another Player Since The New Update Two Days Ago." Also starting today: the **Wall Street Woes** speedrun, running **October 2 to 5**.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> After the fix, players reported on October 3 that heart (revive) icons were staying stuck on screen, and some said it cost them frame rate. Ubisoft deployed a client update on October 6 that "should no longer display heart icons indefinitely on screen," and a related issue, TDR-493 "My F Key Will Not Revive A Player," went to Done on October 15. Some comments on TDR-556 still report revive trouble after the fix, so if that's you, add your details there: Ubisoft asks players to search the bug reporter and contribute to existing reports before filing new ones. The rest of October's fixes are in our [October hotfixes post](/patch-notes/october-2026-hotfixes/).

## The revive fix (TDR-556)

The bug report's title explains most of it: after what players call the September 24 patch, agents couldn't revive teammates in no-respawn zones. Player comments on the issue page point at **boss rooms in Legendary and Elite missions**, naming Precious Cargo on Elite and the Chinatown final boss as places where downed agents couldn't be picked up.

In our understanding, that's about the worst place for this kind of bug: in an area where you can't respawn, a teammate's revive is your way back into the fight. Without it, the downed agent spends the rest of the boss fight as a spectator with opinions.

What Ubisoft did: the October 2 patch fixed it, and TDR-556 is marked Done.

## The Dark Zone respawn fix (TDR-175)

TDR-175 covers agents who couldn't respawn in the Dark Zone after being killed by another player, "since the new update two days ago," per its title. The tracker information we have doesn't show when the report was filed, so we can't say which update that refers to. Ubisoft marked it Done on October 2 as well. The October 2 subreddit post lists only the revive fix, so the Done status is the official word on this one.

For context: since Season 1 Phase 3, Dark Zone spawns and respawns grant a 10-second immunity buff that firing, using skills or throwing grenades cancels. Details are in our [Season 1 Phase 3 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/).

## What was the "September 24 patch"?

It's the players' name for it, straight from the TDR-556 title, and we couldn't find official patch notes under that name. Ubisoft's Help Center patch notes section has nothing newer than the Season 2 Phase 2 notes from September 15, and the game's Steam news feed has no hotfix post for it. The bug reporter did move a separate batch of issues to Done on September 22 ([our coverage](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-september-22/)). Whatever that update was called, the revive problem players blamed on it now has an official fix.

## If it still happens to you

- **Add to the existing issue** rather than filing a duplicate: TDR-556 for revives, TDR-175 for Dark Zone respawns. Mission name, difficulty, platform and what happened all help.
- **Upvote** it while you're there. Ubisoft's Help Center tells players to upvote issues and add details, screenshots or videos.
- **Report a new issue** if yours is different, or contact Player Support through the in-game support icon.

## Also in the October 2 post

The same post said Ubisoft is investigating a possible change to the **Heavy Weight** Skill Mod Combo Set for a future patch. Juggernaut players, our [October hotfixes post](/patch-notes/october-2026-hotfixes/) tracks where that lands.

## Wall Street Woes speedrun: October 2 to 5

Wall Street Woes is back as a Season 2 Phase 2 limited-time event, running **October 2 to 5**, with Ubisoft's brief: "Finish the mission within the time limit to collect rewards." That makes at least three speedrun outings for this mission in 2026: June 26 to July 1 in Season 1 Phase 3, August 7 to 11 in Season 2 Phase 1 (where the rewards included the "Lightspeed Agent" title), and now this one.

Rewards: Ubisoft's Phase 2 article lists the event rewards for the whole phase together (the Diamondback Tactical Augmentation Module, Regular and Exclusive SHD Keys, Electronic components and Encrypted Data) rather than per event, so we can't tell you exactly what this run pays.
