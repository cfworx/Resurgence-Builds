  - question: "What OS Protocol should I use for Tactical Pharma?"
    answer: "Rampart (High-End/Gold) is the best option. While immobile, meaning literally just standing still and not touching your joystick, you get +5% Damage per second up to 6 stacks for +30% total. On a build where your entire job is to stand behind cover and let acid do the talking, 'don't move' is the easiest instruction you'll ever follow. RogueMadras is currently running Brains Over Brawn (Specialized/Blue) but recommends Rampart. Chain Reaction (Superior/Purple) and Tactical Layering (High-End/Gold) are also viable if you can't get Rampart because RNG hates you personally."
  - question: "Why does this build only need 40% Skill Multi-Shot Chance?"
    answer: "Because through Tactical Augmentation you can get 2 additional talents that increase your Skill Multi-Shot damage. At 40% you're already getting multi-shot procs on nearly half your skill ticks, and with the augmentation bonuses those procs hit like a second mortgage. Stacking beyond 40% is like putting a spoiler on a minivan. Technically possible. Practically pointless."

## How to Play

1. **Trap first, at full Health.** Throw the Chemical Trap at a choke point: at full Health it fires Prime Engineer.
2. **Swap to the Vector.** Backup Battery adds +22.5% Engineering for 8 s, and Backup Magazine reloads the rifle.
3. **Hive out, Swarm when it's ready.** The Hive costs 25% of your current Health, so heal back up before the next Trap or Prime Engineer won't fire.
4. **Swap to the rifle.** Every hit stacks Skill Multi-Shot, and damaging 3 enemies fires Multitasking.
5. **Stand still, far back.** Rampart stacks while you don't move, and Glass Cannon costs 10% Damage Reduction, so stay well behind the fight.

## Before You Copy It

- **Recorded before Season 2.** Season 2 Phase 1 reworked mods: new weapon mods roll twice the Engineering and can add Skill Intensity or Skill Crit Damage, and gear mods can now roll Skill Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Multi-Shot and Intensity. The sets, talents, skill mods and OS Protocols here weren't changed. [GGReborn's build](/builds/tactical-pharma-season-2-build-ggreborn/) is the Season 2 version.
- **His Vector is a Vector 45 ACP raised to T2.** The T2 First Wave Vector can't roll Backup Battery.
- **The rifle isn't his Mk16.** Only the Custom 416 G3 (T2) and Enhanced ACR-E (T1) assault rifles roll Multi-Pronged Attack and Multitasking together.
