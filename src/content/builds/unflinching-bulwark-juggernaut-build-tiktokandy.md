Most tanks survive damage. **This one eats it: taking damage has a 70% chance to heal you for 420% of your Toughness, as often as every 2 seconds, so TikTokAndy stacks Toughness until Legendary enemies are basically topping him up.**

Build by **TikTokAndy**, posted November 14, 2026 on Season 2 Phase 2. It's the Bulwark's Juggernaut focus, built, in his words, "around the talents of the gear and the gun." He plays it through a four-player Legendary run of On The Radio, then goes back in alone, lets the enemies shoot him, and opens every card along the way.

Benign Effect and Tough Cookie add up to 20% Toughness each in a fight, and on his screen it shows: mid-mission he read up to 3.41k Toughness and 540,081 Health, against 2.73k and 469,379 at rest. Get all of your Juggernaut stats unlocked, he says, before you call the build done.

## How to Play

1. **Throw the Shockwave Spike and stay near it.** Camp Fire's Armor only counts within 10 m of it. "We've got 15k armor just off throwing the spike," he says, and his screen read 15,138 against 13,405 without. The card says picking the spike back up takes a portion off its 14 s cooldown, and he says it's back in 5 s, so use it "as often as possible."
2. **Keep the MK46 firing.** Steady Suppression's Damage Reduction only runs while you shoot. He hip-fires and says a belt lasts 30 to 45 seconds with Misdirection Insurance. The math is shorter: 130 rounds at 550 RPM is about 14 seconds of trigger time, or about 24 if every shot misses and Misdirection Insurance II hands back 42% of them. In his own clip, 87 rounds went in about 23 seconds of on-and-off fire.
3. **Pull the room.** Deploy the Phalanx Shield and Provocateur makes enemies within 20 m focus you. To pull a single enemy, like a rusher, shoot its pack: yellow on your shots means you're hitting it.
4. **Pop the Breach Combo when a teammate gets low.** It triggers Provocateur too. You get full frontal protection for its 22.28 s, Anchored heals you and the shield on every hit, Waste Not hands your team Extra Health, and the +30% Signature Ability Charge Efficiency on each gun brings it back sooner.
5. **Swap to the Copperhead for Extra Health.** Backup Protection adds Extra Health worth 20% of your Max Health for 8 s and is ready again 8 s later, and the Copperhead's hits stack Thick Skin II's Armor, about +60% at 20 stacks.
6. **Stay out of the way.** "Your job is to stay out of the way when you're not needed." He sees too many Bulwarks running in front of their teammates' bullets, the Vanguard's especially.

His 79.5% is a count he adds up on screen, card by card, for hits from the front. It starts at 40% (the tree's Tough Customer gives +40% Weapon Damage Reduction from frontal hits), adds the two 5% TAM stages on his mask and knee pads, then the Damage Reduction roll on each of the six gear pieces, rounded (about 20% together), and the MK46's 9.92%, counted as 10. For his back he counts on firing (Steady Suppression's 18%) or on Provocateur's stacks (up to 16%) plus the +3.70% Damage Reduction on his Provocateur Firmware. Read Tough Customer before you trust your back to it, though: he counts it as 20% Damage Reduction from the rear, where its card says it "inflicts -20% Weapon Damage Reduction from rear hits." Either way the game stops at 80%: "Damage Reduction above 80% will not be applied," says its tooltip.

## What He Says

- **"It's an unstoppable tank."** He threw it together from his inventory the day he recorded it.
- **"Bulwark and healer go really well together."** With a healer on the team you don't need a revive or healing weapon, so you can spec even harder into tankiness.
- **Team for four-player Legendary:** next to the Bulwark, two Vanguards, or a Vanguard with a Tactical Pharma or a really good Demolitionist. "You definitely should have a Vanguard." His own run had a healer, a Vanguard on a Double Barrel and a Tactical Pharma.
- **Alone on Legendary:** "I'm going to just go and let them shoot me until I die." He fires the MK46 for its Damage Reduction without trying to kill anything, his health stays near full, and he leaves the mission himself.
- **PvP:** he thinks the 2 second Unflinching cooldown could hold up there too, with Last Moment on the shield. He only tested PvE.

**Another Unflinching tank:** Nadie's [Involuntary Nap Time](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/) also pairs Unflinching with Rock Solid, then spends the rest on stuns. More from TikTokAndy on [his creator page](/creators/tiktokandy/).
