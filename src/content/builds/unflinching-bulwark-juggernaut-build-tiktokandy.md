Most tanks survive damage. **This one eats it: every hit has a 70% chance to heal you for 420% of your Toughness, so TikTokAndy stacks Toughness until Legendary enemies are basically topping him up.**

Build by **TikTokAndy**, posted November 14, 2026 on Season 2 Phase 2. It's the Bulwark's Juggernaut focus, built, in his words, "around the talents of the gear and the gun."

## How to Play

1. **Plant the Shockwave Spike and stand in it.** Camp Fire's Armor only counts within 10 m. Pick the spike back up and its cooldown drops from 14 s to 5 s on his build.
2. **Keep the MK46 firing.** Steady Suppression's Damage Reduction only runs while you shoot. He hip-fires: 130 rounds with the extended mag at 550 RPM, stretched by Misdirection Insurance, is 30 to 45 seconds of shooting.
3. **Pull the room.** Deploy the Phalanx Shield and Provocateur makes everything within 20 m look at you. To turn a single NPC, shoot its backpack: yellow hit markers mean you're on the pack.
4. **Pop the Breach Combo when a teammate gets low.** You get full frontal protection while it runs, Anchored heals you and the shield on every hit, and the +30% Signature Ability Charge Efficiency he reads on his MK46 brings it back fast.
5. **Swap to the Copperhead when the burst comes.** Backup Protection adds Extra Health worth 20% of your Max Health for 8 s.
6. **Stay out of the way.** "Your job is to stay out of the way when you're not needed." He sees too many Bulwarks running in front of their Vanguard's bullets.

## What He Says

- **"It's an unstoppable tank."** He threw it together from his inventory the day he recorded it.
- **"Bulwark and healer go really well together."** With a healer on the team you don't need a revive or healing weapon, so you can spec even harder into tankiness.
- **Team for four-player Legendary:** a Bulwark with two Vanguards, or a Vanguard and a Tactical Pharma. "You definitely should have a Vanguard."
- **PvP:** he thinks the 2 second Unflinching cooldown could hold up there too, with Last Moment on the shield. He only tested PvE.

**Another Unflinching tank:** Nadie's [Involuntary Nap Time](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/) also pairs Unflinching with Rock Solid, then spends the rest on stuns. More from TikTokAndy on [his creator page](/creators/tiktokandy/).
