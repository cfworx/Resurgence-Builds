Tank, DPS and healing in one build: that's the pitch on Demø2x's thumbnail. **The loadout behind it puts a damage set on all three Aegis Operations support skills and stacks Engineering and Skill Crit behind them. In his Legendary run it dealt the second most damage on the team and nearly all of its Defense Buff.**

Build by **Demø2x**. His video has no commentary: it's a four-player Legendary run of Take The Castle, then every card of the loadout, with a red circle drawn around each of the three skill mod sets. So this page is what his screen shows, and it makes no claims about why he picked each piece.

## What the Cards Ask For

He never says how he plays it, so this is only what the cards require.

1. **Keep a Mender Drone healing someone.** Combat Enhancer's bonus damage comes from the drone's healing and stacks three times, 20 seconds each. The drone "follows and continuously heals the unit selected by the Agent," its card says, and heals you when no ally is selected.
2. **Fire a First-Aid Firefly before you shoot.** Teamfight's bonus runs for 8 seconds from the moment you deploy one, and the ally it targets gets it too. With a 10.77 s cooldown on his card, it's up most of the time. "Select an ally in the team member list and release a Healing Firefly," says the card; with nobody selected, it heals you.
3. **Park the Defender Drone on the enemies.** Hazardous Area hurts whatever stands inside its 11.62 m. The drone "can either follow the Agent or go to a specific location," and pressing the skill button again destroys it, so press once.
4. **Feed the USC's talents in bursts.** Ten hits within 5 seconds for Torrential Downpour and five within 3 for Rapid Charge: at 650 RPM, that's about a second of fire. The Signature Ability also charges over time, from damage dealt and from healing with skills, per the Defender Drone's card.
5. **Use the Scorpio with a skill out.** In Sync pays in both directions, +12.60% Skill Damage for weapon hits and +12.60% Weapon Damage for skill hits, and doubles when both are running. Reload it behind cover for Calm and Collected.
