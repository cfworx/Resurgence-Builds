faqs:
  - question: "What is the best Combat Medicine Field Medic build in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "Nadie's Muscular Nurse is a top-10 Asia server Combat Medicine build running 4pc Healing Elites, 2pc Self-Propelled Shield, and Pummeling Shield OS Protocol. It produces 334k Health, 15.7k Armor, and 20% base Damage Reduction before any talent procs fire. The build tanks Elite Mission bosses while keeping the entire squad alive with healing stations."
  - question: "Does Combat Medicine scale off Toughness or Engineering?"
    answer: "Toughness, from our understanding. This is the single biggest mistake on Combat Medicine builds. The tooltips imply Engineering affects heal output, but that doesn't seem to hold for the Combat Medicine subclass. Toughness is the stat to stack. Nadie stacks 2,410 Toughness for 334k Health, and that Health pool directly drives the healing output."
  - question: "Is Pummeling Shield good on a Field Medic?"
    answer: "Yes, with an asterisk. The Extra Health proc triggers when you get hit at max health, which a healing station keeps you at constantly. The stun proc triggers every 10 hits while you have Extra Health, but since the June 26, 2026 hotfix it has a 10-second cooldown and only lasts 1.5 seconds (down from 2). So a Field Medic with an M60 still stays topped up and still stuns things, just one nap every 10 seconds instead of a stun-lock. The OS Protocol is not Bulwark-exclusive."
  - question: "Can I solo Elite Missions with this build?"
    answer: "Yes, but slowly. Nadie's DPS is 66k, not a damage build. Elite Mission bosses take a long time at that DPS (call it 15-20 minutes, give or take your patience). The trade-off is that you will not die. If patience is not a problem, this build clears everything in the game."
  - question: "What weapons does The Muscular Nurse use?"
    answer: "Primary: Enhanced AUG A3P (the Tier 2 AUG, an SMG) with Steady Suppression and Precise Support II for team-oriented play. Secondary: Military M60 E4 (the Tier 2 M60, an LMG) with Sadistic Shield II and Conceited II for solo boss tanking. Both are standard weapons, no exotics required, and both are the only AUG and M60 whose talent pools hold these talents."
  - question: "How does this build compare to Involuntary Nap Time?"
    answer: "Same Pummeling Shield OS Protocol (and the same June 26 stun cooldown nerf), completely different role. Involuntary Nap Time is a Bulwark Juggernaut stun-tank for Elite Missions and the Dark Zone. The Muscular Nurse is a Combat Medicine PvE sustainer that heals the team while tanking bosses. Pick based on whether you want to put things to sleep or keep your squad alive through Elite content."

A Field Medic who tanks the boss too. **Every heal stacks Damage Reduction, full Health keeps Pummeling Shield's Extra Health coming, and the Support Station picks up anyone who drops.**

Build by **Nadie** (Asia server, Rank 7 Combat Medicine), sent to our Discord in June 2026 on Patch 1.1.2.2 with screenshots and leaderboard proof. They also made [Involuntary Nap Time](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/).

## How to Play

1. **Support Station where the team will stand, and stand in it.** Staying at full Health is what feeds Pummeling Shield.
2. **Hold the trigger on the AUG.** +18% Damage Reduction while firing, an Armor stack every 6 hits, and a stun every 10 hits, once per 10 s.
3. **Take the hits.** Every heal stacks Hypervigilant and refreshes Rock Solid, and every hit at full Health is more Extra Health.
4. **Boss, or the team avoids your ring? Swap to the M60.** Sadistic Shield adds Extra Health every 5 hits, Conceited taunts and adds Damage Reduction on kills.
5. **Healing Swarm on whoever's in trouble.** Emergency Measures catches anyone who drops to 0 mid-heal.

## Before You Copy It

- **The stun is on a leash.** Since the [June 26, 2026 hotfix](/news/server-hotfix-june-26-2026/), Pummeling Shield stuns for 1.5 s instead of 2, once every 10 s. The Extra Health part wasn't mentioned in that change.
- **Damage Reduction caps at 80%** (same hotfix). Their 20% plus Hypervigilant II, Rock Solid II and Steady Suppression is already about 79% if they add up, so more may be wasted. If they multiply, there's more headroom.
- **Expect a bit less than 66k DPS.** Season 1 Phase 3 fixed Combat Medic dealing more damage than intended at regular intervals, after their submission.
