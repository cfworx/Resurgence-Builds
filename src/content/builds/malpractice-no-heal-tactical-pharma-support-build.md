faqs:
  - question: "What is the Malpractice build in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "Our own Field Medic Tactical Pharma support build, and it never heals. A PKM with Precise Crit gives every ally within 40 meters +15% Weapon and Skill Critical Damage, Target Acquisition makes the enemy you focus take more damage, and an Oxidizing Swarm with Augmented Range inflicts -14.4% Damage Reduction on every enemy it hits from 20 meters or more. Close and Personal then turns each buffed ally within 30 meters into +9% Engineering for you. Gear is 4pc Mechanical Expert plus 2pc Jackpot."
  - question: "Does Precise Crit count for Close and Personal?"
    answer: "By the cards' wording, yes. Close and Personal grants +9% Engineering for each ally within 30 meters \"who possesses a buff that was granted by the Agent\", and Precise Crit grants all allies within 40 meters +15% Weapon and Skill Critical Damage for 10 seconds. Three buffed allies inside 30 meters is +27% Engineering. Hit an enemy 10 times at least every 10 seconds to keep the buff up."
  - question: "Does the Malpractice build work solo?"
    answer: "Partly. Close and Personal needs allies, and Precise Crit's card only names allies, so solo you lose the team half of the build; Target Acquisition and the Swarm's debuff still work. Playing alone, swap the OS Protocol to Brains Over Brawn (hits deal additional damage equal to 24% Engineering), and if you need to stay alive, put Enhanced Formula on the Swarm (enemies it hits deal -12% damage for 20 seconds)."
  - question: "Where should you stand with the Malpractice build?"
    answer: "Just behind your front line, in cover. Augmented Range only debuffs enemies the Swarm hits from at least 20 meters away from you, Close and Personal counts allies within 30 meters, Precise Crit reaches 40, and Brainstorm's second +15% Skill Duration needs an ally within 20 meters. Staying close to your squad and 20 meters or more from the enemy covers all four, and cover is where Accelerated Charge counts your kills and assists."
  - question: "Which body armor and backpack roll Accelerated Charge and Brainstorm?"
    answer: "Accelerated Charge rolls on the Ops Armor (T2, the Mechanical Expert piece this build uses), X-TREYME Pro Ribcage (T2), \"Big Drum\" Body Armor (T2), Athena Missile Vest (T1), Phalanx Precision Vest (T1) and Ranger Ballistics Vest (T0). Brainstorm rolls on the Mountain Hiking Pack (T2, a Jackpot piece), Rally Point Secure Pack (T1), Big Horn Pack (T1), \"Horo\" CarryBack (T1), Demeter Quick-Stash (T0) and Element Outdoors Rucksack (T0)."

## How to Play

1. **Hang back just behind your front line,** in cover: close to your squad, 20 m or more from the enemy.
2. **Trap the pack, Hive out.**
3. **PKM on the pack.** 10 hits on an enemy buffs every ally within 40 m.
4. **Boss or elite? Stay on it** for the Target Acquisition stacks.
5. **Swarm almost ready? Swap to the SA-58.** Backup Battery adds +22.5% Engineering, and 5 quick hits on an enemy add 50 charge (Rapid Charge). Then throw the Swarm at enemies 20 m or more from you.
6. **Back to the PKM** before Precise Crit runs out (10 s).
