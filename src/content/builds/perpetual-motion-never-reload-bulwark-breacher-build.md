faqs:
  - question: "What is the Perpetual Motion build in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "Our own Bulwark Breacher design built around one action: holding the trigger. Steady Handed refills the Military MK46's 100-round magazine every 30 hits, Overwhelming Power stacks up to +36% Weapon Damage and adds +50% Damage Reduction once you have been shooting for 2 seconds, and Steady Suppression adds +18% Damage Reduction while firing. Field Repairs lets your hits restore Shield Health, and below 50% Health Duality and Reciprocal Charge trade damage for defense. Gear is 4pc Phalanx Attack plus 2pc Fearless Warrior."
  - question: "Does Steady Handed really mean you never reload?"
    answer: "With the MK46, close to it. Its in-game card lists a 100-round magazine (118 on paper with Phalanx Attack's +18% Magazine Size), and Steady Handed refills it every 30 hits, so you only run dry if fewer than 30 of those rounds hit. If you do, swap to the Enhanced AUG A3P: its Backup Magazine reloads the MK46 after 4 seconds while the AUG keeps you shooting, and it has Steady Handed too."
  - question: "How much Damage Reduction does Perpetual Motion have while firing?"
    answer: "On the cards: +50% from Overwhelming Power after 2 seconds of shooting, +18% from Steady Suppression while firing and +6% from Fearless Warrior's 2-piece, 74% if they add up, before the Damage Reduction roll on the body armor. Below 50% Health, Duality adds another +15%. The game caps Damage Reduction at 80% (the June 26, 2026 hotfix), so that is as far as it goes."
  - question: "Can you raise the Phalanx Shield with an LMG?"
    answer: "We haven't confirmed it. The guns creators fire from behind the Shield are assault rifles and SMGs: Saiynergy's Breacher runs a Lightweight M4 and a First Wave Vector 45 ACP. That is why the second gun here is an SMG: swap to the Enhanced AUG A3P whenever you want the Shield up. The MK46's loop (Overwhelming Power, Steady Suppression, Steady Handed and the Spike) doesn't need the Shield."
  - question: "Which body armor and backpack roll Duality and Inspiration?"
    answer: "Duality rolls on the Chatzatz Chest (T2), Athena Missile Vest (T1), Flimsy Body Armor v2 (T1) and \"Ericht\" Chest System (T0). Only the Flimsy Body Armor v2 can also be Phalanx Attack, which is why this build's body armor is T1. Inspiration rolls on the All Hazards Nitro Backpack (T2), Roamer Pack \"Sow\" (T2), Big Horn Pack (T1), Element Outdoors Rucksack (T0) and RUSH72 Backpack (T0); the Roamer Pack \"Sow\" is the T2 one that can be Fearless Warrior."

## How to Play

1. **Spike down.** It sits on the ground like any deployed skill, so Inspiration's +30% Rate of Fire should switch on.
2. **Hold the trigger on the MK46.** Two seconds in, Overwhelming Power adds +50% Damage Reduction.
3. **Keep landing hits.** Every 30 refill the magazine.
4. **Want the Shield up? Swap to the AUG** and raise it. Backup Magazine reloads the MK46 for you after 4 s.
5. **Enemy inside 20 m? Breach Combo** for Berserker's +60% Weapon Crit Damage.
6. **Under 50% Health? Keep enemies in the Spike's range.** Duality and Reciprocal Charge switch to defense until you're back up.
