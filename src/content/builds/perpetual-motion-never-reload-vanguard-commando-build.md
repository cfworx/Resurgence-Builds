faqs:
  - question: "What is the Perpetual Motion build in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "Our own Vanguard Commando design built around one action: holding the trigger. Steady Handed refills the Military MK46's 100-round magazine every 30 hits, Overwhelming Power stacks up to +36% Weapon Damage and adds +50% Damage Reduction once you have been shooting for 2 seconds, and Steady Suppression adds +18% Damage Reduction while firing. All three skills work from where you stand: Smart Cover (Optimized Armor) adds Damage Reduction and puts bullets back, Scanning Pulse (Optimal Awareness) adds Weapon Crit Chance for every enemy it scans within 70 m, and Tactical Link (Bugle Call) adds Rate of Fire, Damage Reduction and Movement Speed. Gear is 4pc Phalanx Attack plus 2pc Fearless Warrior."
  - question: "Why is Perpetual Motion a Vanguard build and not a Bulwark?"
    answer: "We first posted it as a Bulwark Breacher, and the Bulwark's skills fought the plan. Field Repairs only works with the Phalanx Shield raised, and that version had you swap off the MK46 to raise it. Reciprocal Charge needs Shockwave Spike hits on enemies, and Berserker needs a Breach Combo with an enemy within 20 m. The gun side of the build works from anywhere, so we kept it and moved it to Vanguard Commando, whose Smart Cover, Scanning Pulse and Tactical Link all feed the gun from the back line."
  - question: "Does Steady Handed really mean you never reload?"
    answer: "With the MK46, close to it. Its in-game card lists a 100-round magazine (118 on paper with Phalanx Attack's +18% Magazine Size), and Steady Handed refills it every 30 hits, so you only run dry if fewer than 30 of those rounds hit. Behind your Smart Cover, Optimized Armor also gives every hit a 4.8% chance to put a bullet back. If you do run dry, swap to the Modified USC: its Backup Magazine reloads the MK46 after 4 seconds."
  - question: "How much Damage Reduction does Perpetual Motion have while firing?"
    answer: "On the cards, behind Smart Cover: +50% from Overwhelming Power after 2 seconds of shooting, +18% from Steady Suppression, +6% from Fearless Warrior's 2-piece, +3.2% from Optimized Armor and +2 to 4% from Bugle Call's 2-piece. That is 79.2 to 81.2% if they add up, and the game caps Damage Reduction at 80% (the June 26, 2026 hotfix). Out of cover it is 76 to 78%. Tactical Link adds +14.4% for 15 seconds on top, and Duality +15% below 50% Health, which is why the body armor rolls Crit Damage instead of Damage Reduction."
  - question: "Which body armor and backpack roll Duality and Inspiration?"
    answer: "Duality rolls on the Chatzatz Chest (T2), Athena Missile Vest (T1), Flimsy Body Armor v2 (T1) and \"Ericht\" Chest System (T0). Only the Flimsy Body Armor v2 can also be Phalanx Attack, which is why this build's body armor is T1. Inspiration rolls on the All Hazards Nitro Backpack (T2), Roamer Pack \"Sow\" (T2), Big Horn Pack (T1), Element Outdoors Rucksack (T0) and RUSH72 Backpack (T0); the Roamer Pack \"Sow\" is the T2 one that can be Fearless Warrior."

## How to Play

1. **Smart Cover down, get behind it.** Inspiration's +30% Rate of Fire switches on, and Optimized Armor adds +3.2% Damage Reduction while you're behind it.
2. **Scanning Pulse.** Every enemy it scans within 70 m adds +1.4% Weapon Crit Chance, up to 8.
3. **Tactical Link.** +14.4% Rate of Fire, Damage Reduction and Movement Speed for 15 s each time you deploy it.
4. **Hold the trigger on the MK46.** Two seconds in, Overwhelming Power adds +50% Damage Reduction.
5. **Keep landing hits.** Every 30 refill the magazine, and behind Smart Cover each hit has a 4.8% chance to put a bullet back.
6. **Something rushing you? Swap to the USC.** Performer adds +18% Weapon Damage within 20 m, and Backup Magazine reloads the MK46 after 4 s.
