## What This Build Does

> Build credit: **Saiynergy** of **ATLAS Division**. Full Breacher walkthrough covering every gear roll, weapon talent, skill firmware, and loot route. Watch his original walkthrough on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28lsRrnJ6Vg) and jump in the [ATLAS Division Discord](https://discord.gg/e7EUUBbBYa) if you want in on the clan.

> [!INFO] Weapon talents: back to Saiynergy's own
> This page used to recommend **Killer + Breadbasket** on the Lightweight M4 instead of Saiynergy's Steady Hands + Medical Accuracy. The game's Build-Making Database (talent Source lists recorded October 29, 2026) shows the Lightweight M4 can't roll either one, and Breadbasket doesn't roll on any assault rifle at all, so the build is back to his original pair. Everything on this page is now Saiynergy's loadout.

This is a **Breacher shield build** that anchors a doorway and refuses to leave. Saiynergy is sitting at **23.17k Power Score, 334.07k DPS, and 7,679 Armor**. The 4pc Fury Strike gives you the crit chance and crit damage backbone. The 2pc Phalanx Attack stacks magazine size so the Vector keeps firing past the point where most builds reload and die. The Phalanx Shield with **Provocateur** draws enemy aggro so your team can work. The Breach Combo with **Positive Feedback** restores bullets on hits. The Shockwave Spike Mk.3 with **King of the Pit** keeps your crit chance topped off. **Death From Afar** OS Protocol turns headshot damage into raw Firepower. The core loop is straightforward: shield up, walk forward, let the Vector pour rounds into whatever the M4 can't reach. Your job is to be the wall. Your fireteam's job is to be the people who do not have to be the wall.

> [!WARNING] Season 2 changes that affect this build
> - **Shockwave Spike (Season 2 Phase 1):** Stun cut from 3.5s to 2s and cooldown raised from 12s to 14s. Fewer Spikes per fight means less King of the Pit crit chance uptime. Season 2 Phase 2 also closed some cover-transition edge cases where Spike could be used when it shouldn't.
> - **Strained (Season 2 Phase 2):** Only the tooltip changed. It used to read 7.3% on Strained and 10.2% on Strained II (Ubisoft's notes call these tier 1 and tier 2, which we read as the talent's rank, not the weapon's tier); the real values are 10% and 14%. Your Vector didn't get stronger, the description just stopped lowballing it.
> - **Weapon mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** New mods get +100% main stat and -25% bonus attributes, and they can no longer roll Shredding or All Weapon Damage. They can roll Weapon Damage %, Headshot Damage % and Weapon Crit Hit Damage % instead, which happen to be this build's priority stats. Old mods keep their rolls.
> - **Gear mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** Gear mods can now roll Weapon Crit Hit Chance, Weapon Crit Hit Damage, Headshot Damage and Weapon Damage. More room for the trio this build stacks.
>
> This build has not been re-tested since Patch 1.1.2.2. Full details: [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) and [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).

**Core roll philosophy:** Firepower on every slot except the Body Armor, which rolls Toughness, the stat Unflinching heals from. The Body Armor is the survivability anchor so the Breacher can hold an angle other classes have to retreat from. Every other slot dumps Firepower into the M4 and Vector.

**Priority sub-attributes:** Weapon Crit Hit Damage, Headshot Damage, Weapon Crit Hit Chance. Saiynergy: *"What's really most important is you always want to look for something that has all weapon damage."*

## Why Each Gear Piece Matters

### Fury Strike 4-Piece

- **2pc:** Optimal Range +10.0%. Mid-range is exactly where the Vector lives.
- **3pc:** Weapon Crit Hit Chance +10.8%. Consistent crits on every burst.
- **4pc:** Weapon Crit Hit Damage +24.0%. This is where the damage ceiling lives.

### Phalanx Attack 2-Piece

- **2pc:** Magazine Size +18.0%. (Rate of Fire +18.0% is the 3pc bonus per our set database, so a 2pc split doesn't get it.) Combined with Strained on the Vector, the SMG fires through a full squad without a reload window for them to peek you.

### Body Armor: Unflinching (Toughness Roll)

*Taking damage has a 70% chance of restoring Health equal to 300% Toughness. Talent Cooldown: 2 seconds.*

The Body Armor is the only Toughness-rolled piece in the build, and Unflinching turns that Toughness into healing: taking damage has a 70% chance of restoring Health equal to 300% of it, at most once every two seconds. That is how Saiynergy holds angles other classes fall back from.

### Backpack: Hidden Impact 2 ("Rally Point Secure Pack")

*Grants +18% Weapon Crit Hit Chance when behind cover for at least 3 seconds.* That's the rank I text from our database. Saiynergy's backpack carries rank II, which usually reads higher, so check your tooltip.

Stacks directly on top of Fury Strike's WCHC. Rewards the playstyle a shield Breacher already wants to run: anchored behind cover, shield up, picking targets.

### Gloves: "Lochy" Hand Savers (T0)

Still in the build at T0. Rolls matter more than tier. WCHC +4.67% and WCHD +8.89% are exactly what the build wants, so the T0 stays until a higher tier rolls the same trio better.

### Knee Pads: Phalanx HX 02 (T1)

WCHD +6.75% and Headshot Damage +10.72%. Headshot Damage feeds Death From Afar, so this piece is doing two jobs at once.

### Proficiency Levels Matter

Saiynergy: *"If you max this out, it actually increases not only your power score, but your weapon's overall damage."* Do not skip the proficiency grind on your good rolls.

## Best OS Protocol for Bulwark Breacher

Saiynergy runs **Death From Afar** as his primary OS Protocol.

### 1. Death From Afar (Superior) · RECOMMENDED

*"Grants Firepower equal to 12% Headshot Damage, capped at 54% Firepower."* The upgraded copy in this build reads 21.60%.

Every piece of gear in the build pushes Headshot Damage, and Death From Afar converts that into raw Firepower up to a 54% cap.

**Base stats (level 1, checked in game on October 28, 2026):** Weapon Damage +12.00%, Firepower +77, All Weapon Damage +62, Health +2,043.

The copy in this build is further along: its main stat reads +24% Weapon Damage at level 50 (leveling doubles it from +12%), and its upgraded talent reads 21.60%. The flat attributes grow with level too, so a leveled copy shows more than the base values above. Our [stats guide](/guides/division-resurgence-stats-explained/) explains how protocols scale.

### 2. Tight Grouping (Superior) · Strong Alternative

*"Weapon Critical Hits behind cover have a 5% chance of granting +10% Weapon Critical Hit Damage for 5 seconds, up to 3 stacks."* Main stat: +18% Firepower.

Best stopgap if Death From Afar hasn't dropped. A flat +18% Firepower main stat, and the behind-cover crit damage proc pairs with Hidden Impact 2.

### 3. Trench Fighter (Superior) · Cover-Anchored Pick

*"Grants +30% Weapon Critical Hit Damage when behind cover."*

Flat +30% WCHD stacks with everything the Breacher is doing. Lower ceiling than Death From Afar because it doesn't scale off Headshot Damage, but consistent.

### 4. Close Quarters Menace (Superior) · Close-Range Pivot

*"Grants +15% Skill Intensity and Weapon Damage when there are enemies within 20 meters."*

For Breachers who close the distance instead of holding it. Shield play forces you into 20-meter range anyway.

## Weapon Talents and Attachments

### Primary: Lightweight M4 (T2, Assault Rifle, Level 45)

- **[Steady Hands](/database/weapon-talents/steady-hands/):** Hip firing grants +20% Stability and +15% Weapon Damage. Behind a Phalanx Shield you fire from the hip most of the time, so this one is on nearly all fight long.
- **[Medical Accuracy](/database/weapon-talents/medical-accuracy/):** Headshots grant +18% Healing Intensity for 10 seconds (rank I values from our talent database).

Saiynergy calls Killer the talent to chase on assault rifles: *"You definitely just need Killer. Killer is most needed on assault rifles."* It just doesn't roll on the Lightweight M4. The game's Source list puts it on five ARs: the [Black Market AK-74](/database/weapons/black-market-ak-74/) and FAMAS (T2), the [Enhanced ACR-E](/database/weapons/enhanced-acr-e/) and Type 81 (T1) and the Military 416 (T0). If you'd rather trade Medical Accuracy for damage on the M4 itself, [Unhinged](/database/weapon-talents/unhinged/) (+18% Weapon Damage, -10% Stability and Accuracy) is in its pool; see the [Lightweight M4 page](/database/weapons/lightweight-m4/) for all 18 talents it can roll.

**Attachments:** Long Range Scope, Suppressor, Extended Magazine, Vertical Grip.

### Secondary: First Wave Vector 45 ACP (T2, SMG, Level 44)

- **Strained:** Firing for 0.5 seconds grants +10% Critical Hit Damage for 5 seconds, stacking up to 3 times, per the talent text in our database. That 10% is the rank I value; Season 2 Phase 2's notes put the next rank (Strained II) at 14%. Talent rank isn't the weapon's T2 tier, since creators find rank II talents on tier 0 and tier 1 guns too, so check whether your Vector's line reads Strained or Strained II. Hold the trigger and the stacks hold with you, which is why the Phalanx mag size matters.
- **Adaptation:** Body shots grant +4.80% Skill Duration for 8 seconds and headshots grant +6% Skill Intensity for 8 seconds (rank I values from our talent database). That's skill-side value for the Shield and Spike, not a survival talent.

**Attachments:** Red Dot, Extended Magazine, Suppressor.

### Attachments & Farming Subs

All weapon attachments should roll for **all weapon damage** or **weapon shredding damage**. Saiynergy: *"You always want to look for something that has all weapon damage."* That advice predates Season 2 Phase 1: new weapon mods can't roll All Weapon Damage or Shredding anymore, so look for Weapon Damage %, Headshot Damage % or Weapon Crit Hit Damage % on fresh mods instead.
