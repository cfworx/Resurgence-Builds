## How to Play

1. **Phalanx Shield up, hold the doorway.** Provocateur pulls enemies within 20 m onto you and adds Damage Reduction for each one, up to 5.
2. **Shockwave Spike the pack.** Each hit adds King of the Pit's +4% Weapon Crit Chance, up to 4 stacks. The stun is 2 s now.
3. **Hip fire the M4 from behind the shield.** Steady Hands is on whenever you hip fire, and headshots add Medical Accuracy's healing.
4. **Vector at mid range, trigger down.** Strained stacks every half second you keep firing, and the bigger Phalanx Attack magazine keeps the stacks going.
5. **Breach Combo, then keep shooting.** For 6 s each hit has a 40% chance to put a bullet back.

## Before You Copy It

- **The body armor has to be Fury Strike.** Unflinching doesn't roll on any Phalanx Attack body armor, so your second Phalanx Attack piece is the mask or the holster.
- **Shockwave Spike is weaker.** Season 2 Phase 1 cut its stun from 3.5 s to 2 s and raised its cooldown from 12 s to 14 s, so King of the Pit stacks less often than in his video.
- **Mods changed.** He says *"you always want to look for something that has all weapon damage,"* but since Season 2 Phase 1 new weapon mods can't roll All Weapon Damage or Shredding. Look for Weapon Damage %, Headshot Damage % or Weapon Crit Damage %. Gear mods can now roll Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Headshot Damage and Weapon Damage too.
