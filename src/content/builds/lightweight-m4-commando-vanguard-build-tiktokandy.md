TikTokAndy benched his Double Barrel for a Lightweight M4 and six-starred floor 20 of the Lone Wolf Challenge on his first try. **Pulse and Tactical Link push crit chance toward 90%, aimed crits set off Explosive Round, and Precise Support stacks Armor while you shoot.**

Build by **TikTokAndy**, posted October 2026 on Season 2 Phase 2. It spins off his [Double Barrel Commando](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/): same sets, same combo sets.

## How to Play

1. **Pulse, then Tactical Link.** His crit chance goes from 51% to about 70% with Scanning Pulse, about 90% with both.
2. **Aim.** Explosive Round only fires on aimed crits, once every 5 s. "You actually have to have some amount of skill to use this build."
3. **Fight from Smart Cover when you can.** 40% Damage Reduction on his screen, and bullet refunds on hits.
4. **Keep hitting things.** Every 6 hits is a Precise Support stack, up to 5.
5. **Reload on purpose.** Each reload is a Suppressive Fire stack, up to 3.
6. **Double Barrel for the close stuff.** Rushers, and bosses in your face.

## Before You Copy It

- **His on-screen build card is off.** ChatGPT made it, he says, and he corrects some of its numbers out loud (we use what he says). He and the card also swap the gear talents: in the game's database Suppressive Fire is a body armor talent and Emergency Measures a backpack one, as in his September 30 and October 6 videos.
- **Looking it up in game?** The in-game database lists Precise Support as Precision Support; his tooltip says Precise Support II.

## What He Says

- **"This gun's kind of broken."** Range, accuracy ("a laser beam") and a reload so fast he turned down a Reload Speed buff.
- **Precise Support is "a really underrated talent."** "We weren't even taking damage there."
- **"I just wouldn't imagine a life without Emergency Measures."**
- **Six stars on floor 20, first try.** His Lone Wolf buff picks: +15% Firepower, +10% Movement Speed (the M4 runs slower), +7% Multi-Shot, and Weapon Damage over Reload Speed.
