## What This Build Does

> Build credit: [**TikTokAndy**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RYtZyN_0P0Y), "Vanguard - Commando With A Lightweight M4," published October 27, 2026, during Season 2 Phase 2. Every number below is his own reading from the video unless we say it came from our database or Ubisoft's patch notes. We haven't re-tested this build ourselves.

TikTokAndy went back to his original Vanguard Commando build to see whether it still holds up, and swapped the star of the show. Instead of the [Double Barrel](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/), the primary is a **Tier 2 Lightweight M4** he found and couldn't stop praising: "this gun's kind of broken." Then he took it into the Lone Wolf Challenge, which had refreshed that day, and cleared **floor 20 with six stars on the first try**.

He calls the build a spin-off of his Double Barrel setup, and it shows: same gear sets, same three skill combo sets, same Firepower plan. What changes is the weapons (the M4 takes the primary slot and the Double Barrel moves to secondary, with Precise Crit in place of Still Alive) and the OS Protocol.

**The build in one breath:**

- **Primary:** Lightweight M4 (T2) with **Unhinged** + **Precise Support II**.
- **Secondary:** Double Barrel with **Misdirection Insurance** + **Precise Crit**.
- **Gear:** Phalanx Attack 4pc + Gunny Johnny 2pc, with **Suppressive Fire II** and **Emergency Measures** as the two gear talents.
- **OS Protocol:** **Explosive Round** (Firepower).
- **Skills:** Smart Cover with **Optimized Armor**, Scanning Pulse with **Field Supplies**, Tactical Link with **Ammo Refill**. Commando tree fully maxed.
- **The goal:** crit stacking, roughly 30% bonus Rate of Fire, and enough Armor to stand in the open when Smart Cover is on cooldown.

> [!INFO] Two things to know about the video
> TikTokAndy shows his build as a one-page diagram and says ChatGPT made it for him, then corrects some of its numbers out loud. We use his spoken numbers, not the diagram. He also calls Suppressive Fire his backpack talent and Emergency Measures his chest talent in this video. His earlier videos put them the other way around: on September 30 Suppressive Fire II is on his chest, and on October 6 Emergency Measures is on his backpack. The game's own Build-Making Database settles it: Suppressive Fire is a Body Armor Talent and Emergency Measures a Backpack Talent (checked October 28, 2026), so that's how we list them.

## Weapons and Talents

| Slot | Weapon | Talents | Rolls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Lightweight M4** (Assault Rifle, T2) | **Unhinged** + **Precise Support II** | Critical Hit Damage and Headshot Damage. He'd rather have Weapon Damage than Headshot Damage. Attachments: Critical Hit Chance, Firepower, Critical Hit Damage. |
| Secondary | **Double Barrel** (Shotgun) | **Misdirection Insurance** + **Precise Crit** | Max Health and Multi-Shot Chance |

His Lightweight M4 shows **850 RPM** and a **53-round magazine**. Those readings most likely include the Rate of Fire and Magazine Size bonuses from the gear above, so a bare M4 probably won't match them. Why he likes it: range ("it's almost like using an SMG at range"), accuracy ("a laser beam") and a reload so fast he turned down a Reload Speed buff during his run. The trade-off: you run a bit slower carrying it.

| Talent | Our database (base version) | What TikTokAndy says |
|---|---|---|
| **Unhinged** | +18% Weapon Damage, -10% Stability and -10% Accuracy | A good trade on the M4 "because it's a laser beam to begin with." In September he added that raising weapon proficiency to level 5 or 6 wins the accuracy back. |
| **Precise Support** | Hitting an enemy 6 times grants +7% Armor and +40% Rescue Efficiency for 10 seconds, up to 5 stacks | His Precise Support II tooltip reads +9.80% Armor per stack (+49% at five; his video rounds it to +50%), with the Rescue Efficiency still at 40%. He calls it "a really underrated talent." |
| **Misdirection Insurance** | A missed shot has a 30% chance of returning the bullet to the magazine | On the shotgun, as in his Double Barrel build |
| **Precise Crit** | Hitting an enemy 10 times grants +15% Weapon and Skill Critical Damage to all allies within 40 meters for 10 seconds | On the shotgun |

### Why Precise Support II is the sleeper pick

TikTokAndy's readings suggest Armor percentages scale whatever Armor you already have. He sits around 10,000 Armor thanks to his chest-piece modules and Armor mods, and with five Precise Support stacks his sheet climbed to about 13,700 and then 14,000 during the run. "We weren't even taking damage there," he said after one floor. The +40% Rescue Efficiency is the team bonus (he counts it once rather than per stack): faster revives for whoever went down trying to melee a Rikers heavy.

His takeaway, and ours: the more Armor you build in, the better this talent gets, so "two or three fully maxed armor mods" on your gear aren't a mistake on this build. The tooltip he shows at the start of the video reads +9.80% Armor, the same as the Precise Support II on Nadie's [tank medic build](/builds/muscular-nurse-combat-medicine-build-nadie/), so his "10%" is that number rounded. (We listed this talent as "Precision Support" until October 2026; the game calls it Precise Support.)

## OS Protocol: Explosive Round

| OS Protocol | Rarity | Talent (our database) | TikTokAndy's reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explosive Round** | High-End | Critical Hits performed during aiming create an explosion with a radius of 5 meters around the target that deals damage equal to 2000% Firepower to them. 5-second cooldown. | His only copy, level 45: +42.75% Firepower (45% at max, he says, which fits the doubling at level 50), +389 Firepower and +311 All Weapon Damage, plus its Health bonus |

He calls it "the Firepower version of Collateral Damage," and his copy isn't at level 50 yet because he spent the previous week taking a **Collateral Damage** there instead. His expectation: the Firepower alone should beat **Augmented Lethality** in a lot of situations, and the explosion is a free grenade on every aimed crit, better the more enemies stand in a clump. With a 51 to 90% crit chance, the 5-second cooldown is the only thing slowing it down.

Honorable mentions from the same video:

- **Augmented Lethality:** his long-time pick on the shotgun build ([details](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/)).
- **Dominating Presence:** "could be pretty broken" on Vanguard, he says. Its main stat is Weapon Damage, and its talent adds Weapon Multi-Shot Chance for each enemy within 20 meters: +5% at base in our database, +10% on his copy. Ubisoft changed its scaling and cut its maximum to 35/42/49/56/63/70% in [Season 2 Phase 2](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/), so read your own tooltip.

Full text for all of them is in our [OS Protocol database](/database/os-protocols/).

## Skills, Combo Sets and Chips

Same three [Vanguard](/class/vanguard/) Commando combo sets as the Double Barrel build. Combo text is from our [skill mod combo database](/database/skill-mod-combos/); the chip picks are his.

| Skill | Combo set | What it does (3pc) | Chips he runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tactical Link (signature) | **Ammo Refill** | Shots in the first 6 seconds of Tactical Link have a chance to recover 4 ammo, and kills reset the duration. 2pc: Reload Speed. | Tactical Link Duration on every chip, with Weapon Critical Hit Chance, Multi-Shot Chance and Critical Hit Damage |
| Smart Cover | **Optimized Armor** | Behind Smart Cover: +3.20% Damage Reduction, and hits have a 4.80% chance of restoring 1 bullet. 2pc: Reload Speed. | Intensity, with the same weapon stats |
| Scanning Pulse | **Field Supplies** | Kills or assists on enemies you scanned restore 6% of Health lost and refill 6.40% of the magazine. 2pc: Reload Speed (he reads 4%). | Intensity or Radius |

Two numbers from his skill screens:

- **Smart Cover** gives 40% Damage Reduction while you're behind it, and he reads a **15%** base chance to restore a bullet on hits from behind it. He says Optimized Armor adds about 5% and then calls the total 25%. We make that 20%, so check your own screen.
- **Crit chance stacks with your skills:** 51% on the sheet, 70% with Scanning Pulse active and 90% with Pulse plus Tactical Link. That's the whole reason Explosive Round's "Critical Hits performed during aiming" condition isn't a problem.

## The Lone Wolf Run: Floors 14 to 20

The Lone Wolf Challenge had refreshed the day he recorded, and he played it from floor 14 to floor 20 with the M4 as his primary. (His narration also calls it "Lone Star"; he says "Lone Wolf Challenge" too, and that's the mode.) Each floor offers a pick of temporary buffs, and his picks show what the build wants:

| Floor | Buff he took | Why |
|---|---|---|
| About 14 | +15% Firepower for this floor and the next | Took his DPS readout from about 1 million to 1.33 million |
| About 14 | Already active from an earlier pick: +5% Critical Hit Chance and +10% Critical Hit Damage | More crits, more explosions |
| 17 | +10% Movement Speed | The M4's one real drawback is that you run slower carrying it |
| 18 | +7% Multi-Shot Chance | "I love that actually" |
| 19 | Weapon Damage instead of +15% Reload Speed | "Its reload speed is already so good" |
| 20 | Restores 10% of total Health for every enemy you kill | A buff he'd never seen before, picked for the boss fight |

His floor notes:

- **Kill the enemy with the plus sign early** on floor 19, because he says it creates rushers.
- **Save Tactical Link for the heavies** and the named boss on the same floor.
- **Use the M4's range.** He says it beats the Double Barrel there, and "accuracy is pretty nice."
- **The floor 20 boss sometimes parks.** It spawned somewhere it barely moved, which he calls the perfect spawn for a fast time. His floor 20 tips from an earlier run are in our [endgame tips guide](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/).

Result: **six stars on floor 20, first try.** His rewards included skill mod chips (at least one gold), Phoenix Credits in 90-credit payouts, a Tactical Augmentation Manual, gear recalibration manuals, credits and more T2 gear. Our [Phoenix Credits guide](/guides/division-resurgence-phoenix-credits-guide/) has the other free sources.

## How to Play It

1. **Aim.** Explosive Round only fires on Critical Hits while aiming, and the M4 rewards headshots. This is not the Double Barrel; you can't spray and pray. "You actually have to have some amount of skill to use this build," he warns.
2. **Pulse, then link.** Scanning Pulse lifts crit chance to about 70%, and Tactical Link on top takes it to about 90%. Open big fights with both.
3. **Fight from Smart Cover when you can.** 40% Damage Reduction plus bullet refunds on hits.
4. **Keep Precise Support stacked.** Six hits per stack, five stacks, 10 seconds each. A fast-firing M4 rebuilds them almost instantly, so the Armor stays up as long as you keep shooting things.
5. **Reload on purpose.** Suppressive Fire II stacks Rate of Fire on every reload, up to three times.
6. **Swap to the Double Barrel for the close stuff.** Rushers and a boss that walks into your face are what the secondary is for.

**Who should run it:** Commando players who already own the Double Barrel build's gear and want something that works at range, or anyone whose aim is better than their patience for three-shell reload dances. **Who shouldn't:** players who rely on the shotgun's forgiveness. TikTokAndy says himself that the floor 20 boss spawned exactly where the Double Barrel could have set a world record. You'll still clear the floor. You just won't brag about the time.
