faqs:
  - question: "Why does my DPS look terrible on this build?"
    answer: "Because you're reading the wrong number, champ. Your stats show YOUR damage. It does not show the Weapon Damage you're pumping into every teammate through Smart Cover, the roughly +20% vulnerability from Scanning Pulse, or the rate-of-fire buff from Tactical Link. Your team's total DPS is tripling. Yours just isn't the one doing it. Think of yourself as the guy who brought the aux cord to the party. Nobody thanks the DJ, but the party dies without him."
  - question: "Can I run this build solo?"
    answer: "You can also eat soup with a fork. Technically possible, practically miserable. This build is designed to multiply other people's damage. If there are no other people, you are multiplying zero. For solo content, run a Vanguard Commando DPS build instead and come back to Recon when you have friends. Or matchmake. We don't judge."
  - question: "Is the P90 actually better than an LMG for this build?"
    answer: "For this build, absolutely. The P90 fires fast, which means you burn through magazines fast and trigger Fully Charged (+7.50% Engineering) on every empty reload. An LMG with a 100-round belt takes forever to empty, so you're waiting ages between Engineering stacks. But here's the real reason: the build needs BOTH Torrential Downpour and Fully Charged on the same weapon. In the game's Build-Making Database only two SMGs roll both talents: the P90 and the AUG A3 Para XS. Neither Vector rolls Fully Charged and the MP7 doesn't roll Torrential Downpour, which leaves the P90 as the fast-firing pick. An older First Wave Vector can still carry a talent from outside its pool: until Season 2 Phase 1, recalibrating one pulled from the wrong pool. AgentHuntzman didn't pick it randomly."
  - question: "What's the best Vanguard support build in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "You're looking at it, at least as of Season 1. The Vanguard Recon running 4pc Mechanical Expert + 2pc Boom-Shakalaka was the highest-impact support build we'd found when this went up, and AgentHuntzman's pitch is that it triples team DPS. Nobody has been re-crowned in Season 2 yet, mostly because we haven't re-tested it. Until then it keeps the title by default, like the guy who holds the office fantasy trophy because nobody else showed up."
  - question: "Do I need Legendary drops for this build?"
    answer: "Nope. That's the beautiful part. This build already dramatically multiplies your team's damage output. Stop waiting for perfect drops and start running the build. Your squad will thank you immediately."
  - question: "Why Mechanical Expert over Long-term Effect?"
    answer: "Mechanical Expert is the correct choice because the 2pc Skill Duration and 3pc CDR keep your Smart Cover and Scanning Pulse cycling with near-permanent uptime. The 4pc SAC Efficiency bonus means your Tactical Link charges faster. You need permanent skill uptime before you need bigger numbers. A buff that's down for 5 seconds between casts is doing zero damage during those 5 seconds, no matter how large the number is."

## How to Play

1. **Smart Cover where the squad fights.** Everyone behind it gets Weapon Damage from your Engineering (about 24% of it, by community testing), and Foxhole Fortifies them: health on hit equal to 450% of your Engineering for 3 s.
2. **Scanning Pulse the pack.** Exposed makes pulsed enemies take +10% damage, and each one feeds the team 8 Signature charge.
3. **Police M4 to proc, then swap.** 10 hits in 5 s for Torrential Downpour, 5 in 3 s for Rapid Charge's 50 Signature charge.
4. **Run the P90 dry and reload.** Every empty reload is a Fully Charged stack, up to 3.
5. **Tactical Link the moment it's up.** Bugle Call fires on deploy, and Target Rich Environment adds Accuracy, Stability and Reload Speed for each enemy within 20 m.

On his sheet both skills outlast their cooldowns, which is why we lean on Skill Duration. Your own DPS will look low. Judge the build by how fast the squad clears.

## Before You Copy It

- **The sheet is our take on his build.** His video shows a Toughness core on the body armor, Firepower on the backpack, gloves and knee pads, mostly Skill Intensity rolls and Intense Performance II on the backpack. We put Engineering on every piece, swap most of the Intensity for Duration, Cooldown Recovery and Signature charge (nobody has pinned down what Intensity does here), and run Brainstorm, because extra Skill Radius mostly covers empty floor.
- **Recon changed after his video.** Season 1 Phase 3 fixed Recon "passively dealing more damage than expected" without naming the effect, Season 2 Phase 1 gave Tactical Link better auto-aim, and Season 2 Phase 2 fixed some "Enemy take increased damage" effects, which may change how Exposed lands. We haven't retested the build since May 2026.
