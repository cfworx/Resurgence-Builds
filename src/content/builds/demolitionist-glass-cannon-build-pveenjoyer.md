    answer: "Yes. Brains Over Brawn at Level 50 is the cleanest swap. The build scales entirely through skill-side stats."

If those all stack additively, every source live at once is about 54% on paper, more if your gloves and knee pads roll it too. ## How to Play

1. **Artillery Turret first.** Its damage stacks Frag Grenades' Skill Multi-Shot Chance, up to +5%.
2. **Explosive Seeker Mine next.** Its hits stack Applied Demolitions' Skill Crit Damage, up to 3 times.
3. **Stay 20 m back, glass cannon style.** Toymaker only pays while no enemy is within 20 meters.
4. **Swap to the StG 44.** Backup Battery adds +22.5% Engineering for 8 s on the swap (8 s cooldown).
5. **M32A1 on whatever crawls out.** The Fire Cycle 3-piece's +18% Signature Ability Charge Efficiency brings it back sooner.

## Before You Copy It

- **These are Season 1 numbers.** Season 2 Phase 2 cut the Explosive Seeker Mine's base cooldown from 18 s to 14 s (his 15.14 s was on the old base) and buffed other Demolitionist tree nodes. He says to max out the tree, so revisit your points.
- **Two of his weapon rolls are gone.** Skill Shredding Damage on the Warlord and Skill Piercing Damage on the StG 44 no longer roll on weapons since Season 2 Phase 1: All Skill Damage takes that slot. His Quick Release Magazine is a legacy mod too; new mods roll Skill Intensity or Skill Crit Damage.
- **Gear mods got better:** since Season 2 Phase 1 they can roll Skill Multi-Shot Chance, Skill Intensity and the Skill Crit stats.
