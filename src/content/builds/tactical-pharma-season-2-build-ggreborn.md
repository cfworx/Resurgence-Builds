## How to Play

1. **Trap, Hive, keep shooting.** TikTokAndy's whole rhythm, in "one of the least skill intensive builds" in the game.
2. **Keep the Engineering up.** 10 Vector hits in 5 s for Torrential Downpour, empty reloads for Fully Charged.
3. **Fight from cover.** Glass Cannon costs 10% Damage Reduction. Slot your Tactical Augmentations, "especially with how squishy this build is" (TikTokAndy).
4. **Rushers?** The Hive on your backpack hits anything within 15 m, and Swarm hits cut their damage by 12%. GGReborn says Revitalize's heal on kills helps him survive.

## Before You Copy It

- **His Vector's Fully Charged is an older roll.** Recalibrating the First Wave Vector pulled talents from the wrong pool until Season 2 Phase 1, and one you get now can't roll it. The [P90](/database/weapons/p90/) and [AUG A3 Para XS](/database/weapons/aug-a3-para-xs/) roll both of his Vector talents.
- **Fire Cycle goes on the holster and backpack.** He doesn't say, but no Fire Cycle body armor rolls Glass Cannon and no Jackpot backpack rolls Minion Training. His October 28 video matches.
