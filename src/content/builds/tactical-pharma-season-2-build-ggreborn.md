## What This Build Does

> Build credit: [**GGReborn**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OVnksyrlj88) for the build (October 16, 2026) and two follow-up tests in the Lone Wolf Challenge: [three stat setups](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f1q44KbdS6g) (October 22) and [four OS Protocols](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kz9YlTUl_1s) (October 23). The variant and the Dark Zone notes come from [**TikTokAndy's**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f20v8_cv9Ls) October 15 Tactical Pharma video.

Tactical Pharma is the [Field Medic](/class/field-medic/) focus that read the word "medic" and chose violence. GGReborn's Season 2 Phase 2 version is an Engineering skill build: **Jackpot 4pc + Fire Cycle 2pc**, **Glass Cannon** on the body armor, **Minion Training II** on the backpack, and **Brains Over Brawn**, a blue OS Protocol whose talent makes hits deal bonus damage equal to a share of your Engineering (48% on his level 50 copy). **Chemical Trap**, **Oxidizing Hive** and the **Oxidizing Swarm** signature do the killing. In our reading, the Lightweight M4 and First Wave Vector mostly exist to switch on Skill Damage and Engineering buffs. He shows it off in a solo Legendary run, then went back to Lone Wolf to test two of the build's big open questions.

The short answers: four OS Protocols finished **within five seconds** of each other, and the two creators here don't agree on how much Skill Critical Chance you need.

**The build in one breath:**

- **OS Protocol:** Brains Over Brawn (level 50, 1,660 Power Score on his copy).
- **Gear:** Jackpot 4pc + Fire Cycle 2pc, **Glass Cannon** on the body armor, **Minion Training II** on the backpack.
- **Skills:** Oxidizing Swarm with **Enhanced Formula**, Oxidizing Hive with **Hereafter Resilience**, Chemical Trap with **Chemical Recharge**.
- **Weapons:** Lightweight M4 (**In Sync** + **Revitalize**), First Wave Vector (**Torrential Downpour** + **Fully Charged**).
- **Stat focus:** Engineering-heavy mods, then Skill Intensity, Skill Critical Damage and Skill Multi-Shot Chance.

## GGReborn's Gear: Jackpot 4pc + Fire Cycle 2pc

Four pieces of Jackpot and two of Fire Cycle. He doesn't say out loud which two slots carry Fire Cycle, so check his video before you farm. Attribute bonuses as he reads them:

| Slot | Talent | Attribute bonuses |
|---|---|---|
| Mask | none | Skill Critical Damage, Received Healing |
| Body Armor | **Glass Cannon** | Skill Critical Damage, Skill Duration |
| Backpack | **Minion Training II** | Skill Duration, Skill Critical Chance |
| Gloves | none | Skill Critical Damage, Skill Radius |
| Holster | none | Skill Critical Damage, Skill Cooldown Recovery |
| Kneepads | none | Skill Intensity, Skill Multi-Shot Chance |

**Set bonuses:**

- **Jackpot:** 2pc Skill Radius +12%, 3pc Skill Critical Chance +10.8%, 4pc Skill Critical Damage +24%.
- **Fire Cycle:** 2pc Skill Critical Damage +12%.

**Talents (base versions):** Glass Cannon grants +20% Damage and inflicts -10% Damage Reduction. Minion Training makes Skill Critical Hit kills grant +36% Skill Critical Hit Damage for 12 seconds; his is the upgraded II, and he doesn't read out its numbers.

**Gear mods:** his purple and gold mods mix Engineering with Skill Critical Damage, Skill Multi-Shot Chance and Skill Intensity, with Armor and Health rolls in a few slots. Those skill sub-rolls on gear mods arrived with Season 2 Phase 1's gear mod rework.

## Weapons

| Slot | Weapon | Talents | Rolls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Lightweight M4** (Assault Rifle) | **In Sync** + **Revitalize** | Skill Multi-Shot Chance; Engineering attachments with Skill Critical Damage |
| Secondary | **First Wave Vector** (SMG) | **Torrential Downpour** + **Fully Charged** | Engineering attachments with Skill Intensity and Skill Critical Damage |

What the talents do (base versions):

- **In Sync:** weapon hits grant +9% Skill Damage for 8 seconds, skill hits grant +9% Weapon Damage for 8 seconds, and both double while they're active together.
- **Revitalize:** every kill or assist restores 4.20% Max Health for 3 seconds (4-second cooldown). GGReborn says it helps him survive the run.
- **Torrential Downpour:** hitting enemies 10 times within 5 seconds grants +22.50% Engineering for 12 seconds.
- **Fully Charged:** reloading an empty magazine grants +7.50% Engineering for 12 seconds, up to 3 stacks.

In our reading of that text, the Vector is an Engineering battery: spray it to trigger Torrential Downpour, run it dry and reload for Fully Charged stacks, and Brains Over Brawn turns the extra Engineering into bonus damage on your hits. GGReborn doesn't talk through a rotation in the video, so treat that as our interpretation.

## Best OS Protocol for Tactical Pharma

GGReborn runs **Brains Over Brawn** at level 50. Our database lists its base talent as "Hits deal additional damage equal to 24% Engineering"; his copy reads 48%. He singles out that talent as the main reason he's highlighting the protocol, since Engineering feeds directly into the bonus damage.

On October 23 he put four protocols through the Lone Wolf Challenge, keeping his stats as close as he could (he notes identical wasn't possible with different protocols):

| OS Protocol | Rarity | Main stat on his copy | His Skill Crit Chance / Damage | Time left |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brains Over Brawn | Specialized (blue) | 21.6% Skill Critical Chance | 60.76% / 233.15% | 802 seconds |
| Collateral Damage | High-End (gold) | 45% Engineering | 55.93% / 217.3% | 804 seconds |
| Tactical Persistence | Superior (purple) | 48.00% Skill Critical Damage | 60% / 243.37% | 804 seconds |
| Chain Reaction | Superior (purple) | 28.8% Skill Critical Chance | 67.96% / 233.15%* | 807 seconds |

*He says the Collateral Damage run had slightly lower crit stats because that's all he could stack with the gear he had.

Talents (base versions): **Collateral Damage** makes Skill Damage also hit enemies within 5 meters of the target for damage equal to 3000% Engineering (10-second cooldown). **Tactical Persistence** gives Skill Hits a 20% chance of +3% Skill Intensity for 5 seconds, up to 5 stacks. **Chain Reaction** makes kills grant +7.50% Skill Intensity for 10 seconds, up to 2 stacks.

**Our read:** Chain Reaction technically finished first, three seconds ahead of the next two and five ahead of Brains Over Brawn, on the setup with the most crit chance. GGReborn didn't crown a winner, and in his stat test the day before he said completion time can vary with enemy positioning and movement. Five seconds on a Lone Wolf run is a rounding error with a mortgage. Run whichever of these you own, and note that they aren't equally easy to own:

| OS Protocol | OS Protocol Cache tier | Drop chance per item |
|---|---|---|
| Brains Over Brawn | B | 3.14% |
| Chain Reaction | A | 0.40% |
| Tactical Persistence | A | 0.40% |
| Collateral Damage | S | 0.13% |

That's from Ubisoft's official drop-rate table (cache tier odds are S 2%, A 10% and B 88%, with pity at 50 pulls for S and 10 for A). The blue protocol finished two seconds behind a gold one that's roughly 24 times rarer. RNG giveth, mostly in blue.

The day after the test (October 24), GGReborn posted a Tactical Pharma gameplay video on Chain Reaction, titled ["This Tactical Pharma Build with Chain Reaction OS Is INSANE!"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yR7YWOzqVlA). The title is about as close to a verdict as he's given. It's a gameplay video rather than another test, so it doesn't add numbers to the test results above.

TikTokAndy is working his **Tactical Persistence** up to level 50 and thinks that's where his build "will eventually land"; his fallback is **Recursive Treatment** for its flat Engineering (his copy reads 36%), which he says would cost a little Skill Critical Damage. For the wider picture, see our [OS Protocol tier list](/tier-list/os-protocols/).

## How Much Skill Crit Chance? GGReborn's Stat Test

The creators split on this one. GGReborn's build sits around 69% Skill Critical Chance, and he says he's trying to bring it down to 60 or 55% while pushing Skill Critical Damage and Skill Multi-Shot Chance as high as he can. TikTokAndy, at 55%, thinks the build gets good "once you get to like 75 percent." On October 22 GGReborn tested three setups in Lone Wolf:

| Setup | Skill Crit Chance | Skill Crit Damage | Skill Multi-Shot Chance | Skill Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 65.87% | 217.55% | 37.61% | 36.73% |
| 2 | 71.19% | 219.55% | 37.61% | 36.73% |
| 3 | 76.73% | 217.23% | 33.69% | 36.73% |

He framed the test as higher crit chance versus lower crit chance with more multi-shot, warned that the differences were small, and asked viewers to watch the on-screen damage numbers rather than the completion times. The transcript ends on the finishing times without him naming a winner, so we're not naming one either.

## How to Play It

- **Throw, then shoot.** TikTokAndy's in-mission rhythm is simple: throw the trap, drop the Hive, keep shooting. He calls Tactical Pharma "one of the least skill intensive builds" you'll make in this game.
- **Play slower than a gun build.** "The name of the game is effectiveness," not speed, per TikTokAndy.
- **Keep the Engineering buffs rolling.** Brains Over Brawn scales with Engineering, and both Vector talents pay out Engineering (our reading of the talent text above).
- **Watch for rushers.** TikTokAndy expected them to be the hardest part of his solo Legendary attempt (he also worried about boss damage, and a boss is where the run ended). Hereafter Resilience's drones hit anything within 15 meters while the Hive rides on your backpack, and Enhanced Formula's -12% Damage debuff and kill heal give the Swarm some defensive value (database text).
- **Use cover.** Glass Cannon costs you Damage Reduction, and TikTokAndy's Overwatch backpack only pays out after 3 seconds behind cover.
- **Slot your Tactical Augmentations.** TikTokAndy forgot them in a previous video and made a point of adding them this time, "especially with how squishy this build is."
- **Let the signature cycle.** Chemical Recharge feeds Signature Ability Charges back on Corrosive applications, and TikTokAndy noticed his skills coming back quicker the more damage he did.

### Dark Zone PvP: TikTokAndy's Experience

GGReborn's videos don't cover PvP, so everything here is TikTokAndy's experience and opinion, from his October 15 Dark Zone session plus a montage a week later:

- **The setup:** he queued into the crossplay team Dark Zone solo, went rogue, and says roughly two full teams showed up within about 30 seconds.
- **The damage:** he says the build "will melt real players way faster than it'll melt an NPC," and he says he took out a team from the level below them, a spot he recommends for Tactical Pharma. He rates it "probably the best PvP build other than Bulwark."
- **The counter:** a properly built Bulwark. In his words, Dark Zone PvP right now is "toxic pharma versus bulwark and bulwark usually comes out on top."
- **Other threats he calls out:** Demolitionist Explosive Seeker Mines, which he says can one-shot you (for context, Season 2 Phase 2 reduced Explosive Seeker Mine's cooldown from 18 to 14 seconds as part of a set of Demolitionist buffs), and Striker Drones, which he rates as manageable because "all you got to do is shoot the striker drone." Vanguard, he says, is "okay sometimes."
- **The weapon he'd pair with it:** a shotgun that confuses and then stuns or shocks its target. He couldn't remember its name. That description matches Scorpio's Septicemia talent in our database (Confusion at 3 Venom stacks, Shock at 6), but that's our guess, not his.
- **His overall take:** it works, but it isn't his kind of fun. He calls himself an old-school Division player who likes using his gun, and he has reservations about current PvP balance, including how quickly he thinks a free-to-play player can farm a build like this (a couple of days, by his estimate).
- **A week later:** his October 22 montage shows him still running it solo against squads in the Dark Zone, still grumbling about Bulwarks. For a build he says isn't his kind of fun, he sounds very happy with the quiet once a server is cleared.

## What Season 2 Changed That Matters

Going by the official notes, neither Season 2 patch changed Jackpot, Fire Cycle, Glass Cannon, Minion Training, the three Tactical Pharma combo sets used here, Brains Over Brawn, the four weapon talents, or anything in TikTokAndy's variant. The changes that matter are in the stat pools and the loot:

- **Weapon mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** Ubisoft reworked weapon mods "to make the primary stat more important, increase the diversity of the pool and move away from flat bonuses which were more impactful for high RPM weapons than low RPM weapons." Main stats went up 100% (more Engineering per attachment), attribute bonuses went down 25%, and Skill Intensity % and Skill Critical Damage % joined the bonus pool for new mods. Those are exactly the bonus rolls on GGReborn's attachments.
- **Gear mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** Skill Critical Chance, Skill Critical Damage, Skill Multi-Shot Chance and Skill Intensity (plus Armor %, Max Health % and more) joined the gear mod bonus pool, which is what lets both creators stack skill stats on their mods.
- **Weapon rolls (Season 2 Phase 1):** damage-type Skill Damage bonuses (Blast, Shredding, Piercing) left the weapon attribute pool in favor of All Skill Damage.
- **Recalibration (Season 2 Phase 1):** Ubisoft fixed a few weapons, the First Wave Vector 42 ACP among them, getting talents from the wrong pool on recalibration. Worth knowing before you spend materials chasing Torrential Downpour and Fully Charged.
- **Farming (Season 2 Phase 2):** Dark Zone daily quests now reward Skill Mods, Hostile Nest quests reward Weapon and Gear Mods, and Hostile Nests hand out more Weapon and Gear Mods in general. Details in our [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/) and [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/).
