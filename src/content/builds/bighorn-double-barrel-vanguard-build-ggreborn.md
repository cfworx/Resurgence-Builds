GGReborn's Double Barrel Vanguard got a second gun worth having. **The level 40 G36-C is gone, The Bighorn exotic covers everything past shotgun range, and his sheet DPS went from 933k to a little over a million.**

Build by **GGReborn**, posted November 16, 2026 on Season 2 Phase 2. His video has next to no commentary, so everything below is read from his loadout screen and checked against our database. Where the screen doesn't show something, we say so.

## What Changed Since October

| | October 31 | November 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Second weapon | G36-C, level 40 | The Bighorn (exotic), level 50 |
| Double Barrel talents | Steady Reload, Misdirection Insurance | Precise Crit, Last Stand |
| Backpack | Rally Point Secure Pack | All Hazards Nitro Backpack |
| Sets | 3 Quick Draw, 3 Phalanx Attack | Same |
| OS Protocol | Close Quarters Menace | Same |
| DPS on his sheet | 933.32k | 1.01m |

The mask, body armor and gloves are the same pieces as in October, and the holster and knee pads, which his October screen didn't show, are a Jaivardhan Thigh Rig and "Patella" Knee Cups.

## Playing the Pieces

These follow from the talent texts, not from his commentary (the video has next to none).

1. **Shotgun inside 20 m.** Close Quarters Menace only pays with enemies that close, and so does a Double Barrel.
2. **Bighorn for everything else, and aim.** Aiming flips it to semi-auto for the +300% per shot. Hip fire skips the whole point.
3. **Keep the headshots coming.** Big Game Hunter's stacks run to 50, then bleed 10 every 4 seconds unless headshots keep landing.
4. **Shoot the Double Barrel from cover when you can.** Last Stand adds Skill Duration for hits from behind cover.
5. **Land 10 hits early in the fight.** Precise Crit then buffs everyone within 40 m, not just you.
