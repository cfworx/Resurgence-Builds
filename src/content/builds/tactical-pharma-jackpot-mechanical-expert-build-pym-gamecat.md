**A Tactical Pharma built almost entirely out of Skill Crit: Jackpot on four pieces, Mechanical Expert on two, Skill Critical Chance on every piece of gear, and In Sync with Multitasking on both guns. His Attributes screen reads 91.06% Skill Critical Chance and 210.10% Skill Critical Damage.**

Build by **PYM GameCat**. His video has no commentary: it opens the loadout and goes through every card, the OS Protocol, the Attributes screen and the three skills. So this page is what his screens show, and we haven't tested it ourselves.

Want to see a Tactical Pharma of his in a fight? He also runs one solo through a [Weekly Bounty](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/) target, Fredrick "Gearhead" Montano in Wall Street Woes, on Normal in 2:25 ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2x8dTe9CeMw)). He doesn't open his gear in that one, so we can't promise it's this exact loadout.

- **Brainstorm:** +15% Skill Duration, and another +15% within 20 meters of an ally.

## The Guns

**MPX** (level 50 T2, Shredding Damage 1.62k, 850 RPM, 30 rounds): Skill Critical Damage +19.66%, Skill Intensity +8.11%.
- In Sync: weapon hits grant +9% Skill Damage for 8 seconds, skill hits grant +9% Weapon Damage for 8 seconds, doubled while both are active.
- Multitasking: damaging 3 enemies within 5 seconds grants +30% Skill Critical Damage for 12 seconds (8 second cooldown).

**Lady Death** (exotic, level 50 T2, Blast Damage 1.25k, 900 RPM, 32 rounds): Weapon Critical Hit Damage +16.61%, Skill Shredding Damage +7.66%.
- Breathe Free: moving builds stacks (4 a second, 8 sprinting, up to 40); deploying a skill consumes 10 and grants +15% Skill Damage.
- In Sync II: +12.60% each way.
- Multitasking II: +42% Skill Critical Damage.

He also opens a spare Vector 45 ACP with Torrential Downpour II and Rapid Charge II (Headshot Damage +17.1%, Skill Critical Damage +16.7%). It isn't equipped, but it's the obvious swap if you don't have Lady Death.

## OS Protocol and Skills

**Tactical Layering**, level 50: Skill Intensity +30.00%. Deploying a skill grants +15% Engineering, up to 3 stacks. Our database lists +15.00% Skill Intensity at the lowest level.

The skills are **Oxidizing Hive** and **Chemical Trap**, with **Oxidizing Swarm** as the signature ability, each with three gold skill mods. He doesn't open the mod cards, so the sets are unknown.

## Swaps

- **No Lady Death:** the Vector 45 ACP he shows, or any SMG that rolls In Sync and Multitasking.
- **No Mechanical Expert:** a fifth Jackpot piece gives nothing extra (Jackpot tops out at four), so put any skill set with Skill Duration in those two slots.
- **Deep Focus** works at rank I too: +28% Skill Duration instead of +40%.
