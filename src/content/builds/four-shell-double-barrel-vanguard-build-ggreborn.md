faqs:
  - question: "How do you get four shells in the Double Barrel?"
    answer: "Stack Magazine Size. GGReborn runs 3 pieces of Quick Draw (+27%) and 3 of Phalanx Attack (+18%), an extended magazine (+30%) and Suppressive Fire on the body armor (+18%). Without Suppressive Fire he's at three shells; with it, four. In our database, the X-TREYME Pro Ribcage (T2) is the only Quick Draw body armor that rolls Suppressive Fire."
  - question: "What talents go on the Double Barrel in this build?"
    answer: "Steady Reload (+5% Weapon Damage for 10 seconds per reload, up to 5 stacks) and Misdirection Insurance (a missed shot has a 30% chance to go back in the magazine). GGReborn says Steady Reload is what most Vanguard players run, and Precise Crit is the swap if you want more damage."
  - question: "Why 3 pieces of Phalanx Attack instead of 2?"
    answer: "The third piece adds +18% Rate of Fire on top of the 2-piece's +18% Magazine Size. GGReborn finds Double Barrel spam slow without that Rate of Fire, so he keeps three. Two works if you want, he says, but he prefers three."
  - question: "What OS Protocol does GGReborn use for the Double Barrel?"
    answer: "Close Quarters Menace: +15% Skill Intensity and Weapon Damage with enemies within 20 meters at base, and 27% on his copy, which shows four of its five talent upgrades. For more Rate of Fire he suggests the gold Assault Charge, which gives +30% Firepower and +20% Rate of Fire for 20 seconds after you deploy the Signature Ability."
  - question: "How is this different from the three-shell Misdirection build?"
    answer: "TikTokAndy's Misdirection build runs 4-piece Phalanx Attack plus 2-piece Gunny Johnny for three shells, with Still Alive for sustain. GGReborn trades Gunny Johnny and one Phalanx Attack piece for 3-piece Quick Draw (+27% Magazine Size) to reach four, and runs Steady Reload for damage."

## How to Play

1. **Get within 20 m.** Close Quarters Menace only pays with enemies that close, and so does a shotgun.
2. **Reload every chance you get.** Each reload is a Steady Reload stack (up to 5) and a Suppressive Fire Rate of Fire stack (up to 3).
3. **Shoot from Smart Cover.** Optimized Armor's bullet refunds only happen behind it.
4. **Pulse, then finish what you scanned.** Field Supplies refills the magazine on scanned kills.
5. **Keep hitting in Tactical Link.** Every hit is a chance for Tactical Loop to stretch it.

## Before You Copy It

- **Suppressive Fire decides the body armor.** The X-TREYME Pro Ribcage (T2) is the only Quick Draw body armor that rolls it. Want Phalanx Attack on the chest instead? The Ranger Ballistics Vest (T0) is the only one that rolls it.
- **Assault Protection needs Extra Health.** It only stacks while you have some, and the video doesn't show where his comes from.
- **His secondary is a level 40 G36-C.** The video doesn't show its talents, so roll your own.

## What He Says

- **"Three bullets were already crazy, but what about four?"**
- **Misdirection Insurance earns its slot on bad connections.** He says it can keep the spam going when his internet isn't stable.
- **Three Phalanx Attack pieces, not two,** for the 18% Rate of Fire: without it, he finds Double Barrel spam slow.
- **Don't copy it piece for piece.** Balance Magazine Size, Rate of Fire and Multi-Shot Chance with what you have.
