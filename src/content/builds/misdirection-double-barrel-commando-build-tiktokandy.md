## What This Build Does

> Build credit: [**TikTokAndy**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ITIJB2nj3zg) for the full walkthrough (September 30, 2026) and two later videos, a [floor 20 run on his original two-shell build](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9nQ-jGnc7gU) (published October 2) and a [solo Legendary Precious Cargo run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D3yrRSUwhq0) with his updated build (October 6), and [**GGReborn**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bltLVzptY0U), whose version carried him through his first solo Legendary on Vanguard (September 23, 2026). TikTokAndy credits GGReborn for "bringing this build to the table," specifically the Gunny Johnny setup that got him a third shell. Every stat below is the creator's own reading from their video unless we say it came from our database or Ubisoft's patch notes. We haven't re-tested this build ourselves.

The Double Barrel holds two shells. This build's whole personality is refusing to accept that. You stack Magazine Size until the gun holds **three**, then pile every bullet refund in the Vanguard Commando kit on top: **Misdirection Insurance** can put missed shots back, **Smart Cover** and **Optimized Armor** refund bullets on hits, **Ammo Refill** pays out during Tactical Link, and **Field Supplies** tops you up on Pulsed kills. Add the 30% Rate of Fire from **Phalanx Attack 4pc + Gunny Johnny 2pc** and the two-shell shotgun starts living up to the "Automatic" in TikTokAndy's video title.

TikTokAndy calls the Double Barrel "definitely the best gun in the game by a mile" and says he gets through Elite missions and Legendary instances "really without much of a problem" at around 5,200 to 5,300 Armor. By October 6, on mostly T2 gear, his sheet read 10,000 Armor and 131,000 Health. All four videos were published during Season 2 Phase 2, after the Season 2 Phase 1 changes to Commando covered below.

**The build in one breath:**

- **Weapon:** Double Barrel with **Misdirection Insurance** and **Still Alive II**.
- **Gear:** Phalanx Attack 4pc + Gunny Johnny 2pc, **Suppressive Fire II** on the body armor, and on the backpack **Emergency Measures** (TikTokAndy's October pick), **Siege II** (his September one) or **Inspiration** (GGReborn).
- **Secondary:** a Vector with **Torrential Downpour** and **Last Stand**, fired from behind cover before you cast skills (TikTokAndy, October).
- **Skills:** Smart Cover with **Optimized Armor**, Scanning Pulse with **Field Supplies**, Tactical Link with **Ammo Refill**.
- **OS Protocol:** **Cover Shooter** since early October; **Augmented Lethality** before that.
- **The goal:** three shells in the magazine and as many free bullets as the game will hand you.

> [!WARNING] Season 2 changed how this build works
> Season 2 Phase 1 (August 5) reduced Commando's Target Acquisition talent, changed Concerted Strike to trigger on shots, and changed Ammo Refill from a 72% chance of returning 1 bullet to an 18% chance of returning 4. Ubisoft said it wanted the "Bullet Back" mechanic to be less reliable for the Double Barrel while keeping it similar for weapons with larger magazines. Every video here was recorded after those changes. Details below and in our [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/).

## TikTokAndy's October Update

Two later videos fill in both ends of the story. The one published October 2 shows the build he started with, and the October 6 one shows where it went. Same gun, same talents and the same three combo sets throughout; the protocol, the backpack talent and the secondary are what changed.

### Where it started: floor 20 on two shells (published October 2)

TikTokAndy posted a floor 20 run that he says placed **third in the NA region**. His title calls it "Lone Star Stage 20"; our read is that it's the Lone Wolf Challenge. The footage is older than his September 30 walkthrough: he'd just gotten the Double Barrel, ran two shells, and wore what he calls his "original build." He calls the run proof that "the two bullet method actually works just fine if that's all you have."

His sheet for that run: 6,700 Armor, 1.99k Firepower, about 400 Engineering, 55% Critical Hit Chance, 140% Critical Hit Damage, 32% Multi-Shot Chance, 70% Headshot Damage and 43% Weapon Damage, with an Ammo Refill set he says wasn't maxed. He was on Augmented Lethality (and, narrating on October 2, still preferred it to Cover Shooter), paired Phalanx Attack with 2pc Fury Strike (a Siege II backpack was one of the Fury pieces), and had Duality on the chest, which he has since swapped for Suppressive Fire.

His floor 20 tip: the boss sometimes stops at one spot and stays there (he puts it around one run in ten). When it does, crouch on the boxes right beside it, go for headshots, and save Tactical Link for the wave of enemies that comes next. The other outcome he's happy with is the boss running down into the middle, which leaves you the high ground.

What changed, and his reasons:

- **Cover Shooter:** "second to none" for PvE with the Double Barrel, he says, and "I've been very happy with it so far." His copy reads +30% Weapon Damage, and its talent stacks a +16% Critical Hit Damage buff up to three times behind cover, which he counts as +48%. In his October 2 video he still preferred Augmented Lethality, so this one's fresh.
- **Emergency Measures:** "you really need Emergency Measures on one of your pieces," which he calls "basically a second life." Our database: taking damage below 20% Health restores 50% Max Health, 30-second cooldown. He kept the Quick Draw backpack for it (plus high Firepower and Toughness rolls) rather than wait for a Phalanx Attack one.
- **The Vector secondary:** it runs **Torrential Downpour** (he reads +31.5% Engineering for 12 seconds after 10 hits within 5 seconds, the II value on our other builds, and says it takes him to about 600 Engineering) and **Last Stand** (+24% Skill Duration for 10 seconds on hits behind cover). He shoots with it from cover before he casts, and says it makes Tactical Link last "almost twice as long, at least it feels like it does." That's the Engineering-secondary idea from his September video, now in the loadout.
- **Gunny Johnny 2pc stays:** "the only 2 piece set bonus that's going to give you rate of fire," and with Suppressive Fire on the same chest he calls the pair "the icing on the cake." Before them, he says, he struggled to put out big damage in a short window.
- **Field Supplies stays:** he tested the build without it and says it "makes a huge difference."

His skill screens on this build: Smart Cover lasts 43 seconds on a 45-second cooldown ("pretty much up the entire time"), and Scanning Pulse lasts 17 seconds on a 19-second cooldown, scanning up to 8 targets.

### Taking it into Precious Cargo solo

His route notes from the run, in mission order:

1. **Snipers first, then the Grenadier.** The Grenadier is tanky, so get inside your shotgun's range before you commit.
2. **Bank Tactical Link for the second wave.** It comes out of the door on your left; stall the first wave if you have to.
3. **Don't count on Kevin.** He insists on leading, takes your cover and rarely pulls a rusher off you. When a rusher closes with Tactical Link down, back into Smart Cover and fight from there.
4. **Skip Tactical Link on the three-enemy room.** Three enemies don't need it, and he thinks you can respawn there if it goes wrong.
5. **Go upstairs for the next fight.** Elevation wins it, but enemies spawn behind you, so watch the minimap.
6. **The finale: stay off the bridge and the balconies.** He calls it one of the most common mistakes he sees. Run to the right into the small nook by the shipping container and let the enemies file in one at a time. The last boss won't push past the cargo crate, and his grenades mostly land on it. His [team healer video](/builds/aegis-team-healer-tech-operator-build-tiktokandy/) recommends the opposite for that final boss, the bridge you start on, so his own videos disagree on this one.

The mission ends the moment all four bosses are down, and he says it pays out more gear than any other mission, a lot of it T2. His verdict on teammates: a team is no faster "unless you have a really strong team," and randoms mostly slow him down.

## What Season 2 Changed for the Double Barrel

The Double Barrel itself wasn't changed. What changed is how it interacts with Commando's kit. The official Season 2 Phase 1 notes describe the Commando changes as aimed at the Double Barrel's use of the "Bullet Back" mechanic in Commando's Signature Ability, and Ubisoft explained the thinking:

> "We believe that aside from the way the Double Barrel Shotgun can take advantage of it, the mechanic is fun and core to Commando's identity... making the mechanic less reliable for the Double Barrel Shotgun while keeping the way it works similar for weapons with larger magazine size."

| Change (Season 2 Phase 1) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Target Acquisition (Commando talent) | 30% signature ability charge, 90% cap | 5% charge, 20% cap |
| Concerted Strike (Commando talent) | Triggered off hits | Triggers off shots |
| Ammo Refill (Tactical Link combo set) | 72% chance to return 1 bullet | 18% chance to return 4 bullets |

Neither creator talks about Target Acquisition or Concerted Strike in these videos. What they describe is a build that gets its bullets back from other sources, plus a bigger magazine. Ubisoft's note says it wanted the mechanic to keep working similarly on weapons with larger magazines; neither creator tested where a three-shell Double Barrel fits into that. TikTokAndy just says it plays much better with three.

Other Season 2 changes that touch this build:

- **Weapon rolls (Season 2 Phase 1):** Blast, Shredding and Piercing damage bonuses left the weapon attribute pool in favor of All Weapon Damage, because a damage-type bonus on a weapon of a different damage type "didn't bring any benefits and was frustrating," in Ubisoft's words. TikTokAndy's Double Barrel rolls Weapon Damage (close to a max roll) and Critical Hit Damage.
- **Weapon mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** main stats up 100%, attribute bonuses down 25%, and Weapon Damage, Headshot Damage and Weapon Critical Hit Damage joined the bonus pool for new mods. GGReborn's attachments carry Firepower with Critical Hit Damage.
- **Gear mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** the gear mod pool gained Weapon Critical Hit Chance and Damage, Weapon Multi-Shot Chance, Weapon Damage and Headshot Damage, among others. TikTokAndy's gear mods are Firepower with crit rolls, and he says Armor mods are fine too.
- **Daily quests (Season 2 Phase 2):** Weapon Kill quests now reward weapons matching the quest's weapon type, with a chance of T2 at level 40. Handy when you're hunting a Double Barrel with the right talents. Full list in our [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).
- **No end date yet:** on October 21 Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year" ([our coverage](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)), so this is the balance you're building for until then.

## Why It Still Works, According to the Creators

TikTokAndy sums up Misdirection Insurance in one line, "bullet missed is a bullet gained," and then builds around "anything that you could do to restore a bullet." Here's every refund the two creators use:

| Bullet source | What it does | The numbers (and whose they are) |
|---|---|---|
| **Misdirection Insurance** (weapon talent) | Missed shots can return to the magazine | 30% chance on the base version (our database). TikTokAndy says Misdirection Insurance II is even better. |
| **Smart Cover** (skill) | Hits while you're behind Smart Cover can restore a bullet | 15% on TikTokAndy's skill screen |
| **Optimized Armor** (Smart Cover combo, 3pc) | Behind Smart Cover: +3.20% Damage Reduction, and hits can restore 1 bullet | 4.80% chance (our database); TikTokAndy calls it "another five percent" |
| **Ammo Refill** (Tactical Link combo, 3pc) | Shots in the first 6 seconds of Tactical Link can recover 4 bullets, and kills reset the duration | 18% per Ubisoft's Season 2 Phase 1 notes. TikTokAndy's tooltip read 7% on September 30, about a quarter of the 29% our database listed before Season 2, the same cut Ubisoft made at the top (72% to 18%). Our read: the chance scales with combo rarity, so check your own tooltip. |
| **Field Supplies** (Scanning Pulse combo, 3pc) | Kills or assists on Pulsed enemies restore 6% of Health lost and refill 6.40% of the magazine | TikTokAndy isn't sure it ever restores a shell on a magazine this small, but likes the Health |
| **Magazine Size** | Turns two shells into three | Above roughly 50%, per TikTokAndy |

Then there's Rate of Fire:

- **Phalanx Attack 3pc (+18%) + Gunny Johnny 2pc (+12%) = 30% Rate of Fire.** That's TikTokAndy's math, and our gear set database agrees.
- **Suppressive Fire II:** reloading grants +8.4% Rate of Fire for 5 seconds, up to 3 stacks (his reading). With three shells you reload constantly, so in our read those stacks shouldn't have much time to fall off.
- **Inspiration (GGReborn's backpack):** +30% Rate of Fire while a skill is deployed, per our database.

TikTokAndy tested the build on two shells too. "It still works," he says, but it's "way better with three."

## The Third Shell: Magazine Size Math

TikTokAndy's starting point: the Double Barrel "only holds two shells," so you need Magazine Size "above 50 or more" to fit another one. He couldn't get there until he saw GGReborn's Gunny Johnny setup.

| Magazine Size source | Value | Where the number comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Phalanx Attack 2pc | +18% | Our gear set database |
| Suppressive Fire II (body armor talent) | +25% | TikTokAndy's tooltip (base Suppressive Fire is +18% in our database) |
| Extended magazine attachment | Varies | GGReborn runs a 30% one |

On paper that's 43% before the attachment, and a 30% extended magazine carries you well past the line. That's our arithmetic, and we don't know exactly how the game rounds shells, so count yours after everything is equipped. Four shells? TikTokAndy heard someone managed it with a combination of two-piece set bonuses he couldn't quite remember, tried a gold extended magazine himself, and still topped out at three. We'll file four under "RNG willing."

His reasoning for leaning on Phalanx Attack: it gives this build the two things it wants, Magazine Size and Rate of Fire, in one set. **Set bonuses (our database):**

- **Phalanx Attack:** 2pc Magazine Size +18%, 3pc Rate of Fire +18%, 4pc Weapon Multi-Shot Chance +12%.
- **Gunny Johnny:** 2pc Rate of Fire +12%. (3pc is Reload Speed +18% and 4pc is Weapon Damage +12%, if you lean harder on it.)

**Alternatives TikTokAndy mentions:**

- **4pc Gunny Johnny + 2pc Phalanx Attack:** workable, but he makes "a case that four-piece phalanx is actually better" from his own experience.
- **3pc Gunny Johnny + 3pc Phalanx Attack:** also fine by him, as long as some Phalanx Attack stays in the mix.
- **2pc Gunny Johnny + 3pc Phalanx Attack + any backpack:** his own October layout while he hunts a fourth Phalanx Attack piece. The Gunny Johnny pieces are the mask and body armor, and the backpack is a Quick Draw he kept for its Emergency Measures talent, because "the talent on the backpack and the vest is much more important than the actual set bonus."
- **Fury Strike:** he ran 2pc Fury Strike (+10% Optimal Range) with 4pc Phalanx Attack for a while, and says some players run 4pc Fury Strike. He prefers Gunny Johnny's Rate of Fire, couldn't find a suitable backpack anyway, and says "the talent is much more important" than 10% Optimal Range. GGReborn says he'd use Fury Strike if he had the time to farm it.

On survivability, TikTokAndy found the build "very squishy" in September, which is why he paired Siege II with Still Alive II. By October he'd moved to Emergency Measures, and with T2 gear and every Tactical Augmentation slotted on the chest his Armor had roughly doubled to 10,000. For gear mods he runs Firepower with Weapon Critical Hit Chance or Damage, says Armor mods are fine, and tells you to slot any Tactical Augmentations you have.

## How to Play It

1. **Live behind Smart Cover.** That's where the refunds and the Damage Reduction are: base Smart Cover's 15% refund on hits, Optimized Armor's 4.80% on top, and the 40% Damage Reduction on TikTokAndy's skill screen. His tip, which he admits is the most obvious advice ever: the refunds trigger on hits, so actually hit the enemy.
2. **Don't panic about misses.** Misdirection Insurance gives missed shots a chance to come back. It won't make you a better shot. It makes your worse shots cheaper.
3. **Pulse first, then kill what you Pulsed.** Field Supplies only pays out on enemies you scanned.
4. **Chain kills inside Tactical Link.** Ammo Refill works during the first 6 seconds, and kills reset that window.
5. **Reload like it's a hobby.** With three shells you're reloading constantly anyway, and Suppressive Fire II turns each reload into Rate of Fire stacks.
6. **Stay close.** It's a short-range shotgun, so fight at the distance your Optimal Range rolls allow.
7. **Let the talents keep you up.** Still Alive II heals on kills (10-second cooldown), and the backpack covers the rest: Emergency Measures restores half your Health when you drop below 20% (TikTokAndy's October pick), or Siege II drops Extra Health on you when two or more enemies are within 20 meters. TikTokAndy says the only time he dies is "when I screw up my buttons," which is the most relatable sentence in this guide.

If a three-shell shotgun sounds like too much commitment, our [Milkshake Machine](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/) runs Phalanx Attack on a 100-round LMG instead. Opposite philosophy: one reloads every three shots, the other barely reloads at all.
