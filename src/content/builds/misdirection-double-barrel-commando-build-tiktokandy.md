faqs:
  - question: "Is the Double Barrel shotgun still good after the Season 2 nerf?"
    answer: "The two creators still running it say yes. Season 2 Phase 1 reduced Commando's Target Acquisition talent (signature charge 30% to 5%, cap 90% to 20%) and made Concerted Strike trigger on shots instead of hits. Ubisoft's stated goal was to make the 'Bullet Back' mechanic less reliable for the Double Barrel while keeping it similar for weapons with larger magazines, because it sees the mechanic as fun and core to Commando's identity. In late September, TikTokAndy called the Double Barrel the best gun in the game and said he gets through Elite and Legendary content without much trouble, and GGReborn used it for his first solo Legendary clear on Vanguard. In a video published October 2 he showed a floor 20 run on his original two-shell build (in what we take to be the Lone Wolf Challenge) that he says placed third in NA, and on October 6 he soloed Precious Cargo on Legendary with the updated build on this page. The engine changed; the shotgun is still loud. We haven't re-tested it ourselves."
  - question: "How do you get a third shell in the Double Barrel?"
    answer: "Stack Magazine Size. TikTokAndy says you need it above roughly 50% to fit a third shell, and he gets there with Phalanx Attack (its 2-piece bonus is +18% Magazine Size in our database), Suppressive Fire II on the body armor (he reads +25%) and an extended magazine attachment (GGReborn runs a 30% one). He credits GGReborn's Gunny Johnny setup for cracking it. Four shells? He heard someone managed it, tried a gold extended magazine himself and still topped out at three. Two shells is a conversation. Three is an argument."
  - question: "What are the best Double Barrel talents in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "Misdirection Insurance plus Still Alive, on both creators' guns. TikTokAndy calls Misdirection Insurance the 'number one absolute must' (our database lists the base version as a 30% chance that a missed shot returns to the magazine, and he says the II version is better). Still Alive II heals on kills, which he reads as 42% of missing Health on a 10-second cooldown, and he calls it necessary for solo content. Some players swap Still Alive for a reload-triggered talent; he says that works but is squishier. He landed Misdirection Insurance on his third or fourth recalibration and admits that was 'insanely lucky.' Your recalibration materials may have a different story to tell."
  - question: "What OS Protocol is best for the Double Barrel build?"
    answer: "Cover Shooter, as of October. TikTokAndy ran Augmented Lethality (+4% Weapon Damage and +4% Incoming Healing for every enemy within 20 meters) for a long time and still preferred it on October 2, saying Cover Shooter cost him too much Firepower. Four days later his Precious Cargo build ran Cover Shooter, which he called 'second to none' for PvE with the Double Barrel: his copy reads +30% Weapon Damage, and its talent stacks a separate +16% Critical Hit Damage buff up to three times behind cover, which he counts as +48%. He rates Tight Grouping as pretty good, would pass on Deadly Accuracy because it needs headshots, and sticks to Firepower protocols in general. GGReborn shows his protocol on screen but never names it, so we won't guess."
  - question: "Why play Vanguard Commando with the Double Barrel?"
    answer: "Because the bullet refunds live in the Commando kit. Smart Cover can restore a bullet when you hit from behind it (15% on TikTokAndy's skill screen) and Optimized Armor adds a 4.80% chance on top, Tactical Link carries Ammo Refill and, per TikTokAndy, gives you auto aim, and Field Supplies refills 6.40% of the magazine on Pulsed kills. TikTokAndy says he considered other specializations and thinks Vanguard is by far the best fit. The shotgun is the star; Commando is the stagehand that keeps handing it shells."
  - question: "Where do you farm a Double Barrel with Misdirection Insurance?"
    answer: "TikTokAndy says Double Barrels come from daily missions and Elite missions, and that the Elite Legendary instance that week was dropping T2 guns quite regularly (he also warns it's extremely difficult, so bring a full team at first). Season 2 Phase 2 reworked daily quests so Weapon Kill quests reward weapons matching the quest's weapon type, with a chance of T2 at level 40. After that it's recalibration until Misdirection Insurance shows up, which is the part of this build that tests your patience more than your aim."

The Double Barrel holds two shells, and this build refuses to accept that. **Stack Magazine Size until it holds three, then let every bullet refund in the Commando kit keep it fed.**

Build by **TikTokAndy**, posted September 2026 on Season 2 Phase 2 and updated in October. He credits **GGReborn** for the Gunny Johnny setup behind the third shell.

## How to Play

1. **Vector first, from cover.** Torrential Downpour and Last Stand go up before you cast, and Tactical Link lasts "almost twice as long, at least it feels like it does."
2. **Pulse, then kill what you Pulsed.** Field Supplies only pays on scanned enemies.
3. **Live behind Smart Cover and hit things.** Refunds trigger on hits; Cover Shooter only stacks behind cover.
4. **Chain kills inside Tactical Link.** Each kill resets Ammo Refill's 6 s window.
5. **Reload freely.** Each reload is a Suppressive Fire stack, up to 3.
6. **Stay close.** "You can't be beaming guys from across the board."

## Before You Copy It

- **Season 2 Phase 1 nerfed the Double Barrel through Commando.** Target Acquisition's Signature charge fell from 30% to 5% (cap 90% to 20%), Concerted Strike now triggers on shots, and Ammo Refill went from a 72% chance of 1 bullet to 18% of 4 (his set read 7%). Every video here came after it.
- **Three shells need Magazine Size above about 50%,** he says. Phalanx Attack (18%) plus his Suppressive Fire II (25%) is 43%, so add an extended magazine (GGReborn's is 30%) and count your shells. Two still work; three is "way better."
