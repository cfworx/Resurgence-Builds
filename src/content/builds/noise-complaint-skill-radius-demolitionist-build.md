  - question: "What is the Noise Complaint build in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "Our own HE Munitions Demolitionist design, built around one stat: Skill Radius. It wears both gear sets that add it (4-piece Boom-Shakalaka, 2-piece Jackpot) and both talents that add it (Territory Expansion on the body armor, Intense Performance on the backpack), for +93% on paper. Area Suppression, Fragmentation Charge and Concentrated Fire go on the skills, Chain Reaction is the OS Protocol, and a PKM with In Sync and Multitasking does the shooting. Area Suppression wants turret shells that catch two enemies, Multitasking wants three enemies damaged in 5 seconds and Chain Reaction wants kills, and a blast nearly twice as wide serves up all three. The neighbors have been notified."
  - question: "How much Skill Radius can you stack in Division Resurgence?"
    answer: "+93% from gear sets and talents at base values: Boom-Shakalaka's 3-piece bonus (+18%), Jackpot's 2-piece bonus (+12%), Territory Expansion (+30%, only at full Health) and Intense Performance (+33%, with -5% Skill Duration). Those four are the only gear set bonuses and body armor or backpack talents in the Build-Making Database that add Skill Radius, and this build runs all of them. Skill Radius also rolls as a gear attribute, and the Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes list the Demolitionist tree's Strong Arm at 15% skill radius for 6 seconds on a 6-second cooldown, so the real ceiling sits higher. We haven't measured a blast in game yet. If there's a cap, we'll find out the loud way."

## How to Play

1. **Keep your Health full.** Cover, distance, patience. Territory Expansion is 30 of the 93.
2. **Open on the L86.** The swap starts Backup Battery, ten hits start Torrential Downpour.
3. **Turret on the pack.** Every shell that catches two or more enemies stacks Area Suppression.
4. **Mine into the middle.**
5. **PKM on the same group.** Three enemies in 5 s is Multitasking. Your hits plus skill hits is In Sync.
6. **Launcher: every grenade into one crowd, fast.** Five hits in 3 s is Concentrated Fire.
7. **Scratched? Top up before the next cast.**
