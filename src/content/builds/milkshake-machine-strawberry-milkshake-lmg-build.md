**By RapidF5 ·** Patch 1.1.2.2

> 118 rounds. ~32% Multi-Shot with four enemies close. In Sync double-dip from bullet one. Glass Cannon justified by Swift Taunt. Dominating Presence rewarding you for standing in the worst possible spot. We call it **The Milkshake Machine** because it shreds enemies the way a blender shreds ice, and because naming a build "Strawberry Milkshake Optimal" has the same energy as naming your fantasy football team "Team 1."

> **Not meta. Not trying to be.** This is a fun build. It won't top the leaderboard and it's not what you bring when your clan is actually trying. It's what you bring when you want to watch Multi-Shot crits turn a room into a smoothie while your wife asks why you're cackling at your phone at 11pm. Want meta? Check the [Build Tier List](/tier-list/). Want a good time? Keep reading.

The core loop: drop Smart Cover, cast Scanning Pulse (instant In Sync skill-side buff), fire Strawberry Milkshake (In Sync weapon-side buff on first hit). Both buffs double. **+18% Weapon Damage and +18% Skill Damage from bullet one** (rank I values), into Pulsed enemies taking +20% vulnerability, every third-ish bullet firing twice via Multi-Shot, Glass Cannon adding +20% Damage, Inspiration granting +30% RoF. The 118-round mag and Field Supplies refilling on Pulsed kills means you almost never reload. A lot of moving parts. That's the whole point. This one rewards the kind of person who reads patch notes on the toilet.

> [!WARNING] Season 1 Phase 3 and Season 2 changes that affect this build
> - **Strawberry Milkshake (Season 1 Phase 3, June 24):** Fixed SM showing as Piercing instead of Shredding in the Armory and dropping with LVL1 talents instead of LVL2 like other exotics. Ubisoft said everyone who had unlocked it would get an updated copy (one per owned copy). Copies from after that fix, including the reissued ones, should carry LVL2 talents, which read higher than the rank I In Sync and Swift Taunt numbers in this guide (about 1.4x on most talents we've checked). Season 2 Phase 1 then fixed Anarchy's debuff being applied on heal.
> - **Dominating Presence (Season 2):** Phase 1 fixed a bug where it granted Armor instead of Weapon Multi-Shot Chance, so its Multi-Shot likely wasn't applying when we wrote this. Phase 2 then reduced its maximum scaling to 35/42/49/56/63/70%. We've redone the Multi-Shot math below from the current base values (+5% per enemy within 20 meters, capped at 35% at base), but it hasn't been re-tested in game, so retest your total.
> - **Weapon rolls (Season 2 Phase 1):** Blast, Shredding and Piercing damage bonuses were removed from the weapon attribute pool in favor of All Weapon Damage, so the "don't roll Piercing on SM" problem only applies to old copies.
> - **Exotic mods (Season 2):** Phase 1 reworked exotic mods (they can now be unequipped, drop from weekly quests and Elite Mission chests, and sell as weekly DZ vendor crates for 2,500 DZ Credits), and since Phase 2 exotic weapons no longer ship with their exotic mods pre-equipped.
> - **Gear mods (Season 2 Phase 1):** The gear mod pool now includes Weapon Crit Hit Chance, Weapon Crit Hit Damage, Weapon Multi-Shot Chance and Weapon Damage. That's this build's whole stat line.
>
> This build has not been re-tested since Patch 1.1.2.2. Full details: [Season 1 Phase 3 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/), [Season 2 Phase 1 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) and [Season 2 Phase 2 patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/).

> [!DANGER] Glass Cannon Warning
> This build runs Glass Cannon (+20% Damage) permanently. Swift Taunt's Extra Health buffer helps, but you WILL get one-tapped in DZ PvP if someone catches you off-guard. In PvE, stay behind Smart Cover and keep firing. If you're dying more than shooting, swap Glass Cannon for **Duality**, which our database lists as "Grants +15% Weapon Damage when Health is above 50%. Grants +15% Damage Reduction when Health is below 50%." You keep a damage bonus while you're healthy and get Damage Reduction when you're not.

## What This Build Does

- **~118-round magazine** at 550 RPM with Phalanx Attack 4pc.
- **In Sync double-dip:** +18% Weapon Damage and +18% Skill Damage simultaneously (rank I values), from the first bullet of every engagement.
- **~32% Multi-Shot Chance** with four enemies within 20 meters: Phalanx 4pc (+12%) plus Dominating Presence OS (+5% per enemy, so +20% at four). Every extra enemy adds more, until Dominating Presence hits its 35% cap at base.
- **Glass Cannon (+20% Damage)** running permanently, balanced by Swift Taunt's 25% Max Health Extra Health buffer (rank I).
- **Inspiration backpack** grants +30% RoF whenever Smart Cover is deployed, which is basically always.
- **Field Supplies skill mod** refills your magazine by 6.40% on every kill or assist on a Pulsed enemy, so the mag effectively never runs out in crowd fights.
- **Commando skill mod** grants +10.20% WCHC for 10 seconds every time you take part in a kill while Tactical Link is on you, turning your signature window into a crit avalanche.

This is not a glass-cannon-and-die build. The Extra Health from Swift Taunt is up almost permanently because hitting three enemies in five seconds at 550 RPM is trivial. You have a damage floor and a damage ceiling, and the ceiling is the kind of number that makes you screenshot it and send it to your clan Discord at 1am like a proud parent showing off a report card.

## Why Each Gear Piece Matters in This Build

**Body Armor: Glass Cannon.** +20% Damage, -10% Damage Reduction (tier 1 values; Glass Cannon II is +28%). Yes, Glass Cannon. No, you won't die every 4 seconds. Swift Taunt grants 25% Max Health as Extra Health (rank I) for 10 seconds every time you hit three enemies in five seconds. At 550 RPM into a crowd, that buff is basically a permanent force field. Your spouse will lose more health stepping on a LEGO than you'll notice from the -10% DR.

**Backpack: Inspiration (Fury Strike).** +30% Rate of Fire while skills are deployed. Smart Cover is deployed essentially all the time. This is +30% RoF as a passive baseline the moment you press the skill button. Free damage. The kind of free that makes you suspicious, but it's real.

**Mask, Gloves, Holster: Phalanx Attack (Firepower core).** These are your crit roll slots. Stack WCHC on the mask and gloves, WCHD on the holster. The Phalanx 4pc set bonus is the entire reason this build exists, and these three slots round out the 4pc count. If RNG gives you bad rolls, welcome to the club. We meet on Tuesdays.

**Kneepads: Fury Strike, crit rolls.** Fury Strike 2pc gives you +10% Optimal Range, stacking on Strawberry Milkshake's 25m base. Optimal Range isn't in the gear roll pool, so more range has to come from weapon mods. The goal: delete things before they get close enough to remind you that Glass Cannon exists and your reflexes aren't what they were in 2008.

## Best OS Protocol for Strawberry Milkshake in Division Resurgence

**Dominating Presence** is the pick. +12% Weapon Damage as a flat baseline, plus +5% Weapon Multi-Shot Chance per enemy within 20 meters, so +20% with 4 enemies in range. (Those are the current base values. Since Season 2 Phase 2 the Multi-Shot caps at 35% at base, and before Season 2 Phase 1 the talent was granting Armor instead; see the warning at the top.) This OS Protocol literally rewards you for standing in a bad place, which is exactly where The Milkshake Machine wants to be. Combined with Phalanx 4pc's +12% Multi-Shot, that's about 32% Multi-Shot Chance with four enemies in range, climbing with every extra enemy until Dominating Presence hits its cap. That means every third-ish bullet fires twice. Onto Pulsed enemies. With In Sync's +18% WD active (rank I). While Glass Cannon adds +20% Damage. While Inspiration adds +30% RoF.

## Best Weapon Talents for Strawberry Milkshake

### Primary: Strawberry Milkshake (Exotic LMG)

The talents you want on Strawberry Milkshake are **In Sync (slot 1)** and **Swift Taunt (slot 2)**. Anarchy is locked as the exotic talent and was a non-factor on Patch 1.1.2.2 (Season 2 Phase 1 later fixed its debuff being applied on heal).

- **In Sync** (rank I values; a LVL2 copy reads higher, about 1.4x on most talents we've checked): Weapon hits grant +9% Skill Damage for 8 seconds. Skill hits grant +9% Weapon Damage for 8 seconds. Bonus is doubled when both buffs are active simultaneously. Cast Scanning Pulse first. Pulse counts as a skill hit. Skill-side buff activates instantly. Fire SM. Weapon-side buff activates on the first hit. Both active. Both doubled. **+18% Weapon Damage and +18% Skill Damage from bullet one.**
- **Swift Taunt** (rank I value, same LVL2 caveat): Hitting 3 enemies within 5 seconds taunts nearby enemies and grants Extra Health equal to 25% Max Health for 10 seconds. At 550 RPM, this is trivially always on. This is what justifies Glass Cannon.


### Secondary: PPSH-41 (T2, SMG)

This is a survival swap, not a primary damage tool. Talents: **Revitalize** (kills restore 4.20% Max Health for 3 seconds on a 4-second talent cooldown) and **Sentinel Aura** (hits within 20m restore Health based on Toughness, 5-second talent cooldown). Use it when your Extra Health buffer drops and you need a panic heal. Otherwise it lives in your back pocket.

You don't need godrolled everything on day one. Build toward it.

| TIER | SETUP | NOTES |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | Phalanx 4pc + Quick Draw 2pc | The training wheels version. Learn the engine, get comfortable with the loop. No Optimal Range patch yet, but the mods still work and In Sync still double-dips. Good enough to run missions without embarrassing yourself. |
