## What This Build Does

> Build credit: [**TikTokAndy**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHKfmW4BtEk) for the full walkthrough and gameplay (October 10, 2026). He calls it "Striker Aegis"; in game it's the [Tech Operator](/class/tech-operator/) with the Aegis Operations focus. Every stat below is his own reading from the video unless we say it came from our database or Ubisoft's patch notes.

This is a healer that barely shoots. The Defender Drone hangs a field over your team that keeps topping everyone up with Extra Health and, on TikTokAndy's tooltip, gives them +30% Weapon and Skill Damage. Four Mender Drones follow your teammates around healing them, a First-Aid Firefly covers emergencies, and the talent tree turns your team's kills into longer drone timers and shorter cooldowns. The better your DPS players are, the longer everything stays up.

TikTokAndy calls it his personal favorite PvE build and "the perfect complement to any DPS build." His pitch: a massive shield on your team at all times, 20 to 30% extra movement speed for them, and heals every few seconds. His own caveat: it's squishy, and it relies on teammates who can kill things. "This is a true team build," he says. They rely on you for healing, you rely on them for damage.

**The build in one breath:**

- **Kit:** Defender Drone (signature), Mender Drone and First-Aid Firefly, with most of your drones handed to your teammates.
- **Gear:** Long-term Effect 4pc + Mechanical Expert 2pc, Engineering and Toughness main rolls, **Brainstorm** on the backpack.
- **Combo sets:** **Magnetic Recharge** (Defender Drone), **Combat Enhancer** (Mender Drone), **Reinforced Support** (First-Aid Firefly).
- **Weapons:** an MP7 with **Enhancement Trade II** and **Adaptation II** to pump Skill Intensity, and a Vector with **Torrential Downpour II** for Engineering.
- **OS Protocol:** **Recursive Treatment**.
- **The goal:** as much Engineering, Skill Intensity and Skill Duration as you can stack, then keep your team inside the field.

> [!NOTE] Revives work again
> TikTokAndy says the build is viable again because downed teammates can be revived properly. Ubisoft's October 2 patch fixed players not being revivable in No Respawn Zones, which we covered in [our revive bug story](/news/revive-bug-no-respawn-zones-fixed/). A healer is a lot more useful when "rez me" is an option.

## The Aegis Kit

Aegis Operations gives the Tech Operator the [Defender Drone](/class/tech-operator/) as its signature, with Mender Drone and First-Aid Firefly as its two skills. What each one does here:

- **Defender Drone (signature):** a field around you, with a 12.5-meter radius on his screen, that keeps giving everyone inside Extra Health ("three bars," as he puts it) and, per his tooltip, +30% Weapon and Skill Damage. It ticks every second. You can leave it at a fixed spot, but he keeps it on himself so it moves with him. His screen shows a 39.55-second cooldown and a 31.5-second duration, but he says it often lasts much longer in practice. He thinks your team's kills are what extend it, though he admits he doesn't know exactly how.
- **Mender Drone:** goes to the ally you target and keeps healing them. A specialization overview we checked says it heals you when nobody is targeted; TikTokAndy keeps one on himself either way. His tree lets him run four at once.
- **First-Aid Firefly:** a heal you send to a targeted ally (the same overview says it heals you when there's no target).

Two more things on the screen that matter:

- **The Aegis field.** A 15-meter zone around you that several talents care about. Some pay out only while no enemies are inside it, others reward kills outside it. In practice: keep the fight at mid to long range.
- **Explosions kill drones.** Grenades and rockets destroy them. TikTokAndy says NPCs generally don't shoot your drones otherwise.

## The Talent Tree: What He Leans On

TikTokAndy says he took the tree as far as he could before it started asking for **Electronics Omega Components**, and he needs four more to finish it. The nodes below are the ones he highlights.

> **On the names:** only Focused Defense is a name we could confirm (Ubisoft's Season 1 Phase 3 notes fixed its cooldown reduction). The other effects are his readings, and the auto-captions mangle most of the names, a few of them into the names of unrelated gear talents and OS Protocols. Match on the effect text in game.

| Effect (his reading) | Why he cares |
|---|---|
| **Focused Defense:** kills and assists by allies affected by the Defender Drone reduce your skill cooldowns by 7% | He calls this "the most important thing to realize" with the Defender Drone: the more your team kills, the longer it lasts and the sooner it's back. |
| Kills and assists by allies targeted by a Mender Drone add 20 seconds to that drone's duration and restore 30% of its health (5-second cooldown) | Why his Mender Drones feel "basically infinite" in the gameplay. |
| Raises the maximum number of Mender Drones to 4, and to 6 at the next rank | The rank he can't afford yet. Six drones at once is the dream. |
| Each Mender Drone grants its targeted ally +2% Weapon and Skill Damage, up to 6 stacks (+3% per drone at the next rank, he says) | His math: six drones on one DPS player is +18%, which is why he thinks the build works in duos. |
| An Aegis field 15 meters around you grants Engineering while no enemies are inside it (14 on his screen, 21 at rank three) | He tells teammates to give him space. "A lot of the time they don't listen." |
| Kills inside the Aegis field cut skill cooldowns by 4%, kills outside it by 8%; hits on enemies outside the field cut Mender Drone cooldown by 0.6% | The reason he calls this an open-space, mid-to-long-range healer. |
| Mender Drone gives the ally it follows +20% Movement Speed (+30% below 30% Health) | Your whole team "moving around at light speed." |
| First-Aid Firefly heals an extra 30% on allies below 30% Health and gives them 15% Damage Reduction for 5 seconds | An emergency button that rewards waiting until it's an emergency. |
| While the Defender Drone is on them, Mender Drone and First-Aid Firefly healing can overheal, turning 66.6% of the excess into Extra Health for 10 seconds | He has never seen the Firefly overheal and suspects it's bugged. |

## Gear: Long-term Effect 4pc + Mechanical Expert 2pc

**Set bonuses:**

- **Long-term Effect:** 2pc Skill Health +12%, 3pc Skill Duration +14.4%, 4pc Engineering +18%. TikTokAndy reads the same three numbers.
- **Mechanical Expert:** 2pc Skill Duration +9.6%.

He says those set bonuses aren't included in the attribute numbers on his stat screen, so the real totals are higher than the table above. His gear screens settle the split: the mask, body armor (a T1 Athena Missile Vest), backpack (a Mountain Hiking Pack) and kneepads (Urban Warfare Kneepads) are Long-term Effect, and the Mechanical Expert pair is the "Neptune" Protection Gloves and the Jaivardhan Thigh Rig holster. Neither of those two can roll Long-term Effect in the in-game Armory, and our planner link uses the same layout.

| Slot | Main rolls | Attributes and talent | His verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mask (Long-term Effect) | High Engineering | Received Healing, Damage to Elites | He'd reroll Damage to Elites into Skill Intensity, Skill Duration, Skill Radius or Skill Cooldown Recovery once he has the materials. |
| Body Armor (Long-term Effect, T1) | Lots of Engineering and Toughness | Skill Cooldown Recovery; talent **Fearless Charge** (sprinting grants +21% Damage Reduction in our database) | His weakest piece. He uses the sprint Damage Reduction while repositioning but calls the talent "not good" and the vest "terrible." He keeps rolling Emergency Retreat II, which he says is good but not optimal. |
| Backpack (Long-term Effect) | Not read out | Received Healing, Armor; talent **Brainstorm** (+15% Skill Duration, plus another +15% within 20 meters of an ally) | "This is the one you want for the backpack." The attributes are the part he'd improve. |
| Gloves (Mechanical Expert) | Toughness and Engineering (+31 and +230 on screen) | Received Healing (+10.7%), Skill Intensity (+3.17%) | Fine, but Skill Duration or Skill Radius would beat Received Healing. |
| Holster (Mechanical Expert) | A big Engineering roll, Toughness, basically no Firepower (+4 Firepower, +89 Toughness, +189 Engineering on screen) | Skill Duration (+7.8%) and Skill Cooldown Recovery (-3.93%), both close to max | His model piece: "that's kind of what we're looking for on all the pieces." |
| Kneepads (Long-term Effect) | Toughness and Engineering | A max Skill Intensity roll and a high Skill Radius roll | His other favorite. |

**Gear mods:** Engineering, Armor and Skill Intensity, with Signature Ability Charge Efficiency as a bonus. He keeps each mod on the same gear set so swapping builds doesn't get confusing. He calls his mods "pretty bad," then goes through them and decides they're "not as bad as I thought," which is the most honest mod review we've heard. **Tactical Augmentations:** slot them. His forgotten ones were the difference between 6,725 and 8,760 Armor. His backpack's track starts with +11.67% Armor and his holster's with +10% total Health (details in our [Tactical Augmentation Modules guide](/guides/tactical-augmentation-modules-guide/)).

|---|---|---|

| **Adaptation** | Body shots +4.80% Skill Duration, headshots +6% Skill Intensity, each for 8 seconds | II: he reads +6.2% and +8.4%, but his MP7's tooltip shows the body-shot bonus at +6.72%, so both fit the usual rank II step (1.4x the base). | **Last Stand** | Hits behind cover grant +24% Skill Duration for 10 seconds | Same 24% |

**His MP7 trick:** he empties the magazine down to about 15 rounds, then fires single shots inside the last 20% of the magazine to build Enhancement Trade stacks, and says it "jacks up the skill intensity" by a lot. Our database's tooltip ties stacks to each 20% of the magazine you empty, so we can't promise single shots beat steady fire. "Prepping my gun," as he calls it, means building Adaptation and Enhancement Trade stacks first, then casting every skill while they're active.

**The duration test:** he timed a Mender Drone on camera. Cast cold, it lasted 44 seconds. Cast after prepping the MP7 and picking up a couple of kills, it was still going past a minute, roughly double by his count. That was with Brainstorm only half active, because no ally was within 20 meters.

## How to Play It

1. **Hand out the drones.** Tap a teammate's name, then press the skill. Give your DPS players the Mender Drones and keep at least one on yourself.
2. **Prep, then cast.** Build Adaptation and Enhancement Trade stacks with the MP7 before a fight, then cast while the bonuses are up.
3. **Hang back, but keep your team in the field.** He sits 15 to 20 meters behind his DPS player, close enough to watch everyone's Health and never in front of their shots. When the Defender Drone is up, remember its radius is about 12.5 meters on his screen; with two teammates he stands in the middle so both stay inside it.
4. **Keep enemies out of your Aegis field.** Fight in open space at mid to long range. He calls corners the worst place for this build.
5. **Don't recast the Defender Drone early.** Pressing it again ends the active one and starts the cooldown. Wait until it has actually expired.
6. **Save the Defender Drone for the fights that need it.** If your DPS has a room handled, keep it for the next one. When your team is killing a lot, he has seen it last around five minutes.
7. **Watch for explosives.** Grenades and rockets kill drones. NPCs, he says, generally don't shoot them.
8. **Pick your spot for boss fights.** On Precious Cargo's final boss he prefers the bridge you start on to the corner many teams pick, where enemies crowd into your Aegis field and the boss's explosives take out the Defender Drone. He says he's never died on the bridge. Heads up: his solo [Double Barrel video](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/) gives the opposite advice for that ending (stay off the bridge, fight from a nook on the right), so his two videos disagree. Our guess is that it depends on the build: a healer wants open space, a shotgun wants enemies close.

**Team composition:** it's better with a full team of four, and better still if everyone else does serious damage. His examples: two Vanguards with a Tactical Pharma or a Bulwark. He also played it as a duo with a Double Barrel player at 30,000 Power Score, which is about the best-case scenario. For the gun side of that pairing, see our [Misdirection Double Barrel Commando build](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/). If you'd rather heal off Toughness than Engineering, the Field Medic's [Muscular Nurse](/builds/muscular-nurse-combat-medicine-build-nadie/) is the other healer on the site, and our [Tech Aegis build](/builds/tech-aegis-engineering-build-tampishach/) is the Aegis Operations take for players who want the drones to hit things.
