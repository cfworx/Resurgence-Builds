A healer that barely shoots. **The Defender Drone wraps the team in Extra Health, four Mender Drones follow your teammates around, and every kill they get keeps the drones up longer.**

Build by **TikTokAndy**, posted October 10, 2026 on Season 2 Phase 2. He calls it "Striker Aegis": in game it's the Tech Operator's Aegis Operations focus.

## How to Play

1. **Hand out the drones.** Tap a teammate's name, then press the skill. Mender Drones go to your DPS players, but keep one on yourself.
2. **Prep, then cast.** Build Adaptation and Enhancement Trade stacks with the MP7, then cast every skill while they're up.
3. **Hang back, keep the team in the field.** He sits 15 to 20 m behind his DPS player. The Defender Drone's field (12.5 m on his screen) gives everyone inside Extra Health and, on his tooltip, +30% Weapon and Skill Damage.
4. **Keep enemies out of your 15 m Aegis field.** Tree nodes reward an empty field and kills outside it, so fight in open space at mid to long range. Corners are the worst.
5. **Don't recast the Defender Drone early.** Pressing it again ends it and starts the cooldown. Save it for the fights that need it.
6. **Watch for explosives.** Grenades and rockets kill drones. NPCs, he says, generally don't shoot them.

## What He Says

- **"The perfect complement to any DPS build,"** and his favorite PvE build right now. It's "a true team build": they rely on you for healing, you rely on them for damage.
- **Bring killers.** Best with a full team of four doing serious damage, like two Vanguards with a Tactical Pharma or a Bulwark.
- **PvE only.** The kill-driven duration extensions don't work quite the same in the Dark Zone, in his experience.
- **The Defender Drone's duration acts up.** Far longer than the tooltip one fight, early the next. He suspects a bug.
