**Most Bulwark tanks give up on damage. Banjru's doesn't: Healing Elites and Self-Propelled Shield push his Armor to 17,838 and his Health to 430,521, and the guns still roll Unhinged II, Performer II and Weapon Critical Hit Damage for 159.05k DPS on his loadout screen.**

Build by **Banjru** (in game as Banita), on Season 2 Phase 2, and his video is on this page. Every card, roll and number below is read off his in-game screens. We haven't re-tested it ourselves.

Power Score 27.23k: Firepower 516, Toughness 2.40k, Engineering 405, DPS 159.05k, Armor 17,838, Health 430,521. His Specialization is level 30 and his character level 40, both maxed.

For comparison, TikTokAndy's [Unflinching Bulwark](/builds/unflinching-bulwark-juggernaut-build-tiktokandy/) reads 469,379 Health and 13,405 Armor at 2.73k Toughness: more Health, less Armor, and a gun he says is for Damage Reduction, not damage.

## The Guns

**First Wave Vector 45 ACP** (SMG, level 50 T2): Weapon Critical Hit Damage +16.5%, Weapon Multi-Shot Chance +9.93%.
- Unhinged II: "Grants +25% Weapon Damage. Inflicts -10% Stability and -10% Accuracy." Rank I gives +18%.
- Performer II: +25% Weapon Damage on enemies within 20 meters, -8% farther away.

**Scorpio** (exotic shotgun, level 50 T1, Power Score 2493, 130 RPM, 7 rounds): Weapon Critical Hit Damage +13.79%, Damage to Elites +9.34%.
- [Septicemia](/database/exotic-weapons/scorpio/): hits inflict Venom, up to 7 stacks, with Bleed at 1 stack, Confusion at 3, Shock at 6 and extra damage equal to 10% of Damage Dealt at 7.
- Spec-Ops II: "Hits within 20 meters grant +28% Rate of Fire and +7% Incoming Healing for 3 seconds." Rank I gives +20% and +5%.
- Sentinel Aura II: "Hits within 20 meters restore (840 x Toughness)% Health. Talent Cooldown: 5 seconds." Rank I restores (600 x Toughness)%.

## Swaps

- **No Scorpio:** no standard weapon rolls both Spec-Ops and Sentinel Aura. Keep [Sentinel Aura](/database/weapon-talents/sentinel-aura/) if you can, because its healing is what keeps Rock Solid on.
- **No First Wave Vector 45 ACP:** the Modified USC, P90, KSG and Police T821 also roll both Unhinged and Performer.
- **Rank I talents work too:** Unhinged +18%, Performer +18%, Spec-Ops +20% and +5%, Sentinel Aura (600 x Toughness)%, Rock Solid +15%.
- **Want more tank and less gun:** TikTokAndy's [Unflinching Bulwark](/builds/unflinching-bulwark-juggernaut-build-tiktokandy/) puts Damage Reduction on every slot, and Nadie's [Involuntary Nap Time](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/) uses the same Healing Elites, Rock Solid and Unflinching core for stuns.
