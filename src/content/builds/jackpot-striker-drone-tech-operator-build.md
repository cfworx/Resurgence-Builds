## How to Play

1. **Anchor in cover.** Rampart stacks while you stay still, and Overwatch adds +15% Skill Damage after 3 s behind cover.
2. **Striker Drone out, Drone Lock on the target.** Stabilized Flight adds +6% Skill Crit Chance for 20 s.
3. **Drone Overload.** For 30 s, drone hits can trigger Optimized Targeting, and every crit can explode (Thermite Charge).
4. **Dump the M44 magazine.** Every 20% emptied stacks Enhancement Trade; below 30%, Ammo Supply can hand bullets back. The steps depend on magazine size, so count your rounds.
5. **Moving? Mk16.** 10 hits in 5 s is Torrential Downpour's Engineering, and Rapid Charge brings Drone Overload back sooner. Anchor, then back to the M44.

## Before You Copy It

- **Last verified on Patch 1.1.2.2.** Season 2 didn't change Jackpot, Fire Cycle, Rampart, the three drone mod sets or the gun talents, and Phase 2 added a clearer drone duration indicator.
- **Gear mods got better:** since Season 2 Phase 1 they can roll Skill Crit Chance, Skill Crit Damage and Skill Multi-Shot Chance.
- **Why a T1 backpack:** the Ziba Low Profile Pack is the only Jackpot backpack that rolls Overwatch.
