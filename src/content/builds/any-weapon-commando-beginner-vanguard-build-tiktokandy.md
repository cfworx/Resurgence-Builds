## What This Build Does

> Build credit: [**TikTokAndy**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtsTpS78aTA) for the walkthrough (September 19, 2026) and his [solo Heroic Chinatown run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6dGaisNKCyE) with an updated version (September 24, 2026). This is the build he ran before he found a Double Barrel shotgun; his later build is our [Misdirection Double Barrel Commando](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/). Every stat below is his own reading unless we say it came from our database.

This is a Vanguard Commando DPS build that doesn't care which gun you have. The engine is **Weapon Critical Hit Chance**: get the sheet to about 60%, let Scanning Pulse and Tactical Link stack the rest, and spend most fights critting. **Fury Strike 4pc** turns all those crits into damage, **Injured Concealment II** turns getting shot into damage, **Siege II** keeps you alive when the room fills up, and **Augmented Lethality** turns the crowd into extra Weapon Damage.

TikTokAndy is upfront about the downsides: it's "very squishy," and in his words "really slow." His other advice is the most beginner-friendly line in the video: "you don't need to be perfect in this game, you just need to have something you're working toward."

**The build in one breath:**

- **Weapons:** whatever you've got, ideally a high-RPM SMG or an assault rifle with crit and damage rolls. He used an Enhanced ACR or a Lightweight M4 with a P90.
- **Gear:** Fury Strike 4pc + Phalanx Attack 2pc, **Injured Concealment II** on the body armor, **Siege II** on the backpack, crit stats everywhere.
- **Skills:** Tactical Link with **Commando** (later **Bugle Call**), Smart Cover with **Optimized Armor**, Scanning Pulse with **Targeted Surveillance**.
- **OS Protocol:** **Augmented Lethality**.
- **The goal:** a crit chance ladder that tops out near 100% during Tactical Link.

## The Crit Ladder

TikTokAndy's math from his September 24 video, step by step:

| Step | Weapon Critical Hit Chance | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Out of combat | about 60% | Gear and mod rolls. He says he wouldn't go much higher than his 62%. |
| Scanning Pulse up | about 70% | **Targeted Surveillance**: Pulsed allies get Weapon Critical Hit Chance equal to 3.60% of your Engineering for 10 seconds (our database). He says that's "over 12%" at around 400 Engineering. |
| Tactical Link up | about 100% | Tactical Link's own +20%, plus a talent he shows on screen that adds another 10% during Tactical Link |

At that point the **Commando** combo set (+10.20% Weapon Critical Hit Chance for 10 seconds per kill or assist during Tactical Link, our database) is mostly wasted, which is why he was switching Tactical Link to **Bugle Call** (+14.40% Rate of Fire, Damage Reduction and Movement Speed for 15 seconds on deploy). His advice for newer players: start on Commando while your crit chance is low, and move to Bugle Call once the ladder reaches the top without it. He also notes that 60 to 70% crit chance all the time matters, because Tactical Link isn't always up.

## Relentless: the Commando Buff He Says Nobody Talks About

"Relentless is like the key component of this build," TikTokAndy says, and he notes that people talking about Commando builds never mention it. This is his reading of the talent; it isn't in our database yet, so check the numbers in game.

- **What it does:** a stacking buff of +3% Weapon Damage and +1% Critical Hit Damage per stack, up to 10 stacks, so +30% Weapon Damage and +10% Critical Hit Damage at full stacks. He reads it as granting Signature Ability charge recovery as well.
- **How you build it:** by hitting enemies with your gun and using your skills. Grenade hits are supposed to have a 50% chance to add a stack, although he says he never sees it happen.
- **The extra:** Critical Hits while Relentless is active grant +4.40% Reload Speed for 10 seconds, up to 5 stacks.

His point about the numbers: with a crit chance under 100%, Weapon Damage is worth more than Critical Hit Damage, because it applies to every hit and not just the crits. Relentless hands out three times as much of it.

## Gear: Fury Strike 4pc + Phalanx Attack 2pc

**Set bonuses:**

- **Fury Strike:** 2pc Optimal Range +10%, 3pc Weapon Critical Hit Chance +10.8%, 4pc Weapon Critical Hit Damage +24%. With crits landing most of the time, he says the 4pc "basically just works out to a 24% damage boost."
- **Phalanx Attack:** 2pc Magazine Size +18%.

His September 24 pieces (he describes two of them as kneepads, so we can't say which of those two is the holster; our planner link has to pick one, and the set counts come out the same either way):

| Slot | Set | Talent | Attributes and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mask | Phalanx Attack | none | Critical Hit Damage, Multi-Shot Chance; one Tactical Augmentation |
| Body Armor | Fury Strike (T1, level 50) | **Injured Concealment II** | Skill Cooldown Recovery (he'd reroll it) and Critical Hit Damage, with a Firepower roll; three Tactical Augmentations. He says the talent "is really what makes this build shine." |
| Backpack | Fury Strike | **Siege II** | Critical Hit Chance and Multi-Shot Chance, Toughness core |
| Gloves | Fury Strike | none | Critical Hit Chance and Multi-Shot Chance |
| Holster or kneepads | Phalanx Attack | none | Weapon Damage and Critical Hit Damage |
| Holster or kneepads | Fury Strike (T1) | none | A high Firepower roll, Critical Hit Damage and Armor |

**The two talents (base versions; his readings for rank II):**

- **Injured Concealment:** hits taken grant +1.80% Damage for 10 seconds, up to 10 stacks. He reads Injured Concealment II as up to +25% after 10 hits, and pitches it as the "super glass cannon-y" pick: "I'm gonna kill you in one clip and that's it."
- **Siege:** Extra Health equal to 37.50% Max Health for 15 seconds when 2 enemies are within 20 meters, 30-second cooldown. His Siege II reads 52%, and on September 19 he worked out that it would take his roughly 81,000 Health to about 120,000 when it procs. If you want a PvP version, he suggests swapping it for the backpack talent that heals you when you drop below 20% Health (Emergency Measures in our database).

**Stats to chase:** Firepower first, then Critical Hit Chance, Critical Hit Damage, Multi-Shot Chance and Headshot Damage. His mod rule: any mod with Critical Hit Chance or Weapon Damage goes straight to level 20 and gets locked. Armor mods are fine too. And slot your **Tactical Augmentation Modules** ("manuals" in his video), which he calls "the easiest stat gain you're ever gonna get," then check them again every time you change gear, because he forgot once and paid for it.

## Weapons: Use What You Have

The build works with any weapon because the crit, damage and survival all come from the gear and the kit. TikTokAndy's two loadouts:

| Video | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| September 19 | **Enhanced ACR** (T1; our database lists it as the Enhanced ACR-E): Critical Hit Damage (near max) and Weapon Damage, **Strained II** + **Swift Taunt**, proficiency 9 of 10, two Tactical Augmentations | **P90**: **Precise Crit** + **Performer II**, Weapon Damage, a high rate of fire and a big magazine |
| September 24 | **Lightweight M4**: Critical Hit Damage and Headshot Damage, **Unhinged** + **Steady Handed II**, two Tactical Augmentations | The same **P90**, which he calls his real primary and a "god rolled" one |

| Talent | Base version | His notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise Crit** | 10 hits on an enemy grant +15% Weapon & Skill Critical Damage to all allies within 40 meters for 10 seconds | Helps the whole team, not just you |
| **Performer** | +18% Weapon Damage on enemies within 20 meters, -6% beyond | He reads Performer II as +25%, and says it's "why the gun is melting like it is" |
| **Strained** | Firing for 0.5 seconds grants Critical Hit Damage for 5 seconds, up to 3 stacks (10% per stack on Strained, 14% on Strained II, after a Season 2 Phase 2 tooltip fix; Ubisoft's notes call these tier 1 and tier 2) | His is Strained II, on a T1 ACR |
| **Swift Taunt** | Hitting 3 enemies within 5 seconds taunts nearby enemies and grants Extra Health equal to 25% Max Health for 10 seconds | "More of a PvE talent" |
| **Unhinged** | +18% Weapon Damage, -10% Stability and Accuracy | On a laser like the Lightweight M4, he says you won't notice the penalty |
| **Steady Handed** | Hits build Accuracy and Stability stacks; at 30 stacks they're consumed to refill the magazine | He suspects his is bugged, because he's sat on 30 stacks without seeing a refill |

**Tips that come with the guns:**

- **Level weapon proficiency.** He says getting it to level 5 or 6 wins back the accuracy Unhinged costs, and the proficiency upgrades add stability and damage too.
- **Accuracy and reload speed matter on SMGs.** His P90 Tactical Augmentation adds +20% Reload Speed and Accuracy, which took its reload speed to about 35%, and he says the gun felt slow to reload before that.
- **Learn damage types early.** Piercing worked for most of Chinatown, but the final boss needs Blast. Our [damage types guide](/guides/damage-types-color-bar-weakness-guide/) explains the weakness bar.

## Skills, Combo Sets and OS Protocol

| Skill | Combo set | 3pc effect | His notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tactical Link (signature) | **Commando**, later **Bugle Call** | Commando: +10.20% Weapon Critical Hit Chance for 10 seconds per kill or assist during Tactical Link. Bugle Call: +14.40% Rate of Fire, Damage Reduction and Movement Speed for 15 seconds on deploy. | Put Tactical Link Duration on every chip you can, then Critical Hit Chance, Critical Hit Damage, Weapon Damage or Intensity. |
| Smart Cover | **Optimized Armor** | Behind Smart Cover: +3.20% Damage Reduction, and hits have a 4.80% chance of restoring 1 bullet | He thinks it's the best one. **Armored Train** (hits behind Smart Cover have a 5% chance of granting +2% Weapon Critical Hit Chance for 20 seconds, up to 3 stacks) is his pick if your crit chance is still low. |
| Scanning Pulse | **Targeted Surveillance** | Pulsed allies get Weapon Critical Hit Chance equal to 3.60% of your Engineering for 10 seconds | His ranking: Targeted Surveillance, then Field Supplies, then Optimal Awareness (our database: +1.40% per scanned enemy within 70 meters, up to 8 times, so 11.2% at most; he reads it as capping at 8%). |

**OS Protocol: Augmented Lethality.** Our database lists it as a Superior Firepower protocol whose talent grants +4% Weapon Damage and +4% Incoming Healing for every enemy within 20 meters. His upgraded copy reads about 32 to 35% Firepower, and he says the stacking has no limit, so a crowded room is a buff. He tested a level 1 **Cover Shooter** against his level 42 Augmented Lethality and kept Augmented Lethality. **Assisted Infantry** is his other suggestion. Its main stat is Weapon Critical Hit Chance (+14.40% in our database) and its talent adds +2.50% Weapon Damage per second of continuous fire, up to 6 stacks, so he says you could move some crit chance off your gear onto other stats.

## Taking It Into Heroic Chinatown Solo

His September 24 run, as tips:

- **Kill the Grenadier first,** and always know where he is. He also forgot about the enemy with the bat once and paid for it.
- **Keep everything in front of you** and check your minimap; enemies drop in from the balcony.
- **Fight close for most gunfights,** and use the SMG from cover, where he finds it more accurate than an assault rifle.
- **Always use Smart Cover,** and try to line enemies up in a row.
- **Ignore the JTF officer stealing your cover.** On the bright side, he says the JTF make decent distractions.
- **On the final boss, don't blow up the truck.** He says you get more headshots during Tactical Link without it, which is one reason he thinks this part is easier solo than with teammates who always blow it up.
- **Swap to Blast for the final boss.** Piercing carries the rest of the mission.

The rewards are why you'll run it a lot: Superior Fabric, random T1 gear and weapon components to take your weapons to level 50, by his account.

## His Beginner Rules

1. **Pick one specialization and master it** before you build a second one, unless you're spending a lot of money.
2. **Lock the gear you use.** Deleted gear doesn't come back, he warns, and he locks everything he's wearing.
3. **Slot your Tactical Augmentation Modules** and re-check them after every gear swap.
4. **Don't expect perfection.** On September 19 he called his build "so far from being min max" and said it still did okay; five days later an updated version soloed Heroic Chinatown.
5. **Don't spend much money on it.** He says he wouldn't recommend paying for much, because just playing the game gets you "so much free stuff."

When you find a Double Barrel shotgun, his next stop is our [Misdirection Double Barrel Commando](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/) build, which stays on Vanguard Commando with Optimized Armor and Phalanx Attack (he recommends the 4-piece), but moves Tactical Link to Ammo Refill and Scanning Pulse to Field Supplies, swaps Fury Strike for Gunny Johnny and Injured Concealment for Suppressive Fire II, and by October had changed its OS Protocol and backpack talent too.
