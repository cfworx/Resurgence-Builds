Hotfix 1.1.1 was a small, fast-shipping fix focused on sound and UI bugs reported in the first two weeks after launch.

### Fixes Implemented
1. Sound issue on many devices fixed (frequently affecting VIVO devices)
2. Invisible UI when talking to the **Quartermaster** in the initial mission: fixed
3. Leaderboards not displaying players for some users: fixed
4. Multiple crashes across various mobile devices: fixed

No balance changes. Not one talent, gear set or number moved.

The notes encouraged players still hitting these issues to submit an in-game support ticket to provide the team with the data needed for a fast fix.

## Why This Matters

Hotfix 1.1.1 was the first post-launch patch and set the tone for Ubisoft's response cadence. The sound bug hit many devices (VIVO phones especially), and the Quartermaster UI break could soft-lock new players during the opening mission sequence. Both were critical-path issues that needed immediate attention.

For players who started at launch and hit the invisible Quartermaster UI, this was the difference between continuing the game and uninstalling. The leaderboard fix also mattered more than it looks: the notes only say leaderboards weren't displaying players for some users, but in our understanding that included the Conflict PvP rankings, and invisible rankings kill competitive motivation for early adopters fast.

This hotfix landed 13 days after global launch. The next major patch was [Patch 1.1.2](/patch-notes/patch-1-1-2/) on April 28, which shipped alongside the [PC Early Access launch](/news/pc-early-access-launch/). If you're still hitting audio issues after this hotfix, check the [error codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-error-codes/) for the latest Android and iOS audio workarounds.
