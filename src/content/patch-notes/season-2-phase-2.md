Season 2 Phase 2 went live on **Wednesday, September 16, 2026**, with the patch notes posted a day earlier on Steam and in Ubisoft's Help Center. The headliners: the **Diamondback** exotic on the free and premium pass tracks, **Exotic Sealed Caches** that bring back exotics from previous seasons, the biggest **Demolitionist** buff list since launch, no more **daily XP cap**, and a softer start for brand-new agents. Exotic mods also stopped coming pre-installed on exotic weapons, and Dark Zone exotics got 2,500 credits cheaper.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> On October 21 Ubisoft extended Season 2 Phase 2 "until we are ready to release a much bigger update later this year." Here's [what that means](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/). Our launch-day news post is [here](/news/season-2-phase-2-live-diamondback/).

## The short version

- **Diamondback exotic** on the free and premium track of the new Classified Ops Pass, plus Diamondback rewards in Dark Zone vendors, reward caches, weekly rewards and exotic mod sources.
- **Exotic Sealed Caches:** a new way to get exotics from previous seasons. Full odds in our [Exotic Sealed Caches guide](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/).
- **Exotic weapons no longer come with exotic mods**, mods moved into the pass, Dark Zone vendor exotics cut from **17,500 to 15,000 DZ Credits**, and dismantling an exotic now also gives **420 Encrypted Data**.
- **Daily XP cap removed**, and daily quests reworked with better rewards.
- **New player experience:** less enemy health in the tutorial and in the first playthrough of Main Missions 1, 2 and 3.
- **Demolitionist buffs** across the skill tree.
- **OS Protocol changes:** Dominating Presence nerfed, Concerted Strike and Relentless Assault fixed.
- **More mods from Hostile Nests**, and more weapon and gear rewards from replaying Main Missions.

## New items and rewards

### Classified Ops Pass

Ubisoft's article lists a 50-tier pass with a free and a paid track. Rewards include:

- **Diamondback** exotic weapon (free and premium track)
- **Football League** apparel set (premium track)
- **Milky Way** weapon cosmetic (premium track)

Ubisoft's notes add the Diamondback "to the Armory" without spelling out its weapon class, and we're not going to guess and drag a different Division game's Diamondback into this. Our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) tracks what's confirmed about it.

### Exotic Sealed Caches and exotic mods

- **Exotic Sealed Caches** were added as "a new way to acquire Exotic Weapons from previous seasons."
- **New Random Exotic Mod Caches**, and more exotic mod reward sources.
- Diamondback rewards were added to Dark Zone vendors, reward caches, weekly reward pools and exotic mod acquisition sources.

## Progression changes

This is the part that quietly matters most, especially for anyone who bounced off the Steam launch:

- **Removed the daily XP cap** "to support longer progression sessions."
- **Reduced enemy health in the tutorial** and during the **first playthrough of Main Missions 1, 2 and 3**.
- **Increased weapon and gear rewards from replaying Main Missions.**
- **Increased Weapon Mod and Gear Mod rewards from Hostile Nests.** Our [mod farming guide](/guides/how-to-farm-purple-gold-mods/) builds on this.

### Daily quest rewards, reworked

| Daily quest type | New reward |
|---|---|
| Weapon Kill quests | Weapons matching the quest's weapon type, with a chance of T2 at level 40 |
| Main Mission quests | Random weapons and gear, with a chance of T2 at level 40 |
| Dark Zone quests | Skill Mods |
| Hostile Nest quests | Weapon and Gear Mods |
| World Event quests | Upgrade Materials |

If you're freshly level 40, our [endgame tips guide](/guides/division-resurgence-endgame-tips/) turns this table into a daily routine.

### Exotic economy

- **Dismantling exotic weapons** now grants **420 Encrypted Data** on top of the existing materials.
- **Exotic weapons no longer come with pre-equipped exotic mods.**
- **Exotic mods were added to the battle pass** to make up for it, so Ubisoft says you'll still get the same number of mods from it.
- **Dark Zone vendor exotic price:** 17,500 to **15,000 DZ Credits**, again to adjust for the missing mods.

### UI and clarity

- Clearer indicators for **Tech Operator drone duration** and several activity-specific gameplay effects.
- Better cosmetic previews, battle pass reward presentation, store information panels and weapon skin displays.

## Balance updates

### OS Protocols

- **Dominating Presence:** scaling values updated and maximum scaling reduced to **35/42/49/56/63/70%**. The [Milkshake Machine](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/) build is the one on our site that cares most.
- **Concerted Strike:** fixed so it works within its intended **20 meter range**. Ubisoft's bug reporter closed the matching report on October 23 ([our roundup](/news/bug-reporter-fixes-october-23/)).
- **Relentless Assault:** fixed using the wrong damage type, and its tooltip now correctly references Weapon Damage.

Our [OS Protocol tier list](/tier-list/os-protocols/) and the [OS Protocol database](/database/os-protocols/) carry these notes.

### Demolitionist

| Talent or skill | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Explosive Seeker Mine cooldown | 18s | 14s |
| Combat First Aid (Engineering bonus) | 4/8/12% | 10/20/30% |
| Severe Concussion (Bleed chance) | 6/12% | 25/50% |
| Hollow Point Bullets (Bleed chance) | 1/2% | 2/4% |
| Healthy Shooter (rate of fire) | 8/16% | 12/24% |
| Strong Arm | 4% skill radius, 10s duration, 30s cooldown | 15% skill radius, 6s duration, 6s cooldown |
| Charged Shooter (Headshot Damage) | 6/12% | 15/30% |
| Targeted Blast (Vulnerable) | 3/6/9%, weapon damage only | 5/10/15%, now all damage |

Strong Arm going from a 30 second cooldown to 6 seconds is the eye-catcher, even with the shorter duration. Our read: Demolitionist went from "the class you play because you like explosions" to "the class you play because you like explosions and winning." See the [Demolitionist class hub](/class/demolitionist/) and the [Demolitionist Glass Cannon build](/builds/demolitionist-glass-cannon-build-pveenjoyer/), which predates these buffs.

### Weapons and gear

- **Berserker** (Skill Mod Combo Set) wasn't granting its intended weapon critical hit damage bonus. Fixed.
- **Strained** (weapon talent) description fixed: it said 7.3% instead of **10%** for tier 1 and 10.2% instead of **14%** for tier 2. Per Ubisoft's wording, only the description was wrong, so the talent was already using the higher numbers.
- Fixed some instances of "Enemy take increased damage" effects not working properly.

### Dark Zone

- New Dark Zone vendor offerings and Diamondback-related rewards.
- A separate visual presentation for Dark Zone PvE activities.
- Improved Dark Zone matchmaking for mobile and cross-platform groups.

## Bug fixes

- LMG Proficiency Quests were progressing from kills with any weapon instead of LMGs. Fixed (sorry, everyone who leveled LMG proficiency with a pistol).
- **Shockwave Spike** didn't work correctly with the **Bulwark Juggernaut** focus. Fixed, along with edge cases that let Shockwave Spike be used incorrectly when moving between cover and non-cover states. Relevant to [Involuntary Nap Time](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/).
- **Armory:** fixed rewards that couldn't be claimed, progression levels staying locked despite meeting requirements, and **incorrect damage type information shown for certain weapons**.
- **Crossplay settings** sometimes displayed the wrong state. Fixed.
- **Controls:** Android back-button behavior, controller navigation and **Steam Deck control configuration** improved.
- Battle pass UI issues, including wrong prices in insufficient-credit purchase popups.
- Tutorial navigation issues that could leave tutorials blocked.
- Specialization feedback and indicators improved, including Tech Operator drone duration.
- Event progression issues, reward and loot inconsistencies, cosmetic draw and store presentation, and localization "Text Not Found" errors.

## Follow-up patches in October

Phase 2 got three rounds of posted fixes in October, all shared on the official subreddit:

- **October 2:** fixed players not being revivable in No Respawn Zones, and flagged a possible Heavy Weight change Ubisoft was investigating.
- **October 6:** a client update fixed revive heart icons staying on screen.
- **October 14:** the **Heavy Weight** skill mod combo set's additional damage dropped from 25% to 5% of Max Health, plus an Armory reward display fix and a build database fix that names the Diamondback's talent, **Agonizing Bite**.

Full details: [October 2026 hotfixes](/patch-notes/october-2026-hotfixes/).

## What this means for you

- **New or returning players:** this is the friendliest the early game has been. Less enemy health, no XP cap, better replay rewards.
- **Demolitionist players:** time to respec and retest. Everything about cooldown math changed.
- **Exotic hunters:** previous-season exotics are back on the table through Exotic Sealed Caches, but read the [odds](/guides/exotic-sealed-caches/) before you open your wallet. The Dark Zone vendor is also cheaper now.
- **Multi-Shot builds:** Dominating Presence got capped. Redo your numbers.
