Patch 1.2.1 deployed on **May 20, 2026**. This is a targeted hotfix, not a content drop, aimed at the vendor bugs, inventory losses, and Classified Ops Pass purchase problems that followed the Phase 2 launch on May 11 and 12. It also includes a significant line about **Strawberry Milkshake** that every Phase 2 player needs to read. Source: [Ubisoft Mobile Help Center, Patch Note 1.2.1](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/41-the-division-resurgence/faq/2513-patch-note---1-2-1-update-20-may-2026/).

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> None of the official patch notes since list a stat buff for Strawberry Milkshake. What Ubisoft did ship: [Season 1 Phase 3](/patch-notes/season-1-phase-3-a-new-fire/) (June 24) fixed it showing Piercing instead of Shredding in the Armory and dropping with LVL1 talents instead of LVL2, sent owners an updated copy, and fixed Outpost Annihilation. [Season 2 Phase 1](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-1-the-call/) then fixed its talent debuff being applied on heal.

---

## Compensation - Sent to Every Account

Ubisoft is mailing this directly to your in-game mailbox. No claim button, no timer, no special offer. Just open your mail.

- **500,000 Basic Credits**
- **750 Phoenix Credits**

As far as we know, this is the largest blanket compensation the game has issued since launch.

---

### Vendor Fixes

The vendor had several issues after Phase 2 launched. Here's the cleanup:

- Fixed vendors not showing items the player could sell
- Fixed players being able to sell locked or favorited items
- Fixed the Dark Zone vendor displaying no item names

---

### Strawberry Milkshake - Fixes and an Official "Looking Into It"

This is the section everyone's been waiting for.

> *"We are aware that many players report underwhelming perks for the weapon and are looking into how this may be addressed."*
> (Ubisoft, Patch 1.2.1 notes)

That's not a buff announcement. It's an official, in-writing acknowledgement mid-phase, which is still useful: the feedback landed. Our advice at the time: don't spend recalibrations until something actually changes.

Additional Strawberry Milkshake changes in 1.2.1:

- **Anarchy talent description fixed**: per the notes, it wasn't showing the proper effect value. The notes don't list numbers; the in-game tooltip (also in our [exotic database](/database/exotic-weapons/)) reads **2.40% Weapon Damage over 4.50 seconds, up to 50 stacks; death transfers stacks within 12 meters.** The pre-fix tooltip reportedly showed 100% WD / 10s / 25m, numbers that inflated community expectations
- **Weekly Mission Exotic Manual / Booklet rewards** can now correctly drop Strawberry Milkshake recalibration material: worth stockpiling if you plan to recalibrate it later

---

### Lone Wolf Challenge

- Fixed the reset schedule. Lone Wolf now refreshes every 3 weeks as originally intended

---

### Classified Ops Pass

- Fixed players who purchased the pass **not receiving their purchase**
- Fixed pass level upgrades **costing more than the listed in-game price**

If you were hit by either of these bugs, Ubisoft says to contact support via the **in-game support button**. Have your transaction ID ready; support will likely ask for it.

---

### Inventory Items Disappearing

- Fixed the bug where items would randomly disappear when inventory was full

Ubisoft notes that **edge cases may still exist**. Their recommendation: if it happens again, submit a support ticket immediately. Our recommendation: screenshot your inventory before and after every patch until this is fully stable.

Items lost before this patch are being handled case by case through support tickets. Community reports put some pre-1.2.1 support offers as low as 300 credits for lost god-rolls, so the 500K/750 Phoenix Credits blanket payout today is a clear step up.

---

### Localization Fixes

Not glamorous, but real, especially for non-English players who have been staring at mystery text since Phase 2:

- "Sold Out" now translates correctly in the Specials store
- Exotic Weapon Tactical Augmentations Cache now has correct translations in the Dark Zone vendor
- Fixed untranslated text appearing after purchasing deals in non-English languages
- Fixed "Text not found" placeholder showing for the Exclusive SHD Key reward

---

### Known Issues - Still Not Fixed

Ubisoft explicitly acknowledges the following are **not resolved** in this patch. This list is in the official patch notes, unusually transparently:

- Gamepad support issues on iOS
- **"Outpost Annihilation"** side mission not progressing (reportedly broken since beta), still listed as under investigation
- Some purchases still not being received in-game: contact support with a transaction ID
- Tactical Augmentation reward from the Dark Zone quest cannot be claimed
- Weapon proficiency blocked in some cases (a community workaround for iOS: logging into the PC client once reportedly clears it)

---

### What This Patch Doesn't Include

To be clear: **Patch 1.2.1 is not a content patch.** There are no new missions, no new exotics and no balance changes at all (the Strawberry Milkshake line is a "looking into it", not a number change). The next content milestone is **Phase 3 in June 2026**, which brings the Scorpio exotic, the Freemen apparel set, and a refreshed Classified Ops Pass.

We'll have full coverage of Phase 3 the moment it drops.

## Player Impact

Patch 1.2.1 is the trust patch. The 500K Basic Credits and 750 Phoenix Credits compensation acknowledges that Phase 2’s launch was rough, and the transparency of listing known-but-unfixed issues in the official notes is unusual for a live-service studio. Most devs bury those acknowledgments. Ubisoft put them in bold.

For build-focused players, the key takeaway is: **do not invest recalibration materials into the Strawberry Milkshake yet.** Ubisoft's "looking into it" is in writing, but it isn't a promise of new numbers. Farm the Weekly Mission Exotic Manuals now so you're ready to recalibrate if and when the weapon changes.

The inventory fix is the critical one. If you lost items before this patch, your path is a [support ticket](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/app/41-the-division-resurgence/) with screenshots. If you're still experiencing connection issues, check the [error codes guide](/guides/division-resurgence-error-codes/) for the full troubleshooting stack. And if you're wondering what the [Phase 3 Scorpio exotic](/news/season-1-2-3-roadmap/) will bring, we'll have coverage the day it launches.
