Season 2 is here, and it arrived the same day the game launched on Steam: **Wednesday, August 5, 2026**. Phase 1 is called **The Call**, and under the new Classified Ops Pass and the free Bighorn assault rifle sits the biggest systems patch Resurgence has had since launch. Damage type bonus rolls are gone, weapon mods were rebuilt from the stat pool up, exotic mods are now loot, base crafting time dropped nine hours, and the Commando Double Barrel shotgun finally got the nerf the Dark Zone was praying for.

Everything below comes from the official patch notes the developers posted on Reddit (mirrored on The Division Wiki) and Ubisoft's Season 2 articles. Where we add our read on what a change means, we say so.

> [!INFO] Update (October 2026)
> Season 2 Phase 2 followed on September 16 with the Diamondback, Exotic Sealed Caches and a pile of Demolitionist buffs ([patch notes](/patch-notes/season-2-phase-2/)), and on October 21 Ubisoft extended Phase 2 ([details](/news/season-2-phase-2-extended/)).

## The short version

- **New Classified Ops Pass (50 tiers):** The Bighorn exotic assault rifle and its Tactical Augmentation Module are free. Premium adds the Vintage Elegance apparel set and Antique Weaponry skin. An optional Elite Pass was sold "as an exclusive to The Call" (Ubisoft's wording; an Elite Pass was also offered in Season 1 Phase 3).
- **Damage type bonus attributes removed:** weapons now roll All Weapon Damage or All Skill Damage instead, and new weapon mods dropped them (plus All Damage) for Weapon Damage, Skill Intensity, Headshot Damage and crit damage stats.
- **Weapon mod rework:** main stats up 100%, attribute bonuses down 25%, new attribute pool.
- **Exotic mods rework:** removable, lootable, and sold in weekly Dark Zone crates.
- **Gear mod pool expanded** with offensive and defensive stats.
- **Crafting:** 24 hours to 15 hours base, clan reduction 54% to 50%.
- **Nerfs:** Shockwave Spike (stun 3.5s to 2s, cooldown 12s to 14s), Commando Target Acquisition and Concerted Strike, Steady Reload.
- **Reworked:** Ammo Refill (18% chance for 4 bullets instead of 72% for 1: the same average, less reliable) and Recycling (longer cooldown, bigger refill).
- **Quality of life:** a field of view slider, crossplay matchmaking selector, mobile-only Dark Zone instances, SHD Keys in weekly rewards, and the Scorpio added to the Dark Zone vendor.

## New content in The Call

### Classified Ops Pass and The Bighorn

The new pass has 50 tiers on two tracks. Ubisoft's article lists the free track rewards as **The Bighorn** exotic weapon and **The Bighorn Tactical Augmentation Module**, and the premium track as the **Vintage Elegance** apparel set and the **Antique Weaponry** weapon cosmetic. Ubisoft also sold an **Elite Pass** on top of Premium "as an exclusive to The Call," which gives a one-off pass progress boost and bonus items.

The Bighorn is Ubisoft's own description of fun: an assault rifle that fires full auto from the hip and switches to semi-auto when you aim down sights, where "each headshot will add extra damage, and more headshots mean more stacked bonuses." In other words, it rewards the one thing most of us are worst at. Full details and how it compares with the other exotics are in our [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/).

## Damage type bonus attributes are gone

This is the change that quietly rewrites every build guide on the internet. Ubisoft's explanation, verbatim:

> "It was previously possible to get Damage Type bonus attribute on a weapon of a different damage type, which didn't bring any benefits and was frustrating. As such, all damage type bonuses have been removed in favor of 'All Weapon Damage' or 'All Skill Damage'."

In practice: Blast, Shredding and Piercing Weapon or Skill Damage were removed from the pool of bonus attributes for weapons. No more Piercing Damage rolls on a Blast gun. Damage types themselves still matter for enemy color bars, which our [damage types guide](/guides/damage-types-color-bar-weakness-guide/) covers. The notes describe the roll pool, so don't assume a weapon you already own was converted. Check its attributes in the Armory.

## Weapon mod rework

Ubisoft's stated goal was to "make the primary stat more important, increase the diversity of the pool and move away from flat bonuses which were more impactful for high RPM weapons than low RPM weapons." The numbers:

- **Main stats increased by 100%.**
- **Attribute bonus stats reduced by 25%.**
- The Power Score total stays the same for a yellow mod, but the split moves from 20% / 40% / 40% to 40% / 30% / 30%.
- Leveling a legacy mod updates its growth to the new values. Otherwise old mods are unchanged.
- **New weapon mod attribute pool:** Weapon and Skill Piercing, Blast, Shredding and All Damage were removed. Added: Weapon Damage %, Skill Intensity %, Headshot Damage %, Weapon Critical Hit Damage % and Skill Critical Damage %. This only affects new weapon mods.

Our read: legacy mods keep their values unless you level them, but if your build leaned on flat bonuses on a high-RPM SMG or LMG, new mods will feel different, and every reload or fire-rate figure in an older guide (ours included) needs a re-check. Every [build on the site](/builds/) now carries a Season 2 change note for exactly this reason.

## Exotic mods rework

Exotic mods used to come with fixed primary stats and attribute bonuses. Now:

- They follow the same rework as weapon mods.
- **They can be unequipped** from exotic weapons.
- **They can be looted:** one random exotic mod in the Weekly Quest, one random in the Elite Mission Challenge chest, and a random exotic mod crate for each exotic weapon at the Dark Zone vendor, one per week each, for **2,500 DZ Credits**.

## Gear mod rework

The gear mod attribute bonus pool lost Elite Enemy and Enemy Leader Resistance, and gained Max Health %, Armor %, Received Healing %, Weapon and Skill Critical Hit Damage %, Weapon and Skill Multi-Shot Chance %, Weapon Damage %, Skill Intensity %, Headshot Damage %, Weapon and Skill Critical Hit Chance %, and Signature Ability Charge Efficiency %. More ways to make a gear mod useful, fewer ways to roll a resistance you'll never notice.

## Crafting rebalance

- Base crafting time cut from **24 hours to 15 hours**.
- Total clan duration reduction adjusted from **54% to 50%**.
- Crafting level XP now comes faster early and slower later, with fewer crafts needed overall to reach max level.
- Rarity chances per level were raised slightly, so purple items appear earlier.

The clan nerf is smaller than the base-time buff: a fully boosted craft goes from roughly 11 hours (24 × 0.46) to 7.5 hours (15 × 0.5).

## Balance changes

### Bulwark: Shockwave Spike

- Stun duration: **3.5s to 2s**.
- Cooldown: **12s to 14s**.

After the June 26 hotfix put a cooldown on the Pummeling Shield OS Protocol's stun, this is the second hit to stun-lock builds, and Bulwark feels it most. If you run [Involuntary Nap Time](/builds/involuntary-nap-time-bulwark-juggernaut-build/) or the [Breacher build](/builds/bulwark-breacher-build-saiynergy/), read their Season 2 notes before your next Dark Zone trip.

### Vanguard: Commando and the Double Barrel

Ubisoft went after the Double Barrel Shotgun's use of the "Bullet Back" mechanic in Commando's Signature Ability, while trying to keep it intact for everything else:

> "We believe that aside from the way the Double Barrel Shotgun can take advantage of it, the mechanic is fun and core to Commando's identity."

The changes:

- **Target Acquisition** (Commando talent): signature ability charge **30% to 5%**, cap **90% to 20%**.
- **Concerted Strike** (Commando talent): now triggers off **shots** rather than hits. Ubisoft noted the text was only updated in English, with other languages fixed next phase. (These notes call it a Commando talent; the Season 2 Phase 2 notes and the drop-rate table list a Concerted Strike OS Protocol, so check which one your build uses.)

The Double Barrel didn't die, it changed engines. Creators rebuilt it around Misdirection Insurance and ammo restores, which is what our [Double Barrel Commando build](/builds/misdirection-double-barrel-commando-build-tiktokandy/) covers.

### Other talent and combo changes

| Item | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Ammo Refill (Skill Mod Combo Set) | 72% chance, 1 bullet back | 18% chance, 4 bullets back |
| Steady Reload (weapon talent) | +18% Weapon Damage for 5s | +5% Weapon Damage for 10s; hidden buff on "bolting" removed |
| Recycling (body armor talent) | 1s cooldown, refills 4.5% | 5s cooldown, refills 20% of the magazine (tooltip fixed in English) |
| Marksman Rifle ammo reserve | 120 | 200 |

The in-game Build-Making Database still showed Ammo Refill's old text (a 29% chance of recovering 1 ammo at the lowest tier) when we checked on October 28, 2026. TikTokAndy's live tooltip on September 30 already showed the 4-bullet version (at 7% on his set), so trust the patch notes on that one.

### Other balancing

- **Recon:** Tactical Link now benefits from improved auto-aim support. Good news for the [Recon support build](/builds/agenthuntzmans-leaderboard-ranking-vanguard-support-build/).
- **Weekly Bounties:** safeguards against progression and reward desync during the Season 2 rollout.
- **Bounty missions:** several encounters got stronger enemy compositions, with some standard enemies replaced by veterans.
- **Lone Wolf Challenge Floor 14:** star time thresholds raised from 900/720/600/540/480/420/360 seconds to 1080/900/840/780/720/660/600 seconds. Read that as "more time to finish," which is the rare change that makes Lone Wolf less miserable.

## Bug fixes worth knowing

- **Classic AK-47 recalibration** only gave LVL2 talents, and a few other weapons rolled talents from the wrong pool on recalibration (First Wave Vector 45 ACP, RPD, M700 Carbon, M700). Fixed.
- **Stability on mods:** the VX1, ACOG and CQBSS scopes plus three exotic mods (Pincer & Claw grip on the Scorpio, Kiss of Death on Lady Death, Bladed Underbarrel on the Strawberry Milkshake) weren't counting Stability in the mod's Power Score. Fixed.
- **Field Repair** (Skill Mod Combo Set) gave Weapon Damage instead of Fire Rate. Fixed.
- **Dominating Presence** (OS Protocol) gave Armor instead of Weapon Multi-Shot Chance. Fixed. This one affects the [Milkshake Machine build](/builds/milkshake-machine-strawberry-milkshake-lmg-build/), whose Multi-Shot math predates the fix.
- **Strawberry Milkshake:** its talent debuff was being applied on heal. Fixed.
- **Proficiency:** fixed incorrect Proficiency level displays after Proficiency Quests.
- **PC:** Steam language settings are now respected, missing Borderless and Windowed mode text was added, and there's a Resolution selector scroll-down menu. Also fixes for missing PC settings localization, mouse cursor state and menu behavior.
- **UI and controls:** a **field of view slider**, plus fixes for ESC key behavior, animation skipping, controller navigation in pass screens and assorted menu edge cases.
- **Mobile:** trailers play correctly again, no more unwanted popups when using a gamepad on Android, and keyboard mode switching works.
- **Missions and bounties:** fixed the MM08 Bounty boss and elite shield behavior, wrong enemy spawns in several bounty missions, and progression issues in event and bounty objectives.
- Assorted fixes to audio settings (now affecting the main menu), the battle pass reward previews, Armory display overlaps, achievements, store offers, Ubisoft Connect buttons and news images.

## What this means for your build

- **Recheck every damage stat.** If your guide or loadout says "roll Weapon Blast Damage," that advice is dead. All Weapon Damage is the replacement.
- **Stun Bulwarks got weaker again.** Two stun nerfs in six weeks is a message.
- **High-RPM weapons lost some of their mod advantage** by design.
- **Exotic farming got friendlier:** removable mods, DZ mod crates and the Scorpio at the DZ vendor (we track exotic sources in the [exotic weapons list](/guides/division-resurgence-exotic-weapons-list-2026/) and the [exotics database](/database/exotic-weapons/)).
