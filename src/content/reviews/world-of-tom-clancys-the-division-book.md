**Checked November 15, 2026.** If you only buy one Division book, and especially if Resurgence is where you joined the series, this is the one that answers the basic questions. Who are these agents with the orange watches? Who activates them? What happened to New York? It is a large hardcover reference book made by Dark Horse with Ubisoft, and it covers the series up to The Division 2.

## What it is

It is a lore book. There is no plot and no main character. Dark Horse describes it as covering the Division's history, training and structure, its key characters and factions, and a timeline from the outbreak to the events of The Division 2.

![The front cover of The World of Tom Clancy's The Division: a pale cover with an orange phoenix seal reading The Division, SHD, Extremis Malis Extrema Remedia](/images/reviews/world-of-the-division-front-cover.webp "The front cover is the SHD seal.")
![The back cover of The World of Tom Clancy's The Division, with three agents looking out over Washington and the Dark Horse, Ubisoft and Massive logos by the barcode](/images/reviews/world-of-the-division-back-cover.webp "The back: agents overlooking Washington, D.C.")

The seal on the front carries the motto *Extremis malis extrema remedia*. Its first two words are the title of the Dark Horse comic, [Extremis Malis](/reviews/the-division-extremis-malis-comic/).

## Inside our copy

![The World of Tom Clancy's The Division open at Chapter 1, What Is the Division, with a close-up of an agent wearing a watch with an orange ring](/images/reviews/world-of-the-division-what-is-the-division.webp "Chapter 1 starts from zero: what the Strategic Homeland Division is.")

The book is laid out like a briefing. Chapter 1 defines the Strategic Homeland Division and what its agents are allowed to do. From there it moves through how people are chosen for it.

![A spread headed Recruitment showing concept sketches of Division agents in everyday clothes and in field gear](/images/reviews/world-of-the-division-recruitment-pages.webp "The recruitment pages: agents before and after activation, in concept sketches.")

Chapter 3 is Activation and Deployment, the moment the series is built on: sleeper agents called up when everything else has failed. The book carries situation reports on the cities too.

![A spread headed Situation Report: Washington D.C. with concept art of a settlement and an agent in the foreground](/images/reviews/world-of-the-division-washington-dc-pages.webp "A situation report for Washington, D.C., the setting of The Division 2.")

Most of each spread is artwork, with the text in short blocks, so it is a book to dip into.

## If you came here from Resurgence

Ubisoft's page for Resurgence says you play one of the first wave of SHD agents in New York City. This book explains those words: what SHD is, what the first wave was, how activation works. None of it was written about Resurgence, so do not expect the mobile game's characters or missions. Treat it as the background the game assumes you have.

Once you know the world, the rest of the site is about playing in it: start with [which class to pick](/guides/which-specialization-to-pick/).

## Which edition

There are two. The standard hardcover is the one in our photos. Dark Horse also made a Limited Edition in April 2019, in a clamshell case with a lithograph and a cloth poster.
