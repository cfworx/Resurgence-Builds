**Checked November 15, 2026.** New York Collapse is the Division book that does not look like a tie-in. It is made to look like an object from inside the story: a paperback survival guide that someone carried through the outbreak and wrote all over. It came out with the first game in March 2016, and it is now hard to find at a normal price.

## What it is

The cover does not say Tom Clancy or Ubisoft on the front. It says *New York Collapse: A Survival Guide to Urban Catastrophe*, by Warren Merchant, the guide's author inside the story. Turn it over and the first thing you see is handwriting: "Property of April Kelleher".

That is the idea of the whole book. The printed text is the survival guide. The handwriting around it is April's account of the outbreak, and the publisher describes it as her attempt to learn why New York fell.

## Inside our copy

![An open spread of New York Collapse headed Fragile Systems, with a printed chart of New York's commuter population and handwritten notes filling the margins](/images/reviews/new-york-collapse-fragile-systems-pages.webp "The printed guide and the handwritten story share every page. The entries are dated.")

The printed guide is a practical one: our photos show sections on how a city depends on outside supplies, why a pandemic is the biggest threat, and what to pack in a go-bag. The notes run beside it in more than one color of ink, with dates and underlining.

![A spread from New York Collapse about the go-bag, with a red sketch of a backpack and dense handwriting in black and red ink around the printed text](/images/reviews/new-york-collapse-go-bag-handwritten-notes.webp "The go-bag pages. On some spreads the handwriting fills every margin.")

Then there are the loose pieces. Chronicle counts seven removable items. Three of ours are below: the fold-out tourist map of Manhattan, the transit card resting on it, and a folded missing-person poster.

![A fold-out tourist map of Manhattan from New York Collapse with handwritten circles on it and a transit card resting on top](/images/reviews/new-york-collapse-manhattan-map.webp "The pull-out map of Manhattan, marked up by hand, with the transit card.")

![A folded poster from New York Collapse headed Missing, have you seen this woman, with a photo and the line last seen in Hell's Kitchen](/images/reviews/new-york-collapse-missing-poster.webp "The missing poster. Last seen in Hell's Kitchen.")

## If you came here from Resurgence

This book was written for the 2016 game, ten years before Resurgence launched, and nothing in it is about the mobile game. What it shares is the place and the moment. Ubisoft's page for Resurgence says the game is set in New York City and that you play one of the first wave of SHD agents. New York Collapse covers that same city falling apart, from a civilian's side of it.

The poster's Hell's Kitchen is a name Resurgence players know too: it is one of the areas in our [safe codes walkthrough](/guides/division-resurgence-safe-codes/).

## The catch: finding one

It no longer looks to be in print. Chronicle's page had no buy button when we checked, and the copies on AbeBooks that day were around $100 whether new or used, against a $24.95 price at release. If you buy used, the loose items are the thing to ask about, because they are the first things to go missing.

If the price puts you off, the story does continue somewhere cheaper: the novel [Broken Dawn](/reviews/the-division-broken-dawn-novel/) follows April Kelleher after she leaves New York and is still sold new.
