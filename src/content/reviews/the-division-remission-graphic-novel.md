**Checked November 15, 2026.** Remission is the other Division comic. It is a single graphic novel set in Washington, D.C. before the events of The Division 2, first published in France in 2019 and brought out in English by Dark Horse at the end of 2021.

## What it is

The cover carries the line "Based on a Ubisoft creation" above the logo, and the names Morvan and The Tribe below the art: writer JD Morvan and the studio that drew it. The back cover gives the premise. After the virus, Division agents are fighting to protect Washington from violent factions, and four survivors who grew up as friends find themselves pulled apart by that fight.

Dark Horse calls it a prequel to The Division 2, the game set in that city.

## Inside our copy

![Pages 44 and 45 of The Division: Remission, a spread of painted comic panels showing armed figures, a river and smoke over a city](/images/reviews/remission-pages-44-45.webp "Pages 44 and 45 of our copy.")

It comes from the French album tradition: one complete story told in a single 64-page hardcover.

It does not depend on the other comic. [Extremis Malis](/reviews/the-division-extremis-malis-comic/) follows one agent on the road from New York to Washington. Remission is set in Washington itself, with a cast of its own.

## If you came here from Resurgence

Resurgence is a New York game, and this book is about Washington. If the mobile game is your only Division game so far, that setting will be new to you, and the [lore book](/reviews/world-of-tom-clancys-the-division-book/) is the better first buy. If you have played The Division 2, this is a story set in its city.

## Is it still easy to buy?

It was published in English in December 2021, so it is the newest book on this shelf, but one distributor we checked had it out of stock on November 15, 2026.
