**Checked November 15, 2026.** Extremis Malis is the quickest way into the Division's story on paper: a three-issue Dark Horse comic, collected in a slim hardcover, about one agent and one grudge. It is set in the gap between the first game and The Division 2.

## What it is

The story is by Christofer Emgård with art by Fernando Baldó and colors by Michael Atiyeh. The back cover of our copy sets it up: months after a bioweapon devastated New York, Division agent Caleb Dunne loses his partner on a mission that goes wrong, and he goes after the woman responsible. The trail runs from New York City to Washington, D.C., and both the back cover and Dark Horse call the book a prequel to The Division 2.

The title is two words from the motto on the Division's seal. You can see the full motto on the cover of [The World of Tom Clancy's The Division](/reviews/world-of-tom-clancys-the-division-book/).

## Inside our copy

![Pages 30 and 31 of The Division: Extremis Malis, a full-color comic spread of Division agents moving and fighting among trees](/images/reviews/extremis-malis-pages-30-31.webp "Pages 30 and 31. The comic is in full color.")

It is a full-color comic bound as a hardcover. The collected edition marks its chapters with divider pages: Chapter Two opens on a grey page drawn like photographs pinned to a wall.

![The Chapter Two divider page from The Division: Extremis Malis, a grey page with pencil sketches drawn as pinned photographs](/images/reviews/extremis-malis-chapter-two-page.webp "The Chapter Two divider page.")

At 72 pages it is short, and it is priced like it: the cover price printed on ours is $14.99.

## If you came here from Resurgence

This comic has nothing to do with the mobile game's story. It was made for The Division 2 in 2019, and its events lead toward Washington. Read it as more of the same world: Division agents in a country that is coming apart.

## Is it still easy to buy?

Less than it was. Dark Horse published it in 2019, and one bookseller we checked, Porchlight, listed it as sold out on November 15, 2026. Used copies and remaining stock are the likely route.
