**Checked November 15, 2026.** The GameSir G8 Plus works with The Division Resurgence over Bluetooth. It is the controller RapidF5 plays on, and every photo on this page is of his. Ubisoft's own list of supported controllers names PlayStation 5, Xbox and Backbone pads and does not mention GameSir, so this page shows what actually happens when you connect one.

## Does it work with The Division Resurgence?

Yes. With the pad connected, the game draws controller prompts on the HUD.

![Two hands holding a GameSir G8 Plus with The Division Resurgence on the phone, an agent crouched behind a snow-covered police car, with L1 and R1 prompts over the skill buttons at the bottom of the screen](/images/reviews/gamesir-g8-plus-in-hands-playing.webp "Resurgence on the G8 Plus. Look at the bottom of the screen: L1 and R1 sit over the skill buttons, and a triangle sits by the weapon panel.")

Two details from that photo are worth knowing before you buy:

- **The prompts are PlayStation-style.** The game shows L1, R1 and a triangle, while the caps on this pad say A, B, X and Y. They line up by position: L1 and R1 are the LB and RB bumpers, and the triangle is the top face button.
- **The Home light is blue.** GameSir's FAQ gives each mode a color: yellow for Android, blue for DS4, red for Switch and cyan for G-Touch. Blue is the mode named after the PlayStation 4 pad, which fits the prompts the game chose. We have not tried the other modes in Resurgence yet.

We are not the only ones who got it working. TechRadar's review of the game says its writers tested a GameSir G8+ along with pads from Nacon, SteelSeries and 8BitDo.

## A walk around the pad

![A black GameSir G8 Plus controller seen from above on a wooden table: left stick, D-pad, minus and M buttons on the left grip, a textured sliding middle section with the GameSir name on it, and plus, A, B, X, Y, right stick and home button on the right grip](/images/reviews/gamesir-g8-plus-front-top-down.webp "Sticks are offset, Xbox-style. The middle section stretches to take the phone.")

The layout is a standard console one: offset sticks, a D-pad, four face buttons, plus and minus, a Home button with a light ring, and an M button under the D-pad. GameSir uses the M button for extras: M with a trigger switches that trigger to a hair-trigger mode.

![The back of the GameSir G8 Plus: textured grips with one back button on each side, marked L4 and R4](/images/reviews/gamesir-g8-plus-back-buttons-l4-r4.webp "Two back buttons, L4 and R4, sit where your middle fingers rest. Tom's Guide says they are mapped in the GameSir app.")

![The bottom edge of the GameSir G8 Plus, showing a small pairing button with a wireless symbol next to a USB-C port](/images/reviews/gamesir-g8-plus-usb-c-port-pairing-button.webp "The bottom edge: the pairing button and the USB-C port that charges the pad.")

![The top edge of the GameSir G8 Plus, showing the left and right bumpers and triggers with textured faces](/images/reviews/gamesir-g8-plus-triggers-bumpers.webp "Bumpers and triggers. GameSir says the triggers and both sticks use Hall effect sensors.")

One thing you will not find on it is the model name. The pad itself only says GameSir.

## How to pair it

GameSir's steps, which match the buttons in our photos:

1. Hold the pairing button beside the charging port for about two seconds, until the light blinks fast.
2. Open Bluetooth settings on the phone and pick the controller.
3. To change mode, hold Home with A, B, X or Y. The light color tells you which mode you are in.

Ubisoft says the same thing from the game's side: connect a controller to the phone directly with Bluetooth.

## What it lacks, and what we have not tested

- **No headphone jack and no pass-through charging**, according to Tom's Guide, which still ranks the G8 Plus as its best mobile controller overall. The USB-C port charges the pad, not the phone.
- **It has a battery to keep charged.** GameSir gives the capacity (1000 mAh) but we have not timed how long it lasts in Resurgence.
- **iPhone.** Ubisoft's May 20, 2026 patch notes list "Issues with gamepad support on iOS" as a known issue, and we have not found a later note saying it was fixed. That applies to any controller, not just this one.
- **The back buttons in Resurgence.** We have not mapped L4 and R4 for this game yet. GameSir's FAQ talks about its app on Android only.
- **PC.** We have not used this pad on the PC version.

When we have tested those, this page gets updated.

## Where it sits against the alternatives

Review sites that test phone controllers do not agree on one winner. Tom's Guide puts the G8 Plus first, WIRED and TechRadar pick the Backbone One, IGN picks the Backbone Pro, and GamesRadar's newest list is topped by the ELO Vagabond. Backbone is also the only phone-controller brand Ubisoft names for Resurgence. We set all of that out, with what Ubisoft officially supports on each platform, in [which controllers work with The Division Resurgence](/guides/division-resurgence-controller-guide/).
