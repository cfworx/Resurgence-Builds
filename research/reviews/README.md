## The controller

The owner called it "the GameSir elite controller". Nothing on the pad names the model: it says GameSir on the cradle
and carries a maker's label (Dashine Electronics, Shenzhen). We identified it as the **GameSir G8 Plus** (the Bluetooth
one) from what the photos show against GameSir's own FAQ: a pairing button beside a USB-C charging port on the bottom
edge, back buttons marked L4 and R4, an M button under the D-pad, a Home button with a light ring, a stretching cradle
and no plug for the phone. If the owner's box or order says otherwise, the review, the controller guide and the gift
guide all need the name and the Amazon link changed.

What the photos show in the game (4174 to 4180): controller prompts on the HUD (L1 and R1 over the skill buttons, a
triangle by the weapon panel) with the Home light blue, which GameSir's FAQ calls DS4 mode. Another player's name on
the screen is blurred in the pictures we publish.
