# Tom Clancy's The Division: books, comics, audio, tabletop and merchandise

Research report for resurgencebuilds.com. Compiled November 15, 2026. Prices and stock notes are what the cited page showed on that date.

How to read this report

- "Verified" means I saw the fact on the cited page (publisher, Ubisoft, author, retailer, library catalog) or, for Amazon identifiers, in an Amazon search result whose title names the exact book.
- Amazon.com product pages cannot be fetched by our tools (robots block). Every ASIN and ISBN-10 below was seen in an Amazon search-result title or on a third-party page that links the exact title. Amazon price and stock were never seen, so they are "not verified" everywhere.
- Where two sources disagree (page counts, imprint, dates) both values are given. The owner has many of these books and can settle page counts from the physical copy.
- Web search budget for this session ran out part way through section 3, so a few follow-up searches were not run. They are listed at the end.
- No em dashes or en dashes are used in this file, so text can be lifted into site content.

### 1c. Reading order

Official position: I found no complete reading order published by Ubisoft, Aconyte, Dark Horse, Penguin Random House, Chronicle or Titan. The only numbered sequence from a publisher is Aconyte's Operation Crossroads 1, 2, 3 (Recruited, Compromised, Hunted).

Publication order (verified dates, see table 1a):

1. The Art of Tom Clancy's The Division (March 2016)
2. Tom Clancy's The Division: New York Collapse (March 8, 2016)
3. Extremis Malis issue #1 (January 9, 2019); issue #3 (April 3, 2019); hardcover (July 23, 2019)
4. Remission, French original (March 8, 2019)
5. Broken Dawn (UK March 15, 2019; US March 26, 2019)
6. The World of Tom Clancy's The Division (March 19, 2019; Limited Edition April 9, 2019)
7. The Division: Hearts on Fire (July 15, 2021)
8. Remission, English edition (December 8, 2021)
9. Recruited (February 1, 2022 ebook and audio; March 8, 2022 US paperback)
10. Compromised (December 6, 2022 ebook and audio; December 20, 2022 US paperback)
11. Hunted (January 2, 2024)
12. Tom Clancy's The Division Roleplaying Game (Kickstarter May to June 2026)

- Edition details that the list sites skip: US and UK editions of Broken Dawn, limited editions of the art and lore books, ebook and audiobook dates for the trilogy.
- Availability flags: New York Collapse now sells at collector prices; Extremis Malis and Remission show sold out at some retailers; Broken Dawn and the art book look in print.
- Current official merch (the 11 Ubisoft Gear Shop items) separated from discontinued collectibles (figurines, the watch replica, the vinyl), each dated.
- The owner's own photographs of the physical copies, which no competing page has.
- A visible "checked on" date, since prices and stock move.
